\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Dataset shift and importance weighting.}
Covariate shift is one member of a broader taxonomy of train--test mismatch
\citep{quionero2009dataset}.  Direct density-ratio estimation avoids separately
estimating two high-dimensional densities \citep{sugiyama2007direct}.  Kernel
two-sample methods provide useful diagnostics, although failure to reject equality
does not establish the covariate-shift assumption \citep{gretton2012kernel}.
Distributionally robust optimization targets performance across a prespecified set
of distributions or groups \citep{sagawa2020distributionally}; our objective is
instead a confidence bound for one target population.

\paragraph{Distribution-free uncertainty.}
Conformal prediction gives marginal coverage guarantees under exchangeability
\citep{vovk2005algorithmic,lei2018distribution}.  Weighted conformal prediction
extends this logic to covariate shift when likelihood ratios are known
\citep{tibshirani2019conformal}.  Risk-controlling prediction generalizes coverage
to bounded losses \citep{angelopoulos2024risk}.  Our bound is a compact
importance-weighted analogue aimed at post-training model selection rather than
construction of prediction sets.

\paragraph{Selective prediction.}
Abstaining systems trade coverage for conditional accuracy
\citep{geifman2017selective}.  Each abstention threshold can be treated as a
candidate in our finite family, so selection over thresholds is covered without
reusing the certification sample for training.  The guarantee concerns the
unconditional bounded loss specified by the user; conditional risk requires a
separate treatment of the random acceptance probability.
