\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

This manuscript is a layout-complete example, not a report of executed
experiments; all benchmark names and numbers must be replaced by validated
research before submission. Methodologically, nearest-neighbor retrieval can
amplify annotation errors, reveal memorized text, or privilege varieties that
are dense in the datastore. Character-skeleton checks do not cover entities
whose standardization legitimately changes spelling. The controlled benchmark
also omits speech transcription, mixed scripts, and conversational context.
Finally, latency was estimated for a single hardware profile and should not be
generalized to mobile or serverless deployment.
