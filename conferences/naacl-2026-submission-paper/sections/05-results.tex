\subsection{Overall performance}

Table~\ref{tab:main-results} shows that \method improves chrF by 2.3 points
and exact match by 3.9 points over ByT5. Retrieval at every step produces only
a 0.5 chrF gain and increases entity corruption, supporting selective rather
than unconditional use of memory. \method reduces ECR from 7.1 to 4.9, a 31\%
relative reduction. Its 17\% latency overhead is also smaller than
\textsc{AlwaysRetrieve}'s 42\% overhead because only 29\% of spans trigger a
lookup.

\subsection{Performance by variety}

The gain is largest on Coastal, the smallest and most divergent variety
(Table~\ref{tab:variety}). This pattern is consistent with the memory supplying
examples where parametric learning is weakest. North shows a smaller gain but
retains the best absolute score. No variety loses performance.

\subsection{Calibration and coverage}

Figure~\ref{fig:calibration} plots accuracy against reported confidence. The
base model is over-confident in the two lowest-confidence bins. The Brier term
and retrieval features move \method closer to the diagonal, reducing ECE from
6.8 to 3.9. At a gate threshold of 0.7, accepted memory proposals are 91\%
accurate and cover 18\% of output positions.
