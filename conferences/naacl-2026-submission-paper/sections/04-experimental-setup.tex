\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Controlled benchmark}

\dataset{} is an illustrative benchmark with three anonymized varieties:
North, Central, and Coastal. Each contains conversational messages paired
with meaning-preserving standardizations. Table~\ref{tab:data} summarizes the
split. Speakers, conversations, and near-duplicates are disjoint across
splits. A 5,000-pair memory is sampled from the training partition only.
Named entities are annotated on development and test examples.

\subsection{Models and optimization}

We compare identity copying, a character-level Transformer, multilingual T5,
and a byte-level T5 baseline. The two retrieval systems use the same
byte-level backbone: \textsc{AlwaysRetrieve} interpolates at every step,
whereas \method uses Equations~\ref{eq:entropy}--\ref{eq:gate}. All neural
models have approximately 300M parameters. We train for at most 20 epochs with
AdamW, learning rate $3\times10^{-5}$, batch size 64, and early stopping on
development chrF. We set $k=8$, $\tau=0.42$, $\lambda_g=0.5$, and
$\lambda_b=0.1$ on the development set. Results report the mean of three
seeds; paired bootstrap confidence intervals use 1,000 resamples.

\subsection{Metrics}

We report corpus chrF, word error rate (WER; lower is better), exact sentence
match (EM), and entity corruption rate (ECR; lower is better). ECR is the
fraction of source entities whose surface form is changed when the reference
preserves it. Expected calibration error (ECE) uses ten equal-mass bins.
