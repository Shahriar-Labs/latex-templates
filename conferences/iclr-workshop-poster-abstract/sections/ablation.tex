\section{Ablation Study}

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} isolates the contribution of each component
of \modelname{}. We report VQA accuracy and FLOPs on the \dataset{}
validation split. The full model achieves the best accuracy--efficiency
trade-off; removing either the FLOPs regulariser or the residual
injector leads to a measurable accuracy drop, while ablating the
modality-specific masks degrades efficiency gains significantly.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \small
  \caption{%
    \textbf{Component ablation on \dataset{} VQA.}
    \checkmark\ denotes component enabled; \(\times\) denotes removed.
    Residual inj.\ = residual injector for pruned tokens.
  }
  \label{tab:ablation}
  \setlength{\tabcolsep}{5pt}
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
  \resizebox{\columnwidth}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc c c@{}}
    \toprule
    \rowcolor{TableHead}
    \color{white}\textbf{Model Variant}
      & \color{white}\textbf{Mod.\ Masks}
      & \color{white}\textbf{FLOPs Reg.}
      & \color{white}\textbf{Residual Inj.}
      & \color{white}\textbf{Acc.\ (\%)}
      & \color{white}\textbf{FLOPs (G)} \\
    \midrule
    \rowcolor{TableAlt}
    \modelname{} (full)    & \checkmark & \checkmark & \checkmark & \textbf{78.2} & \textbf{17.9} \\
    w/o FLOPs Reg.         & \checkmark & $\times$   & \checkmark & 78.4 & 31.6 \\
    \rowcolor{TableAlt}
    w/o Residual Inj.      & \checkmark & \checkmark & $\times$   & 77.1 & 17.9 \\
    Shared Masks           & $\times$   & \checkmark & \checkmark & 77.8 & 21.4 \\
    \rowcolor{TableAlt}
    No Mask (full attn.)   & $\times$   & $\times$   & $\times$   & 78.6 & 55.7 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}%
}
\end{table}

\noindent
\textbf{Key insight:} The FLOPs regulariser is critical for reaching
the 17.9 G operating point without manual tuning; without it, the model
converges to near-full attention. The residual injector prevents the
1.5\% drop that otherwise occurs when pruned visual tokens lose their
spatial context.

\subsection{Mask Transfer across Scales}

\begin{tcolorbox}[contributionbox, title={Transfer Experiment}]
  \small
  Masks learned on ViT-L/14 are directly applied (zero-shot) to
  ViT-B/16 and ViT-H/14 checkpoints by projecting the scoring
  weights to the target hidden dimension via a linear adapter.
  On \dataset{}-VQA:
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
    \item \textbf{ViT-B/16}: 73.9\% acc., 8.1 G FLOPs
          (vs.\ 73.5\% / 12.4 G for learned-from-scratch masks)
    \item \textbf{ViT-H/14}: 80.6\% acc., 31.2 G FLOPs
          (vs.\ 80.8\% / 29.7 G for learned-from-scratch masks)
  \end{itemize}
  Transferred masks match or nearly match retrained masks within
  \(\pm\)0.2\% accuracy, confirming strong cross-scale generalisation.
\end{tcolorbox}
