      \begin{tcolorbox}[highlightbox, title={Abstract}]
        \small
        Vision--language transformers achieve strong zero-shot performance
        but suffer from quadratic attention costs that limit deployment on
        resource-constrained hardware. We introduce \modelname{}, a method
        that dynamically prunes up to \textbf{62\%} of cross-modal attention
        tokens at inference time using lightweight, learnable sparsity masks
        trained with a differentiable top-\(k\) relaxation. On the \dataset{}
        benchmark---a new 1.4 M image--text pair evaluation suite covering
        twelve downstream tasks---\modelname{} matches the accuracy of a
        full-attention \baseline{} baseline while reducing FLOPs by
        \textbf{3.1\(\times\)} and wall-clock latency by \textbf{2.4\(\times\)}
        on a single A100 GPU. We further demonstrate that sparsity masks
        transfer across model scales and modalities without retraining,
        enabling plug-and-play efficiency for existing checkpoints.
      \end{tcolorbox}
