\section{Conclusion}

We presented \method{}, a terrain-memory interface that estimates both local geometry and
the probability that geometry will mislead a foothold decision. By propagating evidence,
repairing it with contact, calibrating risk, and constraining accumulated foothold risk, the
system remains useful during perceptual dropout instead of silently treating stale terrain
as truth. The illustrative experiments show complementary gains from temporal memory and
risk-aware control. Future work should model correlated map errors, extend the representation
to full 3D structure, and couple locomotion with active viewpoint selection.
