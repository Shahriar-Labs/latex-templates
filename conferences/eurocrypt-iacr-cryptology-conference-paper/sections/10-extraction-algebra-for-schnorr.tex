\section{Extraction Algebra for Schnorr}
\label{app:schnorr}

Suppose both $g^z=AY^c$ and $g^{z'}=AY^{c'}$ hold.  Dividing the equations gives
$g^{z-z'}=Y^{c-c'}=g^{y(c-c')}$.  Since $p$ is prime and $c\ne c'$, the element
$c-c'$ has an inverse in $\Z_p$.  Therefore
$y=(z-z')(c-c')^{-1}\bmod p$, establishing \cref{eq:schnorr-extract}.  A
verifier must first check that $A,Y\in\G$; otherwise the cancellation can take
place in an unintended subgroup and the extraction statement no longer
matches the claimed relation.
