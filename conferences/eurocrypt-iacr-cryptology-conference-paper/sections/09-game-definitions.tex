\section{Game Definitions}
\label{app:games}

For completeness, we spell out the two abort flags used in the proof.  The
simulator stores tuples $(X,c,\mathsf{origin})$ in its oracle table, where
$\mathsf{origin}\in\{\mathsf{adversary},\mathsf{proof}\}$.  Before inserting a
proof-origin entry $X=\mathsf{Enc}(pk,x,a,s)$, it executes
\[
  \mathsf{PreQ}\leftarrow
  \mathsf{PreQ}\lor [X\in\operatorname{dom}(T)].
\]
After insertion, it sets $\mathsf{SaltColl}$ if a different proof request has
the same $X$.  The proof of \cref{thm:main} aborts on either flag.  Keeping the
flags separate matters because they rely on different independence claims:
the pre-query argument conditions on the adversary's current table, whereas
the collision argument compares pairs of simulator-generated salts.
