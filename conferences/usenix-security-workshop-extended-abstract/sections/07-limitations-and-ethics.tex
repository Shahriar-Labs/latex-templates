\section{Limitations and Ethics}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our evaluation is a testbed study, not evidence of production effectiveness.
Its production-derived workflows preserve structural distributions but cannot
capture every form of organizational drift. Operation labels are deliberately
coarse; they reduce data collection but merge some security-relevant actions.
The current Markov scorer also treats two paths with the same adjacent edges as
equivalent even when long-range ordering differs.

Causal traces can reveal sensitive business relationships and user activity.
\system{} records pseudonymous trace IDs, workload identities, coarse operation
labels, and timing---never request bodies or user tokens. Deployments should
apply access control, encryption, short retention, and documented purpose
limitations to collector data. Security staff should review explanations and
deployment events before escalating an alert; the score is not evidence of
employee wrongdoing.
