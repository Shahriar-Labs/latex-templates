\section{Limitations and Broader Impact}

The evaluation uses simulated reverberation and English read speech; spontaneous
conversation, tonal languages, microphones with nonlinear distortion, and more
than two simultaneous speakers may expose different failure modes.  Persistent
state can also preserve an incorrect initial speaker assignment.  A deployment
should detect stream boundaries, offer a state reset, and report uncertainty
rather than silently treating separated audio as ground truth.  Separation can
improve accessibility and communication, but it may also enable unwanted
eavesdropping.  Consent, data minimization, and on-device processing are
appropriate safeguards.
