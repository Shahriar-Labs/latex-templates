\section{Conclusion}
We presented TrailBeam, a motion-predictive beam-steering system that fuses gait-phase estimation with a lightweight angular predictor to anticipate mmWave beam misalignment on wearable devices before it occurs. Across an eight-participant, three-route indoor study, TrailBeam reduced median re-alignment latency by 41\% and per-event energy by 34\% relative to a periodic-sweep baseline, and by 22\% and 12\% respectively relative to a reactive inertial-only tracker, while remaining within 2.1 percentage points of an oracle's link-alignment accuracy. We believe explicitly modeling periodic body motion, rather than only filtering it, is a promising direction for future mobility-aware mmWave access design.
