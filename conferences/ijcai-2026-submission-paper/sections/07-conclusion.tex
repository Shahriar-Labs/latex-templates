\section{Conclusion}
In this paper, we presented Deep Latent Alignment (DLA), a self-supervised representation learning framework designed specifically for dynamic graphs. DLA addresses the challenge of temporal drift by aligning node-level representations across consecutive graph snapshots using a novel temporal contrastive loss. Our evaluations on four temporal benchmark datasets demonstrate that DLA consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines. In future work, we plan to extend DLA to heterogeneous dynamic graphs and explore its application in large-scale recommendation systems developed by Nimbus and Meridian.
