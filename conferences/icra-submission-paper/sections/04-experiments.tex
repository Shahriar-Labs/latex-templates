\section{Experiments}
We validated our framework in both simulation and physical field tests. The simulator represents a dense pine forest with tree densities ranging from 0.1 to 0.5 trees per square meter.

The physical experiments were conducted using the Synapse-V4 quadrotor platform developed by Nexa Technologies \cite{nexa2024forest}. The drone is equipped with an Apex-200 companion computer (featuring a quad-core 2.4~GHz processor) and a single forward-facing Nexa Solid-State LiDAR. The sensor provides a 120-degree horizontal field of view at 20~Hz.
