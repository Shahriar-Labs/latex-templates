\section{Conclusion}
We presented a complete framework for autonomous high-speed UAV flight in dense forest canopies. By combining real-time solid-state LiDAR point clouds with a sparse Vertex-Graph representation, our system achieves low-latency planning and navigation. Simulation and field testing on the Synapse-V4 quadrotor show significant improvements in safety and path efficiency compared to conventional volumetric grid methods.
