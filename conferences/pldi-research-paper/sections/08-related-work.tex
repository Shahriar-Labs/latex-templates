\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Effect-handler implementation.}
Plotkin and Pretnar introduced the semantic account of algebraic effect
handlers \cite{plotkin2009handlers}; later work established their practical
expressiveness \cite{kammar2013handlers}. Type-directed compilation can select
efficient representations when row types expose enough structure
\cite{leijen2017type}. Abstracting effects clarifies how handlers relate to
other control operators \cite{biernacki2020abstracting}. \system is
complementary: it applies when static types deliberately leave the handler
open, using a checked runtime shape to recover a direct representation.

\paragraph{Speculation and deoptimization.}
Dynamic deoptimization was developed to retain source-level observability in
optimized systems \cite{holzle1992debugging}; subsequent formal work made the
correctness obligations of assume instructions precise
\cite{fluckiger2018correctness}. On-stack replacement generalizes transfer
between versions of active functions \cite{delia2018osr}. \system narrows
transfer to effect boundaries and adds multiplicity to the guarded facts. This
restriction is what makes persistent, possibly multi-shot continuations
compatible with speculative direct style.

\paragraph{Inline caching.}
Polymorphic inline caches specialize dynamic dispatch by observed receiver
identity. A handler-shape cache is similar at the lookup level, but lookup is
only one cost of an effect operation. \system additionally changes the
representation of the captured continuation and therefore needs explicit
reconstruction maps and a semantic relation between representations.
