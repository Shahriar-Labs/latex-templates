\starsection{Limitations}

\sys{CiteCheck-6K} covers four domains and three generator models; we do
not know how well \sys{SynapseCite} transfers to domains with sparser
public full-text availability (e.g., clinical medicine, where many cited
papers sit behind access barriers our retrieval index cannot reach) or to
newer generator models released after our corpus was collected. The
existence-check stage depends on bibliographic metadata coverage, which is
uneven across venues and years; a paper published outside our index's
crawl window would be flagged as \textsc{fabricated} incorrectly. All
annotation and generation in this work is in English, and we make no claim
about performance on literature reviews in other languages, where citation
conventions and available bibliographic indices both differ substantially.
Finally, \sys{SynapseCite} is a screening tool, not a substitute for
reading the cited paper: at 91.4 macro F1 it will still miss roughly one in
eleven problematic citations, and a workflow that treats a
\textsc{faithful} verdict as a guarantee rather than a prioritization
signal would inherit that error rate silently.
