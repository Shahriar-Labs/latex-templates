\section{Experimental Setup}

\subsection{The \sys{CiteCheck-6K} Corpus}
\label{sec:data}

We generated literature-review paragraphs by prompting three
instruction-tuned models -- \sys{Nexa-7B-Instruct}, \sys{Synapse-13B-Chat},
and \sys{Vertex-XL} -- with topic descriptions drawn from four research
domains: NLP, biomedicine, materials science, and economics. Each generated
paragraph was decomposed into (claim, citation) pairs, and three annotators
with graduate research experience in the relevant domain labeled each pair
as \textsc{faithful}, \textsc{fabricated}, \textsc{misattributed}, or
\textsc{distorted}, consulting the cited paper directly. Disagreements were
adjudicated by a fourth annotator following the adjudication protocol of
\citet{winter2021adjudication}; inter-annotator agreement before
adjudication was $\kappa = 0.79$.

The resulting corpus, \sys{CiteCheck-6K}, contains 6,400 labeled pairs:
2,190 faithful, 1,780 fabricated, 1,340 misattributed, and 1,090 distorted.
We split by \emph{generated paragraph} rather than by pair, so that no two
pairs from the same paragraph appear on opposite sides of the train/test
boundary, and reserve 20\% of paragraphs for the test set used throughout
Section~\ref{sec:results}.

\subsection{Baselines}

We compare \sys{SynapseCite} against two baselines. \textbf{Retrieval-only}
runs Stage~1 and Stage~2 of \sys{SynapseCite} but skips the entailment
classifier, predicting \textsc{faithful} whenever a passage is retrieved
above a cosine-similarity threshold tuned on a held-out development split,
and \textsc{fabricated} otherwise -- it cannot distinguish misattribution or
distortion from faithfulness. \textbf{Prompted-LLM judge} sends the claim
and the cited paper's abstract to \sys{Apex-Judge-70B} with a four-way
classification prompt and three in-context examples per class, following
the shared-task prompting convention of \citet{nakamura2021survey}.

\subsection{Metrics}

We report macro-averaged F1 across the four labels, and, since fabrication
detection matters most for the downstream use case of flagging a citation
for human review, we separately report recall on the \textsc{fabricated}
class alone. We also report median per-pair inference cost in USD,
estimated from public per-token pricing for each system's model calls
following the cost-aware evaluation protocol of
\citet{ahmadi2023costaware}, since a verifier that only a well-funded lab
can afford to run does not solve the problem for the researchers most
likely to rely on an LLM drafting assistant in the first place.
