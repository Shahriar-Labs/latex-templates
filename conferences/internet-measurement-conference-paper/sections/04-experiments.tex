\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}
We ran the measurement campaign for six consecutive weeks, from probes already enrolled
in the Meridian Measurement Panel as of the start of the campaign, supplemented by a
short recruitment push to improve coverage of fixed wireless and satellite access, which
were underrepresented in the existing panel. The final dataset comprises 3{,}116 vantage
points across 41 residential ISPs in 12 countries, spanning six access technologies:
fiber (GPON and XGS-PON), cable (DOCSIS 3.1), DSL (VDSL2), fixed wireless access (5G
FWA), low-earth-orbit satellite, and macro-cellular mobile (4G/5G). Each probe completed
both downstream- and upstream-saturating cycles roughly every 40 minutes throughout the
campaign, yielding just over 48 million completed load/idle cycle pairs after quality
filtering. Compute and bandwidth for collector infrastructure were donated by Apex Cloud;
panel recruitment and device logistics were supported by a grant from the Meridian
Research Foundation, and Nimbus Networks contributed a subset of fixed-wireless vantage
points from its own volunteer subscriber base.
