\section{Ethics}
\label{sec:ethics}
Panel enrollment is opt-in and was reviewed under Nexa University's human-subjects
protocol. Probes record only timing metadata and coarse ISP and access-technology labels.
No packet payloads, no destination hostnames outside the measurement infrastructure, and
no household-identifying information are collected. Probe traffic is rate-limited so that
saturation tests stay inside each volunteer's own access link and never load the ISP's
shared infrastructure beyond ordinary bulk transfers. Results are reported aggregated at
the technology and ISP level throughout this paper.
