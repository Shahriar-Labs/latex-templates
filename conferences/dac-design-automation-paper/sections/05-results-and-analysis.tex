\section{Results and Analysis}
\label{sec:results}
The simulation results indicate substantial gains in both throughput and energy efficiency. Table \ref{tab:results} summarizes the performance comparison of Vertex-Flow against three baseline architectures.

\begin{table*}[t]
\centering
\small
\caption{Performance and Energy Efficiency Comparison on Meridian Platform}
\label{tab:results}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Architecture} & \textbf{Node [nm]} & \textbf{Freq. [MHz]} & \textbf{Throughput [GOPS]} & \textbf{Energy [pJ/OP]} \\
\midrule
Nexa-Core \cite{nexa2024} & 28 & 500 & 128.4 & 4.56 \\
Synapse-V1 \cite{synapse2023} & 16 & 800 & 512.0 & 2.10 \\
Vertex-Alpha \cite{vertex2025} & 12 & 1000 & 1024.0 & 1.45 \\
\midrule
\textbf{Vertex-Flow (Ours)} & \textbf{12} & \textbf{1200} & \textbf{2048.8} & \textbf{0.78} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table*}

Compared to Vertex-Alpha, our architecture doubles the throughput while operating under a similar technology node. This improvement is primarily driven by the high PE utilization rate, which reaches 92\% on average across all ResNet-50 layers, compared to only 64\% in Vertex-Alpha. Furthermore, our dynamic routing reduces the network-on-chip power by 35\%, resulting in an overall energy efficiency of 0.78\,pJ/OP.
