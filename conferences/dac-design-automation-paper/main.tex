\begin{abstract}
Spatial accelerators dominate deep learning acceleration thanks to their parallelism and
local memory hierarchies, but standard grid-based arrays fall short of the throughput and
energy efficiency modern workloads need under tight power budgets. We introduce
\textbf{Vertex-Flow}, a spatial accelerator for synapse-based deep learning workloads on the
\textbf{Meridian} platform. A hybrid dataflow routes weights and activations dynamically to
keep Processing Elements (PEs) busy, and localized weight buffering with adaptive
accumulation routing cuts energy. Fabricated in a 12nm node, Vertex-Flow delivers
2.0$\times$ the throughput and 46\% lower energy per operation than state-of-the-art spatial
arrays.
\end{abstract}
