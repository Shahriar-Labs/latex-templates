\section{Conclusion}
In this paper, we introduced Meridian-Net, a decentralized self-supervised representation learning framework over latent graph structures. We formulated the task as a joint optimization problem and proposed a communication-efficient alternating minimization algorithm. Our experiments on graph and image classification benchmarks demonstrate that Meridian-Net outperforms traditional decentralized algorithms in both final representation quality and communication efficiency. Future work will explore extending Meridian-Net to dynamic graphs and incorporating differential privacy mechanisms.
