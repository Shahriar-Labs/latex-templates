\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

We presented \textsc{RepairLLM}, an automated program repair framework
that combines spectrum-based fault localisation, LLM-based patch
generation with repository-scale retrieval, and dual validation via
mutation testing. On the Defects4J benchmark, \textsc{RepairLLM} achieves
a plausible-patch rate of 47/83, outperforming prior work by 12\%, and
raises correct-patch precision to 74\%.

Future work includes extending \textsc{RepairLLM} to multi-language
projects (Python, JavaScript), incorporating dynamic execution
feedback into the patch-generation loop, and exploring fine-tuning
strategies that specialise the LLM for repair-specific objectives.
