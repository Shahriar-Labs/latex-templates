\section*{Ethical Considerations}
The session logs behind both benchmarks were collected under the search provider's privacy
policy and released only in aggregated, pseudonymized form: query strings were stripped of
personal identifiers before sampling, and no user identifier survives in the released
graphs. Query reformulation models can steer users toward some results and away from
others, so a reformulation policy trained on historical clicks may amplify existing
popularity bias. We report results stratified by query frequency (Section~\ref{sec:results})
for this reason, and we do not recommend deploying CSG without an online audit of which
reformulations it suppresses.
