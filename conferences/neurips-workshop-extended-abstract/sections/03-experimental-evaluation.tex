\section{Experimental Evaluation}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}

We evaluate on Office-Home~\citep{venkateswara2017officehome},
VisDA-2017~\citep{peng2017visda}, and DomainNet-126, the 126-class subset
commonly used for DomainNet~\citep{peng2019domainnet}. We use a ResNet-50
backbone initialised from ImageNet, train with SGD, and report the mean of three
seeds. Source-only, domain-adversarial training (DANN)~\citep{ganin2016domain},
and fixed-threshold self-training are reimplemented with the same backbone,
augmentations, and optimisation budget. Hyperparameters are selected on held-out
source data without target labels. For \method, we use $\tau_0=0.90$,
$[\tau_{\min},\tau_{\max}]=[0.70,0.97]$, and $\rho=0.95$ throughout.

We measure target top-1 accuracy, expected calibration error (ECE; 15 equal-width
bins), and pseudo-label coverage. The primary metric is mean target accuracy;
lower ECE is better. Reported coverage is the fraction of target examples whose
pseudo-label passes the training gate at the end of optimisation.

\subsection{Results and ablations}

\Cref{tab:main} summarises the main comparison. \method{} improves mean target
accuracy by 2.5 points over fixed-threshold self-training and lowers mean ECE by
3.8 points. Gains are largest on VisDA-2017, where the synthetic-to-real shift
produces sharply overconfident source predictions. Training variance also
decreases, consistent with the gate admitting fewer unstable pseudo-labels.

The ablation in \cref{tab:ablation} isolates the two reliability signals on
Office-Home. Adaptive thresholds alone improve mean-class accuracy, particularly
for the least frequent predicted classes. Agreement alone improves calibration
but reduces coverage. Combining both recovers useful coverage while obtaining
the best accuracy and ECE. Using a single global EMA instead of class-conditional
statistics loses 0.9 accuracy points, supporting the role of class adaptation.

\textbf{Sensitivity.}
Across $\tau_0\in\{0.80,0.85,0.90,0.95\}$, mean Office-Home accuracy varies by
less than 0.7 points. Removing clipping makes rare-class thresholds unstable in
early epochs and reduces accuracy by 1.1 points. EMA momentum is less sensitive
for $\rho\in[0.9,0.99]$, although higher values delay adaptation after abrupt
changes in target composition.
