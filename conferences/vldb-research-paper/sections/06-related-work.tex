\section{Related Work}

Related work should compare mechanisms and assumptions rather than assemble a
list of neighboring papers.  The relational model established a declarative
foundation for data management~\cite{codd1970}.  Cost-based plan selection made
explicit the connection between statistics, alternatives, and physical
operators~\cite{selinger1979}.  Modern studies have shown how estimation errors
and correlated data can affect plan quality~\cite{leis2015}.  Learned components
have since expanded the design space for indexes and other database
structures~\cite{kraska2018}.  A manuscript using this starter should replace
this survey with the closest data-management literature and explain precisely
which limitation remains unresolved.
