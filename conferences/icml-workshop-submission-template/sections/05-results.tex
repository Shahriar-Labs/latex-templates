\section{Results and Performance}
\label{sec:results}
Our experiments show that Vertex-Fed achieves superior accuracy compared to both centralized and decentralized baselines, while significantly reducing communication overhead.

\subsection{Communication and Accuracy Trade-offs}
Table~\ref{tab:results} provides a quantitative comparison of the algorithms after $1{,}000$ training rounds.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Quantitative summary of accuracy and communication costs after $1{,}000$ rounds on the Vertex Image Net benchmark.}
\label{tab:results}
\resizebox{\columnwidth}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Algorithm} & \textbf{Acc. (\%)} & \textbf{Comm. Rounds} & \textbf{Comm. Vol. (GB)} & \textbf{Speedup} \\
\midrule
FedAvg & 78.4 & 1000 & 420.5 & 1.0$\times$ \\
FedProx & 79.2 & 1000 & 420.5 & 0.9$\times$ \\
Decent-SGD & 76.1 & 1840 & 580.1 & 0.7$\times$ \\
\rowcolor{bglight} \textbf{Vertex-Fed (Ours)} & \textbf{81.5} & \textbf{480} & \textbf{162.8} & \textbf{2.6$\times$} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}%
}
\end{table}

As shown in Table~\ref{tab:results}, Vertex-Fed reduces the communication volume by 61.2\% compared to the central FedAvg baseline. More importantly, it improves classification accuracy by 3.1\%, which we attribute to the gradient-aligned consensus grouping preventing interference from conflicting node gradients.
