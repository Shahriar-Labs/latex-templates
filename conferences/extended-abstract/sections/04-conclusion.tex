\section{Conclusion}

AdaGrad-Prune demonstrates that jointly learning sparsity thresholds and
rescaling gradients during training yields networks that are both
accurate and highly energy-efficient on commodity microcontrollers.
The method requires no post-training fine-tuning step and is compatible
with standard training pipelines.  Future work will extend the framework
to structured (channel-level) pruning and hardware-aware energy proxies
derived from lookup-table profiling.
