\section{Conclusion}

\system treats a sample as an energy investment whose value changes with model
uncertainty, recent surprise, age, and neighboring evidence.  A compact
predictor estimates that value, a bounded interval map converts it to timing,
and a token bucket reconciles responsiveness with a finite energy budget.  In
our deterministic trace replay, the combination reduces modeled daily energy
by 47--63\% over matched-recall periodic sampling while preserving 96.1\%
event recall.  The result is not a claim of deployment lifetime; it is evidence
that uncertainty and energy debt form a useful, interpretable control surface.
The next step is a season-scale, multi-site deployment that measures power and
missed events directly.
