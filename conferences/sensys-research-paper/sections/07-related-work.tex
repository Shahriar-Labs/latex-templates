\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Adaptive acquisition.}
Model-driven acquisition reduces communication by answering queries from
probabilistic models and contacting sensors only when uncertainty is
high~\cite{deshpande2004model}.  BBQ and Ken build on this principle for
declarative sensor queries~\cite{deshpande2005model}.  Compressive sensing
exploits signal sparsity to reconstruct fewer measurements~\cite{candes2006robust,jain2004duality}, while event-triggered control communicates only when a state
deviates enough to matter~\cite{astrom2008event}.  \system shares their
value-of-information intuition but couples uncertainty to explicit energy debt,
bounded age, and decentralized redundancy suppression.

\paragraph{Energy-aware sensing.}
Energy-harvesting systems traditionally adapt workload to predicted supply,
from energy-neutral operation~\cite{kansal2007power} to utility-maximizing
schedulers~\cite{raghunathan2006energyscavenging}.  Hibernus and Mementos make
intermittent execution safe under power failures~\cite{balsamo2015hibernus,ransford2011mementos}.  These works govern \emph{whether computation can continue}; our
focus is the marginal sensing decision within an available budget.  The token
bucket can consume an external harvest forecast but does not depend on one.

\paragraph{In-network redundancy.}
LEACH amortizes radio cost through cluster heads~\cite{heinzelman2000energy},
directed diffusion names and aggregates data in-network~\cite{intanagonwiwat2000directed}, and TinyDB pushes acquisitional queries into the network~\cite{madden2004tinydb}.  Spatial correlation has also supported adaptive
sampling and suppression~\cite{guestrin2005distributed}.  \system uses only a
loss-tolerant digest, retaining local autonomy and avoiding a cluster repair
protocol.

\paragraph{Embedded learning.}
Modern tiny-ML systems make inference practical on microcontrollers
\cite{banbury2021benchmarking,lin2020mcunet}.  Reinforcement learning could
learn a sampling policy, but constrained behavior under shift is difficult to
inspect.  Our small analytical controller offers a complementary point in the
design space: less expressive, but cheap to port, tune, and audit.
