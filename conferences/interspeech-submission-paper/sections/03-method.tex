\section{Method}
\subsection{Chunked Bidirectional Context Injection}
Let the input acoustic sequence be partitioned into non-overlapping chunks $c_1, \dots, c_T$ of $L$
frames each. A standard chunk-causal Conformer restricts attention in every layer so that queries in
chunk $c_t$ may attend only to keys and values in $c_1, \dots, c_t$. CBCI relaxes this constraint for a
subset of layers $\mathcal{S} \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$, chosen among the upper half of the $N$-layer
stack, by additionally exposing a bounded future buffer $c_{t+1}[1{:}B]$ of the first $B$ frames of the
next chunk:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:cbci-mask}
M_\ell(i, j) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } j \le i, \\
0 & \text{if } \ell \in \mathcal{S} \text{ and } i < j \le i + B, \\
-\infty & \text{otherwise,}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
where $M_\ell$ is the additive attention mask at layer $\ell$, and $i, j$ index frames in
chunk-relative time. Layers not in $\mathcal{S}$ retain a strictly causal mask ($B{=}0$). The
algorithmic latency contributed by CBCI is $B \cdot \delta$, where $\delta$ is the frame stride,
independent of $|\mathcal{S}|$, since future frames are consumed once when they arrive and cached for
all layers in $\mathcal{S}$.

\subsection{Buffer-Annealed Training}
Training with a fixed $B$ overfits the model to that specific latency budget. We instead sample
$B \sim \mathrm{Uniform}\{0, \dots, B_{\max}\}$ per mini-batch during the second half of training,
after the model has converged under a causal-only warm-up phase. This curriculum lets the same
checkpoint be deployed at multiple latency points by adjusting $B$ at inference time, avoiding the need
to train and store separate models per latency target.

\subsection{Layer Selection}
We select $\mathcal{S}$ via a lightweight search: starting from an empty set, we greedily add the layer
whose inclusion yields the largest validation WER reduction per unit of additional parameter-touching
overhead, stopping once marginal gains fall below 0.05 absolute WER. In our 18-layer encoder, this
consistently selects layers 11--16.
