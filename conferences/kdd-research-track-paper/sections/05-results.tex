\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
Table~\ref{tab:results} reports link-prediction AUC-ROC on all three
datasets, along with parameter count in millions. \sysname{} is the best
performer on every dataset and uses fewer parameters than three of the four
baselines, since type-specific projections are lower rank than the shared
wide layers those baselines rely on.

Figure~\ref{fig:results} shows how AUC-ROC on \textsc{SynapseSocial}
responds to embedding dimension $d$. \sysname{} reaches within 0.4 points
of its peak accuracy at $d=64$, while \textsc{NodeFlow} requires $d=256$ to
approach a comparable score, consistent with our parameter-efficiency claim
in Table~\ref{tab:results}.
