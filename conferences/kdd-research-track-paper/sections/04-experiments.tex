\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}
\subsection{Datasets}
We evaluate on three benchmark graphs constructed for this study:
\textsc{NexaCommerce} (4.8M nodes across \emph{user}/\emph{item}/\emph{seller}
types, 62M timestamped edges), \textsc{SynapseSocial} (2.1M \emph{user}
nodes, 5 interaction-relation types, 38M edges), and
\textsc{VertexCitation} (1.3M \emph{paper}/\emph{author}/\emph{venue} nodes,
14M edges spanning 25 years). Each dataset is split temporally: the final
10\% of edges by timestamp form the test set, the preceding 10\% form
validation, ensuring no future leakage into training.

\subsection{Baselines and Setup}
We compare against four representative baselines re-implemented under a
shared training budget: \textsc{GraphPulse} (homogeneous inductive
aggregation), \textsc{HetEmbed} (static heterogeneous attention),
\textsc{TempoGAT} (temporal attention, type-agnostic), and
\textsc{NodeFlow} (memory-based temporal encoder). All models use embedding
dimension $d=128$, are trained with Adam at learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$
for 40 epochs on a single accelerator, and are evaluated on link prediction
(AUC-ROC) with an 80/10/10 temporal split. Hyperparameters were selected on
the validation split only.
