\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
In this paper, we evaluated \emph{Nexa}, a hardware-assisted remote attestation framework for zero-trust environments. We presented a security and performance analysis of its integration with the \emph{Synapse} key management protocol. Our results show that Nexa can enforce fine-grained security policies with minimal performance degradation, making it suitable for modern enterprise workloads. We also highlighted side-channel risks on legacy hardware and discussed mitigation strategies. Future work will investigate the formal verification of the Nexa protocol under complex adversarial models.
