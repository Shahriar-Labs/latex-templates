\label{sec:results}

\textbf{Ablation.} Removing the retrieval term ($\lambda_1{=}0$) drops the average correct rate by 6.1 points; removing the language-model term drops it by 8.7 points; removing the overfitting prior drops it by 3.4 points. All three terms contribute, and the language-model likelihood is individually the strongest single signal, consistent with prior findings that generation confidence correlates with correctness~\cite{delgado2022llmcode}.
