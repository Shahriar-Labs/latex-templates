\subsection*{Experimental Observations \& Discussion}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=2.0pt]
  \item \textbf{PCR Yield Variation:} The band in Lane~5 (SMP-10) is significantly fainter than that in Lane~4 (SMP-09), indicating a lower template concentration or suboptimal primer binding. A re-amplification is recommended if quantitative downstream analysis (e.g., sequencing) is required.
  \item \textbf{Amplification Failure in Lane~8:} SMP-13 (PCR Exon 5) failed to yield any specific product. The intense primer-dimer signal at the bottom of the lane suggests that the PCR reaction was set up correctly, but amplification did not occur. Potential causes include degraded genomic DNA template or a mutation at the primer binding site. A fresh genomic DNA extraction and nested PCR protocol should be scheduled.
  \item \textbf{Restriction Enzyme Digest Verification:} Restriction digestion profiles in Lanes~6 and 7 (SMP-11 and SMP-12) show two expected bands of \bp{2,000} and \bp{1,000}, indicating successful dual-enzyme cleavage of the plasmids. The insert sizes match the anticipated values of \bp{1,000} \cite{Aaij1972}.
\end{enumerate}
