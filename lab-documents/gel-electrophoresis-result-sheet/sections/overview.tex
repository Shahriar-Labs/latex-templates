\textbf{Purpose \& Scope}\\
This document records the results of the horizontal agarose gel electrophoresis run performed at Meridian BioSciences. The purpose of this run is to analyze the PCR amplification products of target gene regions (exon 4 and exon 5 of the \textit{CFTR} gene) and verify the restriction enzyme digest of plasmids \textit{pMD-102} and \textit{pMD-103}. All procedures were carried out in accordance with standard operating protocol SOP-ML-GEN-012, based on established molecular cloning guidelines \cite{Sambrook1989, Voytas2000}. Fictional control assays and ladder profiles are documented below for quality assurance.
