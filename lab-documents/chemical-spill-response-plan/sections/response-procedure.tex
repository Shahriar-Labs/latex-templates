\section{Response Procedure}
\label{sec:response}

\subsection*{Immediate Actions -- All Spills}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Alert} people nearby. Announce ``chemical spill'' clearly; do not shout in a way
        that causes panic.
  \item \textbf{Assess} the spill against the three tiers in Section~\ref{sec:classification}.
        Identify the chemical from the label or Safety Data Sheet (SDS) if at all possible.
  \item \textbf{Protect} yourself first -- do not walk through vapor, mist, or liquid to reach an
        exit or an injured colleague; go around.
  \item \textbf{Act} according to the flowchart below.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{notebox}
\textbf{\hazicon{\faInfoCircle}\ Any} path through the flowchart above ends at the same
place: an incident log entry. Even a Tier 1 spill cleaned up in two minutes must be logged --
this is how the EHS Office spots recurring hazards before they escalate.
