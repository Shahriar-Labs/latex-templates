\section{Purpose and Scope}

This plan establishes the required response actions for chemical spills occurring in any
teaching, research, or storage laboratory within Vertex Hall, including satellite laboratories
in the Nimbus Annex. It applies to all faculty, staff, postdoctoral researchers, graduate
students, undergraduate researchers, and contractors who handle hazardous chemicals under
Meridian Institute of Technology's Chemical Hygiene Plan (EHS-CHP-001).

\subsection*{Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Protect the health and safety of laboratory occupants and emergency responders.
  \item Limit environmental release and property damage from spilled chemicals.
  \item Provide a single, unambiguous decision path from spill discovery to closure.
  \item Ensure every spill, regardless of size, is logged, reviewed, and used to improve
        practice.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Who Must Know This Plan}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Every lab occupant} must be able to classify a spill (\S\ref{sec:classification})
        and know the location of the nearest spill kit and emergency pull station.
  \item \textbf{Principal Investigators (PIs) and Lab Supervisors} are responsible for
        site-specific training, kit maintenance, and initial incident triage.
  \item \textbf{EHS Office staff} coordinate Tier 2 and Tier 3 response, regulatory reporting,
        and post-incident review.
\end{itemize}

\begin{notebox}
\textbf{\hazicon{\faInfoCircle}\ Scope note.} This plan governs \emph{chemical} spills only.
Biological material releases follow the Biosafety Spill Procedure (EHS-BIO-007); radioactive
material releases follow the Radiation Safety Manual, Section 9. If a spill involves more than
one hazard class, follow all applicable procedures concurrently and notify EHS immediately.
\end{notebox}
