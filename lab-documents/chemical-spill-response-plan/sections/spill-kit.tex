\section{Spill Kit Contents and Locations}

Every laboratory in Vertex Hall and the Nimbus Annex is provisioned with at least one wall-mounted
spill kit. Kits are inspected monthly by the Lab Supervisor and restocked by EHS within two
business days of use. Do not use a kit past its inspection tag expiry -- report it instead.

\subsection*{Kit Locations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Vertex Hall, Floor 1} -- outside Room 104 (Teaching Labs) and Room 118 (Instrument
        Room).
  \item \textbf{Vertex Hall, Floor 2} -- outside Room 210 (Organic Synthesis) and Room 227
        (Analytical Suite).
  \item \textbf{Vertex Hall, Floor 3} -- outside Room 305 (Graduate Research Labs) and the
        Room 318 mercury-thermometer storage cage.
  \item \textbf{Nimbus Annex} -- main corridor, adjacent to the eyewash station.
\end{itemize}

\begin{notebox}
\textbf{\hazicon{\faInfoCircle}\ Kit exhausted?} A partially or fully used kit is a Tier 2 trigger
on its own -- notify EHS at ext.\ 4357 even if the spill itself was minor, so the kit is
restocked before the next incident.
\end{notebox}
