\section{Method Summary}
\sectionrule

\subsection{Principle}
The concentration of a sodium hydroxide (NaOH) titrant is established by direct titration
against potassium hydrogen phthalate (KHP), a primary-standard acid, following the general
acid--base titrimetry principles described by \citet{harris2015}. The standardized titrant is
then used to determine the acetic acid (\ce{CH3COOH}) content of a diluted vinegar process
stream by titration to the phenolphthalein endpoint, consistent with the approach of AOAC
Official Method 930.35 \citep{aoac930}.

\[
  \ce{KHC8H4O4 (aq) + NaOH (aq) -> NaKC8H4O4 (aq) + H2O (l)}
\]
\[
  \ce{CH3COOH (aq) + NaOH (aq) -> CH3COONa (aq) + H2O (l)}
\]

\subsection{Procedure Outline}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Dry KHP for 2~h at \SI{110}{\celsius}; cool in a desiccator.
  \item Weigh three individual KHP portions of approximately \SI{0.51}{\gram} into 250~mL
        Erlenmeyer flasks; dissolve each in 50~mL CO$_2$-free water.
  \item Add 2--3 drops of phenolphthalein and titrate each portion with NaOH from a Class~A
        burette to the first persistent pale-pink endpoint (holds $\geq$~30~s).
  \item Dilute 10.00~mL of the vinegar sample to 100.00~mL with distilled water in a volumetric
        flask.
  \item Titrate four 10.00~mL aliquots of the diluted sample with the standardized NaOH, using
        phenolphthalein as above.
  \item Record all initial and final burette readings directly in Section~\ref{sec:standardization}
        and Section~\ref{sec:sample-assay} before any calculation is performed.
\end{enumerate}
