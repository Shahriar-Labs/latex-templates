\section{Calculations}
\sectionrule

\subsection{Titrant Molarity}
For each standardization replicate, the NaOH molarity follows from the KHP mass and volume
dispensed:
\[
  M_{\text{NaOH}} = \frac{m_{\text{KHP}}\,(\si{\gram})}{\mathit{MW}_{\text{KHP}}\,(\SI{204.22}{\gram\per\mole}) \times V_{\text{NaOH}}\,(\si{\liter})}
\]
Applying this to Replicate~1: $M_{\text{NaOH}} = \dfrac{0.5104}{204.22 \times 0.02517} = \SI{0.09930}{\Molar}$,
consistent with the three values reported in Section~\ref{sec:standardization}
(mean $\SI{0.09953}{\Molar}$, RSD~\SI{0.47}{\percent}), which follows the propagation
treatment for replicate titrations described in \citet{skoog2018}.

\subsection{Acetic Acid Content}
The mass of acetic acid ($\mathit{MW} = \SI{60.05}{\gram\per\mole}$) titrated in each
10.00~mL aliquot of the \emph{diluted} sample is:
\[
  n_{\text{acid}} = V_{\text{NaOH}} \times M_{\text{NaOH}}
\]
Using the mean dispensed volume from Section~\ref{sec:sample-assay}:
\[
  n_{\text{acid}} = 8.355\,\si{\milli\liter} \times 0.09953\,\si{\Molar} = 0.8316\,\si{\milli\mole}
\]
Converting to concentration in the diluted solution and correcting for the 1:10 dilution
factor ($\mathit{DF} = 10$) recovers the concentration in the original process stream:
\[
  C_{\text{acid, original}} = \frac{n_{\text{acid}}}{V_{\text{aliquot}}} \times \mathit{DF}
  = \frac{0.8316\,\si{\milli\mole}}{10.00\,\si{\milli\liter}} \times 10 = 0.8316\,\si{\Molar}
\]
Expressed as \%~w/v acetic acid:
\[
  \%\,\text{w/v} = \frac{C_{\text{acid, original}} \times \mathit{MW}_{\text{acid}}}{10}
  = \frac{0.8316 \times 60.05}{10} = \SI{4.99}{\percent}\ \text{w/v}
\]
