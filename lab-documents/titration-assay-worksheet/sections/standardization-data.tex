\section{Standardization of NaOH Titrant}
\label{sec:standardization}
\sectionrule

Three independent portions of dried KHP were titrated to the phenolphthalein endpoint. The
titrant molarity for each replicate is calculated from
$M_{\text{NaOH}} = \dfrac{m_{\text{KHP}}}{\mathit{MW}_{\text{KHP}} \times V_{\text{NaOH}}}$,
using $\mathit{MW}_{\text{KHP}} = \SI{204.22}{\gram\per\mole}$.
