\subsection{Outlier Screening}
The sample-assay replicate volumes (Section~\ref{sec:sample-assay}) were screened with the
Dixon $Q$-test at 90\% confidence ($Q_{\text{crit}} = 0.831$ for $n=4$). The most extreme value,
Replicate~3 (\SI{8.41}{\milli\liter}), gives:
\[
  Q = \frac{|8.41 - 8.37|}{8.41 - 8.32} = \frac{0.04}{0.09} = 0.444
\]
Since $Q < Q_{\text{crit}}$, no replicate is rejected as a statistical outlier; all four values
are retained in the reported mean.
