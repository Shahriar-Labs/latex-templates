\section{Sample Assay --- Acetic Acid Content}
\label{sec:sample-assay}
\sectionrule

A 10.00~mL aliquot of Batch NC-2247-B was diluted to 100.00~mL with distilled water. Four
10.00~mL aliquots of this diluted solution were titrated in replicate with the standardized
NaOH titrant (\SI{0.09953}{\Molar}, Section~\ref{sec:standardization}).
