\section{Purpose and Scope}

\subsection{Purpose}
This record establishes objective evidence that the analytical balance and pipettes listed in
Section~2 perform within their manufacturer- and standard-defined tolerance limits before
release for use in formulation weighing, titration, and volumetric dispensing tasks. Verification
is performed gravimetrically: mass readings of a certified reference weight (balance) or of
dispensed water (pipettes) are converted to volume and compared against tolerance limits defined
in ISO~8655 \cite{iso8655-1,iso8655-6} and OIML~R76-1 \cite{oiml-r76}, with balance performance
additionally cross-checked against ASTM~E898 \cite{astm-e898} and ASTM~E177 \cite{astm-e177}.

\subsection{Scope}
This record applies to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item One semi-micro analytical balance (Section~4), verified for linearity, repeatability,
        and corner-load (eccentricity) error.
  \item Four single-channel air-displacement pipettes (Section~5), each verified gravimetrically
        at three test volumes per ISO~8655-6 Method A.
\end{itemize}
It does \emph{not} cover multichannel pipettes, bulk dispensers, or the balance's initial
factory calibration certificate, which is held separately in the metrology asset file.

\subsection{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Metrology Technician} --- performs the gravimetric verification, records raw
        readings, computes accuracy, precision, and correction factors, and tags any
        out-of-tolerance instrument for quarantine.
  \item \textbf{QA Manager} --- reviews computed results against tolerance limits, dispositions
        nonconformances, and approves the record for release.
  \item \textbf{Lab Manager} --- schedules the next verification interval and ensures
        out-of-service instruments are withheld from use until requalified.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Reference Standards}
Gravimetric method, environmental correction, and tolerance limits are drawn from
\cite{iso8655-1}, \cite{iso8655-6}, \cite{oiml-r76}, \cite{astm-e898}, \cite{astm-e177}, and
USP~General Chapter \texttt{<41>} on weighing devices \cite{usp41}.
