\section*{Gowning Procedure}

Gowning proceeds in two stages, separated by a physical interlock door. Each step must be
initialled by the wearer and countersigned by the Gowning Room Monitor before advancing to
the next stage. No step may be skipped or reordered.

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=panelgray,colframe=ruleline,boxrule=0.6pt,arc=2pt,
  title={\color{white}\bfseries Stage 1 -- Change Room (Black/White)},
  colbacktitle=steelgray,coltitle=white,fonttitle=\small]
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt,label=\gcheck~\arabic*.]
  \item Remove street shoes, jewellery, and personal outerwear at the black-line boundary.
  \item Don hair cover and beard cover (if applicable); no hair may be exposed.
  \item Perform a 30-second alcohol hand disinfection per SOP-EHS-021.
  \item Don dedicated cleanroom undergarment (bunny suit base layer).
  \item Don first pair of nitrile gloves; tuck cuffs under sleeve.
  \item Don general-area cleanroom shoes; cross the white-line boundary.
\end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}%
\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=panelgray,colframe=ruleline,boxrule=0.6pt,arc=2pt,
  title={\color{white}\bfseries Stage 2 -- Gowning Room (White/White)},
  colbacktitle=cleanblue,coltitle=white,fonttitle=\small]
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt,label=\gcheck~\arabic*.]
  \item Don sterile coverall, closing all closures without touching the exterior surface.
  \item Don sterile boot covers over coverall leg cuffs; overlap sealed with tape.
  \item Don sterile hood, fully enclosing hair cover with no skin exposed at the collar.
  \item Don safety goggles or full face shield per line assignment.
  \item Perform second alcohol hand disinfection; don sterile outer gloves over cuffs.
  \item Visual cross-check by Gowning Room Monitor; record result on entry log.
\end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}

  title={\faFlask\ Materials Required Per Gowning Event},fonttitle=\bfseries,

Sterile coverall (size-matched, lot-traceable), boot covers (2 pr.), sterile hood, nitrile
gloves (2 pr.), sterile outer gloves, safety goggles or face shield, 70\% IPA hand
disinfectant, lint-free wipes. All sterile garments are single-use and discarded at exit;
reusable goggles are wiped with disinfectant between uses per SOP-EHS-021.
