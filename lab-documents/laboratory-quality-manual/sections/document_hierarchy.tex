\section{Quality Management System Document Hierarchy}

\subsection{Hierarchy of Documentation}

The QMS is organized into four tiers of increasing detail and decreasing readership, shown
in Figure~\ref{fig:pyramid}. Each tier is controlled at a level of rigour proportionate to
its impact: this manual requires Laboratory Director approval, while a form template
requires only Section Supervisor sign-off.

\subsection{Document Control Procedure}

New or revised documents at any level follow the same control cycle, detailed in
SOP-DOC-001:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Drafting} --- the process owner drafts or revises the document and assigns a
        unique code and draft revision marker.
  \item \textbf{Review} --- a technically qualified reviewer, independent of the drafter where
        practicable, checks the document against current practice and referenced standards.
  \item \textbf{Approval} --- the approver listed in the table above signs and dates the
        document; approval is recorded in the Master Document Register.
  \item \textbf{Issue} --- the Quality Manager assigns the effective date, updates the register,
        and withdraws all superseded copies from circulation.
  \item \textbf{Periodic Review} --- every controlled document is reviewed at least every 24
        months, or sooner following an audit finding, incident, or regulatory change.
  \item \textbf{Withdrawal} --- obsolete documents are marked ``Superseded'' and archived for
        no less than the retention period in Section~7.1; a single reference copy may be kept
        for historical purposes, clearly stamped as such.
\end{enumerate}
