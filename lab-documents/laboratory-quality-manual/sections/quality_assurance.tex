\section{Quality Assurance and Continual Improvement}

\subsection{Internal Quality Control}

Each testing section maintains internal quality control appropriate to its discipline:
control charts in Chemistry, sterility and growth-promotion checks on media batches in
Microbiology, and no-template and positive-extraction controls in Molecular Diagnostics.
Control results are reviewed by the section supervisor before any associated client result
is released.

\subsection{Nonconforming Work}

Work identified as nonconforming --- through equipment failure, QC breach, or procedural
deviation --- is immediately quarantined, and the analyst notifies the section supervisor
before any further action. The supervisor evaluates the significance of the nonconformity,
decides whether affected results must be recalled, and records the disposition in the
Nonconformity Register. No nonconforming result leaves the Laboratory without an
accompanying corrective action reference.

\subsection{Corrective and Preventive Action Process}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Detect and contain the nonconformity; halt affected work if validity is uncertain.
  \item Record the event in the Nonconformity Register within one working day.
  \item Investigate root cause using a structured method (5-Whys or fishbone).
  \item Define and implement corrective action, with an owner and target date.
  \item Verify effectiveness no sooner than 30 days after implementation.
  \item Close the record and report the outcome at the next Management Review.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Customer Feedback and Complaints}

Complaints may be submitted in writing, by phone, or through the client portal. Receipt is
acknowledged within two working days, and the QA Officer determines whether the complaint
relates to Laboratory activities within the accredited scope. Where it does, a written
response --- including root cause and any corrective action --- is provided within 15
working days. Complaint outcomes are anonymized and included as a standing agenda item at
Management Review to identify systemic trends.

\begin{notebox}{Escalation}
A complaint alleging falsification of data, undisclosed conflict of interest, or breach of
impartiality is escalated directly to the Laboratory Director on the same working day,
bypassing the standard response timeline above.
\end{notebox}
