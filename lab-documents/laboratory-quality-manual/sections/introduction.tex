\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Purpose and Scope}

This Quality Manual (\doccode) describes the Quality Management System (QMS) operated by
\labname\ (``the Laboratory'') in support of its testing activities in chemistry,
microbiology, and molecular diagnostics. The manual is written to satisfy the requirements
of ISO/IEC 17025:2017, \emph{General requirements for the competence of testing and
calibration laboratories} \cite{iso17025}, and, where clinical specimens are involved, the
complementary requirements of ISO 15189:2022 \cite{iso15189}.

The manual applies to all permanent, temporary, and contracted staff working within the
Laboratory's three testing sections, and to all locations listed in the current Scope of
Accreditation held under certificate MBL-ACC-0347. It does not extend to research and
development activities that are explicitly excluded from the accredited scope, although
those activities remain subject to the Laboratory's general safety and ethics policies.

\subsection{Quality Policy Statement}

\begin{policybox}
\labname\ is committed to providing testing services of the highest technical validity,
delivered with impartiality, confidentiality, and strict adherence to scientific and
ethical practice. Management undertakes to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item maintain a Quality Management System compliant with ISO/IEC 17025:2017 and continually
        improve its effectiveness;
  \item ensure all personnel are competent, adequately resourced, and free from any commercial,
        financial, or other pressure that could compromise the impartiality of results;
  \item base all technical decisions on objective evidence, traceable measurement, and validated
        methods;
  \item communicate this policy to all staff and require its acknowledgement as a condition of
        employment; and
  \item review the suitability of this policy at each Management Review (Section~4.7).
\end{itemize}
