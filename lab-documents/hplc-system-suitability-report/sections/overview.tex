\section{Report Overview \& Scope}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.61\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\textbf{Purpose \& Scope}\\
This report documents the system suitability test (SST) performed on the reversed-phase HPLC method used for assay and related-substances testing of NXA-4471 Tablets, 50\,mg, manufactured by Nexa Therapeutics, Inc. The SST was executed prior to sample analysis of Batch~B26-0417 to verify that the chromatographic system, method, and column combination deliver data of acceptable quality, in accordance with SOP-QC-014 \cite{ApexSOPQC014} and the general requirements of USP General Chapter <621> Chromatography \cite{USP621}.
