\section{System Suitability Test Design}
\label{sec:sst_design}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.55\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\textbf{Reference Standard \& SST Solution}\\
The system suitability solution was prepared by combining NXA-4471 Working Reference Standard (WRS Lot~RS-0192) with Impurity-A reference material (\textit{N}-desmethyl-NXA-4471) at a target level of \percent{0.5} relative to the nominal NXA-4471 concentration, in diluent (mobile phase A~:~methanol, 50:50). Six replicate \volinj{10} injections of the SST solution were made under equilibrated system conditions, following two priming injections that were excluded from the calculated statistics \cite{Snyder2010}.

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{Critical Resolution Pair}\\
Impurity-A elutes immediately before the NXA-4471 main peak (relative retention time, RRT~$\approx$~0.54) and represents the closest-eluting, and therefore most critical, resolution pair for this method.
\end{minipage}%

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.42\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\begin{infobox}{Acceptance Criteria (SOP-QC-014)}
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.0em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Resolution ($R_s$), Imp-A/NXA-4471 $\geq$ 2.0
  \item Tailing factor ($T$), main peak $\leq$ 2.0
  \item Theoretical plates ($N$), main peak $\geq$ 2000
  \item \%RSD retention time (n=6) $\leq$ \percent{2.0}
  \item \%RSD peak area (n=6) $\leq$ \percent{2.0}
\end{itemize}
\end{infobox}
\end{minipage}

\par\vspace{4pt}
Criteria are defined per USP <621> \cite{USP621} and ICH Q2(R2) \cite{ICHQ2R2}, and are applied to the peaks in the representative SST chromatogram (Figure~1, Section~\ref{sec:chromatogram}) as detailed in the results in Section~\ref{sec:results}.
