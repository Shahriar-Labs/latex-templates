\section{Quality Control / Acceptance Criteria}
To ensure the isolation is successful, the eluted RNA must meet the criteria in Table~\ref{tab:qc_metrics}.

\begin{table}[H]
\small
\caption{Required quality metrics for RNA integrity and purity.}
\label{tab:qc_metrics}
\vspace{2pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.1}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}l@{\hspace{6mm}}l@{\hspace{8mm}}X@{}}
\toprule
\color{primary}Quality Metric & \color{primary}Acceptance Range & \color{primary}Method of Assessment \\
\midrule
Concentration & $\ge 50\,\text{ng}/\mu\text{L}$ & UV-Vis Spectrophotometry (Nanodrop) \\
$A_{260}/A_{280}$ Ratio & $1.9 - 2.1$ & UV-Vis Spectrophotometry \\
$A_{260}/A_{230}$ Ratio & $\ge 2.0$ & UV-Vis Spectrophotometry \\
RNA Integrity Number (RIN) & $\ge 8.0$ (High Quality) & Bioanalyzer / TapeStation Electrophoresis \\
Genomic DNA Contamination & Not detectable & Negative control PCR (no RT) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
