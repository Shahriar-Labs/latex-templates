\section{Reagent Preparation}
Before beginning the extraction procedure, prepare the following reagents:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Lysis Buffer RLT with $\beta$-ME:} Add 10 $\mu$L of $\beta$-ME per 1 mL of Buffer RLT. This solution is stable for up to 1 month at room temperature ($15{-}25^\circ$C). Prepare under a fume hood.
  \item \textbf{70\% Ethanol solution:} Prepare fresh 70\% (v/v) ethanol by mixing 35 mL of molecular biology grade 100\% ethanol with 15 mL of sterile nuclease-free water.
  \item \textbf{DNase I Stock Solution:} Dissolve lyophilized DNase I (1500 KUnits) in 550 $\mu$L of RNase-free water. Gently mix by inverting. Do not vortex. Store aliquots at $-20^\circ$C.
\end{itemize}
