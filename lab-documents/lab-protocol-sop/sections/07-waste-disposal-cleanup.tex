\section{Waste Disposal \& Cleanup}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Guanidinium Waste:} Collect flow-through from steps 5.2 and 5.3 in organic waste. Do not mix with bleach.
  \item \textbf{Solid Waste:} Dispose of pipette tips and tubes in biohazard solid waste for autoclaving.
  \item \textbf{Spills:} Clean Buffer RLT spills with 10\% laboratory detergent, followed by water. Do not use bleach.
\end{enumerate}
