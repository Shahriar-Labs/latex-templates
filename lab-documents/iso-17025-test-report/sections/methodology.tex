\subsection{Test Procedure Summary}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{Tensile test:} Three specimens (SB-01, SB-02, SB-03) were machined to full-size
  bolt configuration and pulled to fracture at a controlled strain rate of 5\,mm/min in
  accordance with ASTM A370 Annex A. Yield strength was determined using the 0.2\% offset
  method; elongation was measured over a 50\,mm gauge length after fracture.
\item \textbf{Hardness test:} Rockwell C hardness was measured at three locations on the bolt
  shank per ASTM E18, using a diamond indenter under a 150\,kgf major load.
\item \textbf{Dimensional check:} Major diameter, minor diameter and thread pitch were verified
  against ISO 898-1 tolerance tables using calibrated micrometers and thread ring/plug gauges.
\end{itemize}

Specimen SB-02 required re-clamping after minor grip slippage was observed during the initial

validity of the reported results.
