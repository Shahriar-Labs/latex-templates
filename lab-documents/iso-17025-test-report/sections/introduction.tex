\section{Scope and Sample Identification}

\subsection{Scope of Work}
Meridian Analytical Laboratories was instructed by Vertex Components Ltd to perform mechanical
and dimensional verification testing on a batch of hot-dip galvanised anchor bolts intended for
structural steelwork fixings. Testing was carried out under the laboratory's scope of
accreditation to ISO/IEC 17025:2017~\cite{iso17025}, granted by the Nimbus National Laboratory
Accreditation Service (accreditation number NNLAS-2024-0356), which covers mechanical testing of
metallic fasteners in accordance with ASTM F1554~\cite{astmf1554}, ASTM A370~\cite{astma370} and
ISO 898-1~\cite{iso898}.

\subsection{Sample Description}
The samples were received intact, individually bagged and labelled, with no visible damage
recorded on receipt. Chain-of-custody form CoC-2026-3381 was completed and retained on file.

\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox,title=Purpose of Testing]
\small
To verify that the submitted batch of anchor bolts conforms to the mechanical property
requirements of ASTM F1554 Grade 36 and the dimensional tolerances specified in the client
purchase order, in support of structural steelwork acceptance at the client's Halsworth facility.
\end{tcolorbox}
