shift and every three hours thereafter. The acceptable range is 2--8\,\textdegree C per

\subsection{Turnaround-Time Monitoring}
Collect-to-accession interval is tracked as a continuous receiving QC indicator against the
30-minute in-house / 60-minute courier targets in SOP-LAB-014 \S4.2. Of the 34 specimens
logged this shift, the median interval was 8 minutes for in-house draws and 76 minutes for the
two courier deliveries (accessions 26195-0093 and 26195-0112), both of which also failed
acceptability review and are accounted for in Section~\ref{sec:log}. No in-house specimen
exceeded the 30-minute target.
