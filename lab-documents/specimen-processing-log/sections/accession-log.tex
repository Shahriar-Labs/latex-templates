\section{Specimen Accession Log}
\label{sec:log}

Accession numbers follow the site's Julian-date scheme (\texttt{YYDDD-NNNN}): 14 July 2026 is
the 195th day of 2026, so every specimen logged on this shift carries the prefix
\texttt{26195-}. Collected and Received times are entered by the phlebotomy team and the
accessioning technologist respectively; a Collect~$\to$~Receive interval beyond 30 minutes for
an in-house draw, or beyond 60 minutes for a courier delivery, triggers a transport-time review
per SOP-LAB-014 \S4.2. Rows flagged \rejcode{Rejected} reference the reason code defined in
Table~\ref{tab:codes}.
