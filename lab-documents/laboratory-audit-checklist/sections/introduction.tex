\section{Purpose and Scope}

This checklist records the findings of the scheduled internal audit of Vertex Analytical
Laboratories' management system and technical operations against ISO/IEC~17025:2017,
\emph{General requirements for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories}. The
audit was commissioned by the Quality Manager as part of the 2026 internal audit programme
(schedule ref.\ QM-04) and covers the calibration and chemical testing activities performed
at Site~B, Instrumentation Wing.

\subsection{Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Verify that documented procedures under the current quality manual (rev.\ 9) remain
        implemented in day-to-day practice.
  \item Confirm that corrective actions raised at the previous audit (VAL-AUD-2025-031) have
        been closed and are effective.
  \item Identify nonconformances, observations, and opportunities for improvement ahead of
        the external accreditation surveillance visit scheduled for 2026-10-05.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Scope Boundaries}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{In scope:} sample reception, equipment calibration and maintenance records,
        method validation files, environmental monitoring, personnel competency records, and
        chemical/physical safety practices within the Instrumentation Wing.
  \item \textbf{Out of scope:} finance, procurement contracts unrelated to calibration
        services, and the Site~A microbiology suite (covered under a separate audit,
        VAL-AUD-2026-015).
\end{itemize}
