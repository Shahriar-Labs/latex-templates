\section{Sources of Uncertainty}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.4cm,style=nextline,font=\bfseries\color{labteal}]

\item[Repeatability of comparison ($\delta m_A$)]
Scatter of the ten ABBA comparator readings, dominated by short-term air
turbulence in the weighing chamber and residual comparator noise. Evaluated
as Type~A from the observed standard deviation of the mean.

\item[Reference standard certificate ($\delta m_S$)]
The calibration certificate for RS-1KG-A (Cert.\ NMS-CAL-1198, national
metrology institute) states an expanded uncertainty $U = \SI{0.15}{mg}$ at
$k = 2$. This is the single largest contributor to the budget.

\item[Drift of the reference standard ($\delta m_D$)]
Estimated from the history of the last four calibrations of RS-1KG-A. The
maximum observed drift between successive certificates, taken as a
rectangular bound, is $\pm\SI{0.030}{mg}$ over the calibration interval.

\item[Air buoyancy correction ($\delta m_B$)]
Arises from the density difference between the test item (stainless steel,
$\rho \approx \SI{7950}{kg/m^3}$) and the reference standard, combined with
ambient air density computed from measured temperature, pressure, and
humidity via the CIPM~1981/91 formula. Bound estimated at $\pm\SI{0.018}{mg}$.

\item[Comparator display resolution ($\delta m_R$)]
Digital readability of \SI{0.1}{\micro\gram} rounds each reading;
half the resolution is taken as a rectangular half-width and the divisor
is $2\sqrt{3}$ to avoid double-counting the rounding of both the load and
tare readings.

\item[Eccentricity / off-centre loading ($\delta m_E$)]
Residual sensitivity of the comparator to loading position, bounded by the
manufacturer's corner-load specification and the operator's positioning
repeatability, assumed rectangular.

\item[Thermal expansion differential ($\delta m_T$)]
Volume mismatch induced by the residual temperature gradient
($\pm\SI{0.3}{\celsius}$) between test item and reference during
equilibration, assumed rectangular.

\item[Magnetic susceptibility interaction ($\delta m_M$)]
Interaction between the comparator's internal load cell magnet and the
ferromagnetic content of the stainless-steel test item, bounded per
OIML R 111-1 Annex~D and assumed rectangular.

\end{description}
