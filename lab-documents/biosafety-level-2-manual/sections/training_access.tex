\subsection{Access Control}

BSL-2 spaces are secured with RFID card-access control managed by MRI Facilities.
Access is granted upon verification of:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Completed and current mandatory training record in ApexLearn.
  \item Active IBC-approved research protocol (\textbf{must cover the agents and
        procedures to be performed}).
  \item PI or lab manager authorisation submitted via the Biosafety Portal.
\end{itemize}

\begin{warningbox}[Tailgating and Propped Doors]
\small
Allowing un-badged individuals to ``tailgate'' through access-controlled doors is a
\textbf{Level-2 Safety Violation} and may result in immediate suspension of laboratory
access pending review. BSL-2 doors must never be propped open.
\end{warningbox}

\subsection{Working Alone and After-Hours Policy}

Working alone with viable BSL-2 agents outside of normal staffed hours (08:00--18:00,
Monday--Friday) is \textbf{prohibited} unless:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=2pt,
                  label=\textbf{\color{BSLamber}\arabic*.}]
  \item A written \emph{Lone-Worker Risk Assessment} (Form~MRI-LW-02) has been
        approved by the IBC and is on file.
  \item The researcher has registered their after-hours work via the ApexLearn
        after-hours log \textbf{before} entering the facility.
  \item A check-in call with MRI Security (+1 617-555-0220) is made at the start
        and end of the session.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Visitors and Collaborators}

All visitors to BSL-2 spaces must be escorted by an authorised MRI personnel member
at all times. External collaborators conducting experimental work (not merely
observing) must additionally:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Complete MRI-equivalent biosafety training or provide documentation of
        equivalent institutional training for IBC review.
  \item Be listed on an active MRI IBC protocol.
  \item Sign the MRI Visitor Biosafety Acknowledgement (Form~MRI-VBA-01).
\end{itemize}
