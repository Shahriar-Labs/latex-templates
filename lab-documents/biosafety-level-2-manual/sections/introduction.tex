\section{Purpose, Scope, and Institutional Context}
\label{sec:introduction}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.68\linewidth}
This \textbf{Biosafety Level 2 (BSL-2) Laboratory Manual} establishes the policies,
procedures, and containment requirements governing all work with biological agents
classified at Risk Group 2 within the research facilities of
\textbf{Meridian Research Institute} (MRI), Cambridge, MA.

The manual applies to all laboratory personnel—principal investigators, postdoctoral
researchers, graduate students, laboratory technicians, and visiting scholars—who
access BSL-2 designated spaces in Buildings~7, 11, and 14 of the MRI main campus.
Compliance is mandatory and forms part of each employee's and student's terms of
engagement with the Institute.

\subsection{Regulatory Framework}

This manual is issued in conformance with the following regulatory and guidance documents:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textit{Biosafety in Microbiological and Biomedical Laboratories}, 6th edition
        (BMBL-6), Centers for Disease Control and Prevention / National Institutes of
        Health \cite{BMBL2020}.
  \item \textit{NIH Guidelines for Research Involving Recombinant or Synthetic Nucleic
        Acid Molecules} (NIH Guidelines), current revision \cite{NIHGuidelines2019}.
  \item Massachusetts Department of Public Health (MDPH) regulations,
        105~CMR~480.000 — Storage and Disposal of Infectious or Physically Dangerous
        Medical or Biological Waste \cite{MDPH2022}.
  \item Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) Bloodborne Pathogens
        Standard, 29~CFR~\S 1910.1030 \cite{OSHA1991}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Risk Group 2 Agents}

Biological agents assigned to Risk Group 2 (RG-2) are associated with human disease
that is rarely serious and for which preventive or therapeutic interventions are
often available. Examples of agent categories studied at MRI BSL-2 facilities include:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textit{Adeno-associated virus} (AAV) serotypes 1--9 in non-replication-competent
        constructs used for gene-delivery research.
  \item \textit{Staphylococcus aureus} (including methicillin-sensitive clinical
        isolates) studied under the NexaTox antimicrobial resistance program.
  \item \textit{Hepatitis B virus} (HBV) surface antigen constructs — non-infectious,
        sub-genomic fragments only.
  \item Human cell lines (HEK-293, HeLa, Jurkat) treated as RG-2 per BMBL-6
        guidance on human-derived materials.
\end{itemize}

\begin{dangerbox}[Agents Outside This Manual's Scope]
Work with Select Agents, Toxins, or Risk Group 3 / 4 organisms is \textbf{not
covered by this manual}. Such research requires a separate protocol approved by
the IBC and the CDC/USDA Select Agent Program. Contact the Biosafety Office
before initiating any work that may involve agents at higher risk classifications.
\end{dangerbox}
