\section{Containment Requirements}
\label{sec:containment}

BSL-2 containment is achieved through a layered system of physical barriers, safe
work practices, and administrative controls. The following tables summarise the
minimum requirements for all BSL-2 designated laboratories at Meridian Research
Institute.

\begin{warningbox}[Glove Removal Protocol]
\small
Gloves must be removed using the \textbf{beak technique} before touching any surface
outside the immediate work area. Removed gloves are placed directly into the
biohazard waste bag. Hand hygiene (soap and water or 70\% ethanol gel) is performed
immediately after glove removal, and always before leaving the BSL-2 space.
\end{warningbox}

\subsection{Waste Segregation and Disposal}

All biologically contaminated waste generated in BSL-2 areas must be segregated at
the point of generation into one of the following categories:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=3pt,
                  label=\textbf{\color{BSLamber}\arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{Regulated Medical / Biological Waste (RMBW):} Items
        contaminated with RG-2 agents — pipette tips, culture tubes, cell culture flasks,
        gloves. Collected in \textit{red biohazard bags} inside rigid secondary containers.
        Autoclaved on-site before transfer to the campus waste contractor (NexaWaste
        Solutions).
  \item \textbf{Sharps Waste:} Needles, scalpels, broken glass. Collected in
        UN-approved, puncture-resistant containers. Never filled above the marked
        fill-line.
  \item \textbf{Liquid Biological Waste:} Culture supernatants, media, rinse water.
        Treated with 10\% bleach (final concentration) for a minimum of 20~minutes before
        drain disposal, \emph{or} autoclaved.
  \item \textbf{Chemical-Biological Mixed Waste:} Contact the MRI Environmental Health
        \& Safety office (\texttt{ehs@meridian-ri.edu}) before disposal.
\end{enumerate}
