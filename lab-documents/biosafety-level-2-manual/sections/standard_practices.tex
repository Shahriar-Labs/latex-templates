\section{Standard Microbiological Practices}
\label{sec:practices}

The following standard microbiological practices are the foundation of BSL-2
containment and \textbf{apply at all times} in designated BSL-2 spaces, regardless
of whether active experimental work is in progress.

\subsection{General Laboratory Conduct}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{No eating, drinking, handling contact lenses, applying cosmetics, or
        storing food/beverages} in BSL-2 areas. This includes items kept in refrigerators
        used for biological materials.
  \item \textbf{Mouth pipetting is strictly prohibited.} Mechanical pipetting devices
        must be used at all times.
  \item \textbf{Minimise aerosol generation.} Avoid vigorous pipetting, open-container
        centrifugation, and flaming loop techniques that generate splatter.
  \item \textbf{Work surfaces are decontaminated} with an appropriate agent (see
        Table~\ref{tab:decon}) before work begins and after any spill or at the end of
        each work session.
  \item \textbf{All procedures that pose an aerosol or splash risk} (e.g., vortexing
        open tubes, sonication of infectious material) must be conducted in a certified
        Class~II~A2 BSC.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Biosafety Cabinet (BSC) Operation}

\begin{procedurebox}[Class II A2 BSC Operating Procedure]
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=3pt,
                  label=\textbf{\color{BSLmid}\arabic*.}]
  \item Confirm annual certification label is current and cabinet is not overdue.
  \item Turn blower on; allow to run for \textbf{5 minutes} before starting work.
        This purges the cabinet of any contaminants from the prior session.
  \item Wipe work surface with 70\% ethanol. Allow to evaporate before placing
        materials inside.
  \item Place materials in the BSC: large items at the back, active workspace in
        the centre, waste container to one side. \textbf{Do not block the front or rear grilles.}
  \item Perform all work with slow, deliberate movements to avoid disrupting airflow.
        Minimise in-out arm movements.
  \item After work: wipe all items with 70\% ethanol before removing. Discard waste
        into biohazard bag inside the BSC.
  \item Run blower for an additional \textbf{3 minutes} after work is complete.
  \item Wipe work surface with 70\% ethanol or 10\% bleach; allow contact time.
  \item Log BSC use in the cabinet logbook (date, PI, duration, agent used).
\end{enumerate}
\end{procedurebox}

\begin{warningbox}[UV Lights in BSCs]
\small
UV lamps in BSCs \textbf{do not replace chemical decontamination} and must never be
used as the sole decontamination method. UV penetration is limited and will not
decontaminate shadowed areas. UV lamps should be run only when the BSC is unoccupied
and the sash is down. Exposure to UV light can cause serious eye and skin injury.
\end{warningbox}

\subsection{Sharps Safety}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item Hypodermic needles, syringes with needles, and scalpel blades are used only
        when no safer alternative exists (e.g., blunt-end needles, needleless systems).
  \item \textbf{Recapping is prohibited.} Used needles are placed directly into an
        approved sharps container without bending, breaking, or removing from the syringe.
  \item Scalpel blades are removed with a forceps-style remover, never by hand.
  \item Glass Pasteur pipettes are strongly discouraged; plastic alternatives are
        preferred. When glass pipettes are used, they are decontaminated then disposed
        of in a dedicated \emph{broken-glass} container lined with a biohazard bag.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Labelling of Biological Materials}

All containers holding RG-2 agents or materials must be labelled with:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=2pt,
                  label=\textbf{\color{BSLamber}\arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{Biohazard symbol} and the word ``BIOHAZARD'' in orange or red.
  \item Common and scientific name of the agent or cell line.
  \item Date of preparation and concentration/titre (where applicable).
  \item Investigator name and contact number.
  \item Special handling instructions (e.g., ``Keep frozen'', ``Inactivated'').
\end{enumerate}

Labels must remain legible under storage conditions (cryogenic labels for
$-80^\circ$C material; waterproof labels for wet-ice usage).
