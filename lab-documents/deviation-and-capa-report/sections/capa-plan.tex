\subsection{Interim Controls}

Pending closure of CAPA-2 and CAPA-4, the corridor anteroom door is monitored by an
additional twice-daily manual DP spot-check logged on Form QA-014f, and material
transfer through the anteroom is escorted by a gowning monitor per an interim
instruction issued under change control CH-2026-0871.
