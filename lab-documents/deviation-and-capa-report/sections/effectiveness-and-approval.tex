\textbf{Verification conclusion.} DP readings across the three post-CAPA batches
remained within the 10\,Pa cascade minimum required by SOP-QA-014, and viable particle

confirm durability of the CAPA-3 retraining.

confirming no product impact from the excursion described in Section~1.
