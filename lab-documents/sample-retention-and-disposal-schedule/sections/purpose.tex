\section{Purpose and Scope}

\subsection{Purpose}
This schedule defines how long each class of sample handled by Nexa Diagnostics Laboratory
is retained after result reporting or study closeout, the regulatory or contractual basis for
each retention period, and the approved method of disposal once that period has elapsed. It
exists to satisfy three simultaneous obligations: (1)~keep samples available long enough to
support re-testing, audits, and adverse-finding investigations; (2)~free freezer, shelf, and
cold-room capacity on a predictable cycle; and (3)~ensure that disposal of biological,
chemical, and reference material is documented, witnessed, and traceable back to the
authorising signature.

\subsection{Scope}
This document covers all sample material accessioned into the laboratory information
management system (LIMS) under an Nexa sample ID, across the following repositories:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Clinical Testing} -- patient specimens received for diagnostic assays (blood,
        serum, plasma, urine, tissue, swabs).
  \item \textbf{Biobank} -- consented research specimens held under IRB protocol NX-IRB-118
        for longitudinal studies.
  \item \textbf{R\&D Chemistry} -- reference standards, stability samples, and analytical
        method development material.
  \item \textbf{Environmental Monitoring} -- water, air-particulate, and surface swab samples
        collected under the facility's environmental monitoring programme.
\end{itemize}

It does not cover primary reagents, calibrators, or QC material governed by
\emph{QMS-LAB-007 Reagent and Consumable Control}, nor electronic-only data retention, which is
addressed in \emph{QMS-IT-022 Records and Data Retention Policy}.

\subsection{Regulatory Basis}
Retention periods in Section~3 are set to the \emph{longest} of the applicable regulatory,
accreditation, and contractual minimums. Where two clauses conflict, the more conservative
figure governs unless Quality Assurance documents a rationale for departure.

\begin{noticebox}[Governing References]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item ISO 15189:2022 Clause 5.5.9 -- retention of examined samples where re-examination may
        be needed.
  \item CLIA 42 CFR \S 493.1105 -- record and specimen retention requirements for clinical
        laboratories.
  \item OECD Principles of Good Laboratory Practice (GLP), Section II.6 -- retention of raw
        data, specimens, and reserve samples.
  \item Institutional Biosafety Committee (IBC) Charter, Art. 9 -- disposal of biohazardous
        and consented human material.
  \item Sponsor contracts (R\&D Chemistry) -- retention terms per individual study agreement,
        recorded in LIMS field \texttt{contract\_retention\_override}.
\end{itemize}
\end{noticebox}
