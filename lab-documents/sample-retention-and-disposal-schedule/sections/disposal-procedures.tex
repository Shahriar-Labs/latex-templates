\section{Disposal Methods}

block and a Class~E water sample may both require autoclaving if bloodborne pathogens are
suspected. Table~\ref{tab:disposal-methods} lists the routes approved for use at Nexa
Diagnostics Laboratory.

\subsection{Disposal Workflow}
Every disposal follows the same four-step gate regardless of method; no sample leaves storage
for destruction without all four steps recorded in LIMS.

\subsection{Step Detail}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Eligibility flagged.} LIMS runs the nightly retention job against
        Section~3 and marks samples past their retention period as \emph{disposal-eligible}.
        No manual flagging is permitted.
  \item \textbf{Hold check.} The storage custodian queries the Legal Hold register before
        pulling any eligible sample. A sample under Class~F is skipped and remains in place.
  \item \textbf{Method and witness.} The custodian selects the method from
        Table~\ref{tab:disposal-methods} and books a second qualified staff member as witness;
        self-witnessed disposal is not accepted for Class~A or Class~B material.
  \item \textbf{Record and co-sign.} Both parties complete the disposal record in Section~5,
        including sample ID, method, date, and quantity, then co-sign. The record is scanned
        into LIMS within 24 hours; the physical form is retained per Section~3's Class~A
        record-retention line.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{noticebox}[Batch Disposal]
Environmental (Class~E) samples may be disposed of in batches of up to 40 using a single
disposal record listing all sample IDs, provided every sample in the batch shares the same
method, date, and witness pair. All other classes require one record per sample.
\end{noticebox}
