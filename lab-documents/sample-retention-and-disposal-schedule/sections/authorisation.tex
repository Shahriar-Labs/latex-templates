\begin{warnbox}[Escalation]
Any disposal-eligible sample belonging to a study under active sponsor audit, or any Class~A
specimen tied to an open patient complaint, must be escalated to the Quality Assurance
Manager \emph{before} the custodian step in Section~4.2 -- not deferred to the witness step.
Escalations are logged in LIMS under incident type \texttt{RETENTION\_HOLD\_REQUEST}.
\end{warnbox}
