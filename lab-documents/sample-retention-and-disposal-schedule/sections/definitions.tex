\section{Definitions and Sample Classes}

\subsection{Terms}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{3.6cm} Y@{}}
\toprule
Term & Definition \\
\midrule
Retention clock start & The date on which the retention period begins counting, typically
  final result release, study closeout, or last amendment date. \\
Retention period & The minimum time a sample must remain in storage before it becomes
  eligible for disposal. \\
Disposal eligibility & The status assigned in LIMS once the retention period has elapsed and
  no legal hold or open investigation applies. \\
Legal hold & A flag preventing disposal of an otherwise eligible sample, placed by Quality
  Assurance, Legal, or the sponsor pending litigation, audit, or complaint investigation. \\
Witnessed disposal & Destruction performed with a second qualified staff member present who
  co-signs the disposal record in Section~5. \\
Chain of custody & The unbroken, dated record of everyone who accessed, moved, or acted on a
  sample from accession through disposal. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Sample Classes}
Every sample accessioned into LIMS is assigned exactly one of the six classes below at intake;
the class field is locked once testing begins and can only be changed by Quality Assurance
with a documented reason.

\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=paperbg,colframe=deepteal,arc=2pt,boxrule=0.5pt,title={\faVial\ Class A -- Clinical Diagnostic},fonttitle=\bfseries\small\color{white},colbacktitle=deepteal]
\small Patient blood, serum, plasma, urine, and tissue submitted for a reportable diagnostic
result.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=paperbg,colframe=deepteal,arc=2pt,boxrule=0.5pt,title={\faSnowflake\ Class B -- Biobank Consented},fonttitle=\bfseries\small\color{white},colbacktitle=deepteal]
\small Research specimens held under active IRB consent for longitudinal or future-use
studies.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}
\par\vspace{6pt}
\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=paperbg,colframe=deepteal,arc=2pt,boxrule=0.5pt,title={\faFlask\ Class C -- Reference Standard},fonttitle=\bfseries\small\color{white},colbacktitle=deepteal]
\small Certified reference material and calibration standards used across multiple studies.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=paperbg,colframe=deepteal,arc=2pt,boxrule=0.5pt,title={\faFlask\ Class D -- Stability / R\&D},fonttitle=\bfseries\small\color{white},colbacktitle=deepteal]
\small Method development and stability-programme pulls tied to a sponsor study number.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}
\par\vspace{6pt}
\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=paperbg,colframe=deepteal,arc=2pt,boxrule=0.5pt,title={\faTint\ Class E -- Environmental},fonttitle=\bfseries\small\color{white},colbacktitle=deepteal]
\small Water, air-particulate, and surface swabs from the facility monitoring programme.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=paperbg,colframe=clayrust,arc=2pt,boxrule=0.5pt,title={\faExclamationTriangle\ Class F -- Restricted / Hold},fonttitle=\bfseries\small\color{white},colbacktitle=clayrust]
\small Any sample under legal hold, active complaint investigation, or sponsor audit --
disposal suspended regardless of elapsed time.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}
\end{center}
