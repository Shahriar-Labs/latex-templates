\section{Mycoplasma Contamination Screening}

Spent culture medium (collected 24--48 h post-feed, prior to antibiotic exposure) was tested
by quantitative PCR using the Nimbus MycoScreen qPCR Kit, which targets the conserved 16S
rRNA region shared across the \textit{Mycoplasma}, \textit{Acholeplasma}, and
\textit{Spiroplasma} genera most commonly implicated in cell culture contamination
\cite{uphoff_drexler_2011}.

Assay validity criteria were met: the positive control amplified within the expected Ct
window (16--21), both negative controls remained undetermined, and the internal
amplification control confirmed the absence of PCR-inhibiting substances in the test
sample. Per SOP NGC-QA-014, mycoplasma screening is repeated at every fifth passage and
immediately prior to any cryopreservation event.
