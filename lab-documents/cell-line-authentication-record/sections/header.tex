This record documents the identity verification of cell line VXO-114, maintained under
Nimbus Genomics Core Facility SOP NGC-QA-014 (\textit{Cell Line Authentication and
Contamination Screening}). Testing was performed on a cryopreserved working bank vial
thawed and expanded to passage~5 for analysis, in accordance with the ANSI/ATCC
ASN-0002-2011 standard for human cell line authentication by short tandem repeat (STR)
profiling \cite{ansi_atcc_asn0002}. Results, provenance documentation, and passage history
are summarised in the sections below.
