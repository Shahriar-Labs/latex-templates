\section{Purpose and Scope}

\subsection{Purpose}
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the controlled process for receiving,
labeling, storing, and monitoring laboratory reagents at Nexa Biosciences, including
temperature-sensitive and light-sensitive materials. The objective is to prevent reagent
degradation, cross-contamination, and traceability gaps that could invalidate downstream
analytical or cell-based assay results.

\subsection{Scope}
This procedure applies to all reagents, buffers, standards, and consumable chemical stocks
received into the Meridian Campus wet-lab suites (Rooms 2A, 2B, and 3C), including:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Commercially sourced reagents requiring 2--8\,\textdegree C, $-20$\,\textdegree C, or
        $-80$\,\textdegree C storage.
  \item In-house prepared buffers and working solutions assigned an internal lot number.
  \item Reference standards used for analytical method qualification and QC release testing.
\end{itemize}
This SOP does \textbf{not} cover controlled substances (see SOP-QC-021, \emph{Controlled
Substance Custody}) or biological samples under chain-of-custody (see SOP-QC-009).

\subsection{Regulatory Basis}
This procedure is written to satisfy the storage-and-handling expectations of ISO/IEC
17025:2017 \S 6.4 and the internal Nexa Quality Manual QM-01, and is cross-referenced during
annual internal audits \cite{iso17025}.

\begin{notebox}
Deviations from any numbered step in Section~4 must be logged per SOP-QC-002
(\emph{Deviation and CAPA Reporting}) before the affected reagent lot is released for use.
\end{notebox}
