\coasection{Test Methods \& Applicable Standards}

All testing reported in this Certificate of Analysis was performed by the Meridian Biochemicals Quality Control Laboratory, which operates under a quality management system aligned with the general requirements for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories described in ISO/IEC 17025 \cite{iso17025}. Compendial test methods are drawn from the current United States Pharmacopeia--National Formulary General Chapters, including Heavy Metals \cite{usp231}, Residue on Ignition \cite{usp281}, Melting Range or Temperature \cite{usp741}, and Water Determination \cite{usp921}. Where no compendial method exists, internally validated methods are used, each qualified for specificity, accuracy, and precision under document control reference QC-VAL-2024-014.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, label=\textcolor{meridianTeal}{\faCheck}]
\item Instruments used in generating this Certificate are maintained on a documented calibration schedule traceable to national measurement standards.
\item Reference standards and reagents used in testing are within their assigned expiry and are verified against certified reference materials at first use.
\item Raw data, chromatograms, and spectra supporting each result are retained in the laboratory information management system under Lot MB24-071-A and are available upon reasonable request.
\item Out-of-specification results, had any occurred, would trigger investigation under SOP QC-SOP-118 prior to lot disposition; no such investigation was required for this lot.
\end{itemize}
