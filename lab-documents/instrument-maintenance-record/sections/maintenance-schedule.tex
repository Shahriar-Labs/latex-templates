\subsection{Escalation Rule}

If a quarterly or higher-tier task is not closed within 10 working days of its due date, the facility manager escalates to Synapse Instrument Services dispatch and flags the instrument \badgeoverdue{Overdue} on the Core booking calendar until resolved. Users may not book Raman sessions on an instrument flagged overdue for a safety- or calibration-relevant task.
