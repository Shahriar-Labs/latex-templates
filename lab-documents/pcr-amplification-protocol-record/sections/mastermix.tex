\section{Master Mix Calculation}

Reaction volume is 20\,\uL\ per tube. Eleven reactions are run (8 test colonies, 1 no-template
control, 1 positive control, 1 pipetting-loss spare); the master mix below is scaled for
$n=13$ (11~reactions~$+$~2 overage) and excludes template, which is added individually to each
tube after aliquoting.
