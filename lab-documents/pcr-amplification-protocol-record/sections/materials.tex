\begin{cautionbox}
Prepare PCR master mix in the dedicated pre-PCR workstation only. Template addition and all
post-amplification handling (gel loading, imaging) occur at physically separate benches to
prevent amplicon carryover contamination. Never carry gloves, pipettes, or tube racks between
zones.
\end{cautionbox}
