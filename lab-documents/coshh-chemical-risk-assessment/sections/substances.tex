\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox]
  \faInfoCircle\quad
  \textbf{DMSO Carry-through Warning:} DMSO rapidly penetrates intact skin and
  carries dissolved co-contaminants into systemic circulation. Any substance
  dissolved in DMSO must be treated as if dermally toxic. Nitrile gloves alone
  are \textbf{insufficient} --- double-glove with an outer butyl rubber glove
  when handling DMSO-containing solutions.
\end{tcolorbox}
