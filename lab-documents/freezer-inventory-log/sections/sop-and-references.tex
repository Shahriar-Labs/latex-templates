\section{Standard Operating Procedures \& Custody Rules}
\label{sec:sop}

\subsection{Sample Check-In}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., itemsep=2pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Confirm the sample label matches the LIMS accession record (participant code, sample
        type, collection date) before opening the freezer lid.
  \item Select the destination box from the rack map (Section~\ref{sec:rackmap}); pre-stage
        the vial rack on the bench so freezer door-open time is minimised.
  \item Record position (Box-Row-Column), Sample ID, volume, and date frozen in the LIMS
        check-in form. This log's Section~\ref{sec:inventory} is regenerated nightly from that
        record.
  \item Return the box to its mapped slot immediately; do not leave boxes on intermediate
        shelves.
  \item Two-person verification is required for irreplaceable specimens (primary tissue,
        founder cell lines) per \citet{isber2023}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Sample Check-Out / Withdrawal}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., itemsep=2pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Retrieve the entire box, close the freezer lid, then locate the vial on the bench on
        a chill block or dry-ice tray.
  \item Update the freeze-thaw cycle count in the LIMS withdrawal form for any vial that is
        not fully consumed and returned.
  \item Aliquots at 3 freeze-thaw cycles are flagged \badgewarn{RETIRE} and routed to disposal
        per Section~\ref{sec:sop}.4.
  \item Log withdrawing investigator, purpose, and destination assay in the chain-of-custody
        field -- required for all IRB-governed specimens \citep{nih2016}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Power-Outage / High-Temperature Alarm Response}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., itemsep=2pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Acknowledge the SentryLog SMS/voice alarm within 15 minutes; escalate to the on-call
        Biorepository Manager if unacknowledged after 30 minutes.
  \item Confirm generator transfer status; do not open the freezer lid to inspect contents
        during an active excursion.
  \item If temperature exceeds \SI{-60}{\celsius} for more than 2 hours, initiate the
        dry-shipper transfer protocol and notify the PI immediately.
  \item File a deviation record (Section~\ref{sec:templog}) for every alarm event regardless
        of outcome.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{End-of-Study Disposal}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., itemsep=2pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Disposal requires written authorisation from the PI and confirmation that the
        specimen's consent terms and retention period have expired.
  \item Retired specimens are removed from their box, logged as \textsc{disposed} in the LIMS
        with date and witness initials, and the vacated position is marked available on the
        next rack-map revision.
  \item Autoclave or biohazard-stream disposal follows the Biorepository Core's waste-handling
        SOP; disposal is never performed by a single operator acting alone.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Standards \& References}
This freezer's inventory, custody, and monitoring practices follow
\citet{isber2023} and \citet{nih2016} for repository operations,
\citet{clsi2010} for specimen handling, and are audited annually against the
\citet{cap2022} biorepository accreditation checklist.
