\section{Rack \& Box Location Map -- Rack 1}
\label{sec:rackmap}

of 24 boxes per rack (VTX-B01 through VTX-B24). Box VTX-B12, outlined in bold below, is
detailed position-by-position in Section~\ref{sec:boxdetail}. Two slots are held vacant as
reserved growth capacity for incoming VGC-24 accessions.
