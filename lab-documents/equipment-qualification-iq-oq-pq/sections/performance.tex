\section{Performance Qualification (PQ)}
The Performance Qualification phase demonstrates that the Vertex V-Max Autoclave (Model: VM-250-QS) consistently achieves thermal and biological sterilization under worst-case load conditions. Testing was conducted using three consecutive replicate runs with a standard laboratory liquid load (30 bottles of 1\,L media surrogate).

\subsection{Temperature Mapping and Biological Challenge Setup}
A temperature mapping grid of twelve calibrated thermocouples (T1 to T12) was distributed inside the chamber. Thermocouple T12 was placed adjacent to the chamber drain (the predicted cold spot). Biological indicators (BIs, \emph{Geobacillus stearothermophilus} \(1.2 \times 10^6\) spores, Apex Lot GI-4882) were co-located with each thermocouple.

\subsection{Qualification Replicate Runs Summary}

\subsection{Lethality Calculation (F0)}
Lethality was calculated at the drain thermocouple (T12) using the following standard equation \citep{en285}:
\begin{equation}
F_0 = \int_{0}^{t} 10^{\frac{T(t) - 121.0}{10.0}} \, dt
\end{equation}
The calculated \(F_0\) values for all three runs exceed the minimum requirement of 15.0 minutes, verifying that thermal death conditions were successfully met in the coldest part of the chamber.

\begin{acceptancebox}
Performance Qualification requires three consecutive successful validation runs. In each run, the temperature spread across the twelve probes must not exceed 1.5\degC, the exposure time must be at least 20 minutes, the minimum biological indicator challenge must demonstrate complete inactivation (no growth), and the calculated lethality \(F_0\) at the drain must be \(\ge 15.0\) minutes.
\end{acceptancebox}

\begin{verdictbox}
Three replicate performance runs were completed without alarms. The physical mapping results and biological indicator assays demonstrate robust, repeatable sterilization.
