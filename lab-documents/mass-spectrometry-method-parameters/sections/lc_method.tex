\section{Liquid Chromatography Parameters}
Chromatographic separation is achieved using a binary high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) pump coupled to a temperature-controlled autosampler.

\subsection{HPLC Column Configuration}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{4.5cm} X}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Specification} \\
    \midrule
    Stationary Phase & Vertex C18 Analytical Column ($2.1 \times 100$~mm, $1.8~\mu$m) \\
    Guard Column & Vertex C18 Security Guard ($2.1 \times 5$~mm) \\
    Column Temperature & $40.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.5^\circ\text{C}$ \\
    Autosampler Temperature & $8.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$ \\
    Injection Volume & $10.0~\mu$L \\
    Flow Rate & $0.400$~mL/min \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Mobile Phase Composition}
Mobile Phase A consists of aqueous ammonium acetate solution, while Mobile Phase B consists of organic solvent mixture to ensure high ionization efficiency and symmetrical peak shapes \cite{smith2023high}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mobile Phase A:} $2~\text{mmol/L}$ Ammonium Acetate in LC-MS grade water.
    \item \textbf{Mobile Phase B:} $100\%$ LC-MS grade Methanol.
\end{itemize}
