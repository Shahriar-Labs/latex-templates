\section{Troubleshooting}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{L{3.8cm} L{4.5cm} X}
\toprule
\rowcolor{ProtoTeal!15}
\textbf{Observation} & \textbf{Likely Cause} & \textbf{Corrective Action} \\
\midrule
Turbid medium; pH drop (yellow) within 24\,h &
  Bacterial or yeast contamination &
  Discard flask in biohazard bag. Autoclave waste. Investigate media batches and cabinet HEPA filter. \\
\rowcolor{ProtoGray}
Floating strand-like structures in medium &
  Fungal contamination &
  Discard flask. Check water bath for fungal growth. Ensure media caps are not left loose. \\
Cells detach spontaneously without trypsin &
  Over-confluency; inadequate CO\textsubscript{2}; old medium &
  Confirm incubator CO\textsubscript{2} set-point (5\%). Increase passaging frequency. \\
\rowcolor{ProtoGray}
Low post-thaw viability (\textless\,70\%) &
  Rapid freeze rate; high passage number; DMSO toxicity &
  Verify $-1\,^\circ$C/min cool rate. Confirm DMSO added on ice. Discard vials \textgreater\,P30. \\
Poor adherence after thaw &
  Residual DMSO; damaged surface protein &
  Ensure DMSO thoroughly removed by centrifugation. Pre-coat flask with 0.1\% gelatin if needed. \\
\rowcolor{ProtoGray}
Cell morphology change (elongated, irregular) &
  Mycoplasma contamination (most common); passage drift &
  Submit cells for mycoplasma PCR (Form MRD-QC-011). Do not share reagents between cell lines. \\
Inconsistent growth rate between passages &
  Inaccurate cell counts; pipetting errors &
  Recalibrate counter against haemocytometer. Use wide-bore tips for gentle pipetting. \\
\rowcolor{ProtoGray}
Trypsin fails to detach cells within 8\,min &
  Over-growth; cold trypsin; FBS not removed &
  Warm trypsin to 37\,$^\circ$C. Wash twice with DPBS to remove all FBS traces. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\vspace{8pt}

\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox, title=\faMicroscope\enspace Mycoplasma Testing Schedule]
  All cell lines must be tested for mycoplasma contamination upon receipt, after thawing from
  any bank, and \textbf{at least every 3 months} during active culture. Approved testing method:
  PCR using the MRD--MYCO-PCR kit (Meridian BioSciences internal). Results must be filed in
  LIMS and a physical copy retained in the project notebook.
\end{tcolorbox}
