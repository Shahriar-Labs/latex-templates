\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox, title=\faThermometerHalf\enspace Media Preparation Note]
  Prepare complete growth media by supplementing basal medium with 10\% FBS,
  1\% Penicillin--Streptomycin, and \conc{2}{mM} L-Glutamine. Warm to 37\,$^\circ$C before
  use. Prepared media is stable for 4\,weeks at 4\,$^\circ$C. Label every bottle with the
  preparation date, cell line designation, and operator initials.
