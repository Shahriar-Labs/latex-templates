\section{Conclusion \& Recommendations}
Based on the monitoring results, the following recommendations and certification decisions have been made.

\begin{verdictbox}
\textbf{Cleanroom Status: Approved with Qualification} \\
With the exception of the resolved transient excursions at locations L-A03 and L-C03, all cleanroom environments in Apex Suite 4 were found to be operating within their established microbiological and physical states of control. The immediate corrective actions were successful, as demonstrated by the subsequent compliant re-testing. Production was permitted to proceed.
\end{verdictbox}

\subsection{Recommendations}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Tooling Preparation Protocol}: Update SOP-QC-MICRO-402 to mandate that all mechanical adjustment tools used within Grade A/B zones undergo a specialized ultra-clean wipe down and particle screening prior to chamber entry.
  \item \textbf{Gowning Air Flow Check}: Perform a diagnostic check on the HEPA filters in the Gowning Room (L-C03) to ensure appropriate air change rates are maintained during peak shift transitions.
  \item \textbf{Sampling Frequency}: Maintain the current routine monitoring frequency, but increase particle verification at L-A03 to twice per shift for the next two weeks as a precautionary measure.
\end{enumerate}
