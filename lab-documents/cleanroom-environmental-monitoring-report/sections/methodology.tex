\section{Monitoring Methodology \& Limits}
The environmental monitoring program employs a combination of physical and microbiological assays. 

\subsection{Monitoring Protocols}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Non-Viable Particles}: Measured using calibrated \textit{Apex Particle Tracer Pro} optical counters. Probes were placed isokinetically at working height. Sampling volume was 1.0\,\mthree{} (1000\,L) per sample location.
  \item \textbf{Active Air (Viable)}: Conducted using Synapse MAS-100 samplers pulling 1000\,L of air onto Tryptic Soy Agar (TSA) plates.
  \item \textbf{Settle Plates}: 90\,mm TSA plates exposed to cleanroom air for a maximum of 4 hours under laminar flow hoods.
  \item \textbf{Contact Plates}: 55\,mm RODAC plates containing TSA with lecithin and polysorbate 80, applied to surfaces with a standardized pressure for 10 seconds.
\end{itemize}
