\section{First-Week Checklist}
\label{sec:checklist}

Print this page, or keep the Vertex ELN copy open --- either way, work through it in order.
Your mentor and Marcus will check in with you on Friday to close out anything outstanding.

\begin{daybox}{Day 1 --- Orientation}
\begin{checklist}
  \item Get your building badge and key from Marcus Chen (Room 414).
  \item Set up your Vertex ELN, Nexa Reference, and lab chat accounts.
  \item Read Sections~\ref{sec:culture}--\ref{sec:safety} of this handbook.
  \item Meet your assigned mentor and agree a first-month project outline.
\end{checklist}
\end{daybox}

\begin{daybox}{Days 2--3 --- Safety \& Access}
\begin{checklist}
  \item Complete institutional biosafety and chemical-hygiene training.
  \item Locate the eyewash stations, spill kit, and fire pulls on your floor.
  \item Review the SDS binder for the reagents your project will use.
  \item Request your Synapse Cluster account from Owen Fitzgerald.
\end{checklist}
\end{daybox}

\begin{daybox}{Days 4--5 --- Getting Hands-On}
\begin{checklist}
  \item Shadow your mentor on one full experimental run, start to finish.
  \item Book your first supervised rig session in Apex Booking.
  \item Clone the lab's shared code repository and run its example analysis.
  \item Log your Day~4--5 notes in Vertex ELN before you leave Friday.
\end{checklist}
\end{daybox}

\begin{daybox}{By End of Week 2}
\begin{checklist}
  \item Rig-specific training signed off by your mentor in Vertex ELN.
  \item IACUC animal-handling certification scheduled or complete, if applicable.
  \item First one-on-one with Dr.\ Kowalski held.
  \item Individual development plan drafted with your mentor.
\end{checklist}
\end{daybox}
