\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox, title={\faVial\ Study Purpose}]
\footnotesize
This report establishes the Limit of Detection (LOD) and Limit of Quantitation (LOQ) of
the validated HPLC-UV method for Zolarvex, the principal degradation-related impurity
of Nimbufen, in support of the impurity control strategy defined for the finished
tablet product.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[resultbox, title={\faCheckCircle\ Headline Result}]
\footnotesize
\textbf{LOD} = 0.11\,ng/mL\\
\textbf{LOQ} = 0.33\,ng/mL\\
Confirmed by signal-to-noise assessment and a 10-replicate precision study at LOQ
(see Section~6).
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}
