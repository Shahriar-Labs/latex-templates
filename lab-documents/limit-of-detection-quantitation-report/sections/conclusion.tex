\section{Conclusion and Recommendations}

The HPLC-UV method of Nexa SOP QC-042 was demonstrated to have a Limit of Detection of
\textbf{0.11\,ng/mL} and a Limit of Quantitation of \textbf{0.33\,ng/mL} for Zolarvex
in Nimbufen Tablet placebo matrix, calculated from calibration-curve regression
statistics per ICH~Q2(R2) \citep{ich2023q2r2}. These values were corroborated by a
signal-to-noise assessment (Section~5.2) and confirmed by a ten-replicate precision
and recovery study at the calculated LOQ (Section~6, \%RSD 2.80\,\%, mean recovery
105.2\,\%), both comfortably within the SOP's trace-level acceptance criteria.

Considered together with the linearity data of Section~4 ($r^2 = 0.9998$ across
0.5--50\,ng/mL), the method is judged fit for its intended purpose \citep{eurachem2014}:
supporting identification, quantitation and trending of Zolarvex at levels relevant to
the impurity control strategy for Nimbufen Tablets. Applicability to any specific
reporting or qualification threshold under ICH~Q3B(R2) \citep{ich2005q3b} remains the
responsibility of Vertex Pharmaceuticals' Quality organisation, informed by the
sensitivity figures established here.

\subsection{Recommendations}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Adopt LOD = 0.11\,ng/mL and LOQ = 0.33\,ng/mL as the reportable sensitivity
        limits for Zolarvex under Nexa SOP QC-042.
  \item Re-verify LOD/LOQ upon any change of HPLC column lot, detector lamp, or
        instrument platform, and in any case no less frequently than every
        12 months, consistent with USP~$\langle 1225 \rangle$ lifecycle expectations
        \citep{usp1225}.
  \item Retain raw chromatographic data and this report for the retention period
        defined in the Nimbufen Tablets product file.
\end{itemize}
