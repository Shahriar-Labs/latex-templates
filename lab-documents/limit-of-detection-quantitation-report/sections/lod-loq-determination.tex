\section{LOD and LOQ Determination}

\subsection{Regression-Based Calculation}
Per the regression-based approach of ICH~Q2(R2) \citep{ich2023q2r2}, LOD and LOQ are
derived from the residual standard deviation of the calibration line, $S_{y/x}$, and
the slope of the calibration curve, $S$:

\begin{align}
\text{LOD} &= \frac{3.3 \, S_{y/x}}{S} \label{eq:lod}\\[4pt]
\text{LOQ} &= \frac{10 \, S_{y/x}}{S} \label{eq:loq}
\end{align}

Substituting the regression statistics from Table~\ref{tab:regstats}
($S_{y/x} = 612$\,mAU$\cdot$s, $S = 18486$\,mAU$\cdot$s per ng/mL):

\begin{align}
\text{LOD} &= \frac{3.3 \times 612}{18486} = 0.1092\ \text{ng/mL} \\
\text{LOQ} &= \frac{10 \times 612}{18486} = 0.3311\ \text{ng/mL}
\end{align}

Per Nexa SOP QC-042 rounding convention (two significant figures for trace-level
results):

\begin{tcolorbox}[resultbox, title={\faCheckCircle\ Reported Sensitivity}]
\centering
\textbf{LOD = 0.11 ng/mL} \hspace{2em} \textbf{LOQ = 0.33 ng/mL}
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Signal-to-Noise Confirmation}
As an orthogonal check, six blank placebo-extract injections established mean
baseline noise (peak-to-peak amplitude, Zolarvex retention-time window), and
spiked-matrix injections near the calculated LOD and LOQ were evaluated for
signal-to-noise (S/N) ratio using peak height, consistent with common chromatographic
practice for low-level noise assessment.

\centering

\midrule
Blank (unspiked) & not detected & 0.0058 & --- \\

The observed S/N of 3.1 at the calculated LOD and 10.2 at the calculated LOQ closely
match the conventional S/N benchmarks of 3 and 10 respectively, corroborating the
regression-based estimates in Section~5.1.

\begin{tcolorbox}[notebox, title={\faExclamationTriangle\ Interpretation Note}]
\footnotesize
LOD confirms detectability only; it must not be used to report a quantitative result.
Any Zolarvex signal observed between LOD and LOQ should be reported as
``detected, below LOQ'' per Nexa SOP QC-042 Section 9.3, not as a numeric value.
\end{tcolorbox}
