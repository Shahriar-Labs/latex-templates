The response is linear across the tested range, with $r^2 = 0.9998$ exceeding the SOP
QC-042 acceptance criterion of $r^2 \geq 0.995$, and no trend in residuals with
concentration, supporting the unweighted regression model used for the LOD/LOQ
calculation in Section~5.
