\section{Experimental Procedure}

\subsection{Standard Preparation}
A stock solution of Zolarvex Reference Standard was prepared at 1.00\,mg/mL in
diluent. This stock was serially diluted in placebo-matrix extract to yield seven
working calibration standards spanning the anticipated LOD/LOQ region:
0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 50\,ng/mL. Each level was prepared independently in
triplicate (21 discrete solutions) to allow both regression fitting and an estimate of
level-wise precision.

\subsection{Injection Sequence}
System suitability was demonstrated before sample analysis using five replicate
injections of the 10\,ng/mL standard: \%RSD of peak area $\leq 2.0\,\%$, USP tailing
factor $\leq 1.5$, and resolution from the nearest matrix-related peak $\geq 2.0$. The
analytical sequence then consisted of:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item Six blank (unspiked placebo extract) injections, interspersed through the
        run, used to characterise baseline noise (Section~5.2)
  \item Twenty-one calibration injections (seven levels~$\times$ three independent
        preparations), randomised across the sequence
  \item Ten independent replicate preparations of placebo extract spiked at the
        candidate LOQ concentration (0.330\,ng/mL), used for the precision and
        recovery confirmation in Section~6
  \item A closing system suitability check (10\,ng/mL standard, single injection)
        to confirm no drift occurred over the sequence
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Data Treatment}
Peak areas were integrated automatically by the CDS with a threshold of 0 and manually
verified against raw chromatograms by a second analyst. Calibration data were fitted
by unweighted linear regression, and LOD/LOQ calculated per Section~5, then
cross-checked by signal-to-noise comparison at the blank, near-LOD and near-LOQ
concentrations. In total, 39 injections (6 blanks + 21 calibration + 10 LOQ
confirmation + 2 system suitability) were completed within a single 24-hour
autosampler qualification window.
