\PermitHead{Personal Protective Equipment}{FORM MIBR-RS-5}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\textbf{Required for all unsealed-material work:}
\begin{itemize}[label=\OpenBox]
\item Disposable lab coat, buttoned
\item Double nitrile gloves, taped at the cuff
\item Safety glasses or goggles
\item Whole-body TLD badge, worn on the collar
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}%
\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\textbf{Additional for \textsuperscript{125}I and \textsuperscript{32}P:}
\begin{itemize}[label=\OpenBox]
\item Ring dosimeter, worn under the inner glove
\item Lead-glass bench shield in place
\item Absorbent bench-top liner
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
\par

\PermitHead{Standard Operating Procedure}{FORM MIBR-RS-6}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Confirm the permit is active and the planned activity is within the authorized limit for
the radionuclide in use.
\item Don required PPE listed above; verify the survey meter passes its daily source check.
\item Perform a background survey of the bench and shield before opening stock.
\item Work over an absorbent liner, within the designated shielded area, using tongs or a
remote pipetting aid for stock transfers exceeding 1 mCi.
\item Keep containers capped except during active transfer; return stock to the lead pig or
acrylic caddy immediately after use.
\item Survey gloved hands, tools, and the work surface with the pancake probe at the end of the
procedure; decontaminate any reading above 2$\times$ background before continuing.
\item Remove outer gloves and lab coat, survey bare hands and clothing at the doorway monitor,
and record the activity used, start/end time, and survey results in the isotope use log
(Form MIBR-RS-7).
\item Route liquid and solid waste to the correctly labeled decay-in-storage container; never
combine radionuclides with different half-lives in the same container.
\end{enumerate}
