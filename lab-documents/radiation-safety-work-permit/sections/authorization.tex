\PermitHead{Conditions of Permit}{FORM MIBR-RS-15}

\begin{itemize}[label=\OpenBox]
\item All authorized users complete Radiation Worker Training annually and before any change
in radionuclide or activity level.
\item Postings and labels comply with the facility's Radioactive Materials Area signage
standard and are inspected at each quarterly RSO audit.
\item Dosimetry badges are worn whenever unsealed material is handled and are never shared
between individuals.
\item This permit is void if the possession limit, radionuclide list, or authorized location
changes without a written amendment signed by the RSO.
\item The RSO or designee may suspend work under this permit at any time for cause, including a
missed wipe test, an unresolved contamination event, or an expired instrument calibration.
\item Waste is segregated by radionuclide and disposed of only through the Radiation Safety
Office decay-in-storage program; no radioactive material is placed in regular trash or sink
drains without documented RSO release.
\end{itemize}
