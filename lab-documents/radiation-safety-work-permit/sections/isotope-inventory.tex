\textbf{Total possession limit:} the sum of activity on hand for all unsealed radionuclides
listed above shall not exceed 11 mCi at any time, consistent with the possession limit assigned

isotope receipt/use/disposal ledger (Form MIBR-RS-7) within one working day of receipt.
