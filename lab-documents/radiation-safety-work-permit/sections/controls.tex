\PermitHead{Monitoring Requirements}{FORM MIBR-RS-4}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\textbf{Instrumentation.} A calibrated GM pancake survey meter (beta/gamma) shall be present at
each use bench. \textsuperscript{125}I work additionally requires a NaI thyroid probe available
for bioassay. Survey meters are calibrated annually against the \textsuperscript{137}Cs check
source and verified daily with a source-check reading logged in Form MIBR-RS-9, consistent with
performance criteria in~\cite{ansi_n13_11}.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Area surveys.} Meter surveys of bench surfaces, floor, and hood sash are required at the
end of every use session. Results below 2$\times$ background require no action; results at or
above 2$\times$ background trigger decontamination and a wipe test before the area is released.
\end{minipage}%
\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\textbf{Wipe testing.} Weekly wipe tests (100 cm\textsuperscript{2} template) are required for
all active use and storage locations while unsealed material is on hand. Removable contamination
at or above 200 dpm/100 cm\textsuperscript{2} requires decontamination, re-wipe, and an entry in
the contamination log.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Postings.} Room 214A is posted ``Caution -- Radioactive Materials'' with the trefoil
symbol at each entrance. Storage containers and waste receptacles are individually labeled with
radionuclide, activity, and assay date.
\end{minipage}
