\textbf{Description of authorized work.} This permit authorizes the receipt, storage, and use of
unsealed byproduct material for nucleic acid and protein labeling procedures, including
\textsuperscript{32}P and \textsuperscript{35}S incorporation into cDNA and riboprobes for
Northern and Southern hybridization, \textsuperscript{125}I radioimmunoassay and receptor-binding
studies, and \textsuperscript{3}H metabolic labeling of membrane lipids. A sealed
\textsuperscript{137}Cs check source is authorized for daily constancy checks of the laboratory's
liquid scintillation counter and survey instruments. Work is confined to the fume hood and bench
areas designated in Room 214A and posted as a Radioactive Materials Area under
\S 20.1902 of the applicable federal radiation protection standard~\cite{nrc10cfr20}.
