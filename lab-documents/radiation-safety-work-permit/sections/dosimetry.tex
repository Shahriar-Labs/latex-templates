\textbf{Administrative control level.} A written investigation is triggered whenever any
individual's cumulative annual reading reaches 500 mrem (10\% of the annual occupational limit).
No user on this permit has exceeded the control level in the current dosimetry cycle.

\vspace{4pt}

\textbf{Bioassay.} Thyroid bioassay by NaI probe is required within 72 hours of any single
handling event involving more than 1 mCi of volatile \textsuperscript{125}I, and quarterly for
routine users of the isotope regardless of single-handling activity. Results are recorded on
Form MIBR-RS-10 and retained for the duration of employment plus 30 years, consistent with
operational program recommendations in~\cite{ncrp127}.
