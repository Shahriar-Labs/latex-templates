\section{Roles \& Responsibilities}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\color{accent}\bfseries}l@{\hspace{8mm}}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
  \toprule
  Role & Primary Responsibilities \\
  \midrule
  EHS Manager         & Develop and maintain this manual; conduct inspections; manage incident
                        reporting; liaise with regulatory agencies. \\[4pt]
  Principal Investigator (PI) & Ensure group members are trained; maintain current chemical
                        inventory; enforce compliance; conduct lab-specific safety orientation. \\[4pt]
  Lab Supervisor      & Day-to-day oversight of safe work practices; maintain first-aid kits
                        and eyewash stations; update Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). \\[4pt]
  Laboratory Personnel & Complete required training before using chemicals or equipment; report
                        hazards and near-misses immediately; follow posted SOPs. \\[4pt]
  Facilities          & Maintain ventilation, safety showers, eyewash stations, and building
                        infrastructure; respond to EHS requests within 24 hours. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
