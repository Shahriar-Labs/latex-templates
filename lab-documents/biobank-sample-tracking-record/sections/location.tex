\begin{alertbox}{\faExclamationTriangle\quad Cryogenic Handling Warning}
\sffamily\small
Cryovials stored in the vapor phase of liquid nitrogen present an explosion hazard if liquid nitrogen has penetrated the vial seal. Wear full face shield, cryogenic gloves, and protective apron during retrieval. Perform all warming steps inside the designated biological safety cabinet (BSC-04).
\end{alertbox}
