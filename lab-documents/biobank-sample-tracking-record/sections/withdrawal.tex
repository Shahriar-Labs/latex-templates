\section{Withdrawal Handling \& Processing Log}

To maintain sample integrity, the standard operating procedure (SOP-BTR-402) for specimen withdrawal must be executed without deviations. High-priority checks are outlined below.

\subsection{Standard Operating Procedure for Specimen Thawing}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Verification:} Cross-reference the 2D barcode on the cryovial with the SBRS database to verify the donor ID and active research consent.
  \item \textbf{Cold-Chain Maintenance:} Transport vials from Vault 4 using dry ice transport carts (\(-78.5\degC\)). Do not expose vials to ambient temperatures during transport.
  \item \textbf{Thawing Protocol (for PBMCs):} Immerse the lower half of the cryovial in a \(37.0\degC\) water bath for approximately 75--90 seconds. Gently swirl. Remove from the water bath immediately when a tiny ice crystal remains to prevent heat damage.
  \item \textbf{Washing:} Dilute the thawed cell suspension dropwise with pre-warmed RPMI-1640 medium containing 10\% FBS and \(100\,\text{\textmu g\,mL}^{-1}\) DNase I. Centrifuge at \(300 \times g\) for 10 minutes.
  \item \textbf{Volume Log:} Record the exact volume and cell count immediately after centrifugation. Update the SBRS database.
\end{enumerate}
