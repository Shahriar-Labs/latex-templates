\section{Quality Control \& Custody Authorization}

Before final release or long-term archiving, the specimen must be certified against quality standards established under ISO 20387 \cite{iso20387}.

\subsection{Quality Control Metrics}
The following table summarizes the quality control values obtained during the initial processing and subsequent thaws:

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent{\small\sffamily
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3.5pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}X c c c l@{}}
  \rowcolor{bioteal}
  \color{white}\bfseries Parameter &
  \color{white}\bfseries Target Metric &
  \color{white}\bfseries Observed Value &
  \color{white}\bfseries Status &
  \color{white}\bfseries Test Method \\[2pt]
  Total Cell Yield & \(\ge 5.0 \times 10^6\) & \(6.0 \times 10^6\) & \qcpass & Cell Counter \\
  \rowcolor{biogray}
  Post-Thaw Viability & \(\ge 90.0\%\) & 94.2\% & \qcpass & Trypan Blue \\
  DNA Integrity Number (DIN) & \(\ge 7.0\) & 8.4 & \qcpass & TapeStation \\
  \rowcolor{biogray}
  Mycoplasma Screen & Negative & Negative & \qcpass & PCR Assay \\
  Endotoxin Level & \(< 0.1~\text{EU/mL}\) & \(< 0.05~\text{EU/mL}\) & \qcpass & LAL Assay \\
