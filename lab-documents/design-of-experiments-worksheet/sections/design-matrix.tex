\section{Design Matrix and Run Order}

\subsection*{3.1 Design geometry}
The eight factorial vertices of the \(2^{3}\) cube are labelled below in standard Yates
notation; each letter present indicates that factor is at its \emph{high} level, and
\((1)\) denotes all factors low. Three additional centre-point replicates (not shown on the
cube) sit at the geometric middle and are used only to test for curvature.

\begin{center}

  \def\side{3.0}

\begin{notebox}[Worked example --- how to record a response]
Suppose Run~4 (all factors low) yields 44 of 50 panels passing and a mean tensile of
36.2~MPa, read at 07{:}42 by operator J.\,Okafor. Enter: \(Y_1 = \mathbf{88.0}\),
\(Y_2 = \mathbf{36.2}\), Op./Time = \textbf{JO / 07:42}. Record the failing-panel defect
mode in the Appendix notes field, not on this sheet.
\end{notebox}
