\section{Cycle Selection and Parameter Reference}

To ensure complete microbial lethality (sterilisation validation), operators must match the load contents to the verified SOP autoclave cycles below \cite{iso17665moistheat, cdc2008guideline}:

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.49\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \begin{guidebox}[title={\faInfoCircle\ \textbf{Approved Cycle Profiles}}]
    \small
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l L cc}
      \textbf{Cycle ID} & \textbf{Load Types} & \textbf{Target Temp} & \textbf{Target Time} \\
      \midrule
      \textbf{P1-GRAV} & Glassware, metal instruments, dry goods & 121.0\degC & 30\minunit \\
      \textbf{P2-PREV} & Porous loads, garments, filters, tubing & 134.0\degC & 4\minunit \\
      \textbf{P3-LIQD} & Aqueous buffers, growth media, saline & 121.0\degC & 45\minunit\ (Slow) \\
      \textbf{P4-DECN} & Biohazard waste bags, spent plates & 121.0\degC & 60\minunit \\
    \end{tabularx}
  \end{guidebox}
\end{minipage}
\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.49\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \begin{guidebox}[title={\faExclamationTriangle\ \textbf{Key Validation Requirements}}]
    \small
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Chemical Indicators (CI):} Every pack or container must contain a Class 5 or Class 6 integrating indicator tape/strip \cite{rutala2013sterilization}.
      \item \textbf{Biological Indicators (BI):} A \textit{Geobacillus stearothermophilus} spore ampoule must be placed in the coldest point (drain line) weekly.
      \item \textbf{Cycle Verification:} Physical printouts showing critical values must be signed, dated, and archived with this log.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{guidebox}
\end{minipage}
