\subsection{Degradation Trend}
Figure~\ref{fig:trend} plots assay against time for all three conditions on a common
axis, against the lower acceptance limit of 95.0\,\%. The accelerated slope is
approximately 4.5\,$\times$ steeper than the long-term slope, consistent with an
Arrhenius-type thermal degradation mechanism and providing no evidence of a
condition-dependent change in degradation pathway.
