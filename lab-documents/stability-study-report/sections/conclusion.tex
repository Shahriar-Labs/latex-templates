\section{Conclusion and Recommendations}

\begin{shelflifebox}
Based on 12 months of long-term data, pooled across three batches, all attributes
remain well within acceptance criteria and the pooled assay regression supports
retention above the 95.0\,\% lower limit through at least month 34 (lower 95\,\%
confidence bound). Testing continues through the full 36-month commitment; on the
strength of the trend to date:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Provisional shelf life: 24 months} from date of manufacture, to be
        extended to 36 months upon successful completion of the 18- and 24-month
        long-term intervals.
  \item \textbf{Recommended storage statement:} Store below 25\,\degC. Keep the
        blister strip in the outer carton to protect from light and moisture.
  \item \textbf{Retest / re-evaluation date for this report:} the 18-month interval,
        due September 2026.
\end{itemize}
\end{shelflifebox}

\subsection{Recommendations}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Continue the study per protocol through the 36-month long-term pull point and
        the 12-month intermediate pull point already reported here.
  \item No formulation, process, or packaging change is warranted based on this
        interval's data.
  \item Re-run the poolability analysis at each subsequent interval; if a batch
        diverges from the pooled trend by more than 1.5\,\% at any common time point,
        report individually rather than pooled.
  \item Carry the mild accelerated-condition yellowing forward as a watch item for the
        18-month long-term visual assessment, though it is not considered
        stability-limiting at this interval.
\end{enumerate}
