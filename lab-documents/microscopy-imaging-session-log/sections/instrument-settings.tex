\section{Objective, Filter \& Exposure Settings}

Two acquisition passes were configured on the Vertex Ti2-E: a \textbf{survey pass}
at 20$\times$ to locate healthy, well-spread fields, and a \textbf{detail pass} at
60$\times$ oil immersion for quantitative channel capture. Filter cube and camera
settings for each pass are fixed for the duration of the session; any manual
override during acquisition is logged separately in \S\ref{sec:acqlog}.

\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox,title=\faLightbulb\ Illumination Notes]
\begin{itemize}
\item Metal-halide source (Meridian X-Cite 200e), 60\% neutral density filter engaged on all fluorescence channels.
\item Detail-pass Z-stacks: 0.35~\textmu m step, 15 planes, centred on best-focus brightfield slice.
\item Autofocus: software contrast-based, GFP channel, run once per field before the stack.
\item Cy5 cube left in the turret for a planned future far-red marker; not used this session.
\end{itemize}
