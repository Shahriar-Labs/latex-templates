\section{Image File Naming Scheme}

Every raw file from this facility follows a fixed eight-token pattern so that
downstream scripts can parse metadata directly from the filename without
reading embedded TIFF tags. The pattern is:

\begin{center}
\texttt{\textbf{YYYYMMDD\_SAMPLEID\_REP\_OBJ\_CHANNEL\_zNN-NN\_tNN.tif}}
\end{center}

The worked example below is frame 004 from the acquisition log
(\S\ref{sec:acqlog}), with each token colour-matched to its definition in
the table beneath it.

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l l p{6.4cm}@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{inkslate}
\textcolor{paperwhite}{\textbf{Token}} & \textcolor{paperwhite}{\textbf{Example}} & \textcolor{paperwhite}{\textbf{Meaning}} \\
\midrule
Date          & \texttt{20260803}  & Acquisition date, ISO 8601, no separators \\
Sample ID     & \texttt{NX-SLD014} & LIMS slide/sample identifier \\
\rowcolor{fluorgreen!12}
Replicate     & \texttt{R1}         & Biological replicate number on this slide \\
Objective     & \texttt{60x}        & Nominal magnification of the objective used \\
\rowcolor{fluorred!12}
Channel       & \texttt{DAPI}       & Filter cube / channel name (matches \S2) \\
Z-range       & \texttt{z01-15}     & First--last plane index of the Z-stack \\
\rowcolor{fluorfar!12}
Timepoint     & \texttt{t01}        & Field/timepoint index within the replicate \\
Extension     & \texttt{.tif}       & Uncompressed 16-bit TIFF, one channel per file \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox,title=\faFile\ Survey-Pass Variant]
Survey frames drop the Z-range and timepoint tokens in favour of a single
field index, e.g.\ \texttt{20260803\_NX-SLD014\_R1\_20x\_GFP\_f01.tif}. The
\texttt{f}-prefixed field index is never reused across passes, so survey and
detail filenames for the same physical location will not collide.
\end{tcolorbox}
