    \small\color{NexaText}
    This 10$\times$ PBS stock concentrate is formulated for use in mammalian cell
    culture, immunofluorescence, Western blotting, flow cytometry, and general
    molecular biology applications requiring a precisely buffered isotonic saline
    solution.

    \medskip
    Dilute 1:10 with ultrapure water (Type~I, $\geq 18\,\text{M}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$)
    to obtain standard 1$\times$ PBS at physiological ionic strength. The concentrate
    is stable at room temperature for up to \textbf{12 months} and indefinitely
    at $4\,^{\circ}$C when stored in sealed, sterile bottles.

    \medskip
    \textbf{Typical applications:}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=5mm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=1pt]
      \item Cell washing between staining steps (immunocytochemistry)
      \item Tissue cryosection rehydration (10 min at RT)
      \item Antibody and protein dilution buffer
      \item FACS sheath fluid preparation (filtered, 0.2\,\ensuremath{\mu} m)
      \item Equilibration of chromatography columns

\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.54\linewidth}
  \begin{tcolorbox}[tealbox, title={\faFlask\enskip Preparation Protocol}]
    \small\color{NexaText}
    \begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=5mm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=1pt, label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
      \item Weigh all solid components to $\pm0.01\,\text{g}$ accuracy using an
            analytical balance (Nexa PrecisioScale A200).
      \item Dissolve salts in $\sim\!800\,\text{mL}$ ultrapure water in a
            2\,L borosilicate glass beaker with magnetic stirring.
      \item Stir at room temperature (20\,--\,25\,$^{\circ}$C) for
            $\geq 15\,\text{min}$ until fully dissolved.
      \item Adjust pH to \textbf{6.8\,--\,7.0} (see pH Adjustment note).
      \item Bring total volume to exactly \textbf{1.0\,L} with ultrapure water.
      \item Transfer to clean 1\,L glass bottles; cap tightly.
      \item For sterile lots: filter through 0.22\,\ensuremath{\mu} m PES membrane
            under laminar flow (Nexa FlowGuard~II BSC).
      \item Label with lot number, date, preparer, and expiry.
      \item Log in LIMS under protocol PBS--10X--SOP--v3.
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}%
\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.44\linewidth}
  \begin{tcolorbox}[goldbox, title={\faExclamationTriangle\enskip pH Adjustment Note}]
    \small\color{NexaText}
    The 10$\times$ concentrate is titrated to \textbf{pH~6.8\,--\,7.0}. Upon
    dilution to 1$\times$, the pH rises slightly to the target physiological
    range of \textbf{7.2\,--\,7.4}.

    \medskip
    \textbf{Adjustment agents:}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=4mm, itemsep=1pt, topsep=1pt]
      \item Raise pH: 1\,M NaOH (dropwise, with stirring)
      \item Lower pH: 1\,M HCl (dropwise, with stirring)
    \end{itemize}

    \medskip
    \textbf{Measurement:} Calibrate pH electrode immediately before use
    with NIST-traceable buffers at pH~4.0, 7.0, and 10.0. Allow
    reading to stabilise ($>30\,\text{s}$) before recording. Temperature-compensate
    to $25\,^{\circ}$C. Cross-check with indicator strips (range 6\,--\,8)
    as a secondary method.

    \medskip
    \begin{tcolorbox}[warnbox]
      {\color{NexaWarn}\faExclamationCircle}\enskip
      {\bfseries\color{NexaWarn} Do not use autoclave-sterilised lots}
      for pH-sensitive applications. Autoclaving may shift pH by
      $\pm0.3$ units.
    \end{tcolorbox}
  \end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}
