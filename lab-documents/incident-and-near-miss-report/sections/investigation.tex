\section{Incident Investigation \& Root Cause Analysis}
\thinrule

\vspace{0.5em}

This section outlines the findings of the joint investigation conducted by the EHS Committee and the Organic Synthesis Lab Management. It identifies the root causes and lists the Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) to prevent recurrence.

\subsection{Investigation Findings}
A physical inspection of the reaction apparatus and interview with the involved personnel revealed the following:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Condenser Blockage:} The glass reflux condenser gas outlet (which should vent to the atmosphere or a bubbler) was completely blocked by a white crystalline substance. Chemical analysis confirmed this was a mixture of acetyl chloride hydrolysis products and recrystallized substrate, which accumulated due to cooling parameters and poor vent flushing.
    \item \textbf{Pressure Buildup:} Since the reaction was heated in a closed system with a blocked vent, volatile dichloromethane ($bp \approx 40$\,\textdegree{}C) and acetyl chloride vapors built up pressure. The ground-glass joints were clamped with standard plastic Keck clips, which held until the pressure exceeded their mechanical resistance, resulting in a sudden ejection of the glass joint.
    \item \textbf{Fume Hood sash height:} The fume hood sash was positioned at 18 inches. While the hood's face velocity was within the safety range ($105 \pm 5$ fpm), the high sash height allowed the pressurized spray to escape the physical containment zone and strike the operator.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Root Cause Analysis (5-Whys Method)}
To determine the systemic failures, the investigation team applied the 5-Whys methodology:
\begin{description}
    \item[Why 1?] Why did the chemical splash occur? \emph{Because a ground-glass joint on the reaction flask popped open, spraying liquid mixture out of the flask.}
    \item[Why 2?] Why did the joint pop open? \emph{Because pressure built up inside the round-bottom flask during heating.}
    \item[Why 3?] Why did pressure build up? \emph{Because the venting pathway on the reflux condenser was blocked by solid byproducts.}
    \item[Why 4?] Why was the blockage not identified before heating? \emph{Because the Standard Operating Procedure (SOP-ORG-042) did not require verification of vent path clearance before heating reactions.}
    \item[Why 5?] Why was the SOP lacking this requirement? \emph{Because the risk assessment for acylation reactions had not been updated to reflect the hazards of sublimation/byproduct blockage in venting ports (Root Cause).}
\end{description}
