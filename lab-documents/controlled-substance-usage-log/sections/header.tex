\begin{storagebox}
\textbf{WARNING: This log book is a legally binding compliance record.} All entries must be made chronologically in indelible blue or black ink immediately at the time of substance receipt, administration, transfer, or waste. Under federal regulations (21 CFR \S1304), any discrepancies, spillages, or unexplained losses must be reported immediately to the DEA Field Division and the Meridian Compliance Officer. 

Controlled substances must be stored in a securely locked, substantially constructed cabinet (double-lock system) accessible only to authorized registrants listed on the approved drug protocol.
\end{storagebox}

\begin{instructionbox}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stock Receipt:} Record receipt of new drug stock on a new ledger sheet immediately upon opening.
  \item \textbf{Entry Timing:} All usage (administration, dilution, or transfer) must be logged immediately. Do not defer entries.
  \item \textbf{Running Balance:} Calculate and record the running volume/mass balance after every single transaction.
  \item \textbf{Wasting \& Disposal:} Any residual drug disposal or wasting must be performed in the presence of a second authorized researcher who must sign as the witness in the designated column.
  \item \textbf{Close-out:} When the vial/container balance reaches 0.00, the Principal Investigator and Compliance Officer must perform a physical audit and sign the close-out verification section.
\end{enumerate}
\end{instructionbox}
