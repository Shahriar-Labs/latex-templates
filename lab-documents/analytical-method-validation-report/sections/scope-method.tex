\section{Scope and Method Summary}

\subsection{Scope}
This validation applies to the assay determination of Nimbatinib in Nimbatinib Tablets, 50\,mg,
across the labelled dosage strength and manufacturing process registered under Meridian
Pharmaceuticals product code MER-NMB-050. The method is stability-indicating: the same
chromatographic conditions are used to resolve and quantify the principal specified degradation
product, Impurity~A (des-methyl nimbatinib), from the active moiety. Results in this report were
generated on the Nimbus LC-3200 platform; a bridging verification on an alternate HPLC platform
is documented separately under transfer protocol VT-AM-0142-01 and is out of scope here.
