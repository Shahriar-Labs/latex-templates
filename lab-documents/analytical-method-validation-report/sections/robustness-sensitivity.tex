\section{Robustness and Sensitivity}

\subsection{Robustness}

conditions to evaluate their effect on system suitability. The working standard solution
(0.500\,\mgml) was injected in duplicate at each condition.

\begin{criteriabox}
Under every deliberate variation: resolution vs.\ Impurity~A $\geq$\,2.0; tailing factor

\subsubsection{Solution Stability}
Standard and sample solutions, stored capped at 2--8\,\degC{} and protected from light, were
re-injected at 24\,h and 48\,h against freshly prepared reference solutions. Peak area for the
Nimbatinib peak differed from the initial (0\,h) result by no more than 0.8\% at 24\,h and 1.4\%
at 48\,h (criterion: $\leq$\,2.0\% difference). Both solutions are assigned a validated
stability hold time of \textbf{48 hours at 2--8\,\degC}.

\subsection{Limit of Detection and Limit of Quantitation}
LOD and LOQ were established for Impurity~A, the principal specified degradation product
co-quantified by this method, using the standard-deviation-of-response/slope approach on the
Section~3 calibration curve (residual standard deviation $\sigma = 2150$; slope
$S = 4{,}860{,}000$) \citep{ich2005q2}.

\[
\text{LOD} = \frac{3.3\,\sigma}{S} = 0.00146\,\mgml\ (0.29\%\ \text{of nominal}) \qquad
\text{LOQ} = \frac{10\,\sigma}{S} = 0.00442\,\mgml\ (0.88\%\ \text{of nominal})
\]

Both limits were confirmed experimentally: six replicate injections at the calculated LOQ level
gave a mean peak area with \%RSD of 4.8\% (criterion: $\leq$\,10\% at LOQ, \passbadge); the
LOD-level solution produced a signal-to-noise ratio of 3.4:1 (criterion: $\geq$\,3:1,
\passbadge).
