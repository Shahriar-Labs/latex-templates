\subsection*{\color{valnavy}Purpose}
This report documents the validation of analytical method \textbf{AM-HPLC-0142, Version 3.0},
used for the quantitative determination of Nimbatinib in Nimbatinib Tablets, 50\,mg, by
reversed-phase high-performance liquid chromatography (RP-HPLC) with ultraviolet detection.
Validation was executed against approved protocol VP-AM-0142-03 and evaluates specificity,
linearity, range, accuracy, precision, detection/quantitation limits, and robustness, in
accordance with ICH Q2 guidance and the sponsor's Analytical Quality Agreement. The method is
intended for routine release and stability testing of the drug product.
