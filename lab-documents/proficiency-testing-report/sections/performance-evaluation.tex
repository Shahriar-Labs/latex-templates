\section{Performance Evaluation}
\label{sec:performance-evaluation}

\subsection{Individual Verdict --- Participant L07}

\begin{tcolorbox}[critbox, title={\faTimesCircle\ Performance Verdict: UNSATISFACTORY (Lead, Pb)}]
Participant \textbf{L07} reported \textbf{10.20~\(\mu\)g/L} against an assigned value of
8.20~\(\mu\)g/L, giving \(z = 3.08\). This exceeds the \(|z| \geq 3\) action-signal threshold
defined in Section~\ref{sec:statistical-methods} and constitutes a formal \textbf{Unsatisfactory}
result requiring documented investigation under ISO/IEC 17025:2017 clause 7.10 (nonconforming
work) and clause 8.7 (corrective action).
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Root-cause hypotheses to be investigated by L07}, in order of likelihood for a
high positive Pb bias of this magnitude:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Calibration drift} --- verify the calibration curve against an independently
        sourced check standard; a stale or improperly stored Pb stock is the most common cause.
  \item \textbf{Spectral/matrix interference} --- confirm internal standard recovery and
        polyatomic interference corrections were applied correctly for this batch.
  \item \textbf{Digestion/acid-matrix mismatch} --- confirm the digestion blank and recovery
        spike results for this batch.
  \item \textbf{Transcription or dilution error} --- rule out a factor-of-error in transcription
        or an incorrect dilution factor.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Required response.} L07 must submit a written root-cause investigation and
corrective action plan (CAPA) to Meridian within \textbf{30 calendar days} of this report's issue
date (deadline: \textbf{21 May 2026}). Meridian will issue L07 a complimentary re-test sample for
Pb only, free of charge, to verify the effectiveness of the corrective action once implemented.
Failure to submit a CAPA within the deadline will be noted in L07's PT participation history and
may be requested by L07's accreditation body.

\subsection{Round-Level Verdict}

\begin{tcolorbox}[satbox, title={\faCheckCircle\ Overall Round Verdict: VALID AND SATISFACTORY}]
The scheme design, homogeneity/stability testing, and Algorithm A assigned-value computation for
PT Round PTS-2026-Q2-METALS-014 are judged \textbf{valid} for performance evaluation purposes.
71.4\% of participants (10 of 14) returned a Satisfactory result for the primary analyte, within
the range typically observed across Meridian's metals-in-water schemes (65--85\% Satisfactory).
The two Unsatisfactory results (L07, L11) show deviations of opposite sign and materially
different magnitude, and are consistent with independent laboratory-specific root causes rather
than any systematic bias in sample preparation or assigned-value derivation.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{0.6em}
No corrective action is required of Meridian or of the scheme design itself. Individual
corrective-action requirements for participants L07 and L11 are issued separately to each
laboratory concerned; the requirement applicable to L07 is detailed above. Participant L13, whose
result fell in the Questionable band (\(z = 2.38\)), is not subject to a mandatory CAPA but is
advised to review the relevant measurement step as a precaution, per Section~\ref{sec:recommendations}.
