\subsection{Observation Relevant to L07}
L07's Unsatisfactory Pb result (Section~\ref{sec:performance-evaluation}) is the finding requiring
mandatory corrective action. However, Table~\ref{tab:multi} also shows a Questionable score for
copper (\(z = -2.56\)) for the same participant. Although this does not on its own trigger a
formal CAPA, Meridian recommends that L07's root-cause investigation into the Pb result also
review whether the same instrument, calibration batch, or digestion run was shared with the
copper determination, since a shared negative bias across two analytes on the same instrument run
is a common early indicator of a single upstream cause (most often calibration or internal
standard recovery) rather than two unrelated issues.
