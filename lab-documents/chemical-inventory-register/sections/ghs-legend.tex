\registersection{GHS Pictogram Legend}

\noindent\small Every pictogram reproduced below is drawn to the proportions of Annex~V of
the GHS Purple Book \cite{ghs2023}: a black symbol centred on a white field inside a
red-bordered diamond. The Inventory Register (page~\pageref{sec:register}) prints a
scaled-down copy of the relevant pictogram(s) beside each entry.

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent

\centering \ghsExplosive{1.6cm} \\[3pt]
{\bfseries\color{hazardred}GHS01 \textbullet\ Explosive} \\[1pt]
{\footnotesize Unstable explosives, self-reactives, organic peroxides.}
\end{minipage}%
