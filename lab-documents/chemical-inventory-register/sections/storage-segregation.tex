\registersection{Storage Locations \& Segregation Groups}

\noindent\small Cabinets are physically separated by at least 3\,m or a fire-rated partition
wherever an incompatibility is marked below, per NFPA 704 \cite{nfpa704} and site policy
EHS-P-07. Locked cabinets require a two-person checkout logged in the LIMS.
