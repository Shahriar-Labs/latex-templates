\section{Gating Strategy \& Instrument QC}
\label{sec:gating}

\subsection{Gating Hierarchy}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Time gate --- exclude flow instability (flag any run with a
  fluidics interruption before analysis).
\item Singlets --- FSC-H vs.\ FSC-A, then SSC-H vs.\ SSC-A.
\item Lymphocyte scatter gate --- FSC-A vs.\ SSC-A.
\item Live cells --- Zombie-UV negative.
\item CD3\(^{+}\) T cells.
\item CD4\(^{+}\)CD8\(^{-}\) and CD4\(^{-}\)CD8\(^{+}\) lineage split.
\item Within CD4\(^{+}\): CD25\textsuperscript{hi}CD127\textsuperscript{lo}
  candidate Treg gate, confirmed against the CD25 and CD127 FMOs.
\item FoxP3\(^{+}\) confirmation within the candidate Treg gate.
\item CCR7 vs.\ CD45RA on the CD4\(^{+}\) and CD8\(^{+}\) populations for
  na\"ive / central-memory / effector-memory / effector subsetting.
\end{enumerate}
