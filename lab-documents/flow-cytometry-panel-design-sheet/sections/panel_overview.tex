\subsection{Instrument Configuration}
The Nimbus CytoSense 5L analyser is configured with five spatially separated
lasers --- ultraviolet (355\,nm), violet (405\,nm), blue (488\,nm),
yellow-green (561\,nm), and red (640\,nm) --- feeding eighteen photomultiplier
detectors. Panel design below assigns each fluorophore to a single primary
detector and lists secondary laser excitation only where it materially
affects compensation.
