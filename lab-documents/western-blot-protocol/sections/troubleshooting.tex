\section{Troubleshooting Appendix}
\label{sec:troubleshooting}

The table below covers the most common problems encountered during western blot
workflows at Nexa Biologics, based on accumulated facility data (2019--2025).

\par\vspace{6pt}

{\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.30}
\begin{longtable}{@{}P{3.6cm}P{4.2cm}P{5.4cm}@{}}
  \toprule
  \rowcolor{NexaBlue}
  \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Problem}} &
  \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Probable Cause(s)}} &
  \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Corrective Action}} \\
  \midrule
  \endfirsthead
  \toprule
  \rowcolor{NexaBlue}
  \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Problem}} &
  \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Probable Cause(s)}} &
  \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Corrective Action}} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  \midrule
  \multicolumn{3}{r}{\footnotesize\itshape Continued on next page\ldots} \\
  \endfoot
  \bottomrule
  \endlastfoot
  %
  \textbf{No signal / very faint band} &
  Protein not expressed; degraded sample; antibody issue; poor transfer &
  Verify expression by RT-qPCR. Check sample freshness and protease inhibitor use.
  Increase protein load. Increase primary antibody concentration. Confirm transfer
  efficiency with Ponceau\,S. \\[3pt]
  %
  \rowcolor{RowShade}
  \textbf{High background (entire membrane)} &
  Over-blocking; high antibody concentration; insufficient washing; ECL
  over-exposure &
  Reduce primary/secondary antibody dilution. Extend wash steps to $5 \times
  \mintime{10}$. Reduce ECL incubation time or switch to low-sensitivity substrate. \\[3pt]
  %
  \textbf{High background (speckled)} &
  Dry spots during blocking; particulates in antibody solution &
  Ensure membrane stays wet throughout. Filter antibody solutions through
  0.22\,\ensuremath{\mu} m membrane before use. Increase rocking speed during incubation. \\[3pt]
  %
  \rowcolor{RowShade}
  \textbf{Multiple / non-specific bands} &
  Antibody cross-reactivity; protein degradation; antibody concentration too high &
  Reduce primary antibody concentration. Add $0.1\%$ Tween-20 to primary
  antibody diluent. Check sample for proteolysis. Use validated positive control
  lysate. \\[3pt]
  %
  \textbf{Band at wrong molecular weight} &
  Post-translational modifications; isoforms; running artefact &
  Consult antibody datasheet for all reported isoforms. Verify with positive
  control. Check ladder alignment. Consider dephosphorylation treatment if
  MW shift is \textasciitilde\,2--10\,kDa. \\[3pt]
  %
  \rowcolor{RowShade}
  \textbf{Smearing / streaking of bands} &
  Overloaded lane; salt in sample; partially denatured protein &
  Reduce protein load. Dialyse or dilute sample to remove excess salt. Increase
  denaturation time at \degC{95}. \\[3pt]
  %
  \textbf{Wavy or distorted bands} &
  Uneven gel polymerisation; air bubbles; uneven current distribution &
  Use pre-cast gels. Ensure level gel tank. Check electrode contact. Run at
  lower voltage for more uniform migration. \\[3pt]
  %
  \rowcolor{RowShade}
  \textbf{White (reversed) bands} &
  Hook effect from antigen excess overwhelming antibody &
  Reduce protein load. Serially dilute samples to confirm dose-dependence. \\[3pt]
  %
  \textbf{Inconsistent signal between lanes} &
  Unequal loading; pipetting error; edge effects &
  Re-quantify all samples. Verify loading with colloidal Coomassie staining
  or total protein normalisation stain. Avoid outermost lanes for critical
  comparisons. \\[3pt]
  %
  \rowcolor{RowShade}
  \textbf{Membrane tears or holes} &
  Excessive stripping; over-drying PVDF; mechanical damage &
  Limit harsh stripping cycles to two per membrane. Never allow PVDF to dry
  completely. Handle membrane only by the edges using blunt-nosed forceps. \\[3pt]
  %
  \textbf{ECL substrate crystallisation} &
  Reagents mixed too far in advance; temperature too low &
  Mix substrates immediately before use. Warm to room temperature before mixing.
  Use within 15 minutes of preparation. \\
\end{longtable}
}

\par\vspace{4pt}
\begin{notebox}{Escalation Procedure}
If a problem cannot be resolved after two attempts using corrective actions above,
contact the Core Facility Director (Dr.~Marcus Hale) and log a trouble ticket in
the NexaLIMS portal (lims.nexabio.internal). Attach blot images and the completed
Experiment Record Form (ERF-NB-WB-004).
\end{notebox}
