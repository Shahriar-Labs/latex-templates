\section{Blocking \& Antibody Incubation}
\label{sec:antibodies}

\subsection{Blocking}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Incubate membrane in \textbf{blocking buffer} at room temperature for
        \hrtime{1} with gentle rocking.
  \item \textbf{Blocking buffer choice:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item 5\% non-fat dry milk in TBS-T — for non-phospho targets
    \item 5\% BSA in TBS-T — for phospho-protein detection (milk contains casein,
          which is phosphorylated and causes background)
    \item NexaBlock\texttrademark~(Cat.\ NB-BLK-002) — universal, for total and
          phospho targets on the same membrane
  \end{itemize}
  \item Discard blocking buffer. Do \textit{not} wash between blocking and
        primary antibody incubation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Primary Antibody Incubation}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Dilute primary antibody in the appropriate blocking buffer (see
        dilution table below).
  \item Incubate membrane with primary antibody solution at \degC{4} overnight
        (16--18\,h) with gentle rocking, or at room temperature for \hrtime{2}
        if validated.
  \item Wash with TBS-T: $3 \times \mintime{10}$ at room temperature.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Secondary Antibody Incubation}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Dilute HRP-conjugated secondary antibody in blocking buffer (typically
        1:5\,000--1:10\,000, see table).
  \item Incubate for \hrtime{1} at room temperature with gentle rocking.
        Protect from light if using fluorescent secondary.
  \item Wash with TBS-T: $3 \times \mintime{10}$.
  \item Final wash with TBS (without Tween-20) for \mintime{5} to remove
        residual detergent before ECL.
\end{enumerate}

\par\vspace{6pt}
\begin{tipbox}{Antibody Stripping \& Re-probing}
To re-probe for another target on the same membrane, use NexaStrip\texttrademark\
Harsh Stripping Buffer (Cat.\ NB-STR-004): incubate at \degC{50} for \mintime{20}
with rocking. Confirm complete stripping with ECL before re-blocking and re-probing.
Limit stripping cycles to two to preserve membrane integrity.
\end{tipbox}
