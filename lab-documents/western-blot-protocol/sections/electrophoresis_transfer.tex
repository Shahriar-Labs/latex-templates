\par\vspace{6pt}
Pre-cast NexaGel\texttrademark~Pro gels are recommended for consistency. If
pouring gels, de-gas the acrylamide solution under vacuum for \mintime{10} before
adding APS and TEMED.

\subsection{Electrophoresis}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Assemble gel in the electrophoresis tank. Fill inner chamber with $1\times$
        running buffer (25\,mM Tris, 192\,mM glycine, 0.1\% SDS, pH~8.3).
  \item Load \vol{5} BrightMark\texttrademark~ladder into lane~1. Load prepared
        samples into remaining lanes. Include a positive control and negative control
        in every run.
  \item Run at \textbf{80\,V} through the stacking gel (\textasciitilde\mintime{20}),
        then increase to \textbf{120\,V} through the resolving gel
        (\textasciitilde\mintime{60--90}).
  \item Stop electrophoresis when the dye front reaches 0.5\,cm from the gel base.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Protein Transfer}

\subsection{Membrane Preparation}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{PVDF membrane:} Activate in 100\% methanol for \mintime{30~seconds},
        then equilibrate in transfer buffer for \mintime{5}. \textit{PVDF is
        preferred for low-abundance proteins due to lower background.}
  \item \textbf{Nitrocellulose membrane:} Equilibrate directly in transfer buffer
        for \mintime{10}. Suitable for most standard targets.
  \item Cut membrane to gel dimensions. Mark orientation with pencil.
\end{enumerate}

\par\vspace{8pt}

\begin{enumerate}[resume*]
  \setcounter{enumi}{3}
  \item After transfer, stain membrane with \textbf{Ponceau\,S} (0.1\% in 5\%
        acetic acid) for \mintime{5} to confirm transfer efficiency. Photograph
        before washing.
  \item Wash with TBS-T (\mintime{5}, $3\times$) to remove Ponceau\,S completely.
\end{enumerate}

\par
\begin{warnbox}{Transfer Efficiency Check}
Poor Ponceau\,S staining indicates failed transfer. Common causes: incorrect
buffer composition, air bubbles in the sandwich, or reversed membrane/gel orientation.
Re-transfer from the gel if it still stains with Coomassie.
\end{warnbox}
