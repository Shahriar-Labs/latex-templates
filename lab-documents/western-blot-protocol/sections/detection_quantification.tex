\section{Detection \& Signal Quantification}

\subsection{ECL Chemiluminescent Detection}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Prepare NexaLuminol\texttrademark~ECL substrate by mixing equal volumes of
        Reagent\,A and Reagent\,B immediately before use. Use \vol{1000} per
        $8 \times 10$\,cm membrane.
  \item Drain excess wash buffer from the membrane. Place protein-side up on a
        clean sheet of plastic wrap.
  \item Apply ECL substrate evenly across the membrane. Incubate for exactly
        \mintime{1} at room temperature. Do not agitate.
  \item Drain excess substrate. Wrap membrane in plastic wrap; avoid bubbles.
  \item Transfer membrane to the VertexImager\texttrademark~5000 imaging cassette.
  \item Acquire images with exposure times ranging from \textbf{10 seconds} to
        \textbf{10 minutes} to determine optimal exposure. Use automatic exposure
        mode as a starting point.
  \item Save raw images in 16-bit TIFF format for quantification. Never adjust
        contrast on raw images prior to densitometric analysis.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Image Quantification}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Open raw 16-bit TIFF in NexaQuant\texttrademark~or ImageJ/Fiji.
  \item Draw rectangular selection boxes of equal area over each band of interest
        and corresponding loading control bands.
  \item Measure integrated density (or mean grey value $\times$ area). Subtract
        local background from each measurement.
  \item Normalise target band densitometry to the loading control (e.g., GAPDH
        or \ensuremath{\beta}-Actin) for each lane.
  \item Express results as fold-change relative to the untreated / control condition.
  \item Statistical analysis: perform at minimum three independent biological
        replicates. Report mean~$\pm$\,SEM. Use two-tailed Student's $t$-test or
        one-way ANOVA with post hoc correction (Tukey or Dunnett) as appropriate.
\end{enumerate}

\par
\begin{notebox}{Linear Dynamic Range}
Ensure band signals fall within the instrument's linear range. Saturated bands
(clipped white pixels in 16-bit mode) must be re-imaged at lower exposure.
Quantification of saturated bands is \textbf{not acceptable} for publication.
\end{notebox}

\subsection*{Data Recording Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Record all experimental conditions, antibody lots, and exposure times
        in the Nexa ELN (Electronic Lab Notebook) system.
  \item Retain all raw image files and original uncropped blot images in
        the \texttt{/projects/NB-WB/raw\_data/} shared drive.
  \item Minimum retention period: 10 years per Nexa Data Integrity Policy
        (DIP-NB-003).
  \item Cropped bands in figures must be clearly delineated with a visible
        border; lane splicing must be disclosed in figure legends.
\end{itemize}
