\section{Protocol Overview \& Scope}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.62\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\subsection*{Purpose}
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the complete workflow for performing
western blot (immunoblot) analysis at Nexa Biologics Research Division. It covers
sample preparation through chemiluminescent detection of target proteins, with special
emphasis on reproducibility, signal quantification, and troubleshooting.

\medskip
\subsection*{Scope}
Applicable to all research scientists and laboratory technicians in the Protein
Analysis Core Facility. Covers SDS-PAGE under denaturing conditions (reducing and
non-reducing), wet and semi-dry transfer, and HRP-based ECL detection.

\subsection*{Required Equipment \& Reagents}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\textbf{\color{NexaBlue}Equipment}
\begin{itemize}
  \item SDS-PAGE electrophoresis system (NexaGel\texttrademark~Pro)
  \item Trans-Blot wet transfer cell or semi-dry transfer unit
  \item PVDF or nitrocellulose membrane (0.2\,\ensuremath{\mu} m or 0.45\,\ensuremath{\mu} m pore)
  \item Chemiluminescence imaging system (VertexImager\texttrademark~5000)
  \item Rocking/orbital shaker (variable speed)
  \item Microcentrifuge (16\,000\,$\times$\,\textit{g} capable)
  \item Heating block or water bath (\degC{95--100})
  \item Protein quantification spectrophotometer
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}%
\hfill%
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\textbf{\color{NexaBlue}Key Reagents}
\begin{itemize}
  \item NexaLysis\texttrademark~RIPA Buffer (Cat.\ NB-LYS-001)
  \item Protease inhibitor cocktail (Cat.\ NB-INH-003)
  \item Laemmli sample buffer (4\,$\times$, reducing)
  \item BrightMark\texttrademark~Pre-stained Protein Ladder (10--250\,kDa)
  \item Primary antibodies (see Section~\ref{sec:antibodies})
  \item HRP-conjugated secondary antibodies
  \item NexaLuminol\texttrademark~ECL substrate (Cat.\ NB-ECL-010)
  \item TBS-T (Tris-buffered saline + 0.1\% Tween-20)
  \item Blocking reagent: 5\% non-fat dry milk or 5\% BSA in TBS-T
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}

\par\vspace{6pt}
\begin{warnbox}{Hazard Notice}
SDS is a hazardous irritant. Acrylamide monomer (in gel preparation) is a
suspected neurotoxin and carcinogen. Always wear appropriate PPE (nitrile gloves,
lab coat, safety glasses). Dispose of acrylamide waste per Nexa Biologics
Waste Disposal SOP-NB-ENV-001.
\end{warnbox}
