\section{Preparation Procedure}
\label{sec:prep}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=4pt, label=\textbf{\color{labteal}\arabic*.}]
  \item Verify that the analytical balance (Asset ID BAL-014) and the benchtop pH meter
        (Asset ID PH-006) are within their current calibration window. If either is due
        within 7 days, record the concern on the Instrument Log and delay preparation
        until recalibration is confirmed.
  \item Tare a clean, dry 250\,mL beaker on the analytical balance. Weigh
        \textbf{186.12\,g} of EDTA disodium dihydrate (Lot LN26-3391) directly into the
        beaker.
  \item Measure 800\,mL of DI water into a 1000\,mL graduated cylinder and transfer it to
        a clean 1\,L beaker fitted with a magnetic stir bar, on a stir plate at moderate
        speed.
  \item Add the weighed EDTA to the stirring water gradually, in small portions. The
        solution will remain cloudy at this stage --- this is expected and resolves once
        the pH is raised in the next step.
  \item While stirring continuously, add NaOH pellets (Lot LN25-9042) in small increments,
        checking pH after each addition. Continue until the solution clarifies and the pH
        stabilizes at \textbf{8.00 $\pm$ 0.05} (approximately 20\,g NaOH for this batch).
  \item \textbf{Checkpoint --- pH verification.} Confirm the final pH with the calibrated
        pH meter before proceeding. Record the reading, initials, and time below.
  \item Transfer the solution to a 1000\,mL volumetric flask. Rinse the preparation beaker
        twice with small volumes of DI water and add both rinses to the flask.
  \item Bring the flask to a final volume of \textbf{1000\,mL} with DI water and invert at
        least 10 times to mix thoroughly.
  \item Filter the stock through a 0.22\,$\mu$m PES bottle-top filter directly into a
        sterile, amber glass storage bottle.
  \item Immediately prepare the three working dilutions per the schedule in
        Section~\ref{sec:dilution}, drawing from the freshly filtered stock.
  \item Label the stock bottle and each working-dilution bottle per SOP-CHM-007 labeling
        requirements: reagent name, concentration, lot number, preparation date, expiry
        date, and preparer's initials.
  \item Store the stock bottle at room temperature, protected from light; store all
        working dilutions at 4\,$^\circ$C.
\end{enumerate}
