\section{Purpose and Scope}
\label{sec:doccontrol}

This worksheet documents the preparation of a \SI{0.5}{\Molar} ethylenediaminetetraacetic
acid (EDTA), disodium salt, stock solution at pH 8.0, together with the working dilution
series required for downstream nucleic acid extraction and metal-chelation assays performed
by the Cell Biology Unit. It is issued under SOP-CHM-007 (\emph{Preparation and
Standardization of Volumetric Reagents}) and constitutes the batch record for lot
\texttt{EDTA-0526-014}. Completion of every field on this worksheet, including the
prepared-by and checked-by signatures in Section~\ref{sec:signoff}, is required before the
solutions may be released for use outside the Analytical Chemistry Core.

This worksheet is distributed to the requesting unit, retained in the Core Facility batch
record binder, and archived electronically in the LIMS reagent module for the retention
period defined in the Laboratory Quality Manual (7 years from expiry).
