\section{Quality Verification}
\label{sec:qc}

Before release, the completed stock and its working dilutions must meet the acceptance
criteria below. pH measurement is performed and recorded consistent with USP General
Chapter \textless791\textgreater~\cite{usp791}.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{2.6cm}Xp{3.4cm}p{1.6cm}p{1.4cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Acceptance Criterion} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Result} & \textbf{P/F} \\
\midrule
pH & 8.00 $\pm$ 0.05 & Calibrated benchtop pH meter (PH-006) & 8.02 & Pass \\
\rowcolor{labteal!5}
Appearance & Clear, colorless, free of visible particulates & Visual inspection against black/white background & Clear, colorless & Pass \\
Filter Integrity & Bubble point $\geq$ 50\,psi (0.22\,$\mu$m PES) & Post-use bubble point test & 54\,psi & Pass \\
\rowcolor{labteal!5}
Fill Volume (Stock) & 1000\,mL $\pm$ 5\,mL & Graduated cylinder verification & 998\,mL & Pass \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\begin{hazardbox}{\faExclamationTriangle\ On Any Failing Result}
If any parameter above fails its acceptance criterion, do not release the batch. Label the
stock and all working dilutions \textbf{``DO NOT USE --- QUARANTINED,''} initiate a
Deviation Report per SOP-QA-002, and notify the Core Facility supervisor before any further
action is taken.
\end{hazardbox}

The 6-month shelf life assigned to this lot (Section~\ref{sec:signoff}) reflects the stability
data summarized in the Core Facility's internal technical bulletin on chelating-agent stock
solutions~\cite{meridian_tb}.
