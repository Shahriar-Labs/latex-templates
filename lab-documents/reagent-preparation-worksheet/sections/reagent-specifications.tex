\begin{hazardbox}{\faExclamationTriangle\ Handling Precautions}
Sodium hydroxide pellets are corrosive (GHS05) and dissolve exothermically. Wear nitrile
gloves, splash goggles, and a buttoned lab coat. Add NaOH to the stirring solution in small
increments --- never add water to solid NaOH, and never add pellets faster than the solution
can absorb the heat of dissolution. Work at a ventilated bench away from open flames. Refer
to the Safety Data Sheet binder, Section 4 (Cabinet C-12), for full first-aid and spill
guidance.
\end{hazardbox}
