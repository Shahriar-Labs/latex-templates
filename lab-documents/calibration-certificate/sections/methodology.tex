\CertSection{Calibration Method}

Calibration was performed in accordance with internal procedure \textbf{VX-CP-014, Rev.\ D
-- Calibration Procedure for Digital Multimeters}, which is based on the manufacturer's
published specifications for the Apex DM-8500 and the general safety requirements of
IEC~61010-1 \cite{iec61010}. The comparison method was used throughout: known reference
voltages, resistances, and currents were applied to the instrument under test (UUT) by a
multi-product calibrator, and the UUT indication was compared against the applied value as
verified by an independently traceable reference multimeter.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Functions calibrated: DC voltage, AC voltage (50/60~Hz), DC current, resistance,
        and continuity.
  \item Test points were selected at nominal 10\%, 50\%, 80\%, and 100\% of each active
        range, per procedure VX-CP-014 \S4.2.
  \item As-found readings were recorded prior to any adjustment. No adjustment was
        required; as-found results are reported as as-left results throughout this
        certificate.
  \item Each reported reading is the mean of five repeated observations taken after the
        UUT indication had stabilized.
  \item Measurement uncertainty was evaluated in accordance with the JCGM 100:2008
        \emph{Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement} (GUM) \cite{gum2008},
        using a coverage factor $k=2$, corresponding to an approximate confidence level
        of 95\%.
\end{itemize}
