\CertSection{Uncertainty Budget (Representative)}

The following budget illustrates the evaluation of measurement uncertainty for the
20~V DC voltage range at the 18.0000~V test point, evaluated in accordance with
JCGM~100:2008 \cite{gum2008}. Uncertainty contributions for all other test points in
this certificate were evaluated using the same method and are available in the
laboratory's internal calibration record on request.

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccccc@{}}
  \toprule
  Source of Uncertainty & Type & Distribution & Value & $u_i$ & $\nu_i$ \\
  \midrule
  Reference standard (cal.\ cert.) & B & Normal & 0.70 mV & 0.35 mV & $\infty$ \\
  UUT resolution & B & Rectangular & 0.52 mV & 0.30 mV & $\infty$ \\
  Repeatability ($n=5$) & A & Normal & 0.45 mV & 0.20 mV & 4 \\
  Reference standard drift & B & Rectangular & 0.35 mV & 0.20 mV & $\infty$ \\
  Temperature influence & B & Rectangular & 0.17 mV & 0.10 mV & $\infty$ \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
  \small
  Combined standard uncertainty:
  \[ u_c = \sqrt{\textstyle\sum u_i^2} \approx 0.56~\text{mV} \]
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
  \small
  Expanded uncertainty ($k=2$, $\approx$95\% confidence):
  \[ U = k \cdot u_c \approx 1.1~\text{mV} \]
\end{minipage}
\par

\vspace{4pt}
{\small\color{certgray} Effective degrees of freedom were computed using the
Welch--Satterthwaite formula. The reported coverage factor $k=2$ is a conservative
approximation to the Student's-$t$ value at the effective degrees of freedom
determined, consistent with laboratory policy VX-QP-009.}
