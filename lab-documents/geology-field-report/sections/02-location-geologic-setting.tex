\section{Location \& Geologic Setting}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Red Rock Canyon lies at the southern margin of the El Paso Mountains, where
Neogene basin-fill sediments of the Mojave Desert are exposed in colourful badland
escarpments up to 120\,m high.
The study area occupies a half-graben bounded to the east by the
\textit{Ricardo Fault Zone}, a series of NNE-trending normal faults with
cumulative Miocene extension of 8--12\,km [\ref{Loomis1984}].
The regional stratigraphic framework is dominated by the
\textit{Ricardo Group} (Miocene) and overlying \textit{Dove Spring Formation}
(late Miocene--Pliocene), which together constitute the basin-fill sequence examined in
this study [\ref{Whistler1989}].
Average dip of preserved strata is 6--14\textdegree\ eastward, reflecting progressive
half-graben rotation.
