\section{Field Methods}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Measured section:} A 285\,m composite section was measured
        along three sub-parallel traverses using a Jacob staff (2\,m) and
        Brunton compass; beds thinner than 5\,cm were lumped.
  \item \textbf{Strike \& dip:} Planar attitudes were recorded at $>$120 stations
        with a Brunton compass (right-hand rule); data georeferenced with a
        Garmin GPSMAP 66i ($\pm$3\,m horizontal accuracy).
  \item \textbf{Sampling:} Fresh rock chips ($\sim$300\,g) were collected with
        a geologic hammer, labelled, and double-bagged in Ziploc pouches.
        Sample locations were photographed and GPS-logged.
  \item \textbf{Photography:} A Canon EOS R7 with 24--105\,mm lens was used;
        a 30\,cm scale bar was placed in all outcrop photographs.
  \item \textbf{Safety:} Heat-mitigation protocol followed; all personnel
        carried 4\,L water minimum; no personnel worked alone.
\end{itemize}
