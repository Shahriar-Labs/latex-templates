\section{Cloud Resource Scheduling}
Classical cluster schedulers such as proportional-share and
constraint-based bin-packing allocate resources using fixed policies that
do not adapt to observed outcomes. Table~\ref{tab:related} summarizes
representative approaches along the dimensions most relevant to this
thesis: adaptivity to workload shifts and runtime overhead.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Comparison of representative scheduling approaches.}
\label{tab:related}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X c c@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Approach} & \textbf{Technique} & \textbf{Adaptivity} & \textbf{Overhead} \\
\midrule
Round-Robin        & Fixed rotation across nodes            & Low    & Very Low \\
\rowcolor{softGreen}
Static Threshold    & Rule-based capacity cutoffs             & Low    & Low \\
Heuristic Bin-Pack  & Greedy best-fit placement               & Medium & Medium \\
\rowcolor{softGreen}
Control-Theoretic   & Feedback controller on utilization       & Medium & Medium \\
Proposed RL Agent   & Learned policy over cluster telemetry    & High   & Medium \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
