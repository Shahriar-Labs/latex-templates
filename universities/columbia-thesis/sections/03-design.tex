\chapter{Design}
\label{ch:design}

The system keeps its task table in a replicated log managed by a consensus group of five
machines. Data partitions are not replicated; when a machine fails, the scheduler recomputes
its lost partitions from their lineage, placing the recomputation on machines that have not
failed recently.
