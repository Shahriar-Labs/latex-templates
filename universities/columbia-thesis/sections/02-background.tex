\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

Consensus protocols let a group of machines agree on a sequence of operations despite crashes
\citep{lamport1998paxos, ongaro2014}. Ordering events across machines without a shared clock
relies on logical time \citep{lamport1978}. If each of $n$ machines fails independently with rate
$\lambda$, a job of duration $T$ that needs all of them sees an expected
\begin{equation}
  \mathbb{E}[F] = n \lambda T
  \label{eq:failures}
\end{equation}
failures, which grows linearly with both scale and duration.
