\chapter{Evaluation}
\label{ch:evaluation}

We ran an iterative graph computation on 512 nodes and injected machine failures at increasing
rates. Table~\ref{tab:throughput} reports throughput as a fraction of the failure-free run.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Throughput relative to a failure-free run.}
  \label{tab:throughput}
  \begin{tabular}{lccc}
    \toprule
    Failures per hour & Checkpointing & Full replication & This design \\
    \midrule
    1  & 0.93 & 0.81 & 0.99 \\
    8  & 0.84 & 0.80 & 0.97 \\
    32 & 0.71 & 0.78 & 0.94 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Checkpointing degrades fastest because each failure rolls work back to the last checkpoint;
full replication pays its cost even when nothing fails.
