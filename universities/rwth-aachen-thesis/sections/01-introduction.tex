\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

An electric drivetrain is efficient on average but not uniformly: a machine running at low
torque and high speed may waste several times the energy it wastes near its best operating
point. Vehicles with two drive units can exploit this by splitting the demanded torque between
them, and the machine controller can choose the current references that minimize losses for a
given torque. Doing both optimally, at the rate an inverter requires, is the subject of this
dissertation.
