\chapter{Loss-Minimizing Predictive Control}
\label{ch:method}

We approximate the combined inverter and machine losses by a convex quadratic in the currents
of both units, fitted to bench measurements. The controller solves a small quadratic program
at every control period, tracking the demanded total torque while minimizing predicted losses
under current and voltage limits.
