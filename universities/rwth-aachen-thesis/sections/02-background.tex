\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

In the rotor reference frame, the torque of a permanent-magnet synchronous machine with $p$ pole
pairs is
\begin{equation}
  T = \tfrac{3}{2}\, p \bigl(\psi_{\mathrm{PM}}\, i_q + (L_d - L_q)\, i_d i_q\bigr),
  \label{eq:torque}
\end{equation}
so the same torque can be produced by many current pairs $(i_d, i_q)$ with different copper and
iron losses. Model predictive control chooses inputs by optimizing a model's predictions over
a receding horizon \citep{rawlings2017}.
