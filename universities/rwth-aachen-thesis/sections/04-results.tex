\chapter{Test-Bench Results}
\label{ch:results}

Table~\ref{tab:losses} reports drivetrain losses over a standard urban driving cycle.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Drivetrain losses over an urban driving cycle.}
  \label{tab:losses}
  \begin{tabular}{lcc}
    \toprule
    Strategy & Losses (kJ) & Relative \\
    \midrule
    Fixed 50/50 split, MTPA currents & 412 & 1.000 \\
    Rule-based split, MTPA currents  & 395 & 0.959 \\
    Predictive control (this work)   & 380 & 0.922 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The predictive controller saves most where the demanded torque is low, when shutting down one
unit and running the other near its efficient region beats sharing the load.
