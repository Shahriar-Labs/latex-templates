\chapter{A Hierarchical Controller}
\label{ch:method}

A policy network observes proprioception and a local height map and outputs footholds and a
body trajectory at 50~Hz. A model-predictive controller at 500~Hz computes contact forces that
track the trajectory while satisfying Equation~\eqref{eq:centroidal} and the friction
constraints.
