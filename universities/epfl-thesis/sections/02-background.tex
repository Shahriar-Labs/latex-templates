\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

For a quadruped with stance feet at positions $r_i$ applying ground forces $f_i$, the centroidal
dynamics are
\begin{equation}
  m\,\ddot{c} = \sum_i f_i + m g, \qquad \dot{L} = \sum_i (r_i - c) \times f_i ,
  \label{eq:centroidal}
\end{equation}
with each force constrained to its friction cone. Model-predictive control optimizes these
forces over a short horizon \citep{dicarlo2018}; reinforcement learning trains policies by trial
and error in simulation \citep{schulman2017ppo}.
