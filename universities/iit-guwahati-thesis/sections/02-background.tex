\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

In the first-order radio model, transmitting $k$ bits over distance $d$ costs
\begin{equation}
  E_{\mathrm{Tx}}(k, d) = E_{\mathrm{elec}}\,k + \varepsilon_{\mathrm{amp}}\,k\,d^{2},
  \label{eq:radio}
\end{equation}
and receiving costs $E_{\mathrm{elec}} k$. LEACH organizes nodes into clusters whose heads
aggregate data and forward it to the base station, rotating the head role at random to spread
the load \citep{heinzelman2000}.
