\chapter{Residual-Energy Clustering with Aligned Duty Cycles}
\label{ch:method}

Our protocol elects cluster heads with a probability weighted by residual energy and by the
expected transmission count of their links, so that weak nodes and poor links rarely become
heads. Members wake only in slots announced by their head, which removes idle listening.
