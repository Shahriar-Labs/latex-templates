\section{Thesis Organization}

The remainder of this dissertation is structured as follows.
Chapter~\ref{ch:background} surveys related work in edge computing, online
algorithms, and fair scheduling. Chapter~\ref{ch:placement} presents the
Meridian Scheduler. Chapter~\ref{ch:energy} details the ecoPlace energy
management layer. Chapter~\ref{ch:fairness} develops the PDE framework and
approximation algorithm. Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation} reports experimental
results from simulation and real-world deployment. Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusion}
concludes with a summary and directions for future work.
