\section{Online Algorithms for Placement}

The problem of placing workloads on heterogeneous nodes has been studied
extensively in the online algorithms community. The classic ski-rental and
\(k\)-server problems capture important special cases, but edge workloads
introduce correlations across both time and space that break the worst-case
adversarial model. Our work builds on the \emph{online learning with
optimization} (OLO) framework of~\citet{hazan2016introduction}, combining
regret-minimizing predictors with robust integer programming.

\begin{definition}[Competitive Ratio]
\label{def:cr}
An online algorithm \(\mathcal{A}\) has competitive ratio \(\alpha\) if, for
every finite input sequence \(\sigma\),
\[
\operatorname{cost}_{\mathcal{A}}(\sigma)
\;\leq\;
\alpha \cdot \operatorname{cost}_{\textsc{opt}}(\sigma) + \beta,
\]
where \(\textsc{opt}\) is the offline optimal algorithm with full knowledge of
\(\sigma\) and \(\beta\) is a constant independent of \(\sigma\).
\end{definition}
