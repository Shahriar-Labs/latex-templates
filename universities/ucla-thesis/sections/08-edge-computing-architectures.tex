\section{Edge Computing Architectures}

The vision of edge computing was articulated by~\citet{satyanarayanan2009case}
as ``cloudlets'' --- small-scale data centers placed at the network edge.
Subsequent work by~\citet{ha2014towards} demonstrated the feasibility of
offloading mobile workloads to nearby nodes, while~\citet{mao2017survey}
provided a comprehensive survey of mobile edge computing (MEC) standards and
architectures.
