\section{Thesis Outline}

Chapter~2 surveys the literature on regime-switching models in finance, convex risk measures, and computational Bayesian methods. Chapter~3 develops the changepoint-regularized HMM estimator with full algorithmic details and convergence diagnostics. Chapter~4 formulates the regime-conditional CVaR portfolio problem and derives the ADMM algorithm. Chapter~5 presents the simulation study and empirical backtests. Chapter~6 discusses extensions to transaction costs, ESG constraints, and multi-period rebalancing. Chapter~7 concludes with limitations and directions for future work.
