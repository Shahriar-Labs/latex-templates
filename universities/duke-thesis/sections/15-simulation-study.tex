\section{Simulation Study}

We evaluate the sampler on synthetic data with known ground truth. Table~\ref{tab:recovery} reports recovery rates for $K$ across 500 replications under three data-generating processes.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Regime count recovery across simulation settings ($N=500$ replications).}
\label{tab:recovery}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l c c c c@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{True $K$} & \textbf{$T=500$} & \textbf{$T=1000$} & \textbf{$T=2000$} & \textbf{Avg.\ Runtime (s)} \\
\midrule
$K=2$ & 0.94 & 0.97 & 0.99 & 42.3 \\{}
$K=3$ & 0.88 & 0.93 & 0.97 & 67.8 \\{}
$K=4$ & 0.79 & 0.87 & 0.94 & 98.2 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Recovery is excellent for $K \leq 3$ with $T \geq 1000$, broadly adequate for $K=4$, and the sampler correctly avoids over-fitting (the mean posterior $K$ exceeds the truth by at most $0.18$ across settings).
