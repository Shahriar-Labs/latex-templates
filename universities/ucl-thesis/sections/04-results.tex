\chapter{Evaluation}
\label{ch:results}

We trained a tumour segmentation network on 40 real scans alone and with added synthetic scans.
Table~\ref{tab:dice} reports the Dice score on 120 held-out real scans.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Tumour segmentation Dice on held-out real scans.}
  \label{tab:dice}
  \begin{tabular}{lc}
    \toprule
    Training data & Dice \\
    \midrule
    40 real scans                                  & 0.71 \\
    + 400 unconditioned synthetic scans            & 0.72 \\
    + 400 anatomy-conditioned synthetic scans      & 0.78 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Unconditioned synthesis barely helps: its images are realistic but their tumours do not match
any label. Conditioned synthesis supplies correctly labelled variety.
