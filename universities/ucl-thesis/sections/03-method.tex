\chapter{Anatomy-Conditioned Diffusion}
\label{ch:method}

We condition the denoiser on a segmentation map $c$ injected at every resolution through
spatially adaptive normalization, so that each synthetic image follows the given anatomy.
Label maps can be taken from real scans or edited to create new configurations, for example to
move or resize a tumour.
