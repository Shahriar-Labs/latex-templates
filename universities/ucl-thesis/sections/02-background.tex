\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

A denoising diffusion model learns to reverse a fixed noising process that turns an image $x_0$
into Gaussian noise over $T$ steps. It is trained by predicting the added noise,
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}_{t, x_0, \epsilon}\bigl\|\epsilon - \epsilon_\theta(x_t, t, c)\bigr\|^2 ,
  \label{eq:ddpm}
\end{equation}
where $c$ is an optional condition \citep{ho2020}. The denoiser is typically a U-Net
\citep{ronneberger2015}, the same architecture widely used for medical image segmentation.
