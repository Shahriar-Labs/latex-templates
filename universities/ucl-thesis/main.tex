\chapter*{Impact Statement}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Impact Statement}
Hospitals rarely have enough labelled scans to train reliable software, and privacy rules make
pooling scans between hospitals difficult. The methods in this thesis create realistic
synthetic scans whose anatomy is controlled, so that small datasets can be enlarged without
sharing patient data. Within academia, the evaluation protocol gives other researchers a way to
check whether synthetic images are anatomically faithful, not merely convincing. Outside
academia, the approach could shorten the time needed to build segmentation tools for rare
conditions, where scans are scarcest. Any clinical use would first need prospective validation
and regulatory review.
