\chapter{Uncertainty-Aware Detection and Planning}
\label{ch:method}

We extend the detector to predict, for each box, a Gaussian over its corner coordinates. The
predicted standard deviation $\sigma$ inflates the obstacle when the planner checks for
collisions, and the planner caps the commanded speed at
\begin{equation}
  v_{\max} = \min\Bigl(v_{\mathrm{nom}},\; \sqrt{2 a_{\max} (d - k\sigma)}\Bigr),
  \label{eq:speed}
\end{equation}
where $d$ is the distance to the obstacle, $a_{\max}$ the braking limit and $k$ a safety
factor. Uncertain detections therefore slow the vehicle rather than being discarded.
