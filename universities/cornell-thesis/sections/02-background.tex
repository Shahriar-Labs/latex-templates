\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

Feature-based visual odometry tracks keypoints between frames and estimates camera motion by
minimizing reprojection error \citep{murartal2015}. Single-stage detectors predict object
boxes and classes in one pass over the image \citep{redmon2016}, usually on a residual
backbone \citep{he2016}. Neither reports a calibrated uncertainty by default, and both can be
fooled by perturbations that a human would ignore \citep{goodfellow2015}.
