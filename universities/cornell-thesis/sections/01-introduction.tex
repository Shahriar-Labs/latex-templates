\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

An aerial vehicle flying through clutter has perhaps a second to detect an obstacle and react.
Its perception pipeline, typically a camera feeding a visual odometry system and a learned
detector, was usually developed on datasets recorded from cars or handheld cameras
\citep{geiger2012}. Aerial footage differs: the camera rotates quickly, blur is common, and
light changes as the vehicle passes under canopy.

This dissertation asks what a planner should do when perception becomes unreliable, and
answers that the perception system should report how unreliable it is.
