\chapter{Flight Experiments}
\label{ch:flights}

We flew 214 missions through a forested test area at dawn, midday and dusk.
Table~\ref{tab:flights} summarizes the outcomes.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Outcomes over 214 forest flights.}
  \label{tab:flights}
  \begin{tabular}{lccc}
    \toprule
    Configuration & Collisions & Mean speed (m/s) & Missions completed \\
    \midrule
    Standard detector, fixed speed      & 9 & 4.1 & 201 \\
    Uncertainty-aware, speed-limited    & 1 & 3.6 & 212 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Most collisions with the standard detector happened at dusk, when motion blur hid thin branches.
The uncertainty-aware system detected the same branches with large $\sigma$ and slowed down.
