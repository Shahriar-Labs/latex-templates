\section{Fuel Consumption}
Across the three evaluation sites, the MPC controller reduced mean diesel fuel consumption by
18.4\% relative to the incumbent rule-based controller and by 9.7\% relative to the fuzzy-logic
controller, averaged over the twelve-month evaluation window. The largest improvement was
observed at Site C, attributed to its comparatively larger PV array giving the forecasting
module more usable signal during partly-cloudy conditions.
