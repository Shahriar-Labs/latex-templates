\section{Model Predictive Dispatch Formulation}
At each control step, the MPC layer solves a finite-horizon optimisation that minimises diesel
fuel consumption and generator switching, subject to battery SoC and load-service constraints:

\begin{equation}
\min_{\{u_t\}} \; \sum_{k=0}^{H-1} \Big( c_f \, P^{\text{diesel}}_{t+k} +
c_s \, \left| \Delta u_{t+k} \right| \Big)
\label{eq:objective}
\end{equation}

\begin{align}
\text{s.t.} \quad
& \text{SoC}_{\min} \le \text{SoC}_{t+k} \le \text{SoC}_{\max}, & \forall k \label{eq:soc} \\
& P^{\text{PV}}_{t+k} + P^{\text{batt}}_{t+k} + P^{\text{diesel}}_{t+k} \ge P^{\text{load}}_{t+k},
  & \forall k \label{eq:balance} \\
& 0 \le P^{\text{diesel}}_{t+k} \le P^{\text{diesel}}_{\max}, & \forall k \label{eq:dieselcap}
\end{align}

\noindent
where $c_f$ and $c_s$ weight fuel cost and switching penalty respectively, and $\Delta
u_{t+k}$ is the change in diesel generator on/off state between consecutive steps. The
switching penalty term $c_s$ directly targets the generator-cycling problem identified in
Chapter~1.
