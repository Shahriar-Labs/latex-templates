\chapter*{Plagiarism Declaration}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Plagiarism Declaration}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item I know that plagiarism is wrong. Plagiarism is to use another's work and pretend that
        it is one's own.
  \item I have used a recognised referencing convention for citation and quotation. Each
        contribution to, and quotation from, the work of others has been attributed and cited
        in this dissertation.
  \item This dissertation is my own original work, prepared for the degree of Master of
        Science in Electrical Engineering at the University of Cape Town. Neither the whole
        work, nor any part of it, has been submitted for a degree at any other university.
  \item I have not allowed, and will not allow, anyone to copy this work with the intention
        of passing it off as their own work.
\end{enumerate}
