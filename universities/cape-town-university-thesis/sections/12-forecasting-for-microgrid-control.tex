\section{Forecasting for Microgrid Control}
Short-horizon (15--90 minute) irradiance forecasting is typically approached either through
sky-imaging techniques or through statistical time-series models applied to historical
irradiance and cloud-cover proxies. Sky-imaging offers higher accuracy but requires dedicated
camera hardware at each site, which is difficult to justify economically for small rural
installations. Statistical approaches trade some accuracy for deployability on the low-power
controllers already installed at existing sites, which motivates their use in this work.
