\section{Limitations}
The evaluation relies on historical telemetry replayed through simulation rather than a live
field trial, so real-world controller interaction effects --- such as sensor drift or
communication dropouts --- are not captured. The forecasting module was also tuned per site
using the full available history, which may overstate accuracy relative to a genuinely
unseen deployment site.
