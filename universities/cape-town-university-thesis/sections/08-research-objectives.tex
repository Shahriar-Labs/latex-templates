\section{Research Objectives}
This dissertation addresses the following objectives:

\begin{enumerate}[label=O\arabic*.,leftmargin=2.2cm]
  \item Characterise the dispatch inefficiencies of incumbent rule-based and fuzzy-logic
        controllers using historical telemetry from three operating microgrid sites.
  \item Design a short-horizon irradiance forecasting module suitable for a low-power embedded
        controller.
  \item Develop a model predictive dispatch controller that consumes the forecasting module's
        output to schedule PV, battery, and diesel generation.
  \item Evaluate the proposed controller against incumbent strategies on fuel consumption,
        generator cycling, and unmet-load frequency.
\end{enumerate}
