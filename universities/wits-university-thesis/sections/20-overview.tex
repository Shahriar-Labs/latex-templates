\section{Overview}
Naledi-Forecast comprises two stages: (i) a temporal convolutional encoder that maps the
observed, gap-marked load history to a point forecast, and (ii) a lightweight Bayesian
correction layer that recalibrates the encoder's residual distribution using a small
held-out calibration window.
