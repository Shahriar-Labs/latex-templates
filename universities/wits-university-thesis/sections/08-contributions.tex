\section{Contributions}
\begin{contribbox}{Summary of Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Naledi-Forecast architecture} --- a TCN encoder with a post-hoc Bayesian
        correction layer, described fully in Chapter~4.
  \item \textbf{A synthetic benchmark suite} of eighteen microgrid load profiles calibrated
        against publicly available South African household consumption surveys.
  \item \textbf{Field validation} on a twelve-week deployment at the Wits Energy Systems
        Laboratory test feeder, reported in Chapter~5.
\end{enumerate}
\end{contribbox}
