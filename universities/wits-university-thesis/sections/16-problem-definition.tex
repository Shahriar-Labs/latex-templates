\section{Problem Definition}
Given an irregularly sampled load history $\{(t_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^{n}$ over a feeder, the task
is to produce a calibrated forecast distribution for load $y_{t+h}$ at horizon
$h \in \{15\text{min}, 1\text{h}, 6\text{h}\}$, expressed as a predictive mean $\hat{\mu}$ and
variance $\hat{\sigma}^2$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:objective}
p(y_{t+h} \mid \mathbf{y}_{1:t}) \approx \mathcal{N}\!\left(\hat{\mu}(\mathbf{y}_{1:t}),\,
\hat{\sigma}^2(\mathbf{y}_{1:t})\right).
\end{equation}
