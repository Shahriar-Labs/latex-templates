\section{Data-Scarcity Model}
Availability $a \in (0,1]$ denotes the fraction of expected 15-minute intervals with valid
telemetry. Missingness is simulated using a two-state Markov process fitted to outage logs
from partner feeders, capturing the bursty nature of real communication failures rather than
independent random dropout.

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:markov}
P(\text{missing}_{t+1} \mid \text{missing}_t) = \pi_{mm}, \qquad
P(\text{present}_{t+1} \mid \text{present}_t) = \pi_{pp}.
\end{equation}
