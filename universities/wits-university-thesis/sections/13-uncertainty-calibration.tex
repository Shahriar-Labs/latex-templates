\section{Uncertainty Calibration}
Point forecasts alone are insufficient for dispatch decisions under uncertainty. Bayesian
and conformal calibration methods have shown promise for recovering trustworthy prediction
intervals from otherwise miscalibrated deep models \cite{gal2016dropout}.
