\section{Training Procedure}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Pre-train the TCN encoder on Sim-A and Sim-B under full data availability.
  \item Fine-tune under synthetic Markov-gap masking at availability levels
        $a \in \{1.0, 0.8, 0.6, 0.4, 0.2\}$.
  \item Fit the Bayesian correction layer on a rolling 10\% calibration split, refreshed
        weekly.
\end{enumerate}
