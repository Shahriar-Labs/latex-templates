\section{Field Deployment}
Over the twelve-week deployment on the Wits Energy Systems Laboratory test feeder, real
telemetry availability averaged 71\%, driven by intermittent cellular backhaul dropouts.
Naledi-Forecast achieved a field MAPE of 9.6\%, closely tracking simulation results at
comparable availability, and sustained median inference latency of 11.8\,ms per feeder-step
on a Raspberry Pi 4-class controller.

\begin{notebox}
Field results should be interpreted with appropriate caution given the single-feeder sample;
Chapter~6 discusses this limitation and proposed multi-site follow-up work.
\end{notebox}
