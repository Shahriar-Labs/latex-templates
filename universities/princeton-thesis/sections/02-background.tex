\chapter{Topological Band Theory}
\label{ch:background}

For a two-dimensional insulator with time-reversal symmetry, the relevant invariant is a
$\mathbb{Z}_2$ index $\nu$. In the presence of inversion symmetry it can be computed from the
parities $\xi_m(\Gamma_i)$ of the occupied bands at the four time-reversal invariant momenta,
\begin{equation}
  (-1)^{\nu} = \prod_{i=1}^{4} \prod_{m=1}^{N} \xi_{2m}(\Gamma_i).
  \label{eq:z2}
\end{equation}
A non-trivial index, $\nu = 1$, guarantees a pair of helical edge states that cross the bulk
gap \citep{hasan2010}. Each contributes one conductance quantum, so a clean sample with
two edges has a two-terminal conductance of $2e^2/h$.
