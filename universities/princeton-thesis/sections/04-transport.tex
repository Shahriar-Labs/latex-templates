\chapter{Edge Transport in Strained Devices}
\label{ch:transport}

Four devices were strained on a flexible substrate and measured at 1.6~K.
Table~\ref{tab:devices} lists the strain at which each device first showed a conductance
plateau within 10\% of $2e^2/h$.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Onset of the edge-conductance plateau in four devices.}
  \label{tab:devices}
  \begin{tabular}{lccc}
    \toprule
    Device & Channel length ($\mu$m) & Onset strain (\%) & Plateau ($e^2/h$) \\
    \midrule
    D1 & 0.8 & 1.7 & 1.94 \\
    D2 & 1.2 & 1.8 & 1.91 \\
    D3 & 2.0 & 1.9 & 1.83 \\
    D4 & 4.5 & 2.1 & 1.62 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The plateau degrades with channel length, as expected when backscattering between the two
edges becomes possible through puddles in the bulk. Extrapolating to short channels gives a
transition strain of $1.8 \pm 0.1\%$, in agreement with the model of Chapter~\ref{ch:model}.
