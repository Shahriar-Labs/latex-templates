\chapter{A Strained Monolayer Model}
\label{ch:model}

We describe the monolayer by a four-band tight-binding Hamiltonian with nearest- and
next-nearest-neighbour hopping and intrinsic spin-orbit coupling $\lambda$. Biaxial strain
$\epsilon$ enters through the hopping amplitudes, which scale as
\begin{equation}
  t_{ij}(\epsilon) = t_{ij}^{(0)} \, \exp\!\bigl[-\beta\,\epsilon\bigr],
  \label{eq:hopping}
\end{equation}
with a Gr\"uneisen-like parameter $\beta$ fitted to first-principles bands. Tensile strain
narrows the gap at $\Gamma$ until the bands invert, at which point the parities in
Equation~\eqref{eq:z2} change and the index becomes non-trivial.
