\chapter{Parity Eigenvalues of the Four-Band Model}
\label{app:a}

At each time-reversal invariant momentum the Hamiltonian commutes with the inversion operator,
so the occupied Bloch states can be chosen with definite parity. The parities at $\Gamma$
change sign when the two central bands exchange order, while those at the three $M$ points
remain fixed across the strain range studied, so the product in Equation~\eqref{eq:z2} flips
exactly once.
