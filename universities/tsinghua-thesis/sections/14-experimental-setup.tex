\section{Experimental Setup}

\subsection{Datasets and Tasks}

We evaluate RANO on three benchmark tasks spanning distinct domains:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Image classification.} ImageNet-1k~\cite{goodfellow2016}
  with 1.28 million training images across 1,000 categories. We report
  top-1 and top-5 accuracy.

  \item \textbf{Object detection.} COCO 2017 with 118k training images
  and 5k validation images. We report mean average precision at IoU 0.5
  (mAP@0.5).

  \item \textbf{Natural language inference.} The GLUE benchmark
  suite~\cite{devlin2019bert}, focusing on the MNLI, QQP, and SST-2
  tasks. We report accuracy and F1 score as appropriate.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hardware Platforms}

We deploy and benchmark models on four representative edge platforms,
as summarised in Table~\ref{tab:devices} (Chapter~1). All latency
measurements are averaged over 1,000 inference runs after a 100-run
warm-up period to ensure stable thermal conditions.

\subsection{Baselines}

We compare RANO against the following methods:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Unstructured magnitude pruning}~\cite{goodfellow2016}
  followed by fine-tuning.
  \item \textbf{Structured channel pruning} with $\ell_1$ regularisation.
  \item \textbf{INT8 post-training quantisation} with per-channel
  symmetric scaling.
  \item \textbf{Quantisation-aware training (QAT)}~\cite{kingma2014adam}
  with 8-bit weights and activations.
  \item \textbf{Standard knowledge distillation} with temperature
  $\tau = 4$ and a fixed teacher model.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Implementation Details}

All models are implemented in PyTorch and trained on Quanta A100 GPUs.
We use the Adam optimiser~\cite{kingma2014adam} with an initial learning
rate of $10^{-3}$, a cosine annealing schedule, and a batch size of 256.
Training runs for 200 epochs on ImageNet and 100 epochs on COCO and
GLUE tasks. The RANO-specific hyperparameters are set to
$\lambda_p = 10^{-4}$, $\lambda_q = 5 \times 10^{-5}$, and
$\lambda_h = 10^{-3}$, determined via grid search on a held-out
validation subset.
