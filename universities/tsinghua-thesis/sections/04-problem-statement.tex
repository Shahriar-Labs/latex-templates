\section{Problem Statement}

This thesis addresses the following central research question:

\begin{quote}
\itshape How can we train and deploy deep neural networks on
resource-constrained edge devices while preserving predictive accuracy,
minimising latency, and reducing energy consumption?
\end{quote}

This question decomposes into several interrelated sub-problems:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)]
  \item \textbf{Model compression.} Given a large, accurate teacher
  model, how can we produce a compact student model that retains most of
  the teacher's predictive power while satisfying hard constraints on
  model size and inference latency?

  \item \textbf{Quantisation-aware training.} How can we train models
  that are robust to low-precision arithmetic (8-bit or even 4-bit
  integer operations) without suffering catastrophic accuracy
  degradation?

  \item \textbf{Hardware-aware architecture design.} How can we design
  neural architectures that are not merely accurate in the abstract, but
  also efficient when executed on specific target hardware platforms?

  \item \textbf{Joint optimisation.} How can we combine pruning,
  quantisation, and architecture search into a unified framework that
  optimises the Pareto frontier of accuracy versus resource consumption?
\end{enumerate}
