\chapter{Streaming Community Detection}
\label{ch:streaming}

Our streaming method processes edges in arrival order and keeps, for each vertex, only its
current community label and degree. When an edge $(u, v)$ arrives, it evaluates the change in
Equation~\eqref{eq:modularity} from moving $u$ into $v$'s community, using running totals of
community volume. A second pass refines the labels with the standard local-move step.
