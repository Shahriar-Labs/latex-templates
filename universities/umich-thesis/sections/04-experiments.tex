\chapter{Experiments}
\label{ch:experiments}

Table~\ref{tab:graphs} compares the streaming method with the in-memory Louvain implementation
on four public-scale graphs generated to match the degree distributions of real networks
\citep{leskovec2005}.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Modularity and peak memory, in-memory versus streaming.}
  \label{tab:graphs}
  \begin{tabular}{lrcccc}
    \toprule
    Graph & Edges & $Q$ in-memory & $Q$ streaming & Memory ratio \\
    \midrule
    Social-A   & 0.4B & 0.712 & 0.709 & 0.21 \\
    Citation-B & 1.1B & 0.648 & 0.645 & 0.18 \\
    Web-C      & 2.3B & 0.803 & 0.800 & 0.16 \\
    Web-D      & 3.1B & 0.791 & 0.788 & 0.16 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Across all four graphs the streaming method loses at most 0.4\% of modularity while using
between a fifth and a sixth of the memory.
