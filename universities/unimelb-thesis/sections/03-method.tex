\chapter{A Hierarchical Spatial Model}
\label{ch:method}

Stations share parameters through a hierarchical prior whose correlation decays with distance, so
that sparsely observed stations borrow strength from their neighbours. Inference uses a
variational approximation with a factorized posterior over stations.
