\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

We model a station's monthly anomaly $y_t$ with a latent state $x_t$ that evolves over time,
\begin{equation}
  y_t = F x_t + \varepsilon_t, \qquad x_t = G x_{t-1} + \eta_t ,
  \label{eq:ssm}
\end{equation}
a dynamic linear model \citep{west1997}. Gaussian processes provide flexible priors for smooth
trends \citep{rasmussen2006gp}. Probabilistic forecasts are compared with proper scoring
rules such as the continuous ranked probability score \citep{gneiting2007}.
