\chapter{Prevalence-Aware Private Aggregation}
\label{ch:method}

Weighting clients by size alone lets large hospitals with low outcome prevalence dominate the
model. We instead weight each client by its effective number of positive and negative cases,
and add calibrated Gaussian noise to the clipped updates before aggregation so that the whole
training run satisfies differential privacy.
