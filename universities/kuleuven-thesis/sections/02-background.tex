\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

In federated averaging, $K$ clients each train on local data and the server combines their
parameters,
\begin{equation}
  \theta^{t+1} = \sum_{k=1}^{K} w_k\, \theta_k^{t+1}, \qquad w_k = \frac{n_k}{\sum_j n_j},
  \label{eq:fedavg}
\end{equation}
where $n_k$ is the number of examples at client $k$ \citep{mcmahan2017}. Differential privacy
bounds how much any single patient can change the output \citep{dwork2014}.
