\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

Hospitals collect detailed records that could train models to predict complications,
readmissions and adverse drug events. A model trained in one hospital often fails in another,
because patients, practice and coding differ. Pooling data would help, but patient records are
among the most sensitive data there are. Federated learning offers a middle way
\citep{mcmahan2017}: the model travels, the data stay put.
