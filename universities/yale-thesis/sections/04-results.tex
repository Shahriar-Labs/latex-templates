\chapter{Simulations and an Expression Study}
\label{ch:results}

We simulated genotypes with realistic linkage structure for $n = 1{,}000$ individuals and
$p = 20{,}000$ variants, with $s = 40$ causal variants. Table~\ref{tab:fdr} reports the
false discovery rate and power at a nominal level of $0.10$, averaged over 200 replicates.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{False discovery rate and power at a nominal level of 0.10 (200 replicates).}
  \label{tab:fdr}
  \begin{tabular}{lcc}
    \toprule
    Method & FDR & Power \\
    \midrule
    Debiased lasso, per variant & 0.23 & 0.71 \\
    Knockoffs, per variant      & 0.19 & 0.64 \\
    Block-debiased (this work)  & 0.09 & 0.68 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The per-variant methods exceed the nominal level because correlated neighbours of a causal
variant are declared significant in its place. The block procedure holds the level while
losing little power. Applied to expression measurements for 312 genes, it reports 47
associated blocks, 41 of which contain a variant previously reported in the literature.
