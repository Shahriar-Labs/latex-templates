\chapter{Proof Sketch for Block False Discovery Rate Control}
\label{app:a}

The block statistics $W_b$ satisfy the flip-sign property of \citet{barber2015}: swapping a
null block with its knockoff changes the sign of $W_b$ and leaves the other statistics
unchanged in distribution. Conditional on $|W_1|, \dots, |W_B|$, the signs of the null
statistics are then independent fair coins, and the usual supermartingale argument bounds the
false discovery rate by the target level $q$.
