\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

Much of a cell's regulation depends on proteins finding their targets, which they do by a
combination of diffusion and brief binding. Ensemble measurements report averages; single-
molecule tracking watches individual molecules and so can separate populations that averages
hide \citep{manley2008}. Inside living cells, however, background fluorescence and fast motion
make trajectories short. This dissertation improves both the measurement and its analysis.
