\chapter{Light-Sheet Tracking and State Analysis}
\label{ch:method}

We image cells with a thin light sheet at 100 frames per second and link localizations into
trajectories. A two-state hidden Markov model assigns each step to a bound or a free state,
with state-specific diffusion coefficients and transition rates estimated by maximum
likelihood \citep{persson2013}.
