\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

For free diffusion in two dimensions, the mean squared displacement grows linearly with lag
time $\tau$,
\begin{equation}
  \langle r^2(\tau) \rangle = 4 D \tau + 4\sigma^2 ,
  \label{eq:msd}
\end{equation}
where $D$ is the diffusion coefficient and $\sigma$ the localization precision. Light-sheet
illumination excites only a thin plane of the sample, reducing out-of-focus background compared
with wide-field illumination \citep{huisken2004}.
