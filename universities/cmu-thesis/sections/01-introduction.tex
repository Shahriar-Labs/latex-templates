\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

A model that knows who wrote a novel and where that author was born should be able to say
where the author of the novel was born. Humans compose knowledge this way without effort;
neural sequence models often do not \citep{lake2018, keysers2020}. Large language models
narrow the gap \citep{brown2020}, and prompting them to reason step by step narrows it further
\citep{wei2022}, but the remaining errors are systematic.

This thesis measures those errors precisely and proposes a way to reduce them.
