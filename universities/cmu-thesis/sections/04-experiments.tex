\chapter{Experiments}

Table~\ref{tab:depth} reports exact-match accuracy by composition depth for a single model under
three prompting strategies.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Exact-match accuracy (\%) by composition depth.}
  \label{tab:depth}
  \begin{tabular}{lcccc}
    \toprule
    Strategy & Depth 1 & Depth 2 & Depth 3 & Depth 4 \\
    \midrule
    Direct answer          & 88 & 61 & 42 & 22 \\
    Step-by-step           & 90 & 74 & 51 & 31 \\
    Plan-then-answer       & 89 & 79 & 67 & 58 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The gain from planning grows with depth, consistent with Equation~\eqref{eq:chain}: isolating
each step keeps its error rate close to the atomic rate $1 - p$.
