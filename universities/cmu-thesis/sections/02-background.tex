\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

We model a compositional question as a tree whose leaves are atomic facts and whose internal
nodes are operations such as lookup, comparison and counting. Its depth $k$ is the length of the
longest path from the root to a leaf. For a model that answers each atomic step correctly with
probability $p$ and independently, the chance of answering a depth-$k$ chain is
\begin{equation}
  P_k = p^{\,k},
  \label{eq:chain}
\end{equation}
so accuracy should fall geometrically with depth. Measured accuracy falls faster, which is the
signature of a model that does not decompose the question at all.
