\chapter{A Constrained Graph Scheduler}
\label{ch:method}

We encode the cluster as a bipartite graph between queued jobs and machines, and a graph neural
network produces a score for every feasible job-machine pair, following the design of
\citet{mao2019}. A constraint layer masks any pair that would exceed a machine's capacity
before the softmax, so infeasible actions have probability zero rather than being penalized
after the fact.
