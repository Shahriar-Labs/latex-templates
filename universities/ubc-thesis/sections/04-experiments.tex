\chapter{Experiments}
\label{ch:experiments}

We replayed four weeks of public cluster traces through the simulator.
Table~\ref{tab:jct} reports average job completion time relative to shortest-job-first.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Average job completion time relative to shortest-job-first (lower is better).}
  \label{tab:jct}
  \begin{tabular}{lcc}
    \toprule
    Scheduler & Relative completion time & Capacity violations \\
    \midrule
    Shortest-job-first           & 1.00 & 0 \\
    Dominant resource fairness   & 1.07 & 0 \\
    Learned, unmasked            & 0.79 & 312 \\
    Learned, masked (this work)  & 0.81 & 0 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The unmasked policy is slightly faster only because it overcommits machines; the masked
policy keeps almost all of the gain with no violations.
