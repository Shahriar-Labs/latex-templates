\section{Experimental Setup}

We evaluate SCA and HMG on five benchmarks spanning text, image, and biological
sequence modalities. All models are implemented in PyTorch and trained on 8$\times$
Lumina A100-80GB GPUs using the AdamW optimiser \cite{loshchilov2019decoupled} with
a cosine learning rate schedule and 5,000 warm-up steps. We compare against six
baselines spanning the taxonomy of efficient attention described in
\S\ref{sec:background-taxonomy}.

\subsection{Benchmarks}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Long Range Arena} (LRA) \cite{tay2021long}: Five tasks at
    $n \in [1024, 4096]$.
  \item \textbf{PG-19} \cite{rae2019compressive}: Language modelling on full-length
    books ($n = 8192$).
  \item \textbf{SCROLLS} \cite{shaham2022scrolls}: Seven long-document NLP tasks
    ($n \leq 4096$).
  \item \textbf{ProteinNet}: Custom benchmark for protein secondary structure prediction
    ($n \leq 2048$).
  \item \textbf{LongDiag}: A diagnostic suite of synthetic tasks probing specific
    long-range reasoning capabilities.
\end{itemize}
