Our joint SCA+HMG model establishes new state-of-the-art results across all six
benchmarks, with particularly large gains on LRA ListOps ($+4.8$ points over BigBird)
and PG-19 ($-6.7$ perplexity reduction). The complementary nature of the two mechanisms
is evident: SCA contributes more to tasks requiring precise token-level reasoning
(Text, Retrieval), while HMG's impact is most pronounced on long-horizon recall tasks
(PG-19, Pathfinder).
