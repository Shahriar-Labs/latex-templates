\section{Efficiency Analysis}

Table~\ref{tab:efficiency} compares the computational efficiency of our models against
the full-attention transformer baseline.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Computational efficiency at $n=4096$. Memory measured as peak GPU memory
    during training (GB); throughput in tokens/second.}
  \label{tab:efficiency}
  \begin{tabularx}{0.8\linewidth}{@{}>{\color{edinburghblue}\bfseries}l@{\hspace{6mm}}>{\centering\arraybackslash}X>{\centering\arraybackslash}X>{\centering\arraybackslash}X@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Model} & \textbf{Memory (GB)} & \textbf{Throughput} & \textbf{Speedup} \\
    \midrule
    Transformer    & 72.4 & 1,240 & 1.00$\times$ \\
    SCA            & 28.1 & 2,180 & 1.76$\times$ \\
    HMG            & 31.5 & 1,950 & 1.57$\times$ \\
    SCA+HMG        & 26.8 & 2,340 & 1.89$\times$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The joint model achieves a $63\%$ reduction in peak memory and $89\%$ increase in
training throughput relative to the standard transformer, demonstrating that our
mechanisms deliver substantial practical benefits beyond benchmark improvements.
