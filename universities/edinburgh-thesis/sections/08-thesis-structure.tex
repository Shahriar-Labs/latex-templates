\section{Thesis Structure}

The remainder of this thesis is structured as follows.
Chapter~\ref{chap:background} reviews the transformer architecture, surveys existing
approaches to efficient attention, and establishes the theoretical framework used throughout.
Chapter~\ref{chap:sca} presents Sparse Contextual Attention, including its design rationale,
implementation details, and a theoretical analysis of its approximation guarantees.
Chapter~\ref{chap:hmg} introduces Hierarchical Memory Gating and describes how multi-resolution
memory structures can be integrated into standard transformer layers.
Chapter~\ref{chap:evaluation} provides a comprehensive empirical evaluation of SCA and HMG,
both independently and jointly, across a diverse suite of benchmarks. Finally,
Chapter~\ref{chap:conclusion} summarises our findings and discusses promising directions for
future work.
