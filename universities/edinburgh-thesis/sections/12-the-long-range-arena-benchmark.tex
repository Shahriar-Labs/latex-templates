\section{The Long Range Arena Benchmark}

The Long Range Arena (LRA) \cite{tay2021long} is a standardised benchmark designed to
evaluate models on tasks requiring long-range reasoning. It comprises five tasks spanning
text, image, and mathematical modalities, with sequence lengths ranging from 1,024 to
4,096 tokens. LRA has become the de-facto evaluation suite for efficient transformers and
serves as a primary benchmark in our evaluation (Chapter~\ref{chap:evaluation}).
