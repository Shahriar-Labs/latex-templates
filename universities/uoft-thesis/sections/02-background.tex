\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

Given an encoder $f_\theta$ and two augmented views $x_i, x_j$ of the same image, contrastive
methods minimize a normalized temperature-scaled cross-entropy,
\begin{equation}
  \ell_{i,j} = -\log \frac{\exp(\mathrm{sim}(z_i, z_j)/\tau)}
  {\sum_{k \neq i} \exp(\mathrm{sim}(z_i, z_k)/\tau)},
  \label{eq:nce}
\end{equation}
where $z = g(f_\theta(x))$ is a projected embedding and the denominator runs over the other
views in the batch \citep{chen2020simclr}. Distillation methods drop the negatives and predict
one view's embedding from the other's \citep{grill2020byol}, while masked autoencoders
reconstruct hidden patches of a single view \citep{he2022mae}.
