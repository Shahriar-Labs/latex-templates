\chapter{Content-Aware View Selection}
\label{ch:method}

Random crops often produce views that share little content, such as two patches of sky from a
photograph of a boat, and Equation~\eqref{eq:nce} then asks the encoder to match unrelated
patches. We train a lightweight scorer that predicts the semantic overlap of a candidate crop
pair from a low-resolution saliency map, and sample pairs with probability proportional to the
predicted overlap, tempered so that some hard pairs remain.
