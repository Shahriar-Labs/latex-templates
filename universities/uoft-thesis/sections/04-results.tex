\chapter{Evaluation}
\label{ch:results}

All methods use a ViT-S/16 encoder \citep{dosovitskiy2021} trained for 300 epochs on the same
images. Table~\ref{tab:probe} reports linear-probe and ten-shot accuracy.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Linear-probe and ten-shot accuracy (\%) at equal compute.}
  \label{tab:probe}
  \begin{tabular}{lcc}
    \toprule
    Method & Linear probe & 10-shot \\
    \midrule
    Contrastive, random crops    & 73.1 & 51.4 \\
    Distillation, random crops   & 74.6 & 53.0 \\
    Distillation, content-aware  & 75.9 & 56.2 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The gain is largest in the low-shot setting, where the features must separate classes from very
few examples, and it persists on semantic segmentation, where the content-aware model improves
mean intersection-over-union by 1.1 points.
