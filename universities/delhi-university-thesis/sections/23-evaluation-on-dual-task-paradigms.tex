\section{Evaluation on Dual-Task Paradigms}

We evaluated APCA-WM on three dual-task paradigms:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em, style=nextline]
  \item[Task A (N-back + tracking)] Participants (or the model) performed a 2-back letter
        task concurrently with a visual tracking task.
  \item[Task B (Mental arithmetic + listening)] Participants added sequences of
        single-digit numbers while monitoring a spoken narrative for target words.
  \item[Task C (Spatial planning + categorisation)] A Tower-of-Hanoi variant was
        interleaved with picture categorisation.
\end{description}

Table~\ref{tab:wm-results} shows accuracy on the primary (WM) component of each task.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Dual-task WM accuracy (\% correct, higher is better).  Human norms are from
  the DU Cognitive Battery (DUCB-2022, $n = 148$).  $p$-values from Wilcoxon signed-rank
  tests comparing APCA with each baseline.}
\label{tab:wm-results}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}Xcccc@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Paradigm} & \textbf{Human norm} & \textbf{ACT-R\,5.0}
  & \textbf{GRU baseline} & \textbf{APCA (ours)} \\
\midrule
Task A (N-back)          & $81.3 \pm 6.1$ & $74.2$ & $76.8$ & $\mathbf{80.1}$ \\
Task B (Arithmetic)      & $77.6 \pm 7.4$ & $69.5$ & $72.3$ & $\mathbf{76.2}$ \\
Task C (Spatial)         & $72.4 \pm 8.3$ & $65.1$ & $68.9$ & $\mathbf{71.8}$ \\
\midrule
\textbf{Mean}            & $77.1$         & $69.6$ & $72.7$ & $\mathbf{76.0}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
