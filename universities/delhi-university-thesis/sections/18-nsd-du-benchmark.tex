\section{NSD-DU Benchmark}

\highlightbox{%
\textbf{NSD-DU Dataset.} The Neural Scene Decoding -- Delhi University (NSD-DU) benchmark
comprises 64-channel EEG recordings from 24 healthy adult volunteers (12 female, mean
age $27.4 \pm 3.1$ years) while they viewed 2400 natural scene images.
Each image was presented for 500~ms with a 1000~ms inter-stimulus interval.
The dataset is available for research use through the DU Open Neuroscience Repository
(DONR-2023-001).%
}

Table~\ref{tab:nsd-results} summarises prediction-error results on NSD-DU compared with
the classical Rao--Ballard model.
