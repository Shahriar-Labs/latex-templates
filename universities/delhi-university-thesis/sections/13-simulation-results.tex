\section{Simulation Results}

We simulated a 100-neuron linear network trained on 20 synthetic input patterns drawn
from a mixture of four Gaussians in $\mathbb{R}^{100}$.  Table~\ref{tab:hebbian-results}
reports the mean squared weight error (MSWE) at convergence and the number of epochs
required.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Convergence of the Reward-Modulated Hebbian rule under varying reward time
  constants $\tau_r$ and weight decay $\epsilon$.}
\label{tab:hebbian-results}
\setlength{\arrayrulewidth}{0.5pt}
\begin{tabularx}{0.86\linewidth}{@{}lllcc@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Rule} & $\boldsymbol{\tau_r}$ (ms) & $\boldsymbol{\epsilon}$
  & \textbf{MSWE} & \textbf{Epochs} \\
\midrule
BCM (baseline)         & ---    & 0.01  & $0.142 \pm 0.018$ & 380 \\
RM-Hebbian             & 20     & 0.01  & $0.083 \pm 0.011$ & 290 \\
RM-Hebbian             & 50     & 0.01  & $0.071 \pm 0.009$ & 310 \\
RM-Hebbian             & 100    & 0.01  & $0.069 \pm 0.012$ & 330 \\
RM-Hebbian             & 50     & 0.005 & $0.065 \pm 0.008$ & 345 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
