\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

Water flux through a reverse-osmosis membrane follows the solution-diffusion model,
\begin{equation}
  J_w = A\,(\Delta P - \Delta \pi),
  \label{eq:flux}
\end{equation}
where $A$ is the water permeance, $\Delta P$ the applied pressure and $\Delta \pi$ the osmotic
pressure difference. Increasing $A$ without increasing salt passage has proved difficult,
reflecting a permeability-selectivity trade-off observed across membrane materials
\citep{park2017}.
