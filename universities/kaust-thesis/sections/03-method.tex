\chapter{Nanosheet-Reinforced Polyamide Layers}
\label{ch:method}

Graphene-oxide nanosheets were dispersed in the aqueous amine phase and aligned by a shear flow
before interfacial polymerization with trimesoyl chloride. A sacrificial nanoparticle
interlayer produced a crumpled polyamide film with a larger effective area.
