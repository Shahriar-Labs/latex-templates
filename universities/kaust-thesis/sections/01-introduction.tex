\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

Freshwater scarcity affects billions of people, and in arid coastal regions desalination is a
primary source of drinking water. Reverse osmosis dominates because it uses far less energy than
thermal distillation \citep{elimelech2011}. Its remaining energy cost depends on the membrane:
a more permeable membrane of equal selectivity needs less pressure for the same output.
