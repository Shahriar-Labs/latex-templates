Deep representation learning underlies modern machine learning. As data and models grow, learning
transferable representations efficiently under a fixed compute budget becomes a central problem.
This thesis studies representation learning at scale and makes three contributions: a contrastive
method with a memory bank that supplies many negatives without large batches; a distributed
training scheme with hierarchical gradient compression that reduces communication by about 60\%;
and a systematic evaluation of transfer to image and text tasks. At equal compute, the proposed
method improves linear-evaluation accuracy by 2.7 percentage points over the baseline.
