\chapter{A Hybrid Contact Model}
\label{ch:method}

We write the predicted next state as the sum of an analytical step and a learned residual,
\begin{equation}
  q_{t+1} = f_{\mathrm{phys}}(q_t, u_t; \phi) + g_\theta(q_t, u_t),
  \label{eq:hybrid}
\end{equation}
where $\phi$ are physical parameters estimated online and $g_\theta$ is a small neural network
trained on the residual error. The analytical term guarantees plausible behaviour far from the
data; the residual corrects systematic errors near it.
