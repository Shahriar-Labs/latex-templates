\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

For a planar object pushed by a robot finger, the object's twist $\dot{q}$ follows from the
applied wrench through a limit surface model of friction \citep{goyal1991}. Model-predictive
control then chooses finger motions by optimizing predicted trajectories over a short horizon.
Learned dynamics models such as those of \citet{deisenroth2011} replace the analytical model
with a function fitted to data.
