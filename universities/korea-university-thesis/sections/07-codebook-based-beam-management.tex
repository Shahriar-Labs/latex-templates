\section{Codebook-Based Beam Management}
Standardized mmWave systems typically rely on a predefined codebook of beam
patterns, with the base station and user equipment jointly searching for the
best pair via hierarchical sweeping. This approach is robust and
hardware-agnostic but incurs search latency that scales with codebook size,
which becomes a bottleneck as arrays grow denser to compensate for higher
carrier frequencies.
