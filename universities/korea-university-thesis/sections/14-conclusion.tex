\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

This thesis presented \textsc{Meridian-Beam}, a hybrid beamforming framework
that pairs coarse codebook search with a lightweight reinforcement-learning
refinement stage. Field evaluation on the nine-cell Nimbus urban testbed over
a four-week measurement window showed a 34\% improvement in median downlink
throughput, a 41\% reduction in beam-realignment latency, and a reduction of
95th-percentile outage duration from 812\,ms to 276\,ms, all with less than
3\,ms of added per-decision overhead. Future work will examine multi-cell
coordination, where handover decisions and beam refinement are optimized
jointly rather than per cell, and will extend the measurement campaign to
seasonal traffic variation.
