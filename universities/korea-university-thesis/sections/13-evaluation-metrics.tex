\section{Evaluation Metrics}
Three metrics are reported: (1) median downlink throughput, (2) beam
realignment latency, defined as the time between blockage onset and
restoration of link quality above a $-6$\,dB SNR margin, and (3)
95th-percentile outage duration across all user sessions. All metrics are
computed relative to a hierarchical codebook-search baseline operating on the
same hardware and testbed.
