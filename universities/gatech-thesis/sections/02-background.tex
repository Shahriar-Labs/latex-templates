\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

Dataflow accelerators organize an array of processing elements around a reuse pattern for
weights, inputs or partial sums \citep{chen2016eyeriss}. Sparse accelerators store tensors in a
compressed format and match non-zero operands before multiplying. EIE targets sparse
fully-connected layers \citep{han2016eie}, while SCNN handles convolutions by computing the
Cartesian product of non-zero weights and activations \citep{parashar2017}. If $d_w$ and $d_a$
are the densities of weights and activations, the fraction of effectual multiplications is
\begin{equation}
  \rho = d_w \, d_a ,
  \label{eq:density}
\end{equation}
which bounds the speedup available from skipping.
