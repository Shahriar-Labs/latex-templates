\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

Deep networks are over-parameterized: pruning can remove most of their weights with little loss
of accuracy \citep{han2016deepcompression}. On a dense accelerator, however, a multiplication by
zero costs as much as any other, so the savings stay on paper \citep{jouppi2017}. Turning
sparsity into performance requires hardware that skips ineffectual work without stalling on the
irregular memory accesses that skipping creates.

This dissertation makes three contributions: a characterization of sparsity in pruned models,
a zero-skipping processing element for weights and activations together, and an evaluation
against dense and single-sided sparse baselines.
