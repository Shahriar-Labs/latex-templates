\chapter{Evaluation}
\label{ch:evaluation}

We compared designs of equal silicon area in a cycle-accurate simulator on five pruned models.
Table~\ref{tab:speedup} reports geometric-mean speedup and energy relative to the dense
baseline.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Speedup and energy relative to a dense baseline of equal area.}
  \label{tab:speedup}
  \begin{tabular}{lccc}
    \toprule
    Weight sparsity & Speedup & Energy ratio & Utilization \\
    \midrule
    50\% & 1.6 & 0.71 & 0.83 \\
    75\% & 2.4 & 0.52 & 0.79 \\
    90\% & 3.4 & 0.37 & 0.72 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Utilization falls as sparsity rises because matched pairs become scarce, which is where the
load-balancing network earns its area.
