\chapter{Estimating the Robin Coefficient}
\label{app:a}

For a thin elastic wall of thickness $h$, density $\rho_s$ and Young's modulus $E$, the
coefficient $\alpha = \rho_s h / \Delta t + h E \Delta t / R^2$ approximates the wall impedance
for a vessel of radius $R$, which is the value used in Chapter~\ref{ch:method}.
