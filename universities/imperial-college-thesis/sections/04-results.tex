\chapter{Validation and Aortic Flows}
\label{ch:results}

The solver reproduced the measured pulse-wave speed in a compliant silicone tube to within 3\%.
Table~\ref{tab:iters} compares sub-iterations per time step for three patient-derived aortic
geometries.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Coupling sub-iterations per time step (mean over one cardiac cycle).}
  \label{tab:iters}
  \begin{tabular}{lccc}
    \toprule
    Geometry & Dirichlet-Neumann & Fixed Robin & Stiffness-informed Robin \\
    \midrule
    Aorta A & 25 & 11 & 4 \\
    Aorta B & 31 & 14 & 5 \\
    Aorta C & 40 & 17 & 6 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The gain is largest in Aorta C, whose wall thins markedly along an aneurysmal segment, which a
single fixed Robin coefficient cannot match.
