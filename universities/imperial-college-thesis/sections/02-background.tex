\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

The blood is modelled as an incompressible Newtonian fluid on a moving domain, written in the
arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian (ALE) frame with mesh velocity $\mathbf{w}$,
\begin{equation}
  \rho_f \Bigl(\frac{\partial \mathbf{u}}{\partial t}\Big|_{\chi} + ((\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{w})
  \cdot\nabla)\mathbf{u}\Bigr) = -\nabla p + \mu \nabla^{2}\mathbf{u},
  \qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf{u} = 0 ,
  \label{eq:ale}
\end{equation}
coupled to an elastic wall through continuity of velocity and traction at the interface
\citep{hughes1981}. In a Dirichlet-Neumann partitioned scheme the fluid receives the wall
velocity and the wall receives the fluid traction; when the ratio of wall to fluid density is
small the iteration diverges unless heavily relaxed \citep{causin2005}.
