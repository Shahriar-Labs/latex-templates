\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

A message-passing neural network updates the representation $h_v$ of each atom $v$ from its
neighbours $N(v)$ over $T$ rounds,
\begin{equation}
  h_v^{t+1} = U\Bigl(h_v^{t}, \sum_{w \in N(v)} M(h_v^{t}, h_w^{t}, e_{vw})\Bigr),
  \label{eq:mpnn}
\end{equation}
and a readout function pools the atoms into a molecular representation \citep{gilmer2017}.
Graph convolutional networks are a special case with a fixed, normalized message function
\citep{kipf2017}.
