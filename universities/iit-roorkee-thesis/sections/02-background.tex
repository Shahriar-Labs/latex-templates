\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

In the response-spectrum method, peak modal responses $r_n$ are combined by the square root of
the sum of squares,
\begin{equation}
  r \approx \Bigl(\sum_{n=1}^{N} r_n^{2}\Bigr)^{1/2} ,
  \label{eq:srss}
\end{equation}
which assumes well-separated modal frequencies and elastic behaviour. When the base of a core
wall yields in flexure, the first-mode response is capped while higher-mode shears continue to
grow, a mechanism documented for cantilever walls by \citet{priestley2003}.
