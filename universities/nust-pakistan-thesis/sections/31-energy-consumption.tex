\section{Energy Consumption}
AeroCycle reduced mean energy consumption per delivered sample by 34.6\% relative to
Static-Short and 11.2\% relative to ContikiMAC, while Static-Long consumed the least energy
overall at the cost of latency (Table~\ref{tab:energy}).
