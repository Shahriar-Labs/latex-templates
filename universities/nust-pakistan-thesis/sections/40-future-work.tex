\section{Future Work}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Extend the volatility estimator to a lightweight predictive (rather than purely
        reactive) model to further close the gap to conservative static baselines.
  \item Evaluate AeroCycle under multi-hop mesh topologies with relay-induced negotiation
        delay.
  \item Investigate joint optimization of $\alpha$ and $\gamma$ via on-device online
        learning rather than fixed empirical constants.
  \item Conduct a full-season physical field trial to validate simulation calibration over
        a full annual cycle.
\end{itemize}
