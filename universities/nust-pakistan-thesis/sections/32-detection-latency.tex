\section{Detection Latency}
Despite the energy savings, AeroCycle maintained a 95th-percentile detection latency of
4.2 seconds during high-volatility events, close to Static-Short (3.1\,s) and substantially
better than Static-Long (48.6\,s), as visualized in Figure~\ref{fig:latency}.
