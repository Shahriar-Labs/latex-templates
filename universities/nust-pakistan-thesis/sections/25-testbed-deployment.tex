\section{Testbed Deployment}
A 40-node physical testbed was deployed across a simulated 2-hectare agricultural plot using
Cortex-M0+ nodes equipped with SX1276 LoRa radios, soil-moisture probes, and temperature
sensors. Nodes were powered by 2000\,mAh Li-ion cells with in-line current-sense monitoring
sampled at 10\,Hz for ground-truth energy accounting.
