\section{Limitations}
The evaluation is restricted to star-topology, single-channel deployments and scalar sensing
payloads; multi-hop mesh topologies and higher-bandwidth payloads (e.g.\ periodic imagery)
were not evaluated and may alter the coordination trade-offs analyzed here. The simulation's
calibration error of 3.1\% against testbed traces bounds, but does not eliminate,
sim-to-real gaps at longer horizons.
