\section{Robustness Under Packet Loss}
\label{sec:robustness}
The listen-before-sleep fallback (Section~3.4) was stress-tested under induced intent-frame
loss rates from 0\% to 30\%. Missed-wake probability remained under 1.5\% for loss rates up to
22\%, degrading gracefully to 4.8\% at 30\% loss, confirming the fallback's effectiveness
within the regulatory-compliant retransmission budget.
