\section{Problem Statement}
Existing adaptive duty-cycling schemes either require tight clock synchronization across the
network (imposing coordination overhead disproportionate to LPWAN payload budgets) or rely on
centralized controllers that reintroduce a single point of failure and additional uplink
cost. There is no lightweight, fully decentralized mechanism that adapts sleep intervals to
\emph{both} residual energy budget and locally observed environmental volatility without
external coordination.

\begin{infobox}[title={\faFile\ Research Gap}]
No existing decentralized duty-cycling protocol jointly conditions sleep interval on
battery state \emph{and} short-horizon environmental volatility while remaining within the
0.5--40\,ms processing budget of a Cortex-M0+ class microcontroller.
\end{infobox}
