\section{Volatility Estimator}
The estimator maintains an exponentially weighted moving average of successive sample
deltas $\delta_t = |x_t - x_{t-1}|$:
\begin{equation}
\hat{v}_t = \alpha \, \delta_t + (1-\alpha)\, \hat{v}_{t-1}, \qquad \alpha \in (0,1)
\label{eq:ewma}
\end{equation}
where $\alpha$ controls responsiveness to recent change. The target sleep interval
$T_{\text{sleep}}$ is then computed as a function of estimated volatility $\hat{v}_t$ and
residual battery fraction $b_t \in [0,1]$:
\begin{equation}
T_{\text{sleep}} = T_{\min} + (T_{\max} - T_{\min}) \cdot \left(1 - \hat{v}_t^{\,*}\right)^{\gamma} \cdot f(b_t)
\label{eq:sleep}
\end{equation}
where $\hat{v}_t^{\,*}$ is $\hat{v}_t$ normalized against a rolling maximum, $\gamma$ is a
sensitivity exponent (empirically set to 1.6 in Chapter~5), and $f(b_t)$ is a battery-aware
damping term that linearly extends sleep as $b_t$ falls below a 20\% reserve threshold.

\begin{infobox}[title={\faLock\ Design Invariant}]
The estimator never touches the radio directly. All hardware-facing decisions are deferred to
the Negotiation Module, keeping the volatility computation portable across radio stacks.
\end{infobox}
