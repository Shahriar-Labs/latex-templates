\section{Listen-Before-Sleep Negotiation}
Rather than requiring synchronized clocks, each node broadcasts a short \emph{intent frame}
containing its computed $T_{\text{sleep}}$ immediately before entering sleep. Neighbouring
nodes and the gateway use this frame opportunistically to adjust their own listen windows,
bounding the probability of a missed wake event without a shared time base. The negotiation
sequence is summarized in Table~\ref{tab:negotiation}.

\centering
\caption{Listen-before-sleep negotiation sequence.}
\label{tab:negotiation}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}clX@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{nustgreen}
\textcolor{white}{\bfseries Step} & \textcolor{white}{\bfseries Actor} & \textcolor{white}{\bfseries Action} \\
\midrule
1 & Node    & Compute $T_{\text{sleep}}$ via Eq.~\ref{eq:sleep}. \\
\rowcolor{nustmist}
2 & Node    & Broadcast intent frame (14 bytes) carrying $T_{\text{sleep}}$ and SNR estimate. \\
3 & Gateway & Cache intent, adjust receive-window schedule for that node. \\
\rowcolor{nustmist}
4 & Node    & Enter low-power sleep for $T_{\text{sleep}}$. \\
5 & Node    & Wake, sample sensor, return to Step~1. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
