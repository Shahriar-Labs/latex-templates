\section{Experimental Setup}
FedGuard is compared against three baselines: (i)~\textit{Centralised}, in which all
telemetry is pooled and trained on a single GRU; (ii)~\textit{FedAvg}; and
(iii)~\textit{FedProx} with proximal term $\mu = 0.01$. All schemes share the same GRU
architecture, batch size, and total local-epoch budget for fairness. Each experiment is
averaged over five random seeds.
