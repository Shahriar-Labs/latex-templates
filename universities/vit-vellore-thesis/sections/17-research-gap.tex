\section{Research Gap}
\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox,title=Positioning of This Work]
No prior study combines (i) volatility-aware client weighting, (ii) a DP noise layer
tuned for recurrent gradient structure, and (iii) evaluation on telemetry patterned
after a real multi-machine factory deployment. This thesis addresses that gap.
\end{tcolorbox}
