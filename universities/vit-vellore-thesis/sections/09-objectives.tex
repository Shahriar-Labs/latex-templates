\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,label=\textbf{O\arabic*.}]
  \item Design a federated aggregation scheme, \textbf{FedGuard}, that adapts client
        contribution weights to local data volatility.
  \item Integrate a differential-privacy noise layer into the gradient-sharing step and
        quantify its privacy--utility trade-off.
  \item Build a realistic 40-node IIoT simulation testbed patterned on factory-floor
        telemetry characteristics.
  \item Benchmark FedGuard against centralised training, FedAvg, and FedProx on
        accuracy, bandwidth, and reconstruction-attack resistance.
\end{enumerate}
