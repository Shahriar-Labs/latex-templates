\section{Federated Learning Fundamentals}
FedAvg, the canonical federated aggregation algorithm, alternates between local
stochastic-gradient-descent rounds on each client and weighted averaging of client
model parameters at a central server, with weights proportional to local dataset size.
Under non-IID data, FedAvg is known to converge slowly and can drift toward clients
with the largest datasets rather than the most representative ones. \keyword{FedProx}
partially addresses this by adding a proximal regularisation term that discourages
local models from straying far from the global model between rounds, at the cost of an
additional hyperparameter that requires site-specific tuning.
