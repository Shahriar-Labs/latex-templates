\section{Summary}
This thesis presented FedGuard, a federated learning scheme for predictive maintenance
in industrial IoT settings that combines volatility-aware client weighting with a
tuned differential-privacy gradient layer. Evaluated on a realistic 40-node simulation
testbed, FedGuard achieved 94.6\% failure-prediction accuracy — within 1.1 percentage
points of centralised training — while reducing uplink bandwidth by 71\% and
substantially limiting gradient-inversion reconstruction risk.
