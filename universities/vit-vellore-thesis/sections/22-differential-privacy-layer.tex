\section{Differential Privacy Layer}
Before upload, each client clips its per-example gradient to an $L_2$ norm of $C$ and
adds Gaussian noise calibrated to a target privacy budget $(\varepsilon, \delta)$. We
adopt the moments-accountant method to track cumulative privacy loss across
communication rounds and halt training if the budget is exhausted before convergence.
