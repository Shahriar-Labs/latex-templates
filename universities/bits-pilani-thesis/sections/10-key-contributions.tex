\section{Key Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\lbrack C\arabic*\rbrack,leftmargin=2.6em]
  \item An online latency predictor requiring under 200~KB of model state, deployable directly on edge nodes.
  \item A bounded-horizon greedy placement policy with provable $O(n \log n)$ decision complexity.
  \item An open evaluation testbed and trace-driven workload generator calibrated against anonymised industry telemetry.
  \item Empirical evidence of graceful degradation under failure, exceeding 96\% SLA compliance.
\end{enumerate}
