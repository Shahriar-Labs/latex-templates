\section{Research Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{O\arabic*.},leftmargin=2.2em]
  \item Design a lightweight, online latency-prediction model suitable for edge-resident inference.
  \item Formulate a cost-aware task-placement policy that can be solved within a millisecond-scale decision budget.
  \item Build and evaluate AL-TO on a realistic multi-tier testbed against representative baselines.
  \item Characterise robustness under node failure and network partition.
\end{enumerate}
