\section{Latency Prediction}
A lightweight gradient-boosted regressor, retrained every five minutes on a sliding telemetry window, predicts per-node completion time $\hat{t}_{i}$ for candidate node $i$:
\begin{equation}
\hat{t}_{i} = f_\theta\big(q_i, \rho_i, d_i, c_i\big)
\label{eq:predictor}
\end{equation}
where $q_i$ is queue depth, $\rho_i$ is CPU utilisation, $d_i$ is measured RTT to the requesting client, and $c_i$ is the marginal cost coefficient of node $i$.
