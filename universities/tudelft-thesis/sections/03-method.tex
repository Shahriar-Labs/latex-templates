\chapter{Co-Design Formulation}
\label{ch:method}

We minimize platform mass over the design parameters $p$ and controller gains $K$ jointly,
subject to limits on tower-base fatigue and platform pitch under a set of stochastic load
cases. For fixed $p$ the optimal gains come from a linear quadratic regulator, which lets the
outer optimization treat $K(p)$ as a function of the platform alone.
