\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

Most of the world's offshore wind resource lies over water too deep for fixed foundations.
Floating platforms reach it, but they introduce a new difficulty: the rotor thrust that the
controller regulates also pitches the platform, and a controller tuned for a fixed tower can
destabilize this motion. The usual design process fixes the platform first and then designs a
controller for it. This dissertation designs both together.
