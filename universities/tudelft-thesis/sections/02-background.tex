\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

Near an operating point the coupled turbine and platform dynamics can be linearized as
\begin{equation}
  \dot{x} = A(p)\,x + B(p)\,u + E\,w ,
  \label{eq:linear}
\end{equation}
where $x$ collects platform and rotor states, $u$ the blade pitch and generator torque, $w$ the
wind and wave disturbances, and $p$ the platform design parameters \citep{jonkman2010}. A
controller that ignores the dependence of $A$ and $B$ on $p$ may be optimal for one platform
and poor for another.
