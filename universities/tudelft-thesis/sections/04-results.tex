\chapter{Results}
\label{ch:results}

Table~\ref{tab:designs} compares the sequential and co-designed solutions for a 15-megawatt
reference turbine.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Sequential versus co-designed platform for a 15-megawatt turbine.}
  \label{tab:designs}
  \begin{tabular}{lcc}
    \toprule
    Quantity & Sequential & Co-designed \\
    \midrule
    Platform mass (t)                 & 20\,900 & 18\,400 \\
    Tower-base fatigue (normalized)   & 1.00 & 1.00 \\
    Maximum platform pitch (deg)      & 5.8 & 5.6 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The co-designed platform is 12\% lighter because its controller damps pitch motion that the
sequential design had to resist with ballast and column spacing.
