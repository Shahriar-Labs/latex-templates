\vspace{1em}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Designing a floating wind turbine's platform and its controller separately leaves at least a tenth of the achievable mass reduction unused.\\\emph{This proposition pertains to this dissertation.}
\item A controller that damps platform pitch well in simulation is only as good as the wave model it was tuned against.\\\emph{This proposition pertains to this dissertation.}
\item The cheapest kilogram of steel in an offshore structure is the one a better controller made unnecessary.\\\emph{This proposition pertains to this dissertation.}
\item Open simulation tools have done more for wind energy research than any single turbine design.
\item Engineering courses should grade the assumptions in a model as carefully as its results.
\item A research group's shared code is its real institutional memory.
\item Cycling to work in a headwind is good training for doing a PhD.
\end{enumerate}
