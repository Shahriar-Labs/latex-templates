Neural machine translation works well for language pairs with millions of parallel sentences
and poorly for the many Indian languages that have only thousands. This thesis studies how to
make translation systems useful in that low-resource setting. It compares transfer from related
high-resource languages, back-translation of monolingual text, and multilingual pre-training,
and proposes a script-unified subword vocabulary that lets related languages written in
different scripts share representations. For Telugu to English with 40,000 parallel sentences,
the combination raises BLEU from 11.8 to 23.4.
