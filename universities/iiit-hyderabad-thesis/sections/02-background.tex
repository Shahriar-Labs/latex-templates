\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

A translation model estimates the probability of a target sentence $y$ given a source sentence
$x$ as a product over target tokens,
\begin{equation}
  p(y \mid x) = \prod_{t=1}^{|y|} p(y_t \mid y_{<t}, x),
  \label{eq:nmt}
\end{equation}
where the tokens are subword units learned from the training text \citep{sennrich2016bpe}.
Back-translation creates synthetic parallel data by translating target-side monolingual text
into the source language \citep{sennrich2016bt}.
