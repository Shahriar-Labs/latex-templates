\section{Gaps in the Australian Literature}

Despite the methodological advances reviewed above, the Australian ecological
monitoring literature lags behind its North American and European counterparts
in adopting Bayesian hierarchical models for routine monitoring. Of the 127
peer-reviewed articles published between 2015 and 2024 that analyse data from
the LTERN and TERN networks, only 14 (11\%) employed fully Bayesian methods,
and only 3 of those accounted for imperfect detection~\cite{bradshaw2012}. The
present thesis aims to narrow this gap.
