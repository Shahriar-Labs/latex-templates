\section{Research Questions}

This thesis is organised around three interconnected research questions:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{RQ\arabic*:}, leftmargin=*, itemsep=6pt]
  \item Can hierarchical Bayesian occupancy models, fitted to data from
        spatially sparse monitoring networks, reliably detect trends in the
        distribution of rare and cryptic forest-dwelling vertebrates?
  \item How should spatiotemporal models of above-ground biomass be structured
        to account for both measurement error in LiDAR-derived estimates and
        process error in stand dynamics?
  \item What gains in predictive accuracy and ecological interpretability are
        achieved by integrating presence-only, presence--absence, and count data
        within a single joint species distribution model?
\end{enumerate}
