\section{Problem Statement}
Given a distribution feeder serving $N$ EV chargers with stochastic driver arrival and
departure times, the scheduling problem is to determine, at each control interval, the charging
power set-point for every connected vehicle such that:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4cm]
  \item transformer and conductor thermal limits are respected at all times,
  \item bus voltages remain within the $\pm 5\%$ statutory band,
  \item each vehicle reaches its driver-specified departure state-of-charge (SoC) target, and
  \item total energy procurement cost is minimized under time-of-use tariffs.
\end{enumerate}
