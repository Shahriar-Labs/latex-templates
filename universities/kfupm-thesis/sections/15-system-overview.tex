\section{System Overview}
Figure~\ref{fig:arch} illustrates the overall control architecture. The MPC scheduler receives
forecasted driver arrival/departure data, real-time feeder telemetry, and time-of-use price
signals, and outputs per-charger power set-points at each 15-minute control interval.
