\section{Future Work}
Future work should validate the controller against field data from a pilot feeder, extend the
formulation to a distributed or hierarchical architecture for scalability to city-scale
deployments, and incorporate vehicle-to-grid (V2G) discharge as an additional control variable
to further flatten peak demand.
