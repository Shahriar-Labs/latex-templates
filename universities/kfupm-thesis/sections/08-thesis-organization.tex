\section{Thesis Organization}
Chapter~\ref{ch:lit} reviews prior work on EV charging coordination and MPC applications in
distribution systems. Chapter~\ref{ch:method} presents the feeder model, MPC formulation, and
solution methodology. Chapter~\ref{ch:results} reports simulation results and comparative
evaluation. Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusion} concludes with a summary of contributions, limitations,
and directions for future work.
