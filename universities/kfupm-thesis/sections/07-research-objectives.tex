\section{Research Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4cm]
  \item Formulate a receding-horizon MPC problem for coordinated EV charging at feeder scale.
  \item Develop a reduced-order feeder model suitable for real-time optimization.
  \item Benchmark the proposed controller against uncoordinated and rule-based baselines.
  \item Evaluate robustness to demand-forecast uncertainty through sensitivity analysis.
\end{enumerate}
