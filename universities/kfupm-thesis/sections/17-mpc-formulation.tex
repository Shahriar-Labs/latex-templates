\section{MPC Formulation}
At each control step $k$, the scheduler solves, over horizon $H$:
\begin{equation}
\min_{\{P_{i,t}\}} \; \sum_{t=k}^{k+H} \left[ \alpha \, c_t \sum_i P_{i,t} + \beta \, \theta_t^2 +
\gamma \sum_i (V_i - V_{\text{nom}})^2 \right]
\label{eq:objective}
\end{equation}
subject to charger power limits $0 \le P_{i,t} \le P_i^{\max}$, SoC dynamics
$\mathrm{SoC}_{i,t+1} = \mathrm{SoC}_{i,t} + \eta_i P_{i,t} \Delta t / E_i^{\text{cap}}$, terminal
SoC targets at each vehicle's departure time, voltage limits from Eq.~\eqref{eq:distflow}, and
thermal limits from Eq.~\eqref{eq:thermal}. Weights $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ trade off cost,
thermal stress, and voltage quality; values used in this study are given in
Table~\ref{tab:params}.
