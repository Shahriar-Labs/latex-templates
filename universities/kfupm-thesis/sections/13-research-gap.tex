\section{Research Gap}
Prior optimization-based methods largely address either network constraints or cost objectives
in isolation, and few studies jointly evaluate transformer thermal stress alongside voltage
regulation under a receding-horizon framework calibrated to a realistic residential feeder.
This thesis addresses that gap by combining a reduced-order thermal-voltage feeder model with
an MPC scheduler evaluated against multiple demand scenarios and forecast-error conditions.
