\chapter{Safe Reinforcement Learning for Volt-Var Control}
\label{ch:method}

A penalty in the reward discourages violations but does not prevent them. We add a safety layer
that projects each proposed action onto the set of actions predicted by the linearized power
flow to keep all voltages within limits \citep{dalal2018}. The projection is a small quadratic
program solved at every step.
