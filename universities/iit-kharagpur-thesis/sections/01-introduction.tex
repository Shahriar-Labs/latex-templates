\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

Rooftop solar has turned many distribution feeders from one-way conduits into two-way networks.
Midday generation can push voltages above their limits, while evening demand pulls them below.
Utilities regulate voltage with tap changers and capacitor banks designed for slower, more
predictable variation. Smart inverters and batteries respond in seconds, but deciding how they
should respond, under uncertain forecasts, is a hard sequential decision problem.
