\chapter{Background}
\label{ch:background}

For a radial feeder, the linearized DistFlow model relates voltage magnitudes to active and
reactive power injections \citep{baran1989}. Volt-var control chooses inverter reactive power
$q_t$ to keep voltages $v_t$ within $[\underline{v}, \overline{v}]$ while minimizing losses.
We treat it as a Markov decision process and learn a policy with an actor-critic method
\citep{lillicrap2016}, maximizing
\begin{equation}
  J = \mathbb{E}\Bigl[\sum_{t} \gamma^{t} \bigl(-P_{\mathrm{loss},t} - \lambda\,
  \|\max(0, v_t - \overline{v}) + \max(0, \underline{v} - v_t)\|_1\bigr)\Bigr].
  \label{eq:reward}
\end{equation}
