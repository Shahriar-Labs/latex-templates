\section{Cost Analysis}
Amortizing the per-node hardware cost of EGP~3{,}200 (Table~\ref{tab:bom}) over an assumed
five-year service life and a single 0.5-feddan plot yields a levelized cost of approximately
EGP~940 per feddan per season, inclusive of an estimated 8\% annual maintenance allowance.
This compares favorably against the market value of the water saved at the site's blended
agricultural water tariff, which the trial estimated at roughly EGP~1{,}560 per feddan per
season -- suggesting a positive net return within the first growing season under the
assumptions used here.
