\section{Reliability and Fault Behavior}
Across the 165-day trial, field nodes reported valid readings on 96.2\% of scheduled
transmission windows. The fallback timer schedule (Section~3.4) was invoked on 14 occasions
across all plots, most commonly following battery under-voltage events during a five-day
overcast period in June; no node fully depleted its battery reserve.
