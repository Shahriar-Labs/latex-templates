\section{Gaps in Prior Work}
No study identified in this review evaluates a sub-USD-150 controller under real smallholder
field conditions in Egypt specifically. Most precision-irrigation literature targets
commercial-scale center-pivot systems in North America or the Gulf, where cost constraints
and plot geometry differ substantially from the Delta smallholder context. This thesis is
positioned to close that gap.
