\section{Water Consumption}
Figure~\ref{fig:water-bar} compares total seasonal water delivery between controller-managed
and conventionally irrigated plots for each crop.

Averaged across both crops, controller-managed plots consumed 27.4\% less water than paired
conventional plots (cotton: $-$27.1\%; maize: $-$27.8\%). The reduction is consistent with the
20--40\% range reported by Fardous and Michel \cite{fardous2022} for commercial-scale systems, suggesting the
low-cost hardware described in Chapter~3 does not materially compromise scheduling accuracy.
