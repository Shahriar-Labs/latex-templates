\section{Power Budget}
Average daily energy consumption per node was measured at 4.8~Wh, dominated by the
microcontroller's periodic wake cycles and the momentary current draw of valve actuation. With
a 10~W panel and the region's winter-worst-case irradiance of 3.1 peak-sun-hours per day, the
system provides an energy margin sufficient for the five-day autonomy reserve recommended in
Section~2.3, assuming a fully charged 6~Ah battery at the onset of a cloudy period.
