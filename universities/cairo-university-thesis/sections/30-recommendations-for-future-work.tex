\section{Recommendations for Future Work}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item Extend the field trial to multiple seasons and sites to assess performance under a
      wider range of soil and climate conditions, and to capture any cumulative soil-salinity
      effects of altered irrigation timing.
\item Integrate the scheduling service with the national agricultural extension advisory
      platform to enable governorate-scale deployment and centralized fault monitoring.
\item Investigate a lower-cost sensor variant using resistive rather than capacitive
      moisture probes, trading measurement stability for a further reduction in per-node
      cost.
\item Extend the economic analysis to include canal-level water savings and their value to
      downstream users, which fall outside the plot-level accounting used in this thesis.
\end{itemize}
