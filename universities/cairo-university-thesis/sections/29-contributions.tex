\section{Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{C\arabic*.}, leftmargin=2.2em]
\item A low-cost hardware architecture combining capacitive soil-moisture sensing, solar
      power, and LoRaWAN connectivity, specified in full detail suitable for replication.
\item A hybrid sensor-and-forecast scheduling algorithm with a documented fallback mode for
      sensor or network failure.
\item Field-measured water-savings, yield, and cost data from a real smallholder deployment
      in Kafr El-Sheikh Governorate, filling a gap identified in the literature review.
\end{enumerate}
