\section{Summary of Findings}
This thesis designed, built, and field-evaluated a solar-powered, LoRaWAN-connected
irrigation controller intended for smallholder plots in the Nile Delta at a per-unit cost of
approximately EGP~3{,}200. Deployed across matched cotton and maize plots over one growing
season, the system reduced seasonal water consumption by 27.4\% on average with no
statistically significant yield penalty, and achieved a levelized cost below the estimated
value of water saved.
