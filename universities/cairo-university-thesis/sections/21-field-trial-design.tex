\section{Field-Trial Design}
Six plots of 0.5~feddan each were selected in a single cooperative in Kafr El-Sheikh
Governorate: three planted with cotton and three with maize. Within each crop, one plot was
randomly assigned to the controller treatment and the remaining two retained conventional
canal-rotation irrigation as controls. Soil texture, planting date, and cultivar were held
constant across paired plots. Volumetric water delivered was measured at the plot inlet using
calibrated flow meters; yield was measured at harvest by full-plot weighing.
