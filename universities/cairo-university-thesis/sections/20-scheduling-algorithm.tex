\section{Scheduling Algorithm}
The controller evaluates an irrigation decision once per hour using the rule summarized
below.

\begin{tcolorbox}[keybox=Scheduling Rule (simplified)]
\textbf{IF} soil VWC $<$ crop-specific lower threshold \textbf{AND} forecast ET$_0$ over next
48~h exceeds forecast rainfall \textbf{THEN} open valve for the computed duration $t$, where
\[
t = \frac{(\theta_{fc} - \theta_{current}) \cdot d \cdot A}{q}
\]
with $\theta_{fc}$ the field-capacity moisture content, $\theta_{current}$ the measured
content, $d$ the effective root-zone depth, $A$ the plot area, and $q$ the delivery flow
rate. \textbf{ELSE IF} no valid sensor reading has been received for more than 36~h,
\textbf{fall back} to the pre-configured timer schedule for that crop and growth stage.
\end{tcolorbox}
