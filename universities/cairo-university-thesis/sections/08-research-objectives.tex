\section{Research Objectives}
The specific objectives of this thesis are to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{O\arabic*.}, leftmargin=2.2em]
\item Design a solar-powered, low-power-network irrigation controller suitable for
      deployment on plots as small as 0.25~feddan.
\item Develop a scheduling algorithm that combines soil-moisture sensing with short-horizon
      evapotranspiration forecasting.
\item Deploy and evaluate the system across matched treatment and control plots over a full
      growing season in Kafr El-Sheikh Governorate.
\item Quantify water savings, yield impact, and levelized hardware cost, and assess
      scalability to a governorate-wide extension program.
\end{enumerate}
