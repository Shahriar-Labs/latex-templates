\section{Low-Power Sensing Infrastructure}
The proliferation of LPWAN protocols -- notably LoRaWAN and NB-IoT -- has made multi-year,
battery-powered field sensing economically viable for the first time \cite{hassanein2021}.
Table~\ref{tab:lpwan-compare} summarizes the trade-offs relevant to this thesis.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Comparison of candidate LPWAN technologies for field deployment}
\label{tab:lpwan-compare}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lXXX@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{cairoblue!10}
\textbf{Criterion} & \textbf{LoRaWAN} & \textbf{NB-IoT} & \textbf{Sigfox} \\
\midrule
Typical range (rural)      & 8--12 km      & 5--10 km      & 10--15 km \\
Gateway infrastructure     & Self-hosted   & Carrier-owned & Carrier-owned \\
Recurring cost             & None          & SIM data plan & Subscription \\
Node power draw            & Very low      & Moderate      & Very low \\
Coverage in target site    & Adequate      & Sparse        & Unavailable \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

LoRaWAN was selected for this thesis on the basis of range, absence of recurring
carrier cost, and confirmed coverage at the pilot site via a self-hosted gateway.
