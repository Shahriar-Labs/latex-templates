\section{Future Work}

Promising extensions include (1) evaluating ATS on a live edge deployment
rather than synthetic traces, (2) extending the forecast model to capture
cross-node correlation during correlated failures, and (3) exploring
learned, rather than hand-tuned, feedback gains $\beta$ and clip bounds
$\delta$.
