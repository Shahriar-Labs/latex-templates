\section{Summary}

This thesis presented ATS, a scheduling framework for latency-constrained
edge computing that pairs lightweight load forecasting with a bounded
priority-feedback controller. Across a 48-node heterogeneous testbed, ATS
reduced $P_{95}$ latency by 38.4\% and improved utilization by 21.7
percentage points relative to standard baselines, while keeping controller
overhead below 1.2\% of the scheduling budget and remaining stable under
node-failure injection of up to 12\%.
