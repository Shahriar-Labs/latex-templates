\section{Stability Under Node Failure}

Injecting random node failures at rates up to 12\% per epoch, ATS
maintained bounded reassignment rates due to the clipped feedback term
(Equation~\ref{eq:feedback}). Removing the clip (an ablation) caused
reassignment rates to grow superlinearly beyond 8\% failure injection,
confirming the design note in Section~3.1.2.
