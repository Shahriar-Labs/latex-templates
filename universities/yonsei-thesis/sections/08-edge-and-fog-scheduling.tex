\section{Edge and Fog Scheduling}

Early edge scheduling research largely adapted cloud data-center techniques ---
round-robin, least-connections, and static-priority queues --- to smaller,
more heterogeneous node pools. While straightforward to implement, these
approaches assume relatively stable per-node capacity and do not react to
short-term load spikes, which are common at the edge due to intermittent
connectivity and shared uplink bandwidth.
