\section{Controller Overhead}

Figure~\ref{fig:overhead} breaks down per-epoch coordinator time between
forecasting, feedback computation, and assignment. Combined controller
overhead stays below 1.2\% of the 250\,ms epoch budget across all
tested loads.

\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}
\begin{scope}
\foreach \name/\val/\col/\pos in {
  {Assignment}/68/yonseiblue/0,
  {Forecast}/22/yonseigold/1,
  {Feedback}/10/softgray/2}
{
  \pgfmathsetmacro{\barheight}{\val/10}
  \draw[fill=\col, draw=none] (\pos*1.6,0) rectangle ++(1.1,\barheight);
  \node[font=\scriptsize, above] at (\pos*1.6+0.55,\barheight) {\val\%};
  \node[font=\scriptsize, below, align=center] at (\pos*1.6+0.55,-0.15) {\name};
}
\end{scope}
\draw[->, draw=softgray] (-0.3,0) -- (5.2,0);
\end{tikzpicture}
\caption{Share of per-epoch coordinator time by ATS pipeline stage.}
\label{fig:overhead}
\end{figure}
