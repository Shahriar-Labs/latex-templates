\subsection{Workload Traces}

We use three workload profiles derived from production traces at Vertex Cloud Services, sanitized and released for academic use:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{Video Analytics Pipeline.} 480p and 720p streams processed through object detection (YOLO-style) and frame differencing. Target latency: 50~ms per frame.
    \item \textbf{IoT Stream Aggregation.} Time-series window aggregation (median, percentile, anomaly score) over sensor streams at ingress rates up to 10\,000 events per second per node.
    \item \textbf{Inference Serving.} Bert-medium and ResNet-50 serving behind a load balancer, with Poisson arrival at mean 200 queries per second.
\end{itemize}
