\section{Thesis Organization}

The remainder of this thesis is organized as follows. Chapter~\ref{ch:background} surveys background and related work in scheduling, edge computing, and resource allocation. Chapter~\ref{ch:model} develops the formal model for MTEAP and proves key structural properties. Chapter~\ref{ch:edgesched} presents the EdgeSched architecture and algorithms in detail. Chapter~\ref{ch:eval} describes the evaluation methodology and results. Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusion} concludes with a summary of contributions and directions for future work.
