\section{Research Questions}
This thesis addresses the following research questions:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{RQ\arabic*.}, leftmargin=2.1cm]
  \item Can a lightweight edge-resident filter meaningfully reduce uplink volume without
        discarding events that a downstream model would have flagged as anomalous?
  \item Does a hybrid edge--cloud architecture improve detection latency and false-positive
        rate relative to purely centralised baselines at comparable operating cost?
  \item Can an adaptive threshold controller maintain stable detection performance under
        seasonal and load-driven distribution shift without manual retuning?
\end{enumerate}
