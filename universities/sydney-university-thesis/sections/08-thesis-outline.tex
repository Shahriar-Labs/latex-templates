\section{Thesis Outline}
Chapter~\ref{ch:litreview} surveys anomaly detection methods for industrial time series and
positions this work within the literature. Chapter~\ref{ch:method} details the StreamGuard
architecture, the adaptive threshold controller, and the experimental dataset. Chapter~\ref{ch:results}
presents quantitative results against baseline methods and the field trial outcome.
Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusion} summarises findings, discusses limitations, and outlines future work.
