\chapter{Sensor Metadata Schema}
\label{app:schema}
Table~\ref{tab:schema} lists the metadata fields recorded for each sensor channel in the
Apex Manufacturing deployment.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Sensor metadata schema fields.}
\label{tab:schema}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l l p{6.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
Field & Type & Description \\
\midrule
\texttt{sensor\_id} & UUID & Globally unique channel identifier. \\
\texttt{asset\_class} & Enum & One of \{rotating, thermal, electrical\}. \\
\texttt{sample\_rate\_hz} & Integer & Nominal sampling frequency in hertz. \\
\texttt{install\_date} & Date & Commissioning date of the sensor. \\
\texttt{gateway\_id} & UUID & Edge gateway responsible for pre-filtering. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
