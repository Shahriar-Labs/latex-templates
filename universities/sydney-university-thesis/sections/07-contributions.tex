\section{Contributions}
The principal contributions of this thesis are:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6cm]
  \item \textbf{StreamGuard}, a two-stage edge--cloud anomaly detection architecture
        combining a windowed Mahalanobis pre-filter with an ensemble of temporal
        autoencoders (Chapter~\ref{ch:method}).
  \item An adaptive threshold controller that adjusts detection sensitivity using an
        exponentially weighted control-limit update rule, validated against seasonal load
        variation (Section~\ref{sec:adaptive-threshold}).
  \item An empirical evaluation on eighteen months of production telemetry from three
        assembly lines at Apex Manufacturing Ltd., including a four-shift field trial
        (Chapter~\ref{ch:results}).
\end{itemize}
