\section{Field Trial}
An operational trial was conducted across four production shifts on the rotating-equipment
line. Table~\ref{tab:trial} reports precision stability under the seasonal load variation
observed during the trial window.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Field trial precision by shift under varying production load.}
\label{tab:trial}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l c c@{}}
\toprule
Shift & Relative production load & Precision \\
\midrule
Morning & 1.00$\times$ & 0.90 \\
Afternoon & 1.18$\times$ & 0.89 \\
Night (reduced staffing) & 0.62$\times$ & 0.91 \\
Weekend maintenance window & 0.35$\times$ & 0.88 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Precision remained within a narrow band (0.88--0.91) across a load range spanning nearly
$3\times$, supporting the claim that the adaptive threshold controller absorbs seasonal
drift without manual retuning.
