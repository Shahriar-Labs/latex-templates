\section{Dataset}
Telemetry was supplied by Apex Manufacturing Ltd.\ from three assembly lines over an
eighteen-month period. Table~\ref{tab:dataset} summarises the dataset composition.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Summary of the Apex Manufacturing telemetry dataset.}
\label{tab:dataset}
\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{@{}l c c c@{}}
\toprule
Asset class & Sensor channels & Sampling rate (Hz) & Confirmed anomalies \\
\midrule
Rotating equipment & 186 & 500 & 312 \\
Thermal systems & 128 & 50 & 174 \\
Electrical drives & 98 & 200 & 209 \\
\midrule
\textbf{Total} & \textbf{412} & --- & \textbf{695} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}}
\end{table}

Confirmed anomalies were labelled retrospectively by Apex reliability engineers from
maintenance work orders, providing a held-out evaluation set independent of the training
period.
