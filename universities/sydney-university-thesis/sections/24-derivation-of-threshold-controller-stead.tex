\chapter{Derivation of Threshold Controller Steady State}
\label{app:derivation}
Taking expectations of the update rule in Equation~\eqref{eq:threshold-update} and assuming
$\bar{e}^{(k)}$ and $s^{(k)}$ converge to stationary values $\bar{e}^{\ast}$ and $s^{\ast}$
under fixed operating conditions, the fixed point $\tau^{\ast}_{\text{cloud}}$ satisfies:
\begin{align}
\tau^{\ast}_{\text{cloud}} &= \tau^{\ast}_{\text{cloud}} + \alpha\left(\bar{e}^{\ast} + z\,s^{\ast} - \tau^{\ast}_{\text{cloud}}\right) \nonumber\\
0 &= \alpha\left(\bar{e}^{\ast} + z\,s^{\ast} - \tau^{\ast}_{\text{cloud}}\right) \nonumber\\
\tau^{\ast}_{\text{cloud}} &= \bar{e}^{\ast} + z\,s^{\ast}.
\end{align}
The controller therefore converges to the classical $z$-score control limit under stationary
conditions for any $\alpha \in (0,1]$, with $\alpha$ governing only the rate of convergence
and transient tracking behaviour under drift.
