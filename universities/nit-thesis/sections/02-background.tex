\chapter{BACKGROUND}
\label{ch:background}

Classical lamination theory relates in-plane forces $N$ and moments $M$ to mid-plane strains
$\varepsilon^0$ and curvatures $\kappa$ through the stiffness matrices $A$, $B$ and $D$,
\begin{equation}
  \begin{bmatrix} N \\ M \end{bmatrix} =
  \begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ B & D \end{bmatrix}
  \begin{bmatrix} \varepsilon^0 \\ \kappa \end{bmatrix} .
  \label{eq:clt}
\end{equation}
Gaussian processes model an unknown function with a prior over functions and give calibrated
uncertainty \citep{rasmussen2006}, which Bayesian optimization uses to choose the next design
to evaluate \citep{jones1998}.
