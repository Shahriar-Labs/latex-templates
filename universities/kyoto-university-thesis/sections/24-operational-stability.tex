Figure~\ref{fig:stability} shows normalized PCE evolution during
accelerated aging. The \textsc{sam-7} device retains 80\% of its initial
efficiency after approximately 1{,}580~h, an 8.3$\times$ improvement over
the 190~h measured for the untreated control.
