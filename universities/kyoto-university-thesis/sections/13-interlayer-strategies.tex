reported in the perovskite literature, categorized by passivation
mechanism.

While each approach yields measurable gains, direct comparison across
studies is confounded by differing perovskite compositions, fabrication
environments, and testing protocols. This motivates the internally
consistent, single-laboratory screening campaign undertaken in this
dissertation.
