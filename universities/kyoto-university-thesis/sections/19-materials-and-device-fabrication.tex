\section{Materials and Device Fabrication}

Patterned ITO substrates were sequentially cleaned by ultrasonication in
detergent, deionized water, acetone, and isopropanol, followed by 15~min
UV--ozone treatment. A compact SnO$_2$ layer was deposited from
colloidal precursor and annealed at 150\textdegree C for 30~min. Candidate
SAM interlayers were deposited from 1~mM ethanolic solution by spin
coating (3000~rpm, 30~s) followed by a 100\textdegree C anneal. The
perovskite absorber (Cs$_{0.05}$FA$_{0.90}$MA$_{0.05}$PbI$_3$) was
deposited by a one-step antisolvent method, followed by Spiro-OMeTAD hole
transport layer and thermally evaporated gold electrodes (80~nm).
