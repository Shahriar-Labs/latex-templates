\section{Overview}

Figure~\ref{fig:workflow} summarizes the closed-loop discovery pipeline:
descriptor calculation, surrogate model training, Bayesian acquisition,
targeted synthesis, and device fabrication/characterization, with
experimental results fed back into the training set.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}[
  node distance=0.9cm and 1.3cm,
  box/.style={draw=kyotomaroondark, rounded corners=2pt, fill=paperlight, minimum height=1cm, minimum width=2.6cm, align=center, font=\small, text=inkgray},
  arr/.style={->, kyotomaroon, thick}
]
\node[box] (data) {Descriptor\\Dataset ($n{=}214$)};
\node[box, right=of data] (model) {GBRT\\Surrogate Model};
\node[box, right=of model] (acq) {Bayesian\\Acquisition};
\node[box, below=of acq] (synth) {Targeted\\Synthesis};
\node[box, left=of synth] (fab) {Device\\Fabrication};
\node[box, left=of fab] (char) {Characterization\\(PCE, $T_{80}$, KPFM)};

\draw[arr] (data) -- (model);
\draw[arr] (model) -- (acq);
\draw[arr] (acq) -- (synth);
\draw[arr] (synth) -- (fab);
\draw[arr] (fab) -- (char);
\draw[arr] (char.north) |- ($(data.south)+(0,-0.6)$) -| (data.south);
\end{tikzpicture}
\caption{Closed-loop interlayer discovery workflow used in this
dissertation. Characterization results are fed back into the training
dataset for subsequent iterations.}
\label{fig:workflow}
\end{figure}
