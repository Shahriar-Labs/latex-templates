\section{Mechanistic Origin of the Improvement}

KPFM line scans across cleaved cross-sections reveal a 40~mV reduction in
surface potential fluctuation at the SnO$_2$/perovskite interface for
\textsc{sam-7} devices relative to control, consistent with suppressed
ion accumulation at grain boundaries near the buried interface.
Time-resolved photoluminescence decay fits yield a bulk trap density
essentially unchanged between conditions ($\sim 10^{16}$~cm$^{-3}$),
indicating that the observed gains originate predominantly from
interfacial rather than bulk passivation, in agreement with the design
hypothesis motivating this dissertation.
