\section*{Professional Profile}
Agricultural scientist with 10+ years of experience translating field research into resilient, profitable crop-production systems. Expertise spans soil health, plant nutrition, regenerative practices, experimental design, and grower-facing technology transfer across irrigated and rain-fed agriculture. Led multi-site trials totaling 4,800 acres, secured \$3.4M in competitive funding, and delivered recommendations that reduced nitrogen use by up to 18\% while maintaining yield. Trusted collaborator to growers, universities, government agencies, and multidisciplinary R\&D teams.

\section*{Professional Experience}
\role{Senior Agricultural Scientist}{2021--Present}{TerraVerde Agricultural Research, Davis, CA}{Crop Systems \& Sustainability}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Direct a \$1.2M annual portfolio of 14 on-farm and research-station trials evaluating nitrogen management, biological inputs, cover crops, and deficit irrigation in tomato, almond, wheat, and maize systems.
  \item Designed variable-rate nitrogen trials across 1,600 acres; integrated soil nitrate, yield maps, and canopy imagery to cut fertilizer use 18\% with no statistically significant yield penalty.
  \item Built reproducible R workflows for trial randomization, mixed-model analysis, power calculations, and automated reporting, reducing analysis turnaround from three weeks to five days.
  \item Supervise four research associates and six seasonal technicians; standardized field protocols and audit-ready records, improving complete and valid plot data from 89\% to 98\%.
  \item Won \$1.7M in USDA-NIFA and California Department of Food and Agriculture funding as principal or co-principal investigator; manage milestones, subcontractors, and sponsor reporting.
  \item Translate results through field days, technical briefs, and one-on-one consultations reaching 450+ growers and advisers annually; 63\% of surveyed participants adopted at least one recommended practice.
\end{itemize}

\role{Research Agronomist}{2017--2021}{University of California Cooperative Extension, Fresno, CA}{Soils, Water \& Cropping Systems}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Managed 32 replicated field experiments across five counties, coordinating growers, irrigation districts, laboratories, and extension specialists from protocol approval through publication.
  \item Developed sensor-based irrigation schedules for processing tomato and alfalfa that improved water productivity 21\% and saved an average 4.1 acre-inches of applied water per season.
  \item Quantified soil-carbon and aggregate-stability responses to cover crops and reduced tillage over four seasons; findings informed regional conservation-practice guidance.
  \item Created bilingual English--Spanish training materials and delivered 26 workshops on soil sampling, nutrient budgeting, and integrated nutrient management to 900+ participants.
  \item Co-authored seven peer-reviewed papers and 18 extension publications; presented results at ASA, SSSA, and Western Nutrient Management conferences.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Selected Research Impact}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Climate resilience:} Led a five-site drought-response network combining soil-moisture sensing and satellite evapotranspiration; identified management combinations that stabilized tomato yield under 20\% less irrigation.
  \item \textbf{Nutrient stewardship:} Co-developed an open nutrient-budgeting tool used by 120 crop advisers to compare yield goals, soil-supply credits, and fertilizer scenarios.
  \item \textbf{Soil health:} Synthesized 11 years of regional trials to establish locally calibrated benchmarks for soil organic matter, potentially mineralizable nitrogen, and water-stable aggregates.
\end{itemize}
