\resumeRole{Senior Geotechnical Engineer}{May 2021 -- Present}{TerraPoint Engineering Consultants}{Houston, TX}
\begin{itemize}

  \item Designed a \$1.8M subsurface investigation for a 12-mile highway expansion, optimizing 86 borings and 34 CPT soundings through phased data review; reduced planned drilling footage by 18\% while maintaining coverage of critical strata.
  \item Developed driven-pile and drilled-shaft recommendations for six bridge crossings using axial, lateral, settlement, and downdrag analyses; value-engineering refinements reduced estimated foundation cost by \$3.2M.
  \item Evaluated stability and seepage for 7 miles of levees and detention embankments in GeoStudio, identifying staged-construction and ground-improvement measures that achieved target factors of safety under rapid-drawdown and flood conditions.

  \item Mentor five engineers and geologists, review calculations and reports, and maintain QA/QC checklists that helped the group deliver more than 95\% of submittals on schedule with no major design-related claims.
