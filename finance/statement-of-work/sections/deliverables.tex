\subsection{Acceptance and Review Process}
For each deliverable, Synapse will submit a Deliverable Acceptance Form (DAF) to Nexa.
\begin{itemize}[label=\textcolor{secondary}{\faInfoCircle}, leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Review Period:} Nexa will have five (5) business days to review the deliverable against the acceptance criteria.
  \item \textbf{Approval:} If approved, Nexa will sign and return the DAF, triggering the corresponding milestone payment.
  \item \textbf{Rejection:} If rejected, Nexa must provide written feedback detailing the gaps, and Synapse will have five (5) business days to remediate and resubmit.
\end{itemize}
