\section{Project Governance \& Assumptions}
To ensure project success, both parties will adhere to the governance rules and key technical assumptions outlined below.

\subsection{Key Technical Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}[label=\textcolor{secondary}{\faInfoCircle}, leftmargin=*]
  \item Nexa will provide Synapse with access to staging environments and legacy data samples within three (3) business days of kickoff.
  \item Regional databases are accessible via standard network protocols and do not require custom proxy bypasses.
  \item The Nimbus Infrastructure services remain operational with an uptime of at least 99.9\% during migration tests.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Change Control Procedure}
Any deviation from the scope of work outlined in this SOW must be requested in writing. A formal Change Request (CR) document detailing the scope modification, cost impact, and timeline adjustments will be prepared by Synapse. Work on the change will not commence until both parties sign the CR.
