\section{Introduction and Project Overview}
\label{sec:introduction}

Apex Procurement Services, acting as the primary procurement coordinator for \textbf{Nexa Corporation}, is issuing this Request for Quotation (RFQ) to identify qualified suppliers for high-performance server node infrastructure and aggregation networking hardware. The hardware will support the next-generation digital transaction ledgers engineered in cooperation with \textbf{Synapse Systems} and hosted at Nexa's central enterprise hosting facility.

\subsection{Objective}
The objective of this procurement is to establish a contract for the purchase and delivery of certified datacenter components. Vendors must submit comprehensive proposals verifying technical compatibility with the specs in Section~\ref{sec:specifications} and proving quality compliance in accordance with ISO standards \cite{iso9001}.
