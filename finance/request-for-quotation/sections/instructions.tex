\section{Instructions to Bidders}
\label{sec:instructions}

Sourcing compliance is critical for bid acceptance. Bidders must follow the instructions detailed below.

\subsection{Electronic Submission Portal}
Bids must be uploaded to the Meridian Sourcing Portal at \url{https://procure.apex.fictional} prior to the deadline. Emailed or hard-copy submissions will not be processed. The upload package must contain:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em, label=\arabic*.]
  \item The completed and signed Bidder Response Form (Section~\ref{sec:response}).
  \item A detailed unit-pricing spreadsheet matching the specifications table in Section~\ref{sec:specifications}.
  \item Full technical specifications and manufacturer datasheet documents.
  \item Quality management documentation confirming active certification, e.g., \cite{iso9001}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Bid Evaluation Method}
Apex Sourcing Board will score proposals based on the following weights:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, label=\textcolor{secondary}{\faCheckCircle}]
  \item \textbf{Total Proposal Cost (50\%):} The complete cost including shipping, local duties, and support SLAs.
  \item \textbf{Technical Architecture Compliance (35\%):} Verification that the equipment matches all technical details in Section~\ref{sec:specifications}.
  \item \textbf{Supply Chain Delivery Time (15\%):} Commited shipment timeframe to meet the delivery target date.
\end{itemize}
