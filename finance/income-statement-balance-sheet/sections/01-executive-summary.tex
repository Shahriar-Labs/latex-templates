\section*{Executive Summary}
Vertex Holding Group LLC has delivered exceptional financial results for the fiscal year ended October 31, 2025. Total consolidated revenue reached \textbf{\$248.5M}, representing an \textbf{18.3\%} increase year-over-year. Net Income grew by \textbf{42.5\%} to \textbf{\$34.2M}, driven by strong software-as-a-service sales growth and optimized operating margins. The group maintains a robust balance sheet with total assets of \textbf{\$234.0M} and cash reserves of \textbf{\$42.5M}.
