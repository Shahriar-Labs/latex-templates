\subsection{Budget Sensitivity Analysis}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Revenue sensitivity:} A 1\% shortfall in Group revenue against budget
        reduces Adjusted EBITDA by approximately \$8.5M, assuming a fixed-cost base.
        In a full downside scenario (GDP \bpct{0.8}), Group revenue is estimated at
        \$798.0M and Adjusted EBITDA at \$172.0M — an EBITDA margin of \bpct{21.6}.
  \item \textbf{FX sensitivity:} A 5-cent depreciation of the USD against the EUR
        reduces Group revenue by \$6.1M on an unhedged basis. The Group maintains a
        rolling 12-month FX hedge programme covering \bpct{75} of net EUR exposure.
  \item \textbf{Compensation sensitivity:} A 1\% increase in average salary above
        budget increases total compensation cost by \$3.3M. An additional
        unbudgeted tranche of variable pay at the discretionary ceiling would add
        a further \$4.8M.
\end{itemize}
