\section{Executive Summary}

Nexa Capital Group presents this Annual Corporate Budget Proposal for Fiscal Year 2025
(1 January–31 December 2025). Following a year of disciplined capital allocation and
revenue expansion in FY 2024, the Group is entering FY 2025 with a focus on
accelerating its technology infrastructure, strengthening its advisory services
franchise, and prudently managing operating costs across all four business divisions.

\subsection{Strategic Financial Objectives}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Achieve consolidated Group revenue of \textbf{\$847.6M}, representing a
        \bpct{9.3} year-on-year increase over FY 2024 actuals of \$775.4M.
  \item Maintain Adjusted EBITDA margin above \bpct{24}, targeting \$203.4M
        in Adjusted EBITDA.
  \item Reduce the Group's cost-to-income ratio from \bpct{68.1} to \bpct{65.5}
        through operating leverage and process automation.
  \item Invest \textbf{\$62.0M} in capital expenditure (CapEx), primarily directed
        at cloud migration (Division: Synapse Technology) and client-facing analytics
        platforms.
  \item Maintain a Net Debt / EBITDA ratio below \textbf{1.5$\times$}, ensuring
        investment-grade balance sheet strength.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Budget Governance}

The budget has been compiled by the Group Finance function under the direction of
Alexandra Chen (CFO) and reviewed by each divisional Managing Director. The proposal
reflects a bottom-up consolidation of departmental submissions validated against the
Group's three-year strategic plan approved by the Board in November 2024.
All figures are presented in United States Dollars (USD) unless otherwise noted.
Variance analyses compare FY 2025 budgeted amounts against FY 2024 restated actuals.
