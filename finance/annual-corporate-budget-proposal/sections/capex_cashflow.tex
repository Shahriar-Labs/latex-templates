The Group is projected to achieve a closing cash balance of \textbf{\$192.4M} at
31 December 2025, representing approximately \textbf{3.6 months} of operating
expenditure coverage — above the Board's minimum threshold of 3.0 months.
Net Debt is expected to reduce to \$106.5M (Net Debt/EBITDA: \textbf{0.52$\times$}),
well within the Group's covenant limit of 2.5$\times$.
