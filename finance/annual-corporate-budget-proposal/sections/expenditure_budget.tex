\section{Expenditure Budget and Cost Analysis}

Total Group expenditure (excluding depreciation and amortisation) is budgeted at
\textbf{\$644.2M} in FY 2025, compared with \$617.8M in FY 2024 — an increase of
\$26.4M (\bpct{4.3}). The cost-to-income ratio improves from \bpct{68.1} to
\bpct{65.5} on a like-for-like basis, demonstrating operating leverage as revenue
scales faster than the Group's cost base.

\subsection{Compensation and Benefits}

Compensation and benefits remain the largest cost line at \bpct{51.0} of total
expenditure. FY 2025 budget includes a Group-wide salary review effective 1 April 2025,
with an average increase of \bpct{3.8} in line with market benchmarks provided by
Meridian Remuneration Consultants. Headcount is planned at \textbf{2,847 FTE}, an
increase of 84 net FTEs driven primarily by 120 new hires in Synapse Technology
(engineering and data science) offset by 36 redundancies in back-office support
functions as a result of automation initiatives.

Variable compensation (bonuses) is budgeted at \bpct{18} of fixed salary cost,
contingent on Group and divisional performance against approved KPI scorecards.

\subsection{Technology Investment Programme}

The Technology and Infrastructure cost increase of \$12.8M reflects the Group's
commitment to its \textbf{Horizon 2025} modernisation programme:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{\$24.3M} cloud migration (AWS workloads, Synapse division) — Year 2
        of a three-year programme; completion expected Q3 2026.
  \item \textbf{\$18.7M} cybersecurity uplift including endpoint detection, SIEM
        upgrade, and third-party penetration testing.
  \item \textbf{\$14.0M} client analytics and reporting platform (Nimbus and
        Vertex), replacing legacy on-premises infrastructure.
  \item \textbf{\$44.4M} remaining allocated to run-the-bank infrastructure,
        SaaS licence renewals, and helpdesk operations.
\end{itemize}
