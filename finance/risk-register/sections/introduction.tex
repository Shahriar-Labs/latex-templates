\section{Executive Summary \& Context}
Project Meridian is a critical digital infrastructure transformation program undertaken by \textbf{Nexa Corporation} in partnership with \textbf{Synapse Systems Group}. The project aims to consolidate legacy financial transaction ledgers across five regional subsidiaries into a single cloud-native real-time ledger system, hosted on \textbf{Nimbus Infrastructure}. 

As this project involves migrating high-value transaction processing pipeline systems, it carries significant operational, financial, compliance, and technical risks. This Risk Register serves as the definitive tool for tracking identified risk factors, assessing their raw impact, establishing ownership, and outlining mitigation workflows.

\section{Risk Assessment Methodology}
Risk severity is determined using a standard 5$\times$5 matrix. For each risk, the probability of occurrence (Likelihood) and the severity of the consequence (Impact) are assigned a score from 1 to 5.

\subsection{Likelihood Scale}
\begin{itemize}[label=\textbullet]
  \item \textbf{1 -- Rare:} Highly improbable; less than 5\% chance of occurrence.
  \item \textbf{2 -- Unlikely:} Possible but unexpected; 5\% to 20\% chance of occurrence.
  \item \textbf{3 -- Possible:} Might occur at some point; 20\% to 50\% chance of occurrence.
  \item \textbf{4 -- Likely:} Expected to occur; 50\% to 80\% chance of occurrence.
  \item \textbf{5 -- Almost Certain:} Virtually guaranteed; greater than 80\% chance of occurrence.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Impact Scale}
\begin{itemize}[label=\textbullet]
  \item \textbf{1 -- Negligible:} No financial impact; minor inconvenience to operations.
  \item \textbf{2 -- Minor:} Financial loss under \$50,000; localized delays of less than 2 days.
  \item \textbf{3 -- Moderate:} Financial loss of \$50,000 to \$200,000; project delay of 1 to 2 weeks.
  \item \textbf{4 -- Major:} Financial loss of \$200,000 to \$1M; critical milestone missed; external audit failure.
  \item \textbf{5 -- Critical:} Financial loss exceeding \$1M; project termination; systemic regulatory fines.
\end{itemize}
