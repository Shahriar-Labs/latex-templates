\section{Risk Response Strategies \& Governance}
Project Meridian risk management follows the frameworks established in the global corporate governance guidelines \cite{iso31000, nexa2024risk}. Each identified risk must be categorized under one of the four corporate-approved risk response strategies:

\begin{description}
  \item[Avoid:] Alter the project scope, technical design, or deployment strategy to eliminate the risk entirely. This strategy is reserved for critical risks that cannot be mitigated cost-effectively.
  \item[Transfer:] Delegate the risk to a third party. For example, using vendor-managed databases on \textbf{Nimbus Infrastructure} transfers server-level uptime risks to the cloud provider under a Service Level Agreement (SLA).
  \item[Mitigate:] Implement operational, technical, or administrative controls to reduce either the Likelihood or Impact score (e.g., configuring Cloudflare Tunnels as detailed in \cite{jenkins2023enterprise}).
  \item[Accept:] Maintain the current status without additional interventions. This is only permissible for low-level residual risks (score under 5) where mitigation costs exceed potential losses.
\end{description}

\section{Escalation Workflow}
The Steering Committee requires immediate escalation of any risk that experiences an upward rating trend during weekly status reviews. The following protocol defines escalation thresholds:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Residual Risk Score 1--4 (Low):} Managed at the team level by the designated owner. Discussed in bi-weekly sprint reviews.
  \item \textbf{Residual Risk Score 5--9 (Medium):} Monitored by the project manager. Requires monthly updates to the Program Management Office (PMO).
  \item \textbf{Residual Risk Score 10--15 (High):} Requires direct approval of the Program Director. Mitigation plan status reviewed in weekly coordination meetings.
  \item \textbf{Residual Risk Score 16--25 (Critical):} Demands immediate escalation to the Executive Steering Committee. The project may be suspended until a mitigation control is verified.
\end{enumerate}
