\section{Glossary of Terms}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabular}{L{3.8cm} L{9.8cm}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{nexaNavy}
\color{white}\textbf{Term} &
\color{white}\textbf{Definition}\\
\midrule
\textbf{Fully-Diluted (FD)} &
The total share count assuming all authorised options, warrants, and convertible
instruments have been exercised or converted into common stock.\\
\altrow
\textbf{Pre-Money Valuation} &
The agreed enterprise value of the company immediately \textit{before} new investment
capital is added. Used as the denominator for pricing new shares.\\
\textbf{Post-Money Valuation} &
Pre-money valuation plus the capital raised in the current round. Equals the price per
share multiplied by the post-round fully-diluted share count.\\
\altrow
\textbf{Liquidation Preference} &
A contractual right giving preferred shareholders priority in receiving proceeds upon a
liquidity event (sale, merger, IPO). Non-participating preferred converts to common
after preference is satisfied.\\
\textbf{ESOP / Option Pool} &
A reserved block of shares set aside for issuance to employees, directors, and
consultants as equity compensation. Pool size is expressed as a percentage of
fully-diluted shares.\\
\altrow
\textbf{409A Valuation} &
An independent appraisal of a private company's common stock fair market value (FMV),
required by U.S. tax code Section 409A before issuing compensatory stock options.\\
\textbf{Anti-Dilution} &
A protection mechanism for preferred shareholders against future down-rounds. Broad-based
weighted average (BBWA) is the founder-friendly standard; full ratchet is aggressive.\\
\altrow
\textbf{Pro-Rata Rights} &
The contractual right of existing investors to participate in future financing rounds in
proportion to their current ownership, maintaining their ownership percentage.\\
\textbf{SAFE} &
Simple Agreement for Future Equity --- a convertible instrument that grants the right to
receive equity at a future priced round, subject to a valuation cap and/or discount.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\captionof{table}{Glossary of key cap table and venture finance terms}
\end{center}

\section{Dilution Calculation Methodology}

All dilution calculations in this report use the \textbf{post-money method} unless
otherwise stated:

\begin{align}
  \text{New Shares Issued} &= \frac{\text{Round Size}}{\text{Price Per Share}}\\[6pt]
  \text{Price Per Share} &= \frac{\text{Pre-Money Valuation}}{\text{Pre-Round FD Shares}}\\[6pt]
  \text{Post-Round FD Shares} &= \text{Pre-Round FD Shares} + \text{New Shares Issued}\\[6pt]
  \text{Post-Round Ownership}_i &= \frac{\text{Shares}_i}{\text{Post-Round FD Shares}}
\end{align}

For Scenario~C, the pre-money ESOP expansion is treated as follows:

\begin{align}
  \text{ESOP Top-Up Shares} &= \left(\frac{\text{Target Pool \%}}{1 - \text{Target Pool \%}} \times \text{Pre-Round FD Shares}\right) - \text{Existing Pool}\\[6pt]
  \text{Adjusted Pre-Round FD} &= \text{Pre-Round FD Shares} + \text{ESOP Top-Up Shares}
\end{align}

All figures are presented in U.S. dollars (USD). Share counts are rounded to the nearest
whole share. Percentage ownership figures are rounded to two decimal places and may not
sum to exactly 100\% due to rounding. This report does not constitute legal or tax advice;
consult qualified counsel before making financing decisions.
