\section{Options Appraisal}

Four options were assessed by the Finance Transformation Programme Board against five weighted
criteria, scored 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) by a panel of eight stakeholders across Finance,
Operations, and Technology.

\begin{center}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{3.1cm}Y@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{navy}
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{Option}} & \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Description}} \\
\midrule
A. Do nothing & Continue operating Ledgerline as-is; absorb support-cliff risk when it arrives. \\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
B. Enhance legacy & Tactically patch Ledgerline: refactor the reconciliation module, extend
   support via a third-party maintenance firm. \\
C. Migrate to Vertex Cloud (recommended) & Replace Ledgerline with the Vertex Cloud Billing
   Platform, a SaaS product already live at peer insurers. \\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
D. Outsource to BPO & Transfer billing operations, including the legacy system, to a
   third-party business process outsourcer. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Option C scores highest on every criterion except speed to value, where the incremental
patch (Option B) has a shorter path to deployment but resolves none of the underlying
platform risk. The Programme Board's recommendation, endorsed unanimously, is Option C.
