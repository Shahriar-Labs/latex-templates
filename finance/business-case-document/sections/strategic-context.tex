\section{Strategic Context}

\subsection{The problem}
Ledgerline processes 340,000 premium invoices a year across Nexa Group's commercial property,
casualty, marine, and specialty lines. Three structural issues make the status quo untenable:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Vendor support cliff.} The application server underneath Ledgerline reaches
        end-of-extended-support on 30 June 2027. No further security patches will be issued
        after that date.
  \item \textbf{Key-person concentration.} The batch reconciliation logic -- roughly 40,000
        lines of undocumented stored procedures -- is understood in full by two engineers, one
        of whom is eligible for retirement in 2027.
  \item \textbf{Rising error cost.} Manual reconciliation between Ledgerline and the general
        ledger produced 1,140 billing corrections in FY2025, three of which required
        regulatory disclosure under the Group's premium-handling obligations
        \cite{osei2025auditfindings}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Strategic alignment}
Project Cascade advances two commitments in Nexa Group's FY2026--FY2028 strategy
\cite{nexa2026strategy}: the
\emph{Operational Resilience} pillar (retiring single-points-of-failure in regulated financial
processes) and the \emph{Digital Finance} pillar (moving core finance systems to managed cloud
platforms with a five-year technology-refresh cadence). It also responds directly to Finding
4.2 of the FY2025 internal audit, which rated legacy billing infrastructure risk as
\textquotedblleft elevated and worsening\textquotedblright\ \cite{osei2025auditfindings}.

\subsection{Scope}
In scope: premium invoicing, billing adjustments, dunning and collections workflow, and the
general-ledger interface for the four in-scope business lines. Out of scope: claims payment
processing (owned by the Claims Systems programme) and the commission-management module, which
is being retired independently in Q3 2026.

\begin{tcolorbox}[notebox, title={\faExclamationTriangle\ Do-nothing is not neutral}]
Retaining Ledgerline past June 2027 without extended support would require either an
uninsurable security posture or an emergency single-source support contract that peer
benchmarking puts at 2.4$\times$ the cost of a planned migration \cite{castellan2025itspend}.
\end{tcolorbox}
