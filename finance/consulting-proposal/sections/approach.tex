\section{Our Approach}

We propose a four-phase engagement running 16 weeks from kickoff to
handover. Each phase closes with a formal checkpoint so that Vertex retains
control over scope and can redirect effort before the next phase begins. The
phased, checkpoint-driven structure follows standard change-management
practice for operational redesigns \cite{kotter2012leading}, which finds that
transformations lose momentum when staff are asked to commit to the full
scope before seeing evidence from an early, contained phase.

\phaseheader{Phase 1 --- Diagnose}{Weeks 1--3}
\vspace{0.3cm}

We instrument the current state before proposing anything. This phase
combines interviews, transaction-log analysis, and a full trace of the
purchase-order exception workflow.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Extract twelve months of point-of-sale and replenishment data from the
    warehouse-management system for the Council Bluffs distribution center and
    a representative sample of 24 stores across urban, suburban, and rural
    formats.
  \item Shadow planning staff through two full weekly cycles to catalogue
    every manual exception and its root cause.
  \item Deliver a diagnostic memo quantifying the forecast-lag gap by category
    and store cluster, validated jointly with your VP of Supply Chain.
  \end{itemize}

\phaseheader{Phase 2 --- Design}{Weeks 4--7}
\vspace{0.3cm}

Using the Phase 1 findings, we design a reconciled forecasting cadence and the
dashboard architecture that will surface it.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Redesign the store-to-distribution-center forecast cycle to a shared
    72-hour cadence, replacing the current two independent weekly cycles.
  \item Define exception-handling rules that auto-resolve the categories of
    purchase orders responsible for 70\% of current manual volume.
  \item Produce a dashboard specification covering in-stock rate,
    transportation cost, and labour utilization, reviewed with your
    analytics team for build feasibility on existing tooling.
  \end{itemize}

\phaseheader{Phase 3 --- Pilot}{Weeks 8--12}
\vspace{0.3cm}

We test the redesigned cadence and dashboard against live operations in a
contained scope before asking Vertex to commit company-wide.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Run the new forecast cadence for the 24-store sample cluster alongside
    the legacy process, in parallel, for four full weeks.
  \item Stand up the dashboard on Vertex's existing business-intelligence
    platform and validate it against Phase 1 baseline figures.
  \item Deliver a pilot results report with a go/no-go recommendation and any
    required design adjustments before wider rollout.
  \end{itemize}

\phaseheader{Phase 4 --- Scale \& Handover}{Weeks 13--16}
\vspace{0.3cm}

The final phase extends the validated process network-wide and transfers
ownership to your team.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Roll the reconciled cadence and exception rules out to the remaining
    118 stores in two waves, each followed by a one-week stabilization window.
  \item Train the planning team and dashboard administrators through
    structured working sessions, with recorded materials for future onboarding.
  \item Deliver a handover package: process documentation, dashboard
    administration guide, and a 90-day post-engagement support window for
    questions that arise after we step back.
  \end{itemize}
