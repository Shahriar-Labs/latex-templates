\subsection{2.2 Workstream Summary \& Risk Analysis}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Transfer Pricing (High Risk):} The absence of formal transfer pricing documentation between the US parent company and the UK R\&D subsidiary represents a high compliance risk under HMRC/IRS guidelines.
  \item \textbf{Revenue Recognition (Medium Risk):} Transitioning from standard annual upfront contracts to quarterly billing models requires validation of deferred revenue schedules to ensure US GAAP compliance.
  \item \textbf{Quality of Earnings (Low Risk):} Capitalized development costs are in line with industry standards, and monthly recurring revenue (MRR) represents 92\% of total transaction volume.
\end{itemize}
