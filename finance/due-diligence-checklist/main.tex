\section*{Document Overview}
This due diligence checklist outlines the verification tasks, data room requirements, and risk assessment stages for the proposed acquisition of \textbf{Nexa Technologies Inc.} by \textbf{Vertex Capital Partners} (Project Meridian). Diligence is split into legal, financial, and technical workstreams. The statuses are continuously updated by the respective workstream leads as files are submitted and verified in the Virtual Data Room (VDR).
