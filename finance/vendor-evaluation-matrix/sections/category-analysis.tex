\subsection*{Reading the Categories}

\vendorname{Synapse Cloud}{synapseColor} leads on Technical Architecture and Functional Fit,
the two heaviest categories, which drives its overall lead despite mid-pack Commercial Terms.
\vendorname{Vertex Solutions}{vertexColor} inverts that pattern \textemdash\ the cheapest
package by a wide margin, but weakest Functional Fit, reflecting a generalist platform without
manufacturing-specific depth. \vendorname{Apex Technologies}{apexColor} trails in every
category except raw functional coverage, and its Implementation \& Support score (46.0) is the
lowest single result on the matrix, driven by a three-person delivery team with no prior
discrete-manufacturing engagement of Nimbus's scale.
