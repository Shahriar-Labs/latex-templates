\section{SWOT: Nexa in This Field}

\subsection{Strengths and weaknesses}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[swotS, title={\faThumbsUp\ Strengths}]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Fastest median implementation among platforms with real multi-currency support (6 weeks
    versus Vertex's 4--6 months).
  \item Single unified UX for card issuing and expense -- the two-app problem that Apex and
    Synapse both still have.
  \item Highest product NPS in the mid-market segment (61, versus a segment average of 34).
  \item API-first architecture makes custom integrations materially cheaper to build than on
    Vertex's older platform.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[swotW, title={\faThumbsDown\ Weaknesses}]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item No procurement or PO workflow, which now appears in a majority of enterprise RFPs.
  \item Multi-entity consolidation caps out at three entities before requiring manual workarounds.
  \item No EU VAT reclaim capability, closing off most continental European deals outright.
  \item Smaller systems-integrator and accounting-firm partner network than Vertex or Meridian.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}
\par
\vspace{6pt}

\subsection{Opportunities and threats}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[swotO, title={\faArrowUp\ Opportunities}]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Demand for AI receipt-matching is still early outside Nimbus's install base --
    first-mover room remains for a fast follower with strong accuracy.
  \item Vertex renewal cycles are producing visible dissatisfaction with implementation cost;
    displacement deals are winnable if multi-entity gaps close first.
  \item A card-issuing partnership could close the Apex gap in under two quarters, versus
    building issuing infrastructure natively.
  \item Meridian's VAT reclaim acquisition is not yet integrated -- a 6--9 month window exists
    before it becomes a seamless part of their platform.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{tcolorbox}[swotT, title={\faExclamationTriangle\ Threats}]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Synapse is undercutting on price specifically in the 200--500 employee band where Nexa
    currently has the strongest win rate.
  \item Meridian is bundling procurement and expense at incumbent renewal, raising switching
    cost for accounts we would otherwise target.
  \item Regulatory pressure on card interchange economics could compress the float-based
    revenue model Apex and Nexa both rely on.
  \item A horizontal fintech platform entering spend management with an existing SMB card base
    would compress pricing across the whole mid-market segment.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}
