\section{2\quad Actuarial Assumptions}
The following principal economic and demographic assumptions were adopted by the Board on
the advice of the actuary. The discount rate reflects the long-term expected return on plan
assets, net of investment expenses.

\begin{table}[ht]\centering
\caption{Principal actuarial assumptions.}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}X R@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Assumption} & \textbf{Basis}\\
\midrule
Discount rate (net of expenses) & 6.75\%\\
General wage inflation          & 2.75\%\\
Price inflation (CPI)           & 2.30\%\\
Cost-of-living adjustment       & 1.80\%\\
Mortality                       & Pub-2010, MP-2021 projection\\
Assumed retirement age          & 62 (weighted)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The normal cost under the Entry Age Normal method is the level percentage of pay that, if
contributed over a member's career, funds the projected benefit:
\begin{equation}
\text{NC}_x = \frac{\text{PVFB}_{e}}{\ddot{a}^{(\text{pay})}_{e}}\times \text{pay}_x,
\end{equation}
where $\text{PVFB}_e$ is the present value of future benefits at entry age $e$ and
$\ddot{a}^{(\text{pay})}_{e}$ the present value of a career pay annuity.
