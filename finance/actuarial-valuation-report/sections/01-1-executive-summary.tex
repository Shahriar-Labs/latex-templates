\section{1\quad Executive Summary}
This report presents the results of the annual actuarial valuation of the Meridian County
Employees' Retirement Plan as of 31 December 2025. The valuation measures the plan's funded
status, determines the actuarially determined contribution (ADC) for the fiscal year, and
discloses the assumptions and methods used. The plan's funded ratio improved to
\textbf{84.6\%}, driven by investment returns above the assumed rate and stable demographic
experience.
