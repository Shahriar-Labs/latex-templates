\section{Proposed Capital Structure}
The JV will be capitalized with a 60\%/40\% split between senior construction debt and equity.
Nexa will serve as Managing Member and co-invest 10\% of JV equity alongside the incoming
limited partner, who will hold a 90\% preferred equity position with pro-rata governance rights
consistent with the operating agreement's major-decision list.
