\section{Risk Factors}
Prospective investors should consider the following risks prior to committing capital, among
others discussed more fully in the definitive operating agreement and private placement
memorandum.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Construction cost escalation.} Hard costs are fixed under a guaranteed maximum
    price contract with a licensed general contractor, but material and labor pricing outside
    the contract scope remain subject to volatility.
  \item \textbf{Lease-up pace and rent growth.} Projections assume absorption of 12--15 units
    per month at the rents shown in Table~\ref{tab:unitmix}; slower absorption or rent
    concessions would compress projected returns.
  \item \textbf{Interest rate exposure.} The senior construction loan carries a floating rate
    indexed to the reference benchmark plus a fixed spread; the Sponsor intends to purchase a
    rate cap at closing.
  \item \textbf{Exit capitalization rate.} Returns in Table~\ref{tab:returns} assume a
    disposition capitalization rate of 5.25\% in the base case; a 50 basis point widening at
    exit would reduce the base-case LP IRR by approximately 210 basis points.
  \item \textbf{Entitlement and permitting timing.} While rezoning has been approved, final
    building permits remain subject to municipal review and could delay the start of
    construction.
\end{itemize}
