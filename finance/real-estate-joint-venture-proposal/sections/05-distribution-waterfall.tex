\section{Distribution Waterfall}
Cash available for distribution will be distributed in the following order of priority. All
returns are computed on a cumulative, compounded annual basis measured against contributed LP
capital.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Distribution Waterfall Structure}
\label{tab:waterfall}
\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{c X r}
\toprule
\rowcolor{jvnavy}
\textbf{\color{white}Tier} & \textbf{\color{white}Description} & \textbf{\color{white}LP / GP Split} \\
\midrule
1 & Return of contributed capital, pro rata & 90\% / 10\% \\
\rowcolor{jvcream}
2 & Preferred return until LP achieves an 8.0\% cumulative annual return & 90\% / 10\% \\
3 & GP catch-up until GP has received 20\% of profit distributed above Tier 1 & 20\% / 80\% \\
\rowcolor{jvcream}
4 & Remaining cash flow until LP achieves a 15.0\% IRR & 80\% / 20\% \\
5 & Remaining cash flow until LP achieves an 18.0\% IRR & 70\% / 30\% \\
\rowcolor{jvcream}
6 & Residual cash flow thereafter & 60\% / 40\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
