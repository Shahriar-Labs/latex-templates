\subsection{Recommendation}

We initiate coverage of Vertex Dynamics with a \textbf{BUY} rating and a \textbf{\$44.00} price target, representing 23.1\% upside from the \$35.75 current price. Under our fictional rating framework, BUY denotes expected total return of 15\% or more over the following 12 months; HOLD denotes expected total return between 0\% and 15\%; SELL denotes expected total return below 0\%.
