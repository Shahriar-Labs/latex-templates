\section{Executive Summary}

Apex Digital Opportunities Fund LP (the ``Fund'') is a multi-strategy digital asset vehicle managed by Meridian Capital Partners, combining core spot exposure to large-capitalization digital assets with tactical allocations to layer-2 infrastructure tokens and yield-bearing stablecoin reserves. This report presents the Fund's performance for the month of July 2026 and the trailing twelve-month period ended July 31, 2026, together with a review of risk-adjusted metrics, portfolio composition, and the Investment Committee's positioning for the coming quarter.

\begin{thesisbox}
\textbf{Month in review.} The Fund posted a net return of \textbf{+4.8\%} in July, outperforming the Nexa Composite Digital Asset Index (+3.1\%) by 170 basis points and extending the Fund's streak of positive monthly alpha to seven consecutive months. Gains were broad-based across the core book, with the largest single contribution coming from the Fund's layer-2 scaling infrastructure basket, which benefited from elevated on-chain activity ahead of a widely anticipated network upgrade window. The Investment Committee trimmed stablecoin yield reserves by 130 basis points of NAV during the month, redeploying the proceeds into spot exposure as realized volatility across the core book declined to its lowest level since March.
\end{thesisbox}
