\section{Key Risks}

\small
\begin{riskitem}
\item \textbf{Digital asset price volatility.} Digital assets have historically exhibited substantially higher price volatility than traditional asset classes; the Fund's annualized volatility of 42.3\% over the trailing twelve months, while below that of the benchmark, remains well above typical levels for traditional multi-asset portfolios.
\item \textbf{Regulatory uncertainty.} The regulatory treatment of digital assets continues to evolve across the jurisdictions in which the Fund and its counterparties operate; adverse regulatory developments could restrict the Fund's ability to trade, custody, or value certain positions.
\item \textbf{Custody and counterparty risk.} Notwithstanding the custody arrangements described in Section~5.2, digital assets held in custody or on exchange venues remain subject to operational, cybersecurity, and counterparty risks that differ materially from those associated with traditional securities custody.
\item \textbf{Concentration in layer-2 infrastructure.} The Fund's tactical overweight to layer-2 scaling infrastructure, while a positive contributor in July, represents a concentrated thematic exposure that could underperform materially if network upgrade activity fails to sustain elevated on-chain usage.
\item \textbf{Liquidity and redemption risk.} Certain tactical-sleeve positions may experience reduced market liquidity during periods of market stress, which could affect the Fund's ability to meet redemption requests within the stated notice period at prices reflective of the most recent net asset value.
\item \textbf{Benchmark limitations.} The Nexa Composite Digital Asset Index is provided for comparative purposes only; its composition and weighting methodology differ from the Fund's investment universe, and past relative performance is not indicative of future results.
\item \textbf{Model and estimation risk.} Risk-adjusted metrics presented in Section~4, including Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, and parametric value at risk, rely on historical return distributions that may not capture tail risk accurately during unprecedented market conditions.
\end{riskitem}
