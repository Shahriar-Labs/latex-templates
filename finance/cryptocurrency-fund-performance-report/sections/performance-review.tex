\subsection{Return Attribution}

Sleeve-level attribution for July shows the core large-capitalization book contributing +2.9\,pp of the Fund's +4.8\% net return, the tactical infrastructure sleeve contributing +1.6\,pp, and the liquidity sleeve contributing +0.3\,pp before fees. Management and performance fees together reduced gross performance by approximately 0.6\,pp during the month, consistent with the Fund's fee schedule and July's above-hurdle performance.
