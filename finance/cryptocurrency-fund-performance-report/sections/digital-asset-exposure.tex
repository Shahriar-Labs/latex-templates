\subsection{Custody and Counterparty Risk}

Approximately 91\% of Fund assets are held in cold, multi-signature custody arrangements administered by Nimbus Digital Custody Solutions, with the remainder held in hot-wallet and exchange-collateral form to support active trading and the monthly redemption cycle. No single execution counterparty accounted for more than 34\% of trading volume during the reporting month, and the Fund maintains active trading relationships with three independent liquidity venues in addition to Synapse Markets to mitigate concentration risk in execution and settlement.
