\section{Risk-Adjusted Performance}

Risk-adjusted metrics are calculated on a trailing twelve-month basis using monthly net returns, in line with standard industry practice for reporting the Sharpe and Sortino ratios \citep{sharpe1966,sortino1994}. All figures are net of fees. The Fund's benchmark comparison uses the Nexa Composite Digital Asset Index, a market-capitalization-weighted basket of liquid digital assets maintained by the index sponsor and used throughout this report for relative performance purposes.

The combination of a downside capture ratio of 61\% against an upside capture ratio of 88\% is the primary driver of the Fund's Sharpe ratio advantage: the Fund has captured a meaningful majority of benchmark upside while participating in materially less of its drawdowns, consistent with the liquidity sleeve's role as a shock absorber during periods of elevated realized volatility \citep{ang2014}. Beta of 0.71 indicates that roughly seven-tenths of the Fund's return variance is explained by co-movement with the broader digital asset market, with the balance attributable to sleeve rotation and security selection within the tactical and core books.

\begin{thesisbox}
\textbf{Drawdown discipline.} The Fund's maximum trailing 24-month drawdown of -28.4\% compares to -46.7\% for the benchmark over the same window. The Investment Committee attributes this gap principally to the liquidity sleeve's stablecoin yield reserves, which were sized at 12.4\% of NAV entering the most recent drawdown episode and funded opportunistic rebalancing into core-sleeve assets near the trough.
\end{thesisbox}

Value at risk is estimated parametrically from the trailing 24-month return distribution and should be interpreted as an indicative loss threshold under normal market conditions rather than a bound on tail outcomes; digital asset markets have historically exhibited fatter tails than the normal distribution implies, and realized losses in stressed periods have exceeded parametric VaR estimates for both the Fund and the benchmark.
