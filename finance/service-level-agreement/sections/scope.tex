\section{Purpose \& Scope}
\label{sec:scope}

This Service Level Agreement (``SLA'') defines the performance standards, availability
commitments, support obligations, and remedies that govern the delivery of the
\textbf{Nexa Compute Platform} (the ``Service'') by Nexa Cloud Systems, Inc.\ (``Nexa'' or
the ``Provider'') to Synapse Analytics Corporation (``Synapse'' or the ``Customer'') under
the Master Cloud Services Agreement referenced on the cover page. Where this SLA conflicts
with the Master Agreement on a matter of service performance, this SLA governs; on all
other matters, the Master Agreement governs.

This SLA applies to the Production Tier workloads deployed in the \texttt{us-east-1} and
\texttt{eu-west-1} regions under Customer account \texttt{SYN-88213}. Non-production
environments (development, staging, and sandbox) are covered by the best-effort terms in
Schedule C of the Master Agreement and are excluded from the credit remedies in
Section~\ref{sec:credits}.

\begin{definebox}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Availability} --- the percentage of time during a Measurement Window in
    which the Service responds to synthetic health checks within the thresholds defined
    in Section~\ref{sec:availability}.
  \item \textbf{Measurement Window} --- a calendar month, from 00:00 UTC on the first day
    to 23:59 UTC on the last day.
  \item \textbf{Scheduled Maintenance} --- planned Service interruptions announced at
    least seventy-two (72) hours in advance via the Nexa Status Page and customer email.
  \item \textbf{Incident} --- an unplanned event that degrades or interrupts the Service
    and is not attributable to an Excluded Cause under Section~\ref{sec:availability}.
  \item \textbf{Service Credit} --- a percentage reduction applied to the Customer's
    monthly Service fees as remedy for a missed Availability Target.
  \item \textbf{Severity Level} --- the classification (P1--P4) assigned to a reported
    Incident, determining response and resolution targets under Section~\ref{sec:support}.
\end{itemize}
\end{definebox}
