\section{Availability Targets}
\label{sec:availability}

Nexa commits to the Monthly Uptime Percentages set out in Table~\ref{tab:availability} for
each Service component listed in Table~\ref{tab:services}. Monthly Uptime Percentage is
calculated as:

\begin{center}
\begin{tcolorbox}[
  enhanced, colback=PaleSlate, colframe=SteelBlue,
  boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4pt, width=0.9\linewidth,
  top=8pt, bottom=8pt, left=12pt, right=12pt
]
\centering
\[
  \text{Monthly Uptime \%} \;=\; \left(1 - \frac{\text{Total Downtime Minutes}}{\text{Total Minutes in Month}}\right) \times 100
\]
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{center}

\subsection{Exclusions}

The following are Excluded Causes and are removed from Downtime Minutes before Monthly
Uptime Percentage is calculated:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Scheduled Maintenance performed in accordance with the notice period defined in
    Section~\ref{sec:scope}.
  \item Force majeure events, including regional utility outages, natural disasters, and
    upstream internet backbone failures outside Nexa's reasonable control.
  \item Degradation caused by the Customer's applications, code, configuration, or
    misuse of the Service (including exceeding provisioned capacity without prior
    scaling request).
  \item Suspension of the Service for non-payment or breach of the Acceptable Use Policy.
  \item Unavailability of third-party services not operated by Nexa, including
    Customer-managed DNS providers and Customer's own network links.
\end{itemize}

\warnbox{%
  \faExclamationTriangle\enspace\textbf{Note:} Downtime is measured independently per
  component. A component that misses its target does not affect the Availability
  calculation of any other component, and Service Credits (Section~\ref{sec:credits}) are
  calculated and capped separately for each component.
}

\subsection{Reporting}

Nexa publishes real-time and historical Availability data on the Nexa Status Page
(\url{https://status.nexacloud.example}) and provides Synapse with a signed monthly
Availability Report within five (5) business days of each Measurement Window's close via
the Nexa Console. The Customer may dispute a reported figure in writing within fifteen
(15) calendar days of receipt; disputes are resolved under the escalation procedure in
Section~\ref{sec:support}.
