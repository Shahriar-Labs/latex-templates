\section{Project Verdict and Recommendations}
\label{sec:verdict}

The findings of this feasibility study indicate that Project Meridian is technically, economically, and operationally viable. The project addresses a critical vulnerability in Nexa's transaction processing and delivers a high rate of return.

\vspace{0.8em}
\textbf{Core Commitments:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item Funding of \textbf{\$350,000} is approved for immediate release to initiate Phase 1 procurement.
  \item Project integration teams must establish contact with the Synapse systems engineering group within 15 days.
  \item Progress reports will be delivered bi-weekly to the Nexa Steering Committee.
\end{itemize}
