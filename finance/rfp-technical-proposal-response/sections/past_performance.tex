\section{Past Performance}
\label{sec:past_performance}

Nexa and Synapse have a demonstrated history of delivering complex financial integrations and ledger systems. This section outlines two recent projects where the consortium successfully deployed technology matching the requirements of Vertex's RFP.

\begin{tcolorbox}[proposalbox, title=Case Study 1: Nimbus Logistics Systems]
  \textbf{Project Name:} Transaction Settlement Core (Project Apex) \\
  \textbf{Client:} Nimbus Logistics Group \textperiodcentered\ \textbf{Date:} October 2025 \\
  \textbf{Technology Stack:} Nexa-Ledger Core, Apache Kafka, PostgreSQL Clustered DB

  \medskip
  {\color{border}\hrule height 0.5pt}
  \medskip

  \textbf{Scope of Work:} \\
  Nexa designed and deployed a high-capacity transactional accounting database handling clearing and settlement of invoice records. The core ledger database stores more than 12 million transactions per day.
  
  \textbf{Results \& Metrics:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Sustained peak performance of 7,200 writes per second.
    \item Achieved a zero-downtime record over the first 10 months of operations.
    \item Provided seamless automated reporting to the client's internal compliance department.
  \end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[proposalbox, title=Case Study 2: Meridian Capital Group]
  \textbf{Project Name:} Real-Time Payment Clearing Hub \\
  \textbf{Client:} Meridian Capital Ltd. \textperiodcentered\ \textbf{Date:} March 2026 \\
  \textbf{Technology Stack:} Synapse Integration Hub, REST and WebSockets APIs, Redis Cache

  \medskip
  {\color{border}\hrule height 0.5pt}
  \medskip

  \textbf{Scope of Work:} \\
  Synapse Systems Group developed a payment router bridging traditional core banking ledgers with swift electronic processing services, parsing incoming payloads into structured database records in under 12 milliseconds.

  \textbf{Results \& Metrics:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Reduced overall system message-processing latency by 45\%.
    \item Automated 99.8\% of reconciliation activities, drastically cutting administrative labor.
    \item Completed validation audits for SOC2 Type II compliance with zero exceptions.
  \end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
