\begin{minipage}[t]{0.49\textwidth}
  \begin{outlinebox}[title={\textbf{Collateral package}},coltitle=navy]
    \begin{itemize}
      \item First-priority lien on substantially all domestic assets.
      \item Excludes equity in foreign subsidiaries above agreed tax limits, intent-to-use trademarks, and immaterial deposit accounts.
      \item IP subject to a negative pledge at closing; springing lien only after an uncured event of default.
      \item Deposit-account control agreements delivered within 30 days after closing.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{outlinebox}
\end{minipage}
