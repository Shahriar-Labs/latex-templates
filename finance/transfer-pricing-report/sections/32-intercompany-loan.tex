\section{Intercompany loan}
The loan was analyzed at its 1 April 2025 origination date. The assumed standalone
borrower credit assessment, after considering implicit group support, was BB+.
Market observations for unsecured five-year BB/BB+ corporate debt in \Currency,
combined with a consistent floating benchmark, produced an arm's length range of
5.90\%--6.80\%. The contractual 6.35\% rate is within the range. The borrower was
also projected to retain interest coverage above 3.0 times, supporting the
commercial rationality of the borrowing.
