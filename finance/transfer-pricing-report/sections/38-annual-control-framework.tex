\section{Annual control framework}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Transaction capture.} Finance identifies related parties and
    maps every related-party account to an agreement and transaction category.
  \item \textbf{Conduct confirmation.} Business owners confirm that actual
    responsibilities and risk decisions remain consistent with the agreements.
  \item \textbf{Quarterly monitoring.} Finance calculates the relevant PLI and
    forecasts the full-year outcome.
  \item \textbf{Benchmark maintenance.} Group Tax refreshes comparable financial
    data annually and the search strategy at least every three years, or earlier
    if the business changes materially.
  \item \textbf{Year-end review.} Tax, finance, customs, and legal teams review
    any proposed adjustment before invoicing and booking.
  \item \textbf{Documentation.} The local file, master file, agreements,
    calculations, invoices, and evidence of benefits are retained together.
\end{enumerate}
