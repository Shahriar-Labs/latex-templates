\section{Key observations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The Company is characterized as a routine distributor for its resale
    activity and as a limited-risk contract manufacturer for the production
    activity described in this report.
  \item Strategic intellectual property, group financing, and global market
    strategy are controlled outside Exampleland. The Company owns routine local
    operating assets and maintains local customer relationships.
  \item The tested-party operating margin is \(4.6\%\), while the contract
    manufacturing mark-up on total costs is \(7.0\%\).
  \item The service charge is supported by a benefits test, allocation keys,
    and exclusion of shareholder and duplicative activities.
  \item Royalty and loan pricing are supported by transaction-specific
    comparable uncontrolled price analyses.
\end{itemize}
