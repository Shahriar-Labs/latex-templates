\section{The Company}
The Company was incorporated in Exampleland in 2012 and is wholly owned by
Example Holdings plc. Its principal place of business is Example City. It
imports and distributes finished consumer products in Exampleland and operates
a factory that manufactures a limited product range under instructions from
Example IP S.A. At year-end, the Company employed 214 people.
