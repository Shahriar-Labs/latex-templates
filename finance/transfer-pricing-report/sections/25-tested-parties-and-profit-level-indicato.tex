\section{Tested parties and profit-level indicators}
The Company is the tested party for distribution and manufacturing because its
activities are less complex and comparable data are available. The distribution
PLI is operating profit divided by net sales. The manufacturing PLI is operating
profit from the controlled service divided by relevant operating costs.

\begin{align}
  \text{Operating margin} &= \frac{\text{Operating profit}}{\text{Net sales}},\\
  \text{Full-cost mark-up} &= \frac{\text{Operating profit}}{\text{Operating costs}}.
\end{align}

Pass-through costs that do not reflect value-adding functions are excluded only
where supported by the facts. Financing items, income tax, and exceptional
non-operating items are excluded consistently from tested and comparable results.
