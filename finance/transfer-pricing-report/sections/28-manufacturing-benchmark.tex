\section{Manufacturing benchmark}
The assumed search identified independent contract manufacturers that use
routine production assets and do not own unique product intangibles. The
three-year weighted-average full-cost mark-up produced a 4.5\% lower quartile,
6.8\% median, and 9.2\% upper quartile. The interquartile observations were 4.5\%,
5.2\%, 6.1\%, 7.4\%, 8.3\%, and 9.2\%.
