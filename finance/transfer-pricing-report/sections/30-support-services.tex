\section{Support services}
The service provider's direct and indirect costs were allocated to recipients
using drivers aligned with expected benefit. Shareholder costs, duplicative
services, and incidental benefits were excluded. The Company's allocated cost
base was \Currency\ \num{801887}; applying a 6\% mark-up produces a charge of
\Currency\ \num{850000} after rounding. The assumed external benchmark range of
4.0\%--8.0\% supports the mark-up.
