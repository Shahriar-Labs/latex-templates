\section{Scope and reconciliation}
The population of related-party transactions was obtained from the Company's
related-party ledger, matched to counterparty confirmations, and reconciled to
the notes to the audited financial statements. Amounts are stated exclusive of
indirect tax. The scope includes all material cross-border controlled
transactions for FY2025.
