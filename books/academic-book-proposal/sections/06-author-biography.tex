\section{Author Biography}

\textbf{Eleanor M.\ Hartwell} is Associate Professor of Philosophy at Stanford University, where she holds the Weinberg Family Chair in Philosophy of Science. She received her B.A.\ from Oxford University (Keble College) and her Ph.D.\ from Princeton University (2011). Her first book, \textit{Credence and Commitment} (Cambridge University Press, 2018), received the American Philosophical Association's Book Prize in Epistemology. She has published in \textit{Philosophy of Science}, \textit{The British Journal for the Philosophy of Science}, \textit{Synthese}, and \textit{Risk Analysis}. She has advised the European Commission's Joint Research Centre on uncertainty standards and is a current fellow of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences.

She is represented by Claire Dupont at the Glenmore Agency, London.
