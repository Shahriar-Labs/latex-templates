Coverage          & Manufacturing defects, component failures under normal use \\
Exclusions        & Physical damage, liquid submersion beyond IPX5, unauthorised repair \\
Claim Process     & Register at \href{https://warranty.soundwaveaudio.com}{\texttt{warranty.soundwaveaudio.com}} within 30 days of purchase \\
