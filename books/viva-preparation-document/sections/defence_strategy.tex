\section{Summary of Self-Criticism}
While the experimental results validate our core claims, future iterations should focus on:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Decentralized Communication:} Currently, the swarm relies on localized sensing. Integrating decentralized ad-hoc communication networks could allow cooperative path planning.
  \item \textbf{Heterogeneous Swarms:} Testing the compatibility of \sys{SynapseNet} across quadcopters with differing payload capacities and thrust-to-weight ratios.
\end{itemize}
