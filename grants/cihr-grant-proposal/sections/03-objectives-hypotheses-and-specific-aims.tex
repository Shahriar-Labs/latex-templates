  \textbf{Central Hypothesis:} In EOPD, a genetically primed microglial state (EO-MG1) sustains chronic NF-$\kappa$B--driven neuroinflammation, accelerating dopaminergic cell death through IL-6/STAT3 trans-signalling and complement-mediated synapse elimination; pharmacological modulation of this pathway will preserve nigral integrity in vivo.
\end{tcolorbox}
