\subsection{Justification of Resources}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Personnel:} Salaries cover one postdoctoral researcher dedicated to tissue imaging (Strand A) and one technician managing the cell cultures and microfluidics (Strand B).
    \item \textbf{Consumables:} MERFISH reagents and high-throughput sequencing kits represent the primary cost driver.
    \item \textbf{Equipment:} Year 1 includes a custom microfluidic perfusion controller and high-resolution CMOS camera upgrades.
    \item \textbf{Public Engagement:} Funds are allocated for community workshops and interactive science exhibitions in partnership with the Meridian Science Center.
\end{itemize}
