\subsection{Work Plan and Resources}
\label{sec:workplan}

The work plan is structured into five Work Packages (WPs) aligning research, development, validation, and project management tasks:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\lbrack WP\arabic*\rbrack]
    \item WP1: Coordination and Ethics (Synapse)
    \item WP2: Privacy-Preserving Cryptographic Core (Vertex)
    \item WP3: Decentralized Architecture and Orchestration (Nexa)
    \item WP4: Pilot Integration and Demonstration (Nimbus)
    \item WP5: Dissemination, Exploitation and Exploitation Strategy (Meridian)
\end{enumerate}
