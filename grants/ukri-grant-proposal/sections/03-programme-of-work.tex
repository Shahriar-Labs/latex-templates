\subsection{WP1 -- Scaffold Design and Characterisation (Months 1--18)}

\subsubsection{Objectives}
Synthesise and fully characterise NeuraLattice hydrogel matrices; validate mechanical,
optical, and biochemical properties against predefined design criteria.

\subsubsection{Key Activities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Synthesis of PEG--heparin interpenetrating networks at three crosslink densities
    (0.5, 2, and 5~kPa target moduli) using Michael-addition thiol-ene chemistry.
  \item Functionalisation with laminin-derived IKVAV and YIGSR peptide sequences via
    maleimide coupling; quantification by BCA and XPS.
  \item LOVTRAP-BDNF domain expression in HEK293T cells; LOV2 photocycle
    characterisation by circular dichroism and SPR.
  \item Electrospinning of polypyrrole-cellulose composite fibres; conductivity measured
    by four-probe resistivity at 37\textsuperscript{o}C.
  \item Rheological profiling (oscillatory shear, 0.1--100 rad/s) to confirm injectability
    and gelation kinetics (\(<\)90~s at 37\textsuperscript{o}C).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Deliverables}
D1.1 Optimised hydrogel synthesis protocol (M6); D1.2 Characterisation data package for
Synapse Biomedical regulatory dossier (M12); D1.3 Provisional patent application
(M18).

\subsection{WP2 -- In Vitro Neural Integration (Months 6--24)}

\subsubsection{Objectives}
Establish that NeuraLattice scaffolds promote directed neurite outgrowth, reduce CSPG
inhibition, and enhance synaptogenesis in primary neuronal and co-culture models.

\subsubsection{Key Activities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Rat embryonic DRG isolation and seeding onto scaffolds at three moduli;
    neurite length quantified by automated ImageJ macro at 24, 72, and 168~h.
  \item Optogenetic BDNF release (5~mW/cm\textsuperscript{2}, 488~nm, 10~ms pulses)
    tested against slow-release PLGA microsphere controls and vehicle.
  \item Live calcium imaging (GCaMP6f lentiviral transduction) to assess network
    activity under electrical stimulation (50~mV/cm, 20~Hz biphasic pulses).
  \item Transcriptomic profiling (bulk RNA-seq, $n = 4$ per condition) to identify
    mechanotransductive gene signatures (YAP/TAZ, Piezo1).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Deliverables}
D2.1 Dose--response neurite outgrowth dataset (M15); D2.2 RNA-seq data deposited in
ArrayExpress (M20); D2.3 Co-authored manuscript submitted to \textit{Nature Biomedical
Engineering} (M24).

\subsection{WP3 -- In Vivo Efficacy and Mechanistic Studies (Months 18--36)}

\subsubsection{Objectives}
Validate NeuraLattice efficacy in a rat thoracic hemisection model (T9 level);
delineate mechanotransductive signalling cascades mediating functional recovery.

\subsubsection{Key Activities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Home Office PPL held by Dr Okafor (Apex Neuroscience Institute); 60 Sprague
    Dawley rats allocated across sham, acellular hydrogel, NeuraLattice, and positive
    control (Matrigel) cohorts (\(n = 15\) per group; power calculated at 80\% for
    BBB locomotion score difference of 3 points, \(\alpha = 0.05\)).
  \item Functional assessment: Basso, Beattie, Bresnahan (BBB) scale at weekly
    intervals; ladder rung walk; von Frey sensory thresholds.
  \item Light sheet fluorescence microscopy (LaVision BioTec UltraMicroscope II) of
    cleared tissue; 3D axonal tract reconstruction.
  \item Phosphoproteomics (TMT-10plex, Orbitrap Eclipse) at M24 and M30 to map
    RhoA/ROCK and mTOR pathway activation dynamics.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Deliverables}
D3.1 BBB functional dataset with statistical analysis (M30); D3.2 3D atlas of scaffold
integration deposited as open dataset (M33); D3.3 Second provisional patent for
ChABC-microsphere system (M34); D3.4 Final project report and data management plan
update (M36).
