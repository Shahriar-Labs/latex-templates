\section{Pathways to Impact}

\begin{tcolorbox}[sectionbox, title=Impact Summary]
This research targets three pathways to impact spanning academic, clinical, and
commercial dimensions.

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{32mm}p{120mm}@{}}
\textcolor{ukripink}{\textbf{Academic}} &
Publication of \(\geq\)4 high-impact papers (target journals: \textit{Nature
Biomedical Engineering}, \textit{Advanced Materials}, \textit{Biomaterials}); open
data deposition; international conference keynotes (SFN, TERMIS). \\[6pt]
\textcolor{ukripink}{\textbf{Clinical}} &
Mechanistic insights will directly inform a \pounds 3.2M NIHR Invention for Innovation
(i4i) application targeting first-in-human feasibility (planned 2030); Dr Hartley has
a confirmed Letter of Support from Meridian University Hospitals NHS Trust. \\[6pt]
\textcolor{ukripink}{\textbf{Commercial}} &
Two provisional patents will be filed (M18, M34). Synapse Biomedical Technologies Ltd
will lead commercial development; Technology Transfer Office agreement in place. A
technology readiness level (TRL) advance from 3 to 5 is projected within the grant
period. \\
\end{tabular}
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Public Engagement}

Dr Hartley will deliver two public lectures per annum through the University of Meridian
\emph{Science in Society} programme and contribute to the MRC-funded \emph{Brain
Awareness Week} outreach campaign. A dedicated project website will be maintained with
lay summaries, data visualisations, and video explainers accessible to people living
with spinal cord injury and patient advocacy groups including the Spinal Injuries
Association (SIA). Social media engagement (\texttt{\#NeuraLattice}) will amplify reach
across X and LinkedIn.
