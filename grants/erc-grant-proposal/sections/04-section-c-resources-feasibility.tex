\section{Section c) Resources \& Feasibility}

\subsection{Host Institution Environment}
The Technical University of Munich (TUM) is a leading global center for quantum science and technology. As a key partner in the Munich Center for Quantum Science and Technology (MCQST) Cluster of Excellence, TUM offers a world-class research environment. The PI's lab has access to state-of-the-art dilution refrigerators (capable of reaching 10 mK), vector magnet cryostats, and high-frequency microwave electronics.
