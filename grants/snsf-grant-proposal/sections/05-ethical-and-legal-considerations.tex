\section{Ethical and Legal Considerations}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\linewidth}
  \begin{tcolorbox}[snsfsidebar]
    {\small\bfseries\color{SNSFnavy}\faBalanceScale\quad Animal Welfare}\\[4pt]
    {\small
    All animal procedures comply with Swiss animal experimentation law (TSchG, SR 455) and
    cantonal regulations. Experiments have been approved under license BE-X-2024-1172
    (Cantonal Veterinary Office, Bern). The 3Rs principles (Replacement, Reduction,
    Refinement) are central to our design: cohort size is the minimum determined by power
    analysis, and animals are singly housed only during post-surgical recovery.
    }
  \end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\linewidth}
  \begin{tcolorbox}[snsfsidebar]
    {\small\bfseries\color{SNSFnavy}\faLock\quad Data Governance}\\[4pt]
    {\small
    No human subjects or personal data are involved. Neural recording data will be
    stored on University of Solaris encrypted servers (AES-256) during the project,
    then publicly archived via the DANDI Archive in NWB:N 2.7 format. Analysis code
    will be deposited on GitHub (GPLv3). All materials will be available within 30 days
    of manuscript acceptance, consistent with SNSF Open Research Data policy.
    }
  \end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}
