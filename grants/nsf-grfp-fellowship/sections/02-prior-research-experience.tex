\section{Prior Research Experience}
\subsection{Research Project 1: Graph Neural Networks for Protein Structure Analysis}
\textit{Advisor: Dr. Sarah Jenkins, UC Berkeley Department of Bioengineering (Sept 2024 -- Present)}
\newpar
At UC Berkeley, I developed a dynamic Graph Neural Network (GNN) to model flexible protein-protein interfaces, representing structures as residue-level graphs with physical-distance edges. This architecture achieved a 12\% improvement in binding affinity prediction accuracy over state-of-the-art rigid baseline models. This work led to a first-author manuscript currently under review at the \textit{Journal of Computational Biology} and a poster at ISMB 2025.
\newpar
\textbf{\textcolor{accent}{Intellectual Merit:}} This study is the first application of dynamic edge-convolution to flexible docking, demonstrating that message-passing architectures can successfully learn transient physical interactions in biomolecular complexes. \textbf{\textcolor{accent}{Broader Impacts:}} I released my code as the open-source library \texttt{ProGraph} (800+ downloads) and mentored two undergraduate women in my laboratory, guiding them through basic graph theory and PyTorch implementations to support diversity in STEM.

\subsection{Research Project 2: Deep Learning for Genomic Variant Callers}
\textit{Advisor: Dr. Robert Chen, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (June 2023 -- Aug 2024)}
\newpar
As a research assistant at LBNL, I designed a deep convolutional neural network (CNN) that treats genomic read alignments as multi-channel images to detect rare single-nucleotide variants (SNPs) and short indels. Our model reduced false-positive indel calls by 18\% in low-coverage regions, and was integrated directly into LBNL's production pipeline. I presented these findings at the 2024 ASHG Annual Meeting.
\newpar
\textbf{\textcolor{accent}{Intellectual Merit:}} The project proved that convolutional neural networks can learn to differentiate sequencing artifacts from true biological variants using multi-dimensional spatial alignment features without hand-crafted heuristics. \textbf{\textcolor{accent}{Broader Impacts:}} We deployed this model to public cloud infrastructure, making high-accuracy calling accessible to resource-constrained labs. I also organized a workshop at a local high school, introducing 25 underrepresented students to Python genomics.
