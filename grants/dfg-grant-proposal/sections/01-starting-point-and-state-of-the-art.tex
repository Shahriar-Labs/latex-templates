\section{Starting Point and State of the Art}
\label{sec:state}

\subsection{Background and Scientific Context}

Precision sensing at the nanoscale has emerged as a central challenge of modern quantum
technology. \keyword{Nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centres} in diamond host a spin-1 ground state
whose magnetic-field sensitivity approaches $\sim1\;\text{pT}/\!\sqrt{\text{Hz}}$ under
ambient conditions~\cite{barry2020}, making NV arrays ideally suited to imaging exotic
spin textures in quantum materials. Despite rapid experimental progress, two fundamental
obstacles remain unresolved.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Resource allocation}: conventional protocols apply uniform sensing pulses
        irrespective of local signal strength, wasting coherence time in low-contrast
        regions.
  \item \textbf{Adaptive feedback latency}: existing real-time controllers achieve
        update cycles of ${\sim}10\;\mu$s, insufficient to track spin-wave packets in
        frustrated magnets whose group velocities exceed $10^4\;\text{m\,s}^{-1}$.
\end{itemize}

Recent theoretical work by Hartmann \& Becker (2023) demonstrated that Bayesian
estimation combined with reinforcement-learning policies can, in principle, reduce
coherence-time overhead by a factor of $6.3\pm0.4$ while preserving Fisher-information
optimality. Translating this theory into hardware requires custom FPGA firmware
operating at sub-microsecond latencies — the central technological goal of this proposal.

\subsection{Existing Work by the Applicant}

The applicant's group has established three prerequisite competences:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item A $4\times4$ NV-centre array fabricated via focused-ion-beam implantation
        (inter-defect spacing $120\pm8\;\text{nm}$), characterised in Hartmann et al.\
        \textit{Phys.\ Rev.\ Applied} \textbf{21}, 034017 (2024).
  \item An in-house microwave routing matrix (Nimbus-16 architecture) enabling
        site-selective pulse delivery with $\leq3\;\text{ns}$ crosstalk risetime.
  \item A prototype Bayesian scheduler implemented on a Xilinx UltraScale\textsuperscript{+}
        FPGA achieving $840\;\text{ns}$ update cycles in offline tests.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{tcolorbox}[accentbox, title={}]
\textbf{\color{dfgblue}Research Gap.}\quad
No existing experimental platform combines (i) spatially addressable multi-qubit NV
arrays, (ii) real-time Bayesian feedback below $1\;\mu$s, and (iii) self-optimising
resource allocation for condensed-matter sensing. This proposal closes all three gaps
simultaneously.
\end{tcolorbox}
