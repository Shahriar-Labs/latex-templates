\subsection{Motivation}

% Slide 3: Motivation
\begin{frame}{The Challenge of Modern Energy Grids}
  The transition to zero-carbon energy grids introduces significant operational volatility:
  \vspace{0.4cm}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item<1-> Rapid integration of intermittent renewable energy sources (solar and wind).
    \item<2-> High peak-to-average load ratios leading to frequent localized grid instability.
    \item<3-> Carbon-intensive legacy backup generators activated during peak demand hours.
    \item<4-> Critical need for an autonomous, decentralized, and real-time balancing solution.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
