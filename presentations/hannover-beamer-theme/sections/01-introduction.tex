\section{Introduction}

% Slide 3: Background & Context
\begin{frame}{Background \& Context}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Cognitive Systems:} Traditional deep neural architectures require significant energy and high computational overhead.
    \item \textbf{Modern Paradigm:} Edge computing devices demand low-power, high-accuracy inference engines.
    \item \textbf{Our Vision:} Project Synapse blends spiking neural networks (SNNs) with dynamic hardware routing.
    \item \textbf{Partnership:} Funded in cooperation with Vertex Systems and Nexa Corporation.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% Slide 4: Motivation & Problem Statement
\begin{frame}{Motivation \& Problem Statement}
  Why do current models fail under real-world pressure?
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Latency bottlenecks:} Real-time decision systems demand latency $<$ 50\,ms.
    \pause
    \item \textbf{High energy consumption:} Battery-powered edge devices cannot support standard GPUs.
    \pause
    \item \textbf{Rigid network weights:} Static models cannot adapt to changing physical environments in real time.
    \pause
    \item \textbf{Data bandwidth limits:} Heavy cloud communications are bottlenecked by local network infrastructure.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
