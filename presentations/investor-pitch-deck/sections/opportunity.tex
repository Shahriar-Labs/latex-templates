\section{Opportunity}

\begin{frame}{The Problem}{Supply Chains Are Flying Blind}

  Mid-market retailers and manufacturers still plan demand the way they did in 2010 --
  and it shows up directly on the balance sheet.

\begin{frame}{Why Now}{Three Tailwinds Converging}

  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textcolor{accentMint}{\faChartLine}\ \ \textbf{Data volume inflection.} Omni-channel
      retail now generates roughly \textbf{40x the SKU-level signal} of 2018 -- but most
      planning stacks still sample it monthly \cite{nexa2025forecasting}.
    \item \textcolor{accentMint}{\faBolt}\ \ \textbf{Compute got cheap enough.} Transformer-based
      forecasting that cost six figures per model to run in 2020 now runs continuously,
      per-SKU, for a fraction of the cost.
    \item \textcolor{accentMint}{\faGlobe}\ \ \textbf{Post-2024 volatility.} Tariff resets and
      nearshoring have made trailing-twelve-month forecasting models unreliable for three
      consecutive quarters \cite{meridian2026supplychain}.
  \end{itemize}
