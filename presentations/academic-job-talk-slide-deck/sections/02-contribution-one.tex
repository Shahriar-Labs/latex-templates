\section{Contribution I: Structured Denoising}
\subsection{Method and Results}

\begin{frame}{Contribution I: Structured Contrastive Denoising}{Vasquez et al., 2024}
  \small
  \textbf{Structured Contrastive Denoising (SCD)} fits a lightweight
  \emph{corruption model} from unlabeled deployment logs and uses it to
  generate contrastive pairs that mimic real instrument failure
  modes~\cite{vasquez2024contrastive}.

  \begin{itemize}
    \item Three-state corruption model: sensor drift, clipping, burst dropout.
    \item Pulls together views of the \emph{same} signal under different corruptions.
    \item No labels needed at pretraining time -- only raw logged readings.
  \end{itemize}
