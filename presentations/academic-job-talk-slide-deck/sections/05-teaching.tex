\section{Teaching}
\subsection{Philosophy and Courses}

\begin{frame}{Teaching Philosophy}{Build Before You Prove}
  \small
  I teach the way I do research: students get their hands on a noisy, real
  dataset before they see the clean theorem that explains it.

  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Project-based assessment:} every course ends on an unseen dataset.
    \item \textbf{Scaffolded mentoring:} weekly office hours around a research log.
    \item \textbf{Inclusive access:} open-licensed notes and code, no paid software.
  \end{itemize}
