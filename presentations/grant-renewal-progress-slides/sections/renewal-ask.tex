\begin{frame}{Year 4--6 Objectives}
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=3pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item[\textcolor{gold}{\textbf{1.}}] \textbf{Complete field pilot installations} at both confirmed
    municipal sites and instrument panels for two full seasonal cycles.
  \item[\textcolor{gold}{\textbf{2.}}] \textbf{Scale the curing process} from bench pilot to a
    production line rated at 500 panels/month.
  \item[\textcolor{gold}{\textbf{3.}}] \textbf{Publish the full durability dataset} and open a
    building-code certification pathway with regional standards bodies.
  \item[\textcolor{gold}{\textbf{4.}}] \textbf{Establish a commercialization pathway}: exploratory
    spin-out discussions under the working name Vertex BioBuild.
\end{itemize}

\par\vspace{3pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[infobox, title=Why Renew Now]
\scriptsize
Years 1--3 de-risked the core material science: strength targets were
exceeded and the curing process is validated at pilot scale. The
renewal period converts that lab result into field-proven,
code-eligible infrastructure product --- the step that determines
whether this technology reaches deployment at all.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{frame}
