\begin{frame}{Executive Summary}
    Apex represents a paradigm shift in cooperative multi-agent pathfinding (MAPF). By combining decentralized model predictive control with an asynchronous consensus protocol, Apex achieves unparalleled scalability and safety in dynamic environments.
    
    \vspace{0.4cm}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Distributed Consensus:} Decentralized conflict resolution without global knowledge.
        \item \textbf{Real-Time Adaptation:} Sub-millisecond trajectory generation for dynamic obstacles.
        \item \textbf{Scalable Architecture:} Verified up to 1,000 cooperative agents in high-density corridors.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% Slide 4: The MAPF Challenge
\begin{frame}{The MAPF Challenge in Dynamic Arenas}
    Modern logistics applications, such as the Vertex automated distribution centers, require hundreds of agents to navigate complex environments simultaneously.
    
    \vspace{0.3cm}
    Key operational bottlenecks include:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item\textbf{Congestion Hotspots:} High-density areas degrade routing performance.
        \item\textbf{Latency Sensitivity:} Communication delay leads to navigation drift.
        \item\textbf{Deadlock Vulnerability:} Standard heuristic approaches fail in tight corridors.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
