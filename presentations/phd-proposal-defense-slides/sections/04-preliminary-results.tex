\begin{frame}{What the Pilot Suggests}{And What It Cannot Yet Tell Us}
  \begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
    \begin{copperbox}{Encouraging}
      \footnotesize
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Near-miss rate drops by more than half in every environment,
          largest in the debris field where terrain shift is greatest.
        \item Latency overhead of the evidential head is 5--6\,ms --
          inside the Phase~1 go/no-go budget.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{copperbox}
  \end{minipage}\hfill
  \begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
    \begin{notebox}{Still open}
      \footnotesize
      \begin{itemize}
        \item These numbers are simulation-only; Aim~3 is precisely the
          test of whether the gain survives the sim-to-real gap.
        \item Pilot used a single seed family -- the full study pre-registers
          five seeds per cell before Phase~2 is called complete.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{notebox}
  \end{minipage}
\end{frame}
