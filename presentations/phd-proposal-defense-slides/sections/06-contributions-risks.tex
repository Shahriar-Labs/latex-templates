\begin{frame}{Summary}{What This Proposal Asks the Committee to Approve}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item A dynamics model that reports \emph{when it is guessing}, cheap
      enough to run onboard.
    \item A policy that spends a formal, mechanically-grounded safety
      budget instead of an ad hoc heuristic.
    \item A field test at the Meridian Outcrop Test Range that either
      confirms the simulation gains transfer, or tells us precisely where
      they do not.
  \end{itemize}
