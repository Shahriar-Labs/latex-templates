\begin{frame}{Related Work}{Three Threads This Proposal Combines}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
    \item[\faBook] \textbf{Sim-to-real transfer.} Vasquez
      \cite{vasquez2021fieldrobots} documents the gap this proposal targets:
      policies tuned in \textbf{Nexa} degrade sharply once terrain friction
      and compliance leave the training envelope.
    \item[\faBook] \textbf{Uncertainty quantification.} Garcia
      \cite{garcia2024evidential} shows evidential deep learning gives
      single-pass, calibrated variance estimates -- the property Aim~1
      builds on, absent the ensemble's compute cost.
    \item[\faBook] \textbf{Safe policy optimization.} Osei
      \cite{osei2023constrained} and Nandakumar
      \cite{nandakumar2022saferl} formalize constrained MDPs for field
      robots, but both assume the risk signal is already given -- Aim~2
      supplies that signal from Aim~1's estimator.
  \end{itemize}
  \par\vspace{6pt}
  \begin{alertblock}{The gap}
    No published system couples a \emph{calibrated, single-pass} uncertainty
    estimate directly into the constraint of a field-deployed policy, then
    validates the coupling outdoors. Preliminary work
    \cite{chen2025uncertainty} took the first step in simulation only.
  \end{alertblock}
\end{frame}
