\begin{frame}{Related Work}{Three Threads This Proposal Combines}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=1pt]
    \small
    \item[\faBook] \textbf{Calibration theory.} Malik
      \cite{malik2023calibrated} and Ibrahim
      \cite{ibrahim2020ece} establish expected calibration error (ECE) as
      the standard diagnostic -- the metric this proposal adopts and
      extends to generative outputs.
    \item[\faBook] \textbf{Selective prediction.} Novak
      \cite{novak2024selective} shows abstention rules built on a
      calibrated score cut error rate sharply at a modest coverage cost --
      the mechanism Aim~2 adapts to a triage workflow.
    \item[\faBook] \textbf{Clinical trust in AI.} Haddad
      \cite{haddad2022trust} finds clinicians recalibrate their own trust
      \emph{faster} when a tool exposes honest uncertainty, even when
      that uncertainty is sometimes high.
  \end{itemize}
  \par\vspace{3pt}
  \begin{alertblock}{The gap}
    \small
    No published system couples a \emph{single-pass, black-box} confidence
    estimate directly into an abstention rule for LLM-based triage, then
    validates that rule against clinician outcomes. Preliminary work
    \cite{okonkwo2025preliminary} took a first pass at the estimator alone.
  \end{alertblock}
\end{frame}
