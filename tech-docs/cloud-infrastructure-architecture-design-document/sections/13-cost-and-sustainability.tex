\section{Cost and Sustainability}

Costs are allocated by account, environment, service, owner, and cost center. Mandatory tags are enforced where the service supports them. Budgets alert at forecast thresholds of 80\%, 100\%, and 120\%; anomaly detection routes unusual daily spend to the owning team.

Workloads publish utilization and unit-cost metrics. Carbon and cost generally align through higher utilization, appropriate data retention, and fewer unnecessary transfers; resilience capacity is retained where it protects a defined reliability target.
