\section{Roles and Responsibilities}

\subsection{Governance Structure}
Accountability for information security runs from the Board Risk \& Audit Committee,
through the CISO, to line management and, ultimately, to every individual with access to
Nexa systems. The CISO chairs a monthly Security Steering Committee comprising IT
Operations, Data Protection, HR, and a rotating departmental representative, which
reviews open risks, exception requests, and control metrics.
