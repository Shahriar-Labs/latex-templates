\section{Control Objectives}

\subsection{Framework Alignment}
Nexa's control set is mapped to the Annex~A controls of ISO/IEC~27001:2022
\cite{iso27001} and cross-referenced against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework~2.0
\cite{nistcsf} and NIST SP~800-53 Rev.~5 \cite{nist80053} for customers requiring a
US-federal control lineage. Contractual obligations under PCI~DSS~v4.0 \cite{pcidss} and
the SOC~2 Trust Services Criteria \cite{soc2} are satisfied as a subset of the objectives
below; no separate control set is maintained for either.

\subsection{Control Objectives Table}
Table~\ref{tab:controls} states the objective for each control domain, the primary
control that satisfies it, its Annex~A reference, and the accountable owner.

\begin{ctrlnote}{Control exceptions}
  A control objective in this table may be met by a compensating control where the
  primary control is not technically feasible, provided the compensating control is
  documented, risk-assessed, and approved per the exception process in
  Clause~\ref{sec:exceptions}.
\end{ctrlnote}
