\section{Acceptable Use and Access Control}

\subsection{Acceptable Use of Assets}
Nexa-issued accounts, devices, and network access are provided for business purposes.
Incidental personal use is permitted where it does not incur cost, consume material
resources, violate law, or create reputational risk to Nexa. Employees must not:
\begin{itemize}[topsep=2pt, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Share credentials or authentication factors with another individual, including
        colleagues, under any circumstance;
  \item Install software from outside the approved catalogue on managed endpoints;
  \item Copy production customer data to unmanaged storage, personal cloud drives, or
        removable media;
  \item Disable, bypass, or attempt to circumvent an endpoint security control.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Access Control Requirements}
\begin{enumerate}[topsep=2pt, itemsep=3pt]
  \item All access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication; password-only
        access is disabled fleet-wide.
  \item Access requests are approved by the requester's manager and provisioned by IT
        Operations against a documented role, per Table~\ref{tab:raci}.
  \item Access to production customer data requires an additional approval from the Data
        Protection Officer and is logged for audit.
  \item Departmental access reviews occur quarterly; any account unused for 90 consecutive
        days is disabled pending manager confirmation.
  \item All access is revoked within one business day of an employee's last working day,
        coordinated between People and IT Operations.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Data Classification}
Every information asset is classified at creation and handled according to
Table~\ref{tab:classification}.

    \midrule
    Public &
      Approved for external release without restriction. &
      No special handling; standard version control. \\
    Internal &
      Business information not intended for external release. &
      Access limited to employees and authorised contractors. \\
    Confidential &
      Information whose disclosure would cause material harm --- financial models,

      access recertification. \\

  \end{tabularx}
  \caption{Data classification levels and required handling.}
