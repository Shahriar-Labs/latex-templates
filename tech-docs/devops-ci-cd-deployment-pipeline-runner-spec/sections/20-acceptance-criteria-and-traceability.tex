\section{Acceptance criteria and traceability}

General availability is approved only when all mandatory criteria in
Table~\ref{tab:acceptance} have objective evidence and no open critical or high-risk
security finding lacks an approved exception.

\req{AC-02} & A 256-job DAG is planned within objective and scheduled without starvation under mixed tenant load. & \req{FR-003--005}; performance report \\
