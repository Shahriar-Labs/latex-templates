\subsection*{Guardrail metrics}
Smart Alerts must not regress the metrics it depends on. Notification-center engagement
and email open rates are tracked weekly during rollout; if Slack alert volume per
workspace exceeds 15/day median, sensitivity defaults are revisited before wider rollout.
