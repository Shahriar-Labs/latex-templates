FR-01 & Users can subscribe any catalog metric to anomaly monitoring from the metric's
  detail view. & \priobadge{P0} & Subscribe action visible on every catalog metric;
  confirmation toast on success; subscription visible in user's alert list within 2s. \\
\addlinespace
FR-02 & The detection service evaluates each subscribed metric on its native refresh
  cadence (hourly or daily) and flags values outside the expected range. & \priobadge{P0}
  & 95th-percentile detection-to-notification time under 5 minutes for hourly metrics,
  measured in staging load test. \\
\addlinespace
FR-03 & Alert notifications state the metric name, current value, expected range, and
  percentage deviation in plain language. & \priobadge{P0} & Copy reviewed by Design;
  no raw z-scores or model internals shown to the end user. \\
\addlinespace
FR-04 & Users can choose delivery channel(s) per subscription: in-app, email, Slack. &
  \priobadge{P0} & At least one channel required at subscribe time; channels editable
  after creation without re-subscribing. \\
\addlinespace
FR-05 & Users can set a maintenance window (start/end timestamp) during which a metric's
  alerts are suppressed but still logged. & \priobadge{P1} & Suppressed alerts appear
  greyed out in history with a ``muted'' tag; no notification sent through any channel. \\
\addlinespace
FR-06 & Users can mark a received alert as ``expected,'' which retrains the baseline to
  treat similar magnitude changes as normal going forward. & \priobadge{P1} & Marking
  expected updates the model within 24h; verified via a repeat-alert suppression test. \\
\addlinespace
FR-07 & Workspace admins can view all active subscriptions across the workspace,
  filterable by metric and by user. & \priobadge{P1} & Admin view loads under 2s for
  workspaces with up to 500 subscriptions. \\
\addlinespace
FR-08 & Users can set alert sensitivity (low / medium / high) per subscribed metric,
  changing the deviation threshold that triggers a notification. & \priobadge{P2} &
  Threshold change takes effect on the next evaluation cycle; documented mapping from
  sensitivity level to standard-deviation multiplier. \\
\addlinespace
FR-09 & Subscribed users receive an optional weekly digest summarizing all alerts fired
  for their subscriptions. & \priobadge{P2} & Digest opt-in default OFF; sent Monday
  08:00 in the user's workspace timezone. \\
\bottomrule
