\section{Goals}

\subsection*{Product goals}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{G\arabic*.}]
  \item Cut median anomaly-detection lag from 4.2 days to under 1 hour for subscribed
    metrics.
  \item Give every paid Apex workspace at least one active Smart Alert subscription
    within 30 days of GA, without requiring a sales-assisted setup call.
  \item Establish the anomaly-detection service as a reusable primitive other Apex
    surfaces (forecasting, reporting) can build on after v1.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Business goals}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{B\arabic*.}]
  \item Close the \#2 enterprise renewal gap identified in Q1 interviews, supporting the
    Q3 enterprise renewal cohort (\$1.4M ARR under active risk review).
  \item Create a differentiated feature for competitive deals against tools that only
    offer scheduled reports.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Non-goals (out of scope for v1)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Alerting on custom SQL queries or ad-hoc metrics outside the standard metrics
    catalog.
  \item Predictive/forecast-based alerting (``this metric will breach target in 3
    days''). Detection is on realized values only.
  \item Alert routing to third-party incident tools (PagerDuty, Opsgenie). Slack and
    email only for v1.
  \item Team-wide default subscriptions configured by an admin on behalf of others.
\end{itemize}
