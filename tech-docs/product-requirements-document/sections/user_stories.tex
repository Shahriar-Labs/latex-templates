\section{User Stories}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{1.1cm}X>{\centering\arraybackslash}p{1.5cm}@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{prdaccent}
{\color{white}ID} & {\color{white}User Story} & {\color{white}Priority} \\
\midrule
US-01 & As a \textbf{revenue ops lead}, I want to subscribe a dashboard metric to anomaly
  monitoring in two clicks, so that I don't have to configure thresholds manually. &
  \priobadge{P0} \\
\addlinespace
US-02 & As a \textbf{revenue ops lead}, I want an alert to explain \emph{how} unusual a
  change is (e.g.\ ``38\% below the 30-day expected range''), so that I can judge urgency
  without opening Apex. & \priobadge{P0} \\
\addlinespace
US-03 & As a \textbf{data engineer}, I want alerts delivered to a shared Slack channel,
  so that the whole on-call rotation sees a feed failure at the same time. & \priobadge{P0} \\
\addlinespace
US-04 & As a \textbf{data engineer}, I want to mute a metric during a known maintenance
  window, so that a planned migration doesn't page the team with false anomalies. &
  \priobadge{P1} \\
\addlinespace
US-05 & As a \textbf{customer success manager}, I want an alert to include the account
  name and a one-line ``why this matters'' summary, so that I can triage without extra
  context-switching. & \priobadge{P1} \\
\addlinespace
US-06 & As a \textbf{revenue ops lead}, I want to mark an alert as ``expected'' with one
  click, so that the detection model treats similar future changes as normal. &
  \priobadge{P1} \\
\addlinespace
US-07 & As a \textbf{workspace admin}, I want a single view of every active alert
  subscription in my workspace, so that I can audit alert volume before it becomes noise.
  & \priobadge{P1} \\
\addlinespace
US-08 & As a \textbf{revenue ops lead}, I want to set a sensitivity level (low / medium /
  high) per metric, so that a noisy metric doesn't drown out a stable one. & \priobadge{P2} \\
\addlinespace
US-09 & As any \textbf{subscriber}, I want a weekly digest of all alerts I received, so
  that I can spot patterns without re-reading each individual notification. & \priobadge{P2} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

{\footnotesize\color{prdslate} Priority key: \priobadge{P0} must ship for GA
\quad \priobadge{P1} ships within 4 weeks of GA \quad \priobadge{P2} backlog, post-v1.}
