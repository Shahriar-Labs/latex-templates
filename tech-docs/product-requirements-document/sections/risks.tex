\section{Risks and Open Questions}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{1.2cm}Xp{3.3cm}@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{prdaccent}
{\color{white}Risk} & {\color{white}Description} & {\color{white}Mitigation} \\
\midrule
R1 & Alert fatigue: if default sensitivity is too aggressive, users mute or unsubscribe
  before the feature proves value. & Ship conservative default sensitivity; surface
  ``mark expected'' prominently in the first alert a user receives. \\
\addlinespace
R2 & The anomaly-detection service (built for internal ops use) has not been load-tested
  against customer-facing traffic volumes. & Closed Beta explicitly load-tests at 4x
  design-partner volume before GA; Engineering owns a go/no-go checkpoint. \\
\addlinespace
R3 & Slack delivery depends on the existing Apex--Slack integration, which is
  independently owned and mid-refactor per ENG-2026-011. & Sync with Platform team
  before Beta; email remains a fallback channel if Slack delivery slips. \\
\addlinespace
R4 & Seasonal metrics (e.g.\ retail traffic) may produce false positives around known
  events (Black Friday) that the baseline model hasn't seen yet. & Ship a manual
  ``pause during event'' control as part of maintenance windows (FR-05) instead of
  waiting for seasonal model maturity. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection*{Open questions}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Q\arabic*.}]
  \item Should sensitivity default vary by metric type (revenue vs.\ operational), or
    should there be one global default for v1? \emph{Owner: Omar Haddad, due before
    Beta kickoff.}
  \item Do we need per-metric alert history retention limits, or does the standard
    90-day Apex data retention policy apply unchanged? \emph{Owner: Daniel Ruiz.}
  \item Is a single ``mark expected'' click sufficient signal for the model, or does it
    need a reason code to avoid overcorrecting the baseline? \emph{Owner: Data Science,
    resolve during Alpha.}
\end{enumerate}
