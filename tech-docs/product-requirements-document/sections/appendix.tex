\subsection*{Glossary}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{3.4cm}X@{}}
\toprule
\dcell{Anomaly} & A metric value that falls outside its statistically expected range for the
  current time and seasonal context, as computed by the detection service. \\
\dcell{Baseline} & The expected-range model for a metric, built from its historical seasonal
  pattern and recent volatility. \\
\dcell{Detection lag} & Elapsed time between an anomaly's real-world onset and the moment a
  subscribed user is notified. \\
\dcell{Sensitivity} & A per-metric setting controlling how large a deviation must be before it
  triggers a notification. \\
\dcell{Subscription} & A user's opt-in link between a specific metric and one or more delivery
  channels. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection*{Methodology note}
The detection service's baseline model follows standard seasonal-trend decomposition
practice \cite{cleveland1990stl}, with anomaly scoring adapted from the generalized ESD
approach used in production anomaly-detection systems at web scale
\cite{hochenbaum2017anomaly}. Classical ARIMA-family forecasting \cite{box1970time} was
evaluated during early spikes but rejected for v1 due to per-metric retraining cost;
it remains a candidate for the Phase 3 forecasting extension noted in Section~3.
