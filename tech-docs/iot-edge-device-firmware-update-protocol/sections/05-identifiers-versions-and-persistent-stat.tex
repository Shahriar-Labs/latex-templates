\section{Identifiers, versions, and persistent state}

\subsection{Identifiers}

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{31mm}L{45mm}L{76mm}@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Syntax} & \textbf{Requirement} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  \field{device_id} & 1--128 printable ASCII characters & Unique within a fleet;
    opaque to protocol logic. \\
  \field{model} & 1--64 characters matching \code{[A-Za-z0-9._-]+} & Bound to
    hardware or immutable provisioning data. \\
  \field{release_id} & UUID string & Globally unique per signed release. \\
  \field{campaign_id} & UUID string & Identifies rollout policy; not trusted for
    artifact authorization. \\
  \field{artifact_id} & UUID string & Uniquely identifies immutable bytes and
    transfer metadata. \\
  \field{session_id} & 128-bit random value encoded as 32 lowercase hex digits &
    Unique per discovery-to-terminal update attempt. \\
  \field{key_id} & 1--64 ASCII characters & Selects a trust-store verification key. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{longtable}

Identifiers are case-sensitive. Services \must treat identifiers as opaque and
\mustnot embed credentials or personally identifying data in URLs, logs, or MQTT
topics. A device \mustnot derive authorization solely from a mutable model string
reported by application firmware.

\subsection{Persistent state}

The following values \must survive power loss using atomic records, journaling,
or an equivalent mechanism:

\begin{itemize}
  \item the highest confirmed \field{image_security_version} per protected component;
  \item active, confirmed, and trial slot identifiers;
  \item current \field{release_id}, \field{artifact_id}, manifest digest, and session ID;
  \item verified download offset or verified chunk bitmap;
  \item remaining trial boot attempts and confirmation deadline basis; and
  \item the most recent status sequence number for the active session.
\end{itemize}

A persistent update record \must include an integrity check and a monotonically
advancing generation number. If no valid record can be recovered, the device
\must boot the independently verified confirmed slot and restart discovery; it
\mustnot activate partially written content.
