\section{Protocol overview}

\protocol is a device-initiated, manifest-driven protocol. A normal exchange is:

\begin{lstlisting}
Device                 Update Service        Repository        Bootloader
  |-- DISCOVER ------------>|                    |                 |
  |<-- OFFER(manifest) ------|                    |                 |
  |-- ACCEPT --------------->|                    |                 |
  |-- GET Range --------------------------------->|                 |
  |<-- artifact bytes ----------------------------|                 |
  |-- STATUS(verified) ----->|                    |                 |
  |-- install to inactive slot                    |                 |
  |-- STATUS(staged) ------->|                    |                 |
  |-- set trial boot --------------------------------------------->|
  |                         reboot                                  |
  |<------------------------------------------------ trial image ---|
  |-- health checks                                              |
  |-- confirm image --------------------------------------------->|
  |-- STATUS(confirmed) ---->|                    |                 |
\end{lstlisting}

Every offer is independently verifiable. Transfer and installation may span many
connections. Status delivery is at least once, so the service \must process it
idempotently using \field{device_id}, \field{session_id}, and \field{sequence}.

\subsection{Device state machine}
\label{sec:state-machine}

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{29mm}L{75mm}L{48mm}@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{State} & \textbf{Entry action} & \textbf{Normal next state} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  \state{Idle} & Wait for schedule, operator request, or notification. &
    \state{Discovering} \\
  \state{Discovering} & Send inventory and capabilities; validate response envelope. &
    \state{Offered} or \state{Idle} \\
  \state{Offered} & Verify signature, freshness, applicability, policy, and storage. &
    \state{Downloading}, \state{Deferred}, or \state{Failed} \\
  \state{Downloading} & Fetch ranges, verify chunks, and persist progress. &
    \state{Verifying} or \state{Paused} \\
  \state{Verifying} & Verify full artifact digest and authenticated metadata. &
    \state{Staging} or \state{Failed} \\
  \state{Staging} & Write/transform inactive component and verify installed image. &
    \state{Ready} or \state{Failed} \\
  \state{Ready} & Wait for activation policy and create atomic trial-boot record. &
    \state{Rebooting} \\
  \state{Rebooting} & Reboot into the trial image. & \state{Trial} \\
  \state{Trial} & Run health checks before the attempt/deadline limit. &
    \state{Confirmed} or \state{Rollback} \\
  \state{Confirmed} & Commit security counter and report terminal success. &
    \state{Idle} \\
  \state{Rollback} & Select confirmed image, reboot, and report failure. &
    \state{Idle} \\
  \state{Paused} & Preserve verified progress while policy or connectivity blocks work. &
    \state{Downloading} or \state{Idle} \\
  \state{Deferred} & Record a policy reason and retry at/after the advised time. &
    \state{Discovering} \\
  \state{Failed} & Clean unsafe staging state and report a terminal error. &
    \state{Idle} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{longtable}

Security failures such as an invalid signature, digest mismatch, target mismatch,
or rollback attempt are terminal for that manifest. Transient transport and power
conditions are resumable and \mustnot be reported as terminal until configured
retry limits are exhausted.
