\subsection{Headroom Analysis}
Based on linear extrapolation of our current growth trends (approx. 5\% monthly increase in base traffic), we computed the remaining headroom before resource saturation. Saturation is defined as exceeding 85\% average utilization or 95\% peak utilization.

Table~\ref{tab:headroom} presents the projected saturation dates for the critical system metrics.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Vertex Database Cluster resource headroom and saturation projections.}
  \label{tab:headroom}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l c c r X}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Current Value} & \textbf{Growth Rate} & \textbf{Provisional Limit} & \textbf{Projected Saturation} \\
    \midrule
    Primary CPU (Peak) & 88\% & +5.1\% / Mo & 95\% & September 2026 \\
    \rowcolor{lightgray} Primary Memory & 76\% & +4.0\% / Mo & 85\% & October 2026 \\
    Disk Storage & 320\,GB & +22\,GB / Mo & 450\,GB (90\%) & January 2027 \\
    \rowcolor{lightgray} Storage IOPS & 2,850 & +8.5\% / Mo & 3,000 (Baseline) & August 2026 \\
    Replica CPU (Peak) & 46\% & +3.2\% / Mo & 95\% & May 2027 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\textbf{Analysis:} The disk IOPS metrics and primary CPU peak utilization are critical bottlenecks. High storage write-wait latency has already begun degrading API response times, as detailed in Section~\ref{sec:load_testing}.
