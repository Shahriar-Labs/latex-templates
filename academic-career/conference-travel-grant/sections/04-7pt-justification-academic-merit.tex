ICML is the premier international venue for machine learning research, ranked A* by the Computing Research and Education Association (CORE). Attending will place my work before the foremost researchers in the field and enable direct engagement with cutting-edge developments.

\textbf{Relevance to Dissertation.}

Over 40\% of accepted papers address topics in distributed optimization, privacy-preserving ML, and non-IID data --- areas central to my research. Feedback from the oral session and hallway conversations will substantially strengthen the final chapters of my dissertation.

\vspace{3pt}

\textbf{Career Development.}
ICML's Doctoral Consortium, mentoring workshops, and industry research labs exhibition provide structured opportunities for career advancement. Establishing these connections now is essential for my planned transition to a postdoctoral position in Fall 2027.
