\subsection*{Overall Candidate Evaluation}
The examining committee must provide a consensus recommendation by marking one of the following:

\begin{itemize}[label={$\square$}]
  \item \textbf{Pass:} The candidate demonstrates sufficient depth of knowledge to proceed to doctoral candidacy.
  \item \textbf{Conditional Pass:} The candidate must satisfy specific conditions (detailed below) before proceeding.
  \item \textbf{Fail:} The candidate has failed the examination and may re-sit according to departmental guidelines.
\end{itemize}
