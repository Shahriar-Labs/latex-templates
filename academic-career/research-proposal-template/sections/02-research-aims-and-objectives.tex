\section{Research Aims and Objectives}

The overarching goal is to design, implement, and empirically validate a federated learning
framework that delivers clinically useful models under realistic constraints of data
heterogeneity, privacy regulation, and network bandwidth. This goal decomposes into four
specific aims.

\subsection{Aim 1: Adaptive Differential Privacy for Heterogeneous Federated Learning}
\vspace{-2pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Derive a per-site privacy budget that scales with the Jensen--Shannon divergence
    between each site's local data distribution and the estimated global distribution.
  \item Implement moment-accountant-based privacy tracking that reports $(\varepsilon,\delta)$
    guarantees on a per-round and per-site basis.
  \item Evaluate privacy--utility trade-offs on the MIMIC-IV and eICU-CRD benchmark datasets
    under three clinically relevant prediction tasks.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Aim 2: Divergence-Aware Model Aggregation}
\vspace{-2pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Develop a weighted aggregation scheme where site weights are inversely proportional
    to the empirical Wasserstein distance between local and global model updates.
  \item Produce per-site interpretability reports quantifying the contribution of each
    participating institution to the final model's decision boundaries.
  \item Benchmark against FedAvg, FedProx, and SCAFFOLD on five heterogeneous data splits.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Aim 3: Privacy-Preserving Gradient Compression}
\vspace{-2pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Extend top-$k$ sparsification and randomized quantization to operate under DP noise
    without leaking additional information through the compression pattern itself.
  \item Prove a composition theorem bounding the total privacy cost of compressed-DP
    communication rounds.
  \item Measure wall-clock communication time on a simulated hospital network topology
    with realistic bandwidth caps (10--100\,Mbps).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Aim 4: End-to-End Validation at Partner Hospitals}
\vspace{-2pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Deploy the integrated framework across three hospital sites in the Greater Toronto
    Area (Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre, St.\ Michael's Hospital, University Health
    Network) using de-identified electronic health record data.
  \item Conduct a blinded clinician evaluation comparing model predictions against current
    standard-of-care risk scores.
  \item Release the full codebase as open-source software with comprehensive documentation
    and reproducibility scripts.
\end{itemize}
