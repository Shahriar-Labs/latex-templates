\section{Executive Summary \& Research Vision Arc}
This blueprint outlines the whiteboard strategy for a 45-minute faculty chalk talk presentation. The research program, titled \textbf{Engineering Programmable Multicellular Systems for Directed Tissue Morphogenesis}, aims to bridge the gap between synthetic biology and regenerative medicine. The program is built around the development of synthetic morphogen circuits that coordinate cell-to-cell signaling to build functional, three-dimensional tissues in vitro, expanding on pioneering receptor configurations \cite{robins2023engineering}.

\subsection{Whiteboard Mapping Philosophy}
The board is divided into three physical zones:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Board 1 (Left):} The 5-Year Vision Arc, core technical challenges, and the unifying theme of the lab.
    \item \textbf{Board 2 (Center):} Three Specific Aims, showing experimental pathways, preliminary data placeholders, and key readouts.
    \item \textbf{Board 3 (Right):} Funding roadmap, personnel recruitment pipeline, and lab integration within the department.
\end{itemize}
