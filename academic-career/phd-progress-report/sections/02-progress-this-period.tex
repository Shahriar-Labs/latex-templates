\section{Progress This Period}

\textbf{Literature \& baseline.}\enspace
Completed a systematic review of 78 papers on federated NLP and clinical text mining.
Established a centralised (non-private) fine-tuning baseline: Bio-BERT achieves
F\textsubscript{1}\,=\,0.847 on NER of clinical entities in MIMIC-III held-out test
set, confirming the upper bound for subsequent federated experiments.

\textbf{DP-Fed implementation.}\enspace
Implemented \textsc{DP-FedAvg} with Gaussian noise mechanism in PyTorch +
\textsc{Flower} (fl.dev). Ran ablations over privacy budgets
$\varepsilon \in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}$ with $\delta = 10^{-5}$.
At $\varepsilon = 4$ the federated model recovers 96.1\% of baseline F\textsubscript{1}.

\textbf{Audit prototype.}\enspace
Drafted design for a zero-knowledge proof–based audit log;
cryptographic primitives selected; implementation begins Q3 2025.
