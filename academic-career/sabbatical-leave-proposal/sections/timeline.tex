\section{Work Plan \& Deliverables Timeline}
The 10-month sabbatical is structured into four distinct phases, transitioning from theoretical formulation to physical validation and final reporting.

\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\caption{Project Phases and Milestones}
\label{tab:phases}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l l X}
\toprule
\textbf{Phase} & \textbf{Timeframe} & \textbf{Key Focus \& Milestones} \\
\midrule
Phase 1 & Sept -- Nov 2026 & Mathematical formulation of decentralized policy aggregation; simulation setup in PyBullet. \\
Phase 2 & Dec -- Feb 2027 & Implementation of federated safety constraints; initial simulation testing and baseline comparison. \\
Phase 3 & Mar -- May 2027 & Physical deployment on the VRI quadcopter fleet; parameter tuning and data collection. \\
Phase 4 & June 2027 & Final documentation, open-source repository release, and manuscript preparation for IEEE Trans. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
