\section{Practical Advice and Conclusion}
\label{sec:practical_tips}

For students considering an exchange at Meridian University of Science, thorough preparation is key to a successful experience. Below are practical guidelines based on my time in Zurich.

\subsection{Housing and Finance}
Housing in Zurich is highly competitive. I strongly recommend applying for student housing through the university's housing office immediately upon receiving your acceptance. The student residences, such as those in the Meridian Student Village, are well-maintained and offer a lower rent than private apartments.

On average, my monthly budget was structured as follows:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Rent (Student Village):} 750 CHF
  \item \textbf{Food and Groceries:} 450 CHF
  \item \textbf{Health Insurance (Swiss student rate):} 90 CHF
  \item \textbf{Public Transport (ZVV Pass):} 60 CHF
  \item \textbf{Miscellaneous \& Travel:} 450 CHF
\end{itemize}
This total of approximately 1,800 CHF per month was supported by the Nexa Global Scholarship, which allowed me to focus on my studies without financial strain.

\subsection{Exchange Checklist}
To ensure all critical administrative steps are completed, refer to the following guide:

\vspace{5pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[
  colback=lightgray,
  colframe=primarycolor,
  arc=4pt,
  boxrule=1pt,
  title=\sffamily\bfseries\color{white}\faCheckCircle\quad Mandatory Administrative Steps
]
  \begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item Apply for the \textbf{Nexa Global Scholarship} or other funding at least 6 months prior to departure.
    \item Register for housing immediately upon receiving the admission letter from \textbf{Meridian University}.
    \item Apply for the Swiss student visa early, ensuring all financial guarantees are in order.
    \item Budget approximately 1,800 to 2,200 CHF per month for living expenses in Zurich.
    \item Register with the resident services department (\emph{Kreisbüro}) within 14 days of arrival.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Conclusion}
My exchange semester at Meridian University of Science was an incredibly enriching experience. Academically, it exposed me to rigorous methodologies and advanced computing concepts that have prepared me well for graduate-level research. Culturally, it widened my perspective and allowed me to establish a network of lifelong peers. I strongly encourage any eligible student at Apex Institute of Technology to take advantage of this life-changing opportunity.
