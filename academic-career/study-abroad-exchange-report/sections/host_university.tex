\section{The Host Institution: Meridian University of Science}
\label{sec:host_university}

Established as a leading center for technological innovation, \textbf{Meridian University of Science} is consistently ranked among the top research institutions globally. The university operates across two primary campuses in Zurich: the historic City Campus, integrated into the urban center, and the modern Heights Campus, situated on a scenic plateau overlooking the city.

\subsection{Campus Infrastructure and Facilities}
Most of my academic work took place at the Heights Campus, which serves as the center for sciences, engineering, and digital technologies. The campus resembles a self-contained academic city, offering:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The Synapse Research Lab:} A state-of-the-art facility where students and researchers collaborate on high-performance computing and neural network development.
  \item \textbf{The Vertex Innovation Hub:} An incubator space supporting student startups and industry partnerships, hosting regular hackathons and networking events.
  \item \textbf{The Central Science Library:} A multi-story architectural masterpiece providing quiet study zones, group project rooms, and extensive digital and print resources.
\end{itemize}

The campus is fully integrated with Zurich's public transit network, making it easily accessible via commuter trains and dedicated shuttle buses. The dining halls (\emph{Mensen}) offer affordable, high-quality meals with options catering to various dietary requirements, which is a major benefit for students on a budget.

\subsection{Student Life and Integration}
Integration into the local student community was highly facilitated by the Meridian Student Union. The union sponsors dozens of interest groups, ranging from the robotics club to outdoor sports societies. Throughout the semester, I participated in weekly hiking excursions in the nearby alpine regions organized by the university's mountaineering club, which provided an excellent opportunity to bond with both Swiss and international students.
