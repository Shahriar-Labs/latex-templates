\section{Introduction and Preparation}
\label{sec:introduction}

The decision to pursue an academic exchange semester at \textbf{Meridian University of Science} was motivated by a desire to challenge myself in a world-class academic environment and experience the rich cultural tapestry of Switzerland. As a Computer Science major at the \textbf{Apex Institute of Technology}, I sought an institution that could offer advanced coursework in distributed systems and machine learning while exposing me to international research paradigms.

The preparation process began approximately nine months prior to departure. Securing approval from my home academic advisor required detailed mapping of course equivalencies to ensure that credits obtained abroad would transfer seamlessly. A significant milestone during this phase was receiving the \textbf{Nexa Global Scholarship}, which provided crucial financial support and covered travel expenses, helping ease the transition to Zurich's high-cost living environment.

The orientation week organized by the Meridian Student Association was invaluable. It not only facilitated administrative tasks---such as obtaining student transit passes and setting up health insurance---but also introduced exchange students to the city and campus through guided tours and social events.
