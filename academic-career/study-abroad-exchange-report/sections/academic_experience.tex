\section{Academic Experience and Comparison}
\label{sec:academic_experience}

The academic environment at Meridian University of Science is highly rigorous and requires a shift in study habits compared to Apex Institute of Technology. The primary difference lies in the level of student autonomy and the structure of grading and evaluations.

\subsection{Course Enrollment and Content}
I enrolled in a standard course load of 24 ECTS credits, consisting of the following modules:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{CS-402: Advanced Distributed Systems (8 ECTS):} A deep dive into consensus algorithms (Paxos, Raft), fault tolerance, and large-scale storage architectures. The course required completing three extensive programming projects in Go, which were graded on correctness and performance.
  \item \textbf{DS-501: Statistical Machine Learning (8 ECTS):} Covered statistical learning theory, support vector machines, clustering algorithms, and deep neural networks. The course had a heavy mathematical focus, emphasizing proofs alongside practical implementations.
  \item \textbf{HU-101: Swiss German Language and Culture (3 ECTS):} An interactive course that covered basic local vocabulary and cultural norms, which was highly useful for daily interactions outside the university.
  \item \textbf{CS-495: Independent Research Project (5 ECTS):} Conducted in collaboration with the \textbf{Synapse Research Lab}, focusing on optimizing decentralized ledger systems. This work contributed to a broader study on academic mobility systems \cite{mercer2025systems}.
\end{itemize}
