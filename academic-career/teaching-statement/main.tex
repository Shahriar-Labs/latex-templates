\begin{summarybox}
  \noindent\textbf{Teaching Philosophy:} My teaching philosophy is grounded in active learning, cognitive scaffolding, and inclusive mentorship. I aim to demystify complex computer science concepts by building intuitive, hands-on bridges between abstract mathematical theory and real-world system implementations. As an educator, my goal is to equip students from diverse backgrounds with the analytical tools and confidence to become independent thinkers and future leaders in technology.
\end{summarybox}
