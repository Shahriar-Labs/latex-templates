\subsection{Strategic Partnerships}
In addition to federal and foundation grants, I will seek industrial research partnerships:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Apex Semiconductors:} To secure low-cost fabrication runs and multi-project wafer (MPW) access.
    \item \textbf{Vertex Technology Consortium:} To validate our chips on their autonomous hardware testbeds.
    \item \textbf{Synapse Neural Devices:} For collaborative development of low-power sensor interfaces.
\end{itemize}
These partnerships will ensure that the academic discoveries in our lab translate directly into commercial edge devices.
