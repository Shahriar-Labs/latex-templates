\subsection{Shallow bearing resistance}
At a notional founding depth $D_f=\SI{1.8}{\metre}$ within the soft--firm clay, the undrained
net ultimate bearing resistance for a wide footing is screened as
\begin{equation}
q_{\mathrm{net,ult}} = N_c c_u = 5.14(30) = \SI{154}{\kilo\pascal}.
\end{equation}
With a global factor of safety of 3 and overburden $\gamma D_f$, the corresponding gross
allowable pressure is only
\begin{equation}
q_{\mathrm{allow,gross}}
=\frac{154}{3}+18(1.8) \approx \SI{84}{\kilo\pascal}.
\end{equation}
This is a bearing-capacity screen only; settlement governs at an even lower practical pressure.
Footings near excavations or sloping ground would also require reduced resistance.

\subsection{Raft settlement screen}
For a \SI{2.7}{\metre} thick consolidating clay layer, the one-dimensional primary consolidation
estimate is
\begin{equation}
S_c=\frac{C_c H}{1+e_0}\log_{10}\!\left(
\frac{\sigma'_0+\Delta\sigma'}{\sigma'_0}\right).
\label{eq:consolidation}
\end{equation}
Using $C_c=0.24$, $H=\SI{2.7}{\metre}$, $e_0=0.80$,
$\sigma'_0=\SI{58}{\kilo\pascal}$ and average
$\Delta\sigma'=\SI{42}{\kilo\pascal}$ gives
\begin{equation}
S_c=\frac{0.24(2.7)}{1.80}\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{100}{58}\right)
=\SI{0.085}{\metre}\approx\SI{85}{\milli\metre}.
\end{equation}
Immediate settlement and construction variability would add to this value. The untreated raft
therefore does not satisfy the adopted \SI{40}{\milli\metre} criterion.
