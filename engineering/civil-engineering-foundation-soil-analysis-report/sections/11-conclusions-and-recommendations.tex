\section{Conclusions and recommendations}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The illustrative ground model is fill to \SI{1.2}{\metre}, soft--firm clay to
        \SI{4.5}{\metre}, medium-dense silty sand to \SI{10.5}{\metre}, dense sand to
        \SI{18}{\metre}, and stiff clay with sand below. Groundwater is near
        \SI{2.2}{\metre} depth.
  \item Conventional shallow pads are unsuitable for the assumed mid-rise loads. An untreated
        raft has a screened primary consolidation settlement of about \SI{85}{\milli\metre},
        exceeding the adopted \SI{40}{\milli\metre} criterion.
  \item Use \SI{600}{\milli\metre} bored piles approximately \SI{17.5}{\metre} long, with a
        minimum \SI{5}{\metre} penetration into dense sand, as the preferred concept.
  \item Use \SI{900}{\kilo\newton} allowable axial compression per pile for preliminary layout
        only. Confirm the final resistance and load--settlement curve by a preliminary
        maintained-load test and code-compliant design.
  \item Assess group settlement, downdrag, lateral and seismic loading, pile-cap stiffness and
        construction tolerances using the final structural reactions.
  \item Manage groundwater with filtered, monitored dewatering. Design basement slabs and walls
        for the confirmed long-term water level and all temporary stages.
  \item Complete site-specific drilling, CPT, groundwater monitoring, chemical testing and
        seismic characterization before detailed design.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{decisionbox}[Design release condition]
The concept may proceed to coordinated preliminary layout. It shall \textbf{not} be released for
construction until the assumed ground model has been replaced by certified project data, pile
testing has validated the design model, and the geotechnical and structural designers have signed
the final foundation schedule.
\end{decisionbox}
