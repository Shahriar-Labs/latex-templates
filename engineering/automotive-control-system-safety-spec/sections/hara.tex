\section{Hazard Analysis and Risk Assessment}
\label{sec:hara}

A systematic Hazard Analysis and Risk Assessment (HARA) was performed in accordance with ISO 26262-3:2018~\cite{ISO26262_2018} to identify potential hazardous events resulting from malfunctions in the steer-by-wire system. Each identified hazard is analyzed across various operational situations, evaluating Severity ($S$), Probability of Exposure ($E$), and Controllability ($C$).

\subsection{Risk Classification Parameters}
The hazard classification is conducted using the standard rating levels defined below:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Severity ($S$):} Rated from $S0$ (no injury) to $S3$ (life-threatening or fatal injuries).
    \item \textbf{Exposure ($E$):} Rated from $E0$ (incredibly unlikely) to $E4$ (high probability, everyday driving).
    \item \textbf{Controllability ($C$):} Rated from $C0$ (easy to control) to $C3$ (difficult to control or uncontrollable by an average driver).
\end{itemize}
The combination of these parameters determines the Automotive Safety Integrity Level (ASIL), ranging from ASIL A (lowest) to ASIL D (highest). If a hazard does not require safety measures under ISO 26262, it is classified as Quality Management (QM).

\subsection{Safety Goals (SGs)}
Based on the identified hazards, the following Safety Goals (SGs) are established for the steering system. These goals represent the top-level safety requirements that must be met to avoid the hazards.

\begin{asilbox}{Safety Goal 1 (SG\_01): Prevent Unintended Self-Steering}
    \begin{tabular}{p{3cm} l}
        \textbf{Safety Integrity Level:} & ASIL D \\
        \textbf{Safe State:} & Disable affected actuator channel, initiate standby channel, notify driver. \\
        \textbf{FTTI:} & \(50\text{~ms}\) (Fault Tolerant Time Interval)
    \end{tabular}
\end{asilbox}

\begin{asilbox}{Safety Goal 2 (SG\_02): Prevent Complete Loss of Steering Assistance}
    \begin{tabular}{p{3cm} l}
        \textbf{Safety Integrity Level:} & ASIL D \\
        \textbf{Safe State:} & Transition to degraded mode (50\% power assist), signal visual warning. \\
        \textbf{FTTI:} & \(100\text{~ms}\)
    \end{tabular}
\end{asilbox}

\begin{asilbox}{Safety Goal 3 (SG\_03): Prevent Physical Steering Lock}
    \begin{tabular}{p{3cm} l}
        \textbf{Safety Integrity Level:} & ASIL D \\
        \textbf{Safe State:} & Mechanically decouple faulty channel, maintain main steering path. \\
        \textbf{FTTI:} & \(15\text{~ms}\) (Immediate response required)
    \end{tabular}
\end{asilbox}
