\section{Mitigation Measures}

\subsection{Mitigation Hierarchy}

\subsection{Environmental Management Plan}
An overarching Environmental Management Plan (EMP) will be developed pre-construction, incorporating all mitigation commitments from this EIA, together with monitoring protocols, reporting thresholds, and a non-compliance and corrective-action procedure. The EMP will be a live document, reviewed quarterly by the Ecological Clerk of Works and the Developer's project manager.
