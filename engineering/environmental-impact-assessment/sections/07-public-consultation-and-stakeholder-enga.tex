\section{Public Consultation and Stakeholder Engagement}

\subsection{Consultation Programme}
A comprehensive public consultation programme was undertaken in accordance with the Scottish Government's Planning Advice Note 3/2010. The programme comprised:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Scoping Opinion (Feb 2025):} Formal scoping request submitted to The Highland Council; responses received from 8 statutory consultees.
  \item \textbf{Public Exhibitions (Mar 2025):} Two public exhibitions held at Glencoe Village Hall and Fort William Library (combined attendance: 147).
  \item \textbf{Community Council Briefings:} Presentations delivered to Glencoe and Fort William Community Councils.
  \item \textbf{Online Portal:} Project website and interactive map launched January 2025; 2,340 unique visits by July 2026.
  \item \textbf{Statutory Consultation:} Consultation under Section~36 of the Electricity Act 1989 with Scottish Ministers, Scottish Natural Heritage, SEPA, and Transport Scotland.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Key Themes from Consultation}
Written submissions were received from 36 individuals and 5 organisations. The principal themes raised during consultation included visual impact on the Glen Coe landscape (19 submissions), noise disturbance during construction (8), impact on golden eagle and raptor populations (14), traffic disruption on the A82 (11), and peatland disturbance and carbon balance (7). All themes have been addressed within this EIA.
