\plainone{\TxGain}\,dBi antennas and \dBm{\TxPower} transmitter output, the clear-air
received signal is \dBm{\ReceivedPower}.  The value remains \dB{\RainResidual}

licensed-frequency coordination, interference analysis, and confirmation of the
radio vendor's guaranteed power and threshold data.
