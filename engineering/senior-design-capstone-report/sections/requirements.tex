\section{Engineering Requirements}
\label{sec:requirements}

Requirements were elicited from three stakeholder groups --- \ProjectSponsor's
operations lead, Meridian Campus Safety, and the dining-services pilot
partner --- and translated into testable engineering specifications using a
standard House-of-Quality pass. Table~\ref{tab:requirements} lists the
requirements carried into detailed design, each tagged with the verification
method and its status at the end of the spring test campaign.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Top-level engineering requirements and verification status.}
\label{tab:requirements}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.9cm}Xllc@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Target} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Status} \\
\midrule
R-01 & Payload capacity & $\geq 6.0$\,kg & Static load test & \reqstatus{2} \\
R-02 & Payload thermal control & $4\text{--}60\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ zone & Chamber trial & \reqstatus{2} \\
R-03 & Route endurance on one charge & $\geq 8$ operating hours & Field endurance run & \reqstatus{2} \\
R-04 & Cruising speed & $1.4\text{--}2.0$\,m/s & GPS-logged run & \reqstatus{2} \\

R-07 and R-12 are marked \reqstatus{1} (Partial): the navigation success rate
reached 93.3\% against a 95\% target (Section~\ref{sec:testing}), and battery
swap alone met the 5-minute budget but combined battery-plus-bay servicing
averaged 5\,min 40\,s in technician trials. Both are carried forward as
punch-list items in Section~\ref{sec:conclusion}. The R-09 ingress rating
follows the IP-code enclosure test procedure of \citet{iec_ip_rating}, and
the platform's autonomy scope was bounded against the SAE On-Road Driving
Automation taxonomy \citep{sae_j3016_2021} at the sidewalk-robot equivalent
of Level 4, operator-supervised, geofenced to the three pilot routes.
