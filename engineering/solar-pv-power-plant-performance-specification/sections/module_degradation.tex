\section{PV Module Degradation Requirements}

\subsection{Degradation Rate Limits}

Module power output shall not degrade below the thresholds specified in Table~\ref{tab:degradation}.
Degradation is measured relative to nameplate \textsc{stc} power (\perfval{370}{\watt_p} per
module) as confirmed by the flash-test certificate issued at factory acceptance.

\subsection{Measurement Protocol}

Degradation shall be quantified using the \textbf{In-Situ I-V Curve} method (IEC~61829) conducted
during clear-sky conditions (\textsc{poa} irradiance $\geq \SI{800}{\watt\per\meter\squared}$,
module temperature within $\SI{25}{\celsius} \pm \SI{5}{\celsius}$ of standard). Measurements are
to be performed:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item At commissioning baseline (Year~0), within 30~days of \textsc{cod}.
  \item Annually during the O\&M scheduled shutdown in April.
  \item Within 14~days of any hail or wind event exceeding the module's \textsc{iec}
        environmental classification.
  \item On a statistical sample of not less than \highlight{5\%} of total modules per year,
        with stratified sampling across all \textsc{dc} combiner zones.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Soiling Correction}

All degradation measurements shall apply a soiling loss correction factor $S_f$ derived
from the monthly soiling sensor readings. The corrected power $P_{\text{corr}}$ is:

\[
  P_{\text{corr}} = \frac{P_{\text{meas}}}{1 - S_f}
\]

where $S_f$ is the fraction of irradiance blocked by soiling (dimensionless). The long-term
site average soiling loss is modelled at \highlight{\SI{1.8}{\percent}} based on the Nexa
field campaign conducted in Year~0 (Nexa Report \textsc{mer-sl-2025-04}).

\subsection{Electroluminescence Inspection}

A full-plant electroluminescence (\textsc{el}) imaging campaign shall be conducted:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item At Year~1, Year~5, Year~10, Year~15, and Year~20.
  \item Following any event that triggers string-level power reduction $\geq 3\%$ below
        fleet median.
\end{itemize}

Modules exhibiting \textbf{crack propagation} affecting $>20\%$ of cell area, \textbf{inactive
cell regions} $>10\%$, or evidence of \textbf{potential-induced degradation (\textsc{pid})} at
$> 5\%$ module level shall be flagged for replacement within the next scheduled maintenance
window, not to exceed 90~days.
