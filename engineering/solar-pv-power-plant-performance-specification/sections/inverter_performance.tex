\section{Inverter Performance Requirements}

\subsection{Efficiency Specifications}

Each \textbf{Nimbus NX-3400} central inverter shall demonstrate the weighted efficiency values
specified in Table~\ref{tab:inverter_eff}. Weighted European Efficiency ($\eta_{\textsc{eu}}$)
and CEC Efficiency are the primary acceptance metrics.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \caption{Inverter Efficiency Requirements — Nimbus NX-3400}
  \label{tab:inverter_eff}
  \begin{tabular}{@{} l S[table-format=2.2] S[table-format=2.2] l @{}}
    \toprule
    \rowcolor{SolarMist}
    \textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Required (\%)} & \textbf{Tested Value (\%)} & \textbf{Status} \\
    \midrule
    Peak Efficiency ($\eta_{\max}$)        & 98.50 & 98.71 & \textcolor{green!60!black}{\textbf{Pass}} \\
    European Weighted ($\eta_{\textsc{eu}}$) & 97.80 & 98.02 & \textcolor{green!60!black}{\textbf{Pass}} \\
    CEC Efficiency                         & 97.50 & 97.88 & \textcolor{green!60!black}{\textbf{Pass}} \\
    Night-time Standby Power               & \multicolumn{2}{c}{$\leq \SI{50}{\watt}$}
                                           & \textcolor{green!60!black}{\textbf{Pass}} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The European Weighted Efficiency is calculated per IEC~61683:

\[
  \eta_{\textsc{eu}} = 0.03\,\eta_{5\%} + 0.06\,\eta_{10\%} + 0.13\,\eta_{20\%}
                     + 0.10\,\eta_{30\%} + 0.48\,\eta_{50\%} + 0.20\,\eta_{100\%}
\]

where $\eta_{x\%}$ denotes inverter efficiency at $x\%$ of rated \textsc{ac} output power.

\subsection{Availability Requirements}

Inverter availability $A_{\text{inv}}$ is defined as:

\[
  A_{\text{inv}} = \frac{T_{\text{total}} - T_{\text{unplanned}}}{T_{\text{total}}} \times 100\%
\]

where $T_{\text{unplanned}}$ excludes grid curtailment events and scheduled maintenance windows.
The minimum contractual availability floor is \highlight{\SI{98.5}{\percent}} per inverter per
rolling 12-month period.

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\linewidth}
\reqbox{Availability Triggers}{%
  If \( A_{\text{inv}} < 98.5\% \) for any single inverter in a 90-day window, Nimbus Field
  Services shall dispatch a Level~2 technician within \textbf{24~hours}.
  Sustained underperformance $(<97\%$ over 6~months) triggers a hardware audit and potential
  unit replacement at EPC cost.
}
\end{minipage}%
\hfill%
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\linewidth}
\reqbox{Response Time SLA}{%
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
    \item Critical fault: \textbf{4~hr} remote, 24~hr on-site.
    \item Minor fault: 48~hr remote, 5~day on-site.
    \item Scheduled PM: 30-day advance notice.
  \end{itemize}
}
\end{minipage}

\subsection{Harmonic Distortion \& Grid Compliance}

Total Harmonic Distortion (\textsc{thd}) at the point of common coupling (\textsc{pcc}) shall
not exceed \highlight{3\%} under full load, in conformance with IEEE~519-2022. Each inverter
shall incorporate active anti-islanding protection compliant with AS~4777.2:2020.
