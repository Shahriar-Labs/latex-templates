\section{Monitoring, Data Acquisition \& Reporting}

\subsection{SCADA System Requirements}

The plant's Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (\textsc{scada}) system shall be supplied
by \textbf{Apex Digital Systems} and shall meet the requirements of IEC~61724-1:2021
Classification~A (highest accuracy tier). Key specifications:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Data resolution}: \SI{1}{\minute} intervals for all primary sensors;
        \SI{15}{\minute} intervals for energy meters.
  \item \textbf{Data availability}: $\geq 99.0\%$ uptime of the data acquisition backbone
        over any rolling 30-day period.
  \item \textbf{Time synchronisation}: \textsc{ntp}-referenced, $\leq \SI{1}{\second}$ drift.
  \item \textbf{Cybersecurity}: Conformant to IEC~62443-3-3 Security Level~2.
  \item \textbf{Remote access}: Encrypted \textsc{vpn} tunnel to Nexa Engineering portal;
        read access granted to Synapse asset management team.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Reporting Requirements}

Nimbus Field Services shall deliver the following mandatory reports to Synapse Infrastructure
Holdings and Nexa Solar Energy Solutions:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Daily Report} — automated: energy generation, availability, active alarms,
        soiling index. Delivered by 07:00 local time each calendar day.

  \item \textbf{Monthly Technical Report} — within 10~business days of month end: detailed
        energy summary, \textsc{pr} calculation, inverter performance, fault log, maintenance
        completed, and financial reconciliation for \textsc{ld} tracking.

  \item \textbf{Annual Performance Report} — within 30~business days of year-end anniversary:
        full degradation assessment, shading analysis update, 5-year energy forecast revision,
        and warranty claim status.

  \item \textbf{Event-Triggered Report} — within 48~hours of: single-inverter downtime
        $>4$~hours, \textsc{scada} data loss $>2$~hours, grid fault or curtailment event
        $>1\%$ of daily energy budget, or any physical security incident.
\end{enumerate}

\reqbox{Data Retention Obligation}{%
  All raw \SI{1}{\minute} \textsc{scada} data shall be retained for a minimum of \textbf{25~years}
  in a geographically redundant cloud archive (Apex Cloud Vaulting Service or equivalent).
  Synapse Infrastructure Holdings retains unrestricted rights to all operational data generated
  at the Meridian Flats site.
}
