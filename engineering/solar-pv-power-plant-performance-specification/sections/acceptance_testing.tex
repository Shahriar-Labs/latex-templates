\section{Acceptance Testing \& Sign-Off Criteria}

\subsection{Commissioning Test Programme}

Prior to Commercial Operation Date (\textsc{cod}), the following acceptance tests shall be
completed under the supervision of Apex Technical Advisory as Independent Engineer:

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \caption{Commissioning Acceptance Test Programme}
  \label{tab:acceptance}
  \begin{tabular}{@{} l l l c @{}}
    \toprule
    \rowcolor{SolarMist}
    \textbf{Test} & \textbf{Standard} & \textbf{Pass Criterion} & \textbf{Mandatory} \\
    \midrule
    Module flash test (sample 5\%)  & IEC~61829       & Within $\pm2\%$ of nameplate & Yes \\
    Thermal imaging (IR scan)       & IEC~62446-3     & No class-C hotspots          & Yes \\

\end{table}

\subsection{Performance Acceptance Test (\textsc{pat})}

A formal Performance Acceptance Test shall be conducted within 60~days of \textsc{cod} during
a continuous \textbf{7-day} test period. Acceptance criteria:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item Measured net AC energy $\geq$ P90 target for the test period (irradiance-normalised).
  \item \textsc{scada} data availability $\geq 99.0\%$ throughout the test period.
  \item No single inverter downtime exceeding 4~continuous hours during the test.
  \item Measured \textsc{pr} (7-day rolling) $\geq \highlight{79.5\%}$.
\end{itemize}

If the \textsc{pat} is failed on first attempt, Vertex Constructors has 30~days to rectify and
retest. A second failure triggers the \textsc{ld} mechanism defined in Section~5.
