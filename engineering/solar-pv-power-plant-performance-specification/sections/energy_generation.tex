\subsection{Performance Ratio Definition}

The Performance Ratio (\textsc{pr}) quantifies overall plant efficiency relative to ideal
yield for the installed capacity:

\[
  \text{PR} = \frac{E_{\text{ac,net}}}{H_{\text{poa}} \cdot P_{\text{ref,stc}} / G_{\text{stc}}}
\]

where:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item $E_{\text{ac,net}}$ — measured net AC energy delivered to the grid (\si{\kilo\watt\hour}).
  \item $H_{\text{poa}}$ — \textsc{poa} irradiation (\si{\kilo\watt\hour\per\meter\squared}).
  \item $P_{\text{ref,stc}}$ — total nameplate \textsc{dc} capacity at \textsc{stc} (\si{\kilo\watt_p}).
  \item $G_{\text{stc}}$ — reference irradiance at \textsc{stc}, \SI{1.0}{\kilo\watt\per\meter\squared}.
\end{itemize}

The contractual \textsc{pr} floor in Year~1 is \highlight{\SI{79.5}{\percent}} on a rolling
12-month basis, declining by \highlight{\SI{0.3}{\percent}} per year thereafter.

\subsection{Liquidated Damages}

If the measured 12-month rolling net \textsc{ac} energy falls below the \textsc{p90} threshold
in any Year~1--5, Nexa Solar Energy Solutions (as \textsc{epc} warranty administrator) shall
trigger liquidated damages as follows:

\[
  \text{LD} = \bigl(E_{\text{target,P90}} - E_{\text{measured}}\bigr)
              \times \$\,95\,/\,\si{\mega\watt\hour}
\]

Maximum \textsc{ld} exposure is capped at \highlight{15\%} of the total \textsc{epc} contract
value (\$AUD~47.3\,M). \textsc{ld} payments are calculated annually and invoiced by
Synapse Infrastructure Holdings to Vertex Constructors Pty Ltd within 30~days of the
anniversary of \textsc{cod}.
