\section{Shading Analysis \& Loss Budget}

\subsection{Methodology}

Shading analysis was performed using \textbf{Nexa SolarSim v4.3} with 3D terrain and obstacle
modelling derived from \textsc{lidar} survey data (point density \SI{8}{\per\meter\squared}).
The analysis distinguishes three loss categories per IEC~61724-1:2021 Annex~A:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=3pt, label=\lbrack\arabic*\rbrack]
  \item \textbf{Near shading} — inter-row, fence, and inverter shelter obstructions within
        the plant boundary.
  \item \textbf{Far shading} — topographic horizon profile derived from 30~m \textsc{dem}.
  \item \textbf{Electrical mismatch} — string-level power reduction from partial shading
        on non-bypass-diode-isolated cells.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Shading Loss Budget Summary}

The total shading-related loss budget is summarised below. All values are expressed as a
percentage of annual \textsc{ghi} in-plane irradiation (\perfval{1977}{\kilo\watt\hour\per\meter\squared\per\yr}).

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{} l r r @{}}
  \toprule
  \rowcolor{SolarMist}
  \textbf{Loss Component}        & \textbf{Energy Loss (\%)} & \textbf{Limit (\%)} \\
  \midrule
  Inter-row beam shading         & 1.24  & 1.50 \\
  Inter-row diffuse shading      & 1.70  & 2.00 \\
  Far horizon shading            & 0.18  & 0.25 \\
  Electrical mismatch (shading)  & 0.35  & 0.50 \\
  \midrule
  \rowcolor{SolarMist}
  \textbf{Total Shading Loss}    & \textbf{3.47}  & \textbf{4.25} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The modelled total shading loss of \highlight{3.47\%} is within the contractual limit of
\highlight{4.25\%}. If measured annual shading loss (derived from \textsc{scada} irradiance
comparison) exceeds 4.25\%, Vertex Constructors shall conduct a structural alignment audit of
all tracker or fixed-tilt mounting systems within 60~days.
