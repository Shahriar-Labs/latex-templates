\section{Introduction \& Project Scope}

This Performance Specification establishes the technical requirements, acceptance criteria, and
monitoring obligations for the \textbf{Meridian Flats Solar Park} — a ground-mounted
photovoltaic (PV) power plant rated at \perfval{48.6}{\mega\watt_{p}} (\textsc{dc}) /
\perfval{40}{\mega\watt} (\textsc{ac}), located in the Meridian Basin, New South Highlands.
The document governs the relationship between \textbf{Nexa Solar Energy Solutions} (Engineer of
Record) and the Project Owner, \textbf{Synapse Infrastructure Holdings}, throughout the plant's
operational lifetime.

\subsection{Document Purpose}

The specification defines:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item Module-level and string-level degradation limits and measurement protocols.
  \item Inverter efficiency requirements and maximum allowable downtime thresholds.
  \item Shading analysis methodology and permissible energy losses attributable to
        inter-row and near-horizon obstructions.
  \item Annual and lifetime energy generation targets with associated performance ratio
        (\textsc{pr}) floors.
  \item Data acquisition (\textsc{scada}) requirements and reporting cadences.
  \item Warranty and liquidated damages (\textsc{ld}) triggers.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Plant Configuration Summary}

The Meridian Flats Solar Park is configured as follows: \perfval{132}{}~strings of
\perfval{30}{}~\textbf{Vertex VS-370M} monofacial PERC modules (bifacial gain excluded from
base-case \textsc{p50} yield) per string, wired to \perfval{12}{}~\textbf{Nimbus NX-3400}
central inverters each rated \perfval{3333}{\kilo\watt_{ac}}. The plant operates at a
\textsc{dc}/\textsc{ac} clipping ratio of \highlight{1.215}, consistent with prevailing industry
practice for sites with the site's irradiance profile (\perfval{5.42}{\kilo\watt\hour\per\meter\squared\per\day} average \textsc{ghi}).
