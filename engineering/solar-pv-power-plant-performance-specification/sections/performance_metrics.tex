\section{Performance Ratio and Verification Methodology}
\label{sec:performance_metrics}

\subsection{Performance Ratio Definitions}
The primary metric used to evaluate the efficiency of the power plant is the Performance Ratio (PR), which represents the ratio of the actual energy generated to the theoretical energy output under standard test conditions (STC).

\subsubsection{Uncorrected Performance Ratio}
The uncorrected Performance Ratio is calculated over a given period as follows:
\begin{equation}
    PR = \frac{E_{\text{AC}}}{P_{\text{DC}} \times \left( \frac{G_{\text{POA}}}{G_{\text{STC}}} \right)}
\end{equation}
Where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $E_{\text{AC}}$ is the measured net AC energy exported at the revenue meter (MWh).
    \item $P_{\text{DC}}$ is the installed DC nameplate capacity of the solar array (MWp).
    \item $G_{\text{POA}}$ is the plane-of-array solar irradiation accumulated over the period (kWh/m\textsuperscript{2}).
    \item $G_{\text{STC}}$ is the standard test condition irradiance (1.0\,kW/m\textsuperscript{2}).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Temperature-Corrected Performance Ratio}
To eliminate the influence of seasonal module temperature variations on performance metrics, a temperature-corrected Performance Ratio ($PR_{\text{TC}}$) shall be utilized for contractual verification:
\begin{equation}
    PR_{\text{TC}} = \frac{E_{\text{AC}}}{\sum_{t} \left[ P_{\text{DC}} \times \left( \frac{G_{\text{POA}, t}}{G_{\text{STC}}} \right) \times \left( 1 - \gamma \left( T_{\text{cell}, t} - 25 \right) \right) \right]}
\end{equation}
Where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $G_{\text{POA}, t}$ is the average POA irradiance during the time step $t$ (kW/m\textsuperscript{2}).
    \item $\gamma$ is the module temperature coefficient of power ($-0.0034/^\circ$C).
    \item $T_{\text{cell}, t}$ is the measured PV cell temperature at time step $t$ ($^\circ$C).
    \item $t$ is the measurement time step (15-minute intervals).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Performance Guarantee Test (PGT) Protocol}
A formal 14-day Performance Guarantee Test will be conducted by Vertex Infrastructure Corp and audited by Synapse Asset Management. The test will commence only after the facility achieves commercial operation and operates continuously for 72 hours without major faults. The following conditions must be met during the test:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The weather-adjusted $PR_{\text{TC}}$ must meet or exceed the guaranteed threshold of 81.5\%.
    \item Minimum cumulative irradiation of 60\,kWh/m\textsuperscript{2} must be recorded.
    \item Inverter availability must exceed 99.0\% across all operating blocks.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{warningbox}{Liquidated Damages for Performance Shortfall}
If the measured temperature-corrected Performance Ratio during the Performance Guarantee Test is below the guaranteed threshold of 81.5\%, the EPC Contractor (Vertex Infrastructure) shall have a cure period of 60 days. If the shortfall remains unresolved, liquidated damages shall be assessed at \$150,000 per 0.1\% absolute PR deficit, up to a maximum cap of 10\% of the total EPC contract price.
\end{warningbox}
