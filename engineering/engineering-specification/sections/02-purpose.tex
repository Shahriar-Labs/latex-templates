\section{Purpose}
%-----------------------------------------------------------------------

This Engineering Specification defines the technical requirements for the design, materials,
fabrication, inspection, and erection of the steel plate-girder superstructure for the
I-5 Columbia River Crossing (CRC) replacement bridge, Contract\ C-2026-0147. The document
serves as the governing technical baseline against which all deliverables, shop drawings,
test reports, and field inspections are verified.
