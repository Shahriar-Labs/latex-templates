\section{Scope}
%-----------------------------------------------------------------------

The scope of this specification encompasses:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Four continuous welded steel plate girders per span, spanning four segments of
        45\,m--60\,m--60\,m--45\,m (total bridge length\ 210\,m).
  \item Cross-frames, lateral bracing, stiffeners, bearing assemblies, and deck shear
        connectors.
  \item Structural steel conforming to ASTM\ A709\ Grade\ 50W (weathering steel).
  \item Bolted field splices, shop-welded stiffeners, and high-strength bolted connections
        per RCSC\ Specification\ (2020).
  \item Quality assurance and non-destructive examination (NDE) protocols.
\end{itemize}

Excluded from this specification: substructure components (piers, abutments, foundations),
concrete deck slab design (see\ ENG-SPEC-2026-0152), and temporary works.
