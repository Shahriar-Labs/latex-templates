\section{Representative Steel Member Checks}

\subsection{Typical secondary beam B1}
The trial W360$\times$51 beam spans \SI{6.0}{\metre}. The slab provides continuous
lateral restraint to the compression flange in the positive-moment region; connection
and construction-stage restraint must be verified. For a simple span under uniform
load,
\begin{align}
  M_u &= \frac{w_uL^2}{8}
       = \frac{27.24(6.0)^2}{8}
       = \SI{122.6}{\kilo\newton\metre},\\
  V_u &= \frac{w_uL}{2}
       = \frac{27.24(6.0)}{2}
       = \SI{81.7}{\kilo\newton}.
\end{align}
With $Z_x=\SI{895000}{\milli\metre\cubed}$ and $F_y=\SI{355}{\mega\pascal}$,
\begin{equation}
  \phi M_n=0.9F_yZ_x
  =0.9(355)(895000)10^{-6}
  =\SI{286.0}{\kilo\newton\metre}>M_u \quad \ok.
\end{equation}
Using an adopted web shear area $A_w=\SI{2540}{\milli\metre\squared}$,
\begin{equation}
  \phi V_n=1.0(0.6F_yA_w)=\SI{541}{\kilo\newton}>V_u \quad \ok.
\end{equation}
The strength utilization is $122.6/286.0=0.43$ in flexure and $81.7/541=0.15$ in
shear.

For service deflection, $I_x=\SI{141e6}{\milli\metre\tothe{4}}$ and
\begin{equation}
  \Delta_{\max}=\frac{5wL^4}{384EI}.
\end{equation}
The live-load deflection is \SI{4.3}{\milli\metre}, less than
$L/360=\SI{16.7}{\milli\metre}$. Total-load deflection is
\SI{12.1}{\milli\metre}, less than $L/240=\SI{25.0}{\milli\metre}$; both checks
\ok. Camber, vibration, ponding, and composite construction-stage checks remain
open.

\subsection{Interior girder G1}
For the preliminary equivalent uniform load on the \SI{6.0}{\metre} girder,
\begin{align}
  M_u &= \frac{54.48(6.0)^2}{8}
       = \SI{245.2}{\kilo\newton\metre},\\
  V_u &= \frac{54.48(6.0)}{2}
       = \SI{163.4}{\kilo\newton}.
\end{align}
For the trial W460$\times$74, the adopted design resistances are
$\phi M_n=\SI{540}{\kilo\newton\metre}$ and
$\phi V_n=\SI{875}{\kilo\newton}$. Utilization is therefore 0.45 in flexure and
0.19 in shear, both \ok. With $I_x=\SI{332e6}{\milli\metre\tothe{4}}$, total
service deflection is \SI{10.3}{\milli\metre}, less than
$L/240=\SI{25.0}{\milli\metre}$, \ok.

\subsection{Interior column C1}
The base column gravity take-down uses one occupied suspended floor, the roof,
column self-weight, and the applicable variable actions. The representative
second-order frame-analysis envelope is
\begin{equation}
  P_u=\SI{410}{\kilo\newton},\qquad
  M_{ux}=\SI{72}{\kilo\newton\metre},\qquad
  M_{uy}=\SI{28}{\kilo\newton\metre}.
\end{equation}
For W310$\times$60, $A_g=\SI{7640}{\milli\metre\squared}$,
$r_y=\SI{51}{\milli\metre}$, and $KL=\SI{3600}{\milli\metre}$:
\begin{equation}
  \frac{KL}{r_y}=70.6, \qquad
  F_e=\frac{\pi^2E}{(KL/r)^2}=\SI{395}{\mega\pascal}.
\end{equation}
The adopted inelastic buckling result is $F_{cr}=\SI{244}{\mega\pascal}$, giving
\begin{equation}
  \phi P_n=0.9F_{cr}A_g=\SI{1680}{\kilo\newton}.
\end{equation}
Using adopted flexural resistances
$\phi M_{nx}=\SI{480}{\kilo\newton\metre}$ and
$\phi M_{ny}=\SI{210}{\kilo\newton\metre}$, the compression-flexure interaction is
\begin{align}
  \mathcal{I}
  &=\frac{P_u}{\phi P_n}
  +\frac{8}{9}\left(
    \frac{M_{ux}}{\phi M_{nx}}+
    \frac{M_{uy}}{\phi M_{ny}}\right) \\
  &=\frac{410}{1680}
  +\frac{8}{9}\left(\frac{72}{480}+\frac{28}{210}\right)
  =0.50 < 1.00 \quad \ok.
\end{align}
Column splices, panel zones, base plates, local slenderness, fire protection, and
frame out-of-plumb effects require project design.

\subsection{Diagonal brace BR1}
The governing amplified brace compression from the illustrative lateral analysis is
$P_u=\SI{245}{\kilo\newton}$. For HSS152$\times$152$\times$8,
$A_g=\SI{4420}{\milli\metre\squared}$, $r_{\min}=\SI{58}{\milli\metre}$, and an
adopted effective length $KL=\SI{7000}{\milli\metre}$:
\begin{equation}
  KL/r=121,\qquad \phi P_n=\SI{465}{\kilo\newton}.
\end{equation}
Compression utilization is $245/465=0.53$, \ok. The gross-section tension
resistance exceeds the compression resistance. Net-section fracture, connection
eccentricity, gusset yielding and buckling, block shear, brace ductility, and
seismic compactness are not demonstrated and remain mandatory project checks.
