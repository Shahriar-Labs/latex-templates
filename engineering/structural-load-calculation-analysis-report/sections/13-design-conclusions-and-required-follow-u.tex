\section{Design Conclusions and Required Follow-Up}

\subsection{Conclusions for the worked example}
Under the stated illustrative assumptions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the W360$\times$51 secondary beam and W460$\times$74 girder pass the shown
    strength and vertical-deflection checks;
  \item the W310$\times$60 interior column passes the shown compression-flexure
    interaction check;
  \item the HSS152$\times$152$\times$8 brace passes the shown gross-member axial
    compression check;
  \item seismic action governs the global lateral base-shear comparison;
  \item the illustrative amplified storey drift is below the stated limit; and
  \item the \SI{1.50}{\metre} square preliminary footing passes the shown bearing,
    flexure, one-way shear, and punching-shear screens.
\end{itemize}

Passing these representative checks does not establish adequacy of the complete
structure. It only demonstrates internal consistency of the selected calculation
examples.

\subsection{Items required before any project use}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Confirm occupancy, Risk Category, grids, elevations, framing, openings,
    equipment, facade, fire rating, and performance objectives from coordinated
    architectural and building-services documents.
  \item Obtain AHJ-confirmed code editions, local amendments, site wind and snow
    parameters, drainage design, seismic spectra and site class, flood data, and a
    project geotechnical report.
  \item Complete a three-dimensional analysis with actual member properties,
    diaphragm behaviour, torsion, second-order effects, pattern loading, construction
    stages, and verified load combinations.
  \item Design every beam, girder, column, brace, slab/deck, diaphragm, collector,
    connection, splice, base plate, anchor, pedestal, and foundation, including
    robustness and progressive-collapse requirements where applicable.
  \item Check vibration, facade compatibility, wind drift and acceleration, fire,
    fatigue where relevant, durability, thermal movement, ponding, temporary works,
    erection stability, and special inspection requirements.
  \item Perform an independent calculation check, resolve all review comments, and
    obtain the signed and sealed approval of the engineer of record.
\end{enumerate}
