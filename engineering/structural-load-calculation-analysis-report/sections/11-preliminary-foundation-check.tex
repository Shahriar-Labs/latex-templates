\section{Preliminary Foundation Check}

\subsection{Service bearing}
For the interior column, the service load take-down is approximated as
\begin{align}
  P_D &= \SI{202}{\kilo\newton}, &
  P_L &= \SI{86.4}{\kilo\newton}, &
  P_S &= \SI{27.0}{\kilo\newton}.
\end{align}
A \(\SI{1.50}{\metre}\times\SI{1.50}{\metre}\) footing, \SI{0.45}{\metre} thick,
weighs approximately \SI{24.3}{\kilo\newton}. The service bearing pressure is
\begin{equation}
  q_s=\frac{202+86.4+27.0+24.3}{1.50(1.50)}
     =\SI{151}{\kilo\pascal}
     <q_a=\SI{180}{\kilo\pascal}\quad \ok.
\end{equation}
This is a concentric bearing check. Actual minimum and maximum pressures shall
include biaxial moment, load eccentricity, overturning, uplift, and load combinations
consistent with the geotechnical definition of allowable bearing.

\subsection{Footing flexure and shear}
Take a \SI{350}{\milli\metre} square column, factored soil pressure
$q_u=\SI{188}{\kilo\pascal}$, and effective depth
$d=\SI{367}{\milli\metre}$. The cantilever projection is
$\ell=(1.50-0.35)/2=\SI{0.575}{\metre}$. Per metre width,
\begin{equation}
  M_u=\frac{q_u\ell^2}{2}=\frac{188(0.575)^2}{2}
     =\SI{31.1}{\kilo\newton\metre\per\metre}.
\end{equation}
Strength calculation requires less steel than the adopted minimum distributed
reinforcement. Provide, for this example, T16 bars at \SI{240}{\milli\metre}
centres each way at the bottom, giving
$A_s=\SI{838}{\milli\metre\squared\per\metre}$.

At the punching perimeter $d/2$ from the column face, the adopted demand is
$V_u=\SI{298}{\kilo\newton}$, $b_o=\SI{2868}{\milli\metre}$, and
\begin{equation}
  v_u=\frac{V_u}{b_od}=\SI{0.283}{\mega\pascal}
  <\phi v_c=\SI{1.36}{\mega\pascal}\quad \ok.
\end{equation}
The one-way shear stress is \SI{0.11}{\mega\pascal}, below the adopted design
resistance \SI{0.70}{\mega\pascal}, \ok. These checks do not constitute a final
foundation design. Settlement, sliding, frost or scour depth, groundwater,
liquefaction, soil variability, adjacent foundations, property lines, anchor rods,
pedestals, development length, cover, and durability remain open.
