\section{Environmental Loads}

\subsection{Wind action}
The wind calculation is intentionally a screening-level MWFRS calculation. The
assumed basic wind speed is $V=\SI{45}{\metre\per\second}$, with $K_z=0.85$ at the
roof, directionality factor $K_d=0.85$, topographic factor $K_{zt}=1.0$, and air
density coefficient \num{0.613}. Thus,
\begin{equation}
  q_z = 0.613 K_z K_{zt} K_d V^2
      = 0.613(0.85)(1.0)(0.85)(45)^2
      = \SI{0.90}{\kilo\pascal}.
\end{equation}
After applying assumed external and internal pressure effects, the adopted net
design pressure is \SI{1.05}{\kilo\pascal}. Applied to the two projected elevations,
\begin{align}
  W_X &= 1.05(12)(7.2) = \SI{90.7}{\kilo\newton}, \\
  W_Y &= 1.05(18)(7.2) = \SI{136.1}{\kilo\newton}.
\end{align}

and cladding deflection require separate project checks.

\subsection{Seismic action}
\label{sec:seismic}
The example assumes $S_{DS}=0.50g$, response modification coefficient $R=3.0$,
importance factor $I_e=1.0$, and the lower-bound preliminary coefficient
\begin{equation}
  C_s = \frac{S_{DS}}{R/I_e} = \frac{0.50}{3.0}=0.167.
\end{equation}
The assumed effective seismic weights are assembled as follows:
\begin{align}
  w_1 &= (4.25)(216) + \text{facade/framing allowance}
        + 0.25(2.40)(216) = \SI{1298}{\kilo\newton},\\
  w_r &= (1.15)(216) + \text{parapet/plant allowance}
        + 0.20S(216) = \SI{430}{\kilo\newton},\\
  W &= w_1+w_r = \SI{1728}{\kilo\newton}.
\end{align}
The equivalent base shear is therefore
\begin{equation}
  V=C_sW=0.167(1728)=\SI{288}{\kilo\newton}.
\end{equation}
For the short-period illustrative structure, $k=1.0$ is assumed and
\begin{equation}
  F_x = V\frac{w_xh_x^k}{\sum_i w_ih_i^k}.
\end{equation}

The corresponding base overturning moment is
\begin{equation}
  M_o=114.8(7.2)+173.2(3.6)=\SI{1450}{\kilo\newton\metre}.
\end{equation}
This is not a site seismic study. Site class, mapped spectral ordinates,
multi-period spectra, period limits, redundancy, accidental torsion, orthogonal
effects, vertical seismic effect, diaphragm forces, collector forces, anchorage,
overturning, and system-specific detailing must be established for the actual site.

\subsection{Hazard screening}
Flood, tsunami, tornado, atmospheric ice, earth pressure, hydrostatic pressure, and
impact loads are taken as not applicable only for this example. Snow drift, rain
ponding, thermal action, differential movement, and accidental action remain open.
No screened hazard may be omitted from a real design without documented site and
code evidence.
