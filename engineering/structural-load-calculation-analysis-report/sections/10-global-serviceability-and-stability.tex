\section{Global Serviceability and Stability}

The illustrative elastic lateral analysis produced displacements
$\delta_{e,1}=\SI{5}{\milli\metre}$ at Level~1 and
$\delta_{e,2}=\SI{12}{\milli\metre}$ at the roof. With an assumed deflection
amplification factor $C_d=3.0$ and $I_e=1.0$,
\begin{equation}
  \delta_x=\frac{C_d\delta_{e,x}}{I_e}.
\end{equation}

The maximum ratio is 0.0058, less than the illustrative 0.0200 life-safety limit,
\ok. This limit is not a facade criterion. A stricter wind-drift or cladding limit
may govern. The preliminary stability review gives $B_2=1.04<1.10$, indicating
modest second-order amplification for the stated model. A project calculation shall
document the code stability coefficient, notional loads, stiffness reductions, and
all torsional modes.
