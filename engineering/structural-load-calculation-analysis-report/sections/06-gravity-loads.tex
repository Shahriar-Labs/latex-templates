\section{Gravity Loads}

\subsection{Dead-load take-off}
Dead loads include permanent construction and an allowance for fixed services. The
steel framing allowance is included in the area load take-off for column and seismic
weight calculations; explicit beam self-weight is used in the representative beam
line-load calculation.

The facade is assumed to weigh \SI{0.75}{\kilo\pascal} of wall area. Parapets and
localized plant loads are represented by the roof seismic-weight allowance in
Section~\ref{sec:seismic}; they require explicit placement in a project model.

Concentrated loads, storage, corridors, stairs, assembly areas, movable equipment,
solar arrays, suspended services, facade maintenance systems, and construction loads
are not included. In a real building they can govern even when the uniform loads do
not.

\subsection{Representative line loads}
For the typical secondary beam, tributary width $b_t=\SI{3.0}{\metre}$ and an
explicit steel self-weight of \SI{0.35}{\kilo\newton\per\metre} give
\begin{align}
  w_D &= D_f b_t + w_{sw}
       = 4.25(3.0)+0.35
       = \SI{13.10}{\kilo\newton\per\metre}, \\
  w_L &= L b_t
       = 2.40(3.0)
       = \SI{7.20}{\kilo\newton\per\metre}.
\end{align}
For the typical interior girder, an equivalent tributary width of
\SI{6.0}{\metre} is used for preliminary design:
\begin{align}
  w_{D,G} &= 4.25(6.0)+0.70
          = \SI{26.20}{\kilo\newton\per\metre}, \\
  w_{L,G} &= 2.40(6.0)
          = \SI{14.40}{\kilo\newton\per\metre}.
\end{align}
Actual girder design shall use the point reactions and pattern loading from framing
beams.
