\section{Design Trade-off Analysis}

\subsection{Inductor Selection}
Table~\ref{tab:tradeoff-l} compares the selected 2.2\,\textmu H inductor
against a higher-inductance alternative.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{2.8cm} p{2.7cm} p{2.7cm} X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{2.2\,\textmu H (Selected)} & \textbf{4.7\,\textmu H (Alternate)} & \textbf{Design Impact} \\
\midrule
Peak-to-peak ripple current & 1.15\,A & 0.54\,A & Lower $L$ raises conduction loss slightly but improves load-transient slew rate. \\
Output voltage ripple & 28\,mV & 14\,mV & Higher $L$ reduces ripple, easing the REQ-4 margin further. \\
Solution footprint & 4x4x1.8\,mm & 5x5x2.0\,mm & Smaller part saves approximately 2.4\,mm$^2$ of board area. \\
Efficiency at 1\,A & 91.2\% & 92.6\% & Higher $L$ gives a modest efficiency gain from lower RMS current. \\
\midrule
Recommendation & \multicolumn{3}{p{9.2cm}}{2.2\,\textmu H selected to prioritize fast load-transient response for sensor wake events over the small efficiency gain of the larger part.} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Inductor value trade-off analysis.}
\label{tab:tradeoff-l}
\end{table}

\subsection{Switching Frequency Selection}
Table~\ref{tab:tradeoff-f} summarizes the trade-off between the selected
500\,kHz switching frequency and a 1\,MHz alternative.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{3.0cm} p{2.6cm} p{2.6cm} X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{500\,kHz (Selected)} & \textbf{1\,MHz (Alternate)} & \textbf{Design Impact} \\
\midrule
Passive component size & Larger $L_1$, $C_{OUT}$ & Smaller $L_1$, $C_{OUT}$ & 1\,MHz permits a smaller solution footprint. \\
Switching loss & Lower & Higher & 500\,kHz reduces gate-drive and switching losses, favoring efficiency. \\
Conducted EMI & Lower peak, easier filtering & Higher fundamental frequency & 500\,kHz was selected for margin against the REQ-7 CISPR~32 Class~B limit. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Switching frequency trade-off analysis.}
\label{tab:tradeoff-f}
\end{table}
