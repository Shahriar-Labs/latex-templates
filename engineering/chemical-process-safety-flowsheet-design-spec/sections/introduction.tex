\section{Introduction \& Project Scope}
\label{sec:intro}

This document details the process flowsheet design and safety specifications for Project Aether at the \textbf{Meridian Chemical Technologies} facility in Bayport, Texas. The project entails the installation of a new, highly exothermic liquid-phase oxidation reactor system (R-101) designed to convert hydrocarbon reactant A to intermediate compound B. Due to the high reaction enthalpy ($\Delta H_r = -125 \text{ kJ/mol}$) and the volatile nature of the solvent system, this process poses significant risk of thermal runaway and subsequent overpressurization.

To mitigate these risks and ensure compliance with global safety standards, including OSHA Process Safety Management (PSM) regulations and Center for Chemical Process Safety (CCPS) guidelines~\cite{ccps2010, crowl2019}, a comprehensive process safety and flowsheet design specification has been established.

\subsection{Objectives}
The primary objectives of this design specification are to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Define the process flowsheet and establish material balance parameters under normal operating conditions.
    \item Detail the instrumentation and automated safety control loops (Basic Process Control System, BPCS, and Safety Instrumented System, SIS) surrounding the reactor.
    \item Document a preliminary Hazard and Operability (HAZOP) study for the reactor node to identify potential deviations, consequences, and required safeguards.
    \item Specify the sizing methodology and design parameters for the primary reactor relief system, consisting of a rupture disk in series with a pressure safety valve (PSV), in compliance with API Standards 520 and 521~\cite{api520, api521}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Safety Design Philosophy}
Our engineering approach aligns with the principles of inherently safer design (ISD) proposed by Trevor Kletz~\cite{kletz2009}, emphasizing minimization and moderation. Where inherent safety cannot fully eliminate hazards, we deploy robust active and passive protection layers. As illustrated in this specification, these layers include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Prevention (BPCS):} Standard temperature, pressure, and flow controllers to maintain steady-state operation.
    \item \textbf{Mitigation (SIS):} Automated reactor emergency shutdown (ESD) loops to cut off feed and initiate emergency nitrogen purge in the event of coolant failure.
    \item \textbf{Protection (Relief System):} A dual-barrier relief system designed to vent vapor and liquid fractions safely to the flare header in a worst-case runaway scenario.
\end{enumerate}
