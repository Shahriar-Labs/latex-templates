\section{Discussion Questions}

\dq{Self-attention has \(\mathcal{O}(n^2)\) complexity in sequence length.
What architectural modifications have been proposed to reduce this cost,
and what trade-offs do they introduce?}

\dq{BERT uses bidirectional encoder-only Transformers; GPT uses decoder-only
models with causal masking. How do these choices shape the tasks each family
excels at?}

\dq{Attention weights are often cited as evidence of what the model ``focuses on.''
Critically evaluate this claim: when does the interpretation hold, and when does
it mislead?}

\dq{Positional encodings are needed because attention is permutation-invariant.
Could permutation invariance itself be \emph{exploited} as a feature in certain
application domains?}
