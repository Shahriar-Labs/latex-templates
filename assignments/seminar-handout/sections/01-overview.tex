\section{Overview}

The \textbf{Transformer} (Vaswani et al., 2017) revolutionised sequence modelling
by replacing recurrence entirely with \emph{attention mechanisms}, enabling
massively parallel training and superior long-range dependency capture.
Every major language model — BERT, GPT-4, T5, and beyond — is built on this backbone.
