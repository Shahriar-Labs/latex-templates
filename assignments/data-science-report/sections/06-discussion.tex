\section{Discussion}
XGBoost outperformed the other models, yielding an F1-score of $0.827$. The high recall ($0.804$) indicates that the model successfully captures $80.4\%$ of actual churn events. From a business perspective, recall is critical: missing an at-risk customer (False Negative) has a significantly higher cost (loss of customer value) than reaching out to a customer who would not have churned (False Positive).

Feature importance analysis showed that Contract Type (specifically Month-to-Month) and Tenure were the two most predictive features, followed closely by Support Tickets. This suggests that structural tenure issues and friction during customer onboarding are primary drivers of churn.
