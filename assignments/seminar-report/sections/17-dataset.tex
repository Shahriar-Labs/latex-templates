\section{Dataset}

We use the \textbf{SentiMix Hindi-English} dataset introduced in the SemEval-2020 Task~9 shared task \cite{patwa2020}. The dataset contains 20\,000 code-mixed tweets and Synapse comments annotated with three sentiment labels. Table~\ref{tab:dataset} summarizes the label distribution.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{SentiMix Hindi-English dataset statistics.}
\label{tab:dataset}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrr@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Split} & \textbf{Positive} & \textbf{Negative} & \textbf{Neutral} \\
\midrule
Train      & 6\,250 & 3\,840 & 4\,910 \\
Validation &   780  &   480  &   610  \\
Test       & 1\,560 &   960  & 1\,220 \\
\midrule
\textbf{Total} & 8\,590 & 5\,280 & 6\,740 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
