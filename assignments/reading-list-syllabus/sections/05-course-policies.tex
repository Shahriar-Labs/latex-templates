\section{Course Policies}

\textbf{Attendance \& participation.}
Seminar discussion is the heart of this course. More than two unexcused absences will lower your
participation grade; four or more will result in a failing grade for that component.
Please arrive having completed all readings.

\textbf{Late work.}
Response papers may be submitted up to 48\,hours late with a 10\,\% penalty. The research
paper draft and final paper are subject to a 5\,\% per-day deduction. Extensions for
documented emergencies must be requested \emph{before} the deadline.

\textbf{Academic integrity.}
All written work must be your own, submitted in accordance with the \href{https://whitmore.edu/integrity}{Whitmore College Academic Integrity Policy}.
Use of generative AI tools to produce submitted text is not permitted. Citation style:
Chicago author-date.

\textbf{Accessibility.}
Students requiring academic accommodations should contact the Office of Disability Services
(ods@whitmore.edu) and provide documentation within the first two weeks of term.

\textbf{Discussion norms.}
Philosophical inquiry requires respectful, charitable engagement with ideas you may strongly
disagree with. All participants are expected to engage constructively and listen actively.
