\section{Learning Objectives}

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Articulate the major positions in the philosophy of mind (dualism, physicalism,
        functionalism, eliminativism) and their principal objections.
  \item Critically analyse primary philosophical texts, identifying arguments, assumptions,
        and logical structure.
  \item Apply philosophical frameworks to contemporary empirical research in neuroscience
        and artificial intelligence.
  \item Construct well-structured, evidence-based philosophical arguments in written and
        oral form.
  \item Evaluate the ethical implications of theories of mind for issues such as
        personal identity, moral status, and the design of intelligent systems.
\end{enumerate}
