\section{Methodology}
The road network is modeled as a graph $G=(V,E)$ where nodes are intersections and edges
encode adjacency. Each node carries a window of historical flow. The predictor combines a
graph-convolution over $V$ with a temporal gated unit; the one-step loss is

\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}=\frac{1}{|V|}\sum_{v\in V}\bigl\lVert \hat{y}_v - y_v\bigr\rVert_2^2
           +\lambda\lVert\Theta\rVert_2^2,
\end{equation}

with $\Theta$ the trainable parameters and $\lambda$ a regularization weight. Data were
split 70/15/15 by time to avoid leakage.

\subsection{Data Pipeline}
Raw 30-second counts were cleaned (imputing gaps under 3 minutes), aggregated to 5-minute
bins, and normalized per detector. Figure~\ref{fig:pipe} summarizes the flow.
