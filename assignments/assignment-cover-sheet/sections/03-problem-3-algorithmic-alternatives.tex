\section{Problem 3:\ Algorithmic Alternatives}

For specific graph families, algorithms other than general-purpose Dijkstra offer better
constant factors or tighter asymptotic bounds. Table~\ref{tab:alternatives} summarises the
trade-offs.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \caption{Shortest-path algorithms compared by graph characteristics.}
  \label{tab:alternatives}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Algorithm} & \textbf{Complexity} & \textbf{Suitable For} & \textbf{Limitation} \\
    \midrule
    BFS                         & $O(V+E)$            & Unweighted graphs       & Fails with weights \\
    Dial's algorithm            & $O(V+E)$            & Small integer weights   & Bucket count $\propto$ max weight \\
    0--1 BFS (deque)            & $O(V+E)$            & Edge weights $\in\{0,1\}$ & Binary weights only \\
    Bellman--Ford               & $O(VE)$             & Negative edges allowed  & Slower; detects cycles \\
    A* search                   & $O((V+E)\log V)$    & Goal-directed, heuristic & Needs admissible heuristic \\
    Dijkstra (binary heap)      & $O((V+E)\log V)$    & General, $w \ge 0$      & Baseline choice \\
    Dijkstra (Fibonacci heap)   & $O(E+V\log V)$      & Dense, $w \ge 0$        & High constant factor \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
