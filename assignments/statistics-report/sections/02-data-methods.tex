\section{Data \& Methods}
The data used in this analysis are drawn from the Metro Housing Study (2025), a random sample of $N = \num{150}$ single-family home transactions. The dataset includes sales prices and characteristics of properties sold within the metropolitan area.

We specify a classical linear regression model to examine the determinants of house price:
\begin{equation}
\text{Price}_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 \text{GSI}_i + \beta_2 \text{Size}_i + \beta_3 \text{Age}_i + \epsilon_i
\end{equation}
where for property $i$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm, noitemsep]
    \item[\textbf{Price}] is the sales price in thousands of USD (\$\num{1000}s).
    \item[\textbf{GSI}] is the Green Space Index, measured as the percentage of land area within a \qty{500}{\metre} buffer covered by tree canopy or public parks.
    \item[\textbf{Size}] is the finished interior area of the home in hundreds of square feet (\num{100}~sq.\,ft.).
    \item[\textbf{Age}] is the age of the structure at the time of sale, in years.
    \item[\textbf{$\epsilon$}] is the stochastic error term, assumed to be $\text{i.i.d.} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$.
\end{itemize}
