\section{Exploratory Analysis}
Table~\ref{tab:summary_stats} presents descriptive statistics for the variables used in the regression. The average property sales price in our sample is \$\num{482.35} thousand, with a minimum of \$\num{280.00} thousand and a maximum of \$\num{630.00} thousand. The Green Space Index exhibits significant variation, ranging from highly urbanized areas (\qty{12}{\percent} canopy) to heavily forested suburban neighborhoods (\qty{95}{\percent} canopy).
