\section{Return or Destruction of Materials}

\noindent
Upon written request by the Disclosing Party or upon expiration or termination
of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall promptly—and in any event within
\textbf{ten (10) business days}—at the Disclosing Party's election:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item return to the Disclosing Party all tangible materials and electronic
        records containing Confidential Information; or
  \item destroy all such materials and records and certify such destruction in
        writing to the Disclosing Party.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Receiving Party may retain one archival copy
solely to the extent required by applicable law or bona fide document-retention
policies, subject to the continuing confidentiality obligations herein.
