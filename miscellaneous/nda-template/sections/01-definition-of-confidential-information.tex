\section{Definition of Confidential Information}

\noindent
\term{Confidential Information} means any and all non-public information,
data, or know-how—whether oral, written, electronic, or in any other form—
disclosed by one Party (the \term{Disclosing Party}) to the other Party
(the \term{Receiving Party}) in connection with the Purpose, including but
not limited to:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item trade secrets, inventions, designs, algorithms, source code, and
        technical specifications;
  \item business plans, financial projections, customer lists, marketing
        strategies, and pricing information;
  \item information disclosed by third parties that the Disclosing Party
        is obligated to keep confidential; and
  \item any notes, analyses, compilations, or derivatives prepared by the
        Receiving Party that contain or are based upon Confidential Information.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
Information is Confidential Information regardless of whether it is marked
``Confidential'' or ``Proprietary,'' provided it would reasonably be
understood to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of
disclosure.
