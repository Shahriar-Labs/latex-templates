\section{Exclusions from Confidential Information}

\noindent
The obligations under Section~2 shall not apply to information that the
Receiving Party can demonstrate by contemporaneous written records:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure without
        restriction;
  \item is or becomes publicly available through no act or omission of the
        Receiving Party;
  \item is rightfully received from a third party without restriction on
        disclosure; or
  \item is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of
        or reference to the Confidential Information.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
Additionally, the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the
extent required by applicable law, regulation, or court order, provided it
(i) gives prompt prior written notice to the Disclosing Party (to the extent
legally permitted), (ii) cooperates with the Disclosing Party in seeking a
protective order, and (iii) discloses only that portion strictly required.
