\section{Obligations of Receiving Party}

\noindent
With respect to Confidential Information received from the Disclosing Party,
the Receiving Party shall:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence, using at least
        the same degree of care as it uses for its own confidential information
        of similar sensitivity, but in no event less than reasonable care;
  \item use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other
        purpose whatsoever;
  \item disclose Confidential Information only to its directors, officers,
        employees, advisors, and agents who (i) have a need to know such
        information for the Purpose, and (ii) are bound by confidentiality
        obligations no less protective than those set forth herein
        (\term{Authorised Representatives});
  \item promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon becoming aware of any
        unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information; and
  \item not reverse engineer, disassemble, or decompile any prototypes,
        software, or other tangible objects that embody Confidential Information.
\end{enumerate}
