\section{About Me}

A devout Sikh grounded in Gurbani and the teachings of the Guru Granth Sahib.
I strive to live by \textit{Kirat Karo} (honest work), \textit{Naam Japo}
(remembrance of Waheguru), and \textit{Seva} (selfless service). I balance my
spiritual life with a modern professional career and enjoy Kirtan, cycling,
reading Sikh history, and visiting historic Gurdwaras with family.
