\section{About Horizon Digital Studio}

Horizon Digital Studio is a nine-person design and development studio based in Austin, Texas,
focused on e-commerce and hospitality brands. We have shipped 120+ web and commerce projects
since 2014, with a specialty in helping food, beverage, and retail businesses move recurring
revenue online without losing the craft of their brand.
