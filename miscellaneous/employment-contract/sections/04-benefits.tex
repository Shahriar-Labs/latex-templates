\section{BENEFITS}

The Employee shall be eligible to participate in the following benefits, subject to the terms of each plan or policy:

\parsp
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Health Insurance.} Medical, dental, and vision coverage; Company covers 80\% of the employee-only premium.
  \item \textbf{Retirement.} 401(k) plan with 4\% employer match, subject to plan terms and IRS limits.
  \item \textbf{Paid Time Off.} Twenty (20) days PTO per year, pro-rata accrual, plus ten (10) Company holidays.
  \item \textbf{Sick Leave.} In accordance with New York State and New York City law.
  \item \textbf{Professional Development.} \$3,000 annual budget for conferences, courses, and certifications.
  \item \textbf{Commuter Benefits.} Pre-tax program for transit and parking expenses.
\end{itemize}
