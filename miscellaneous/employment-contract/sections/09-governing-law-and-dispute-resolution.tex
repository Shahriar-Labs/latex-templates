\section{GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION}

\parsp
\textbf{Governing Law.} This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

\parsp
\textbf{Arbitration.} Any dispute arising out of this Agreement or the Employee's employment shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) under its Employment Arbitration Rules. The arbitration shall take place in New York County, New York. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

\parsp
\textbf{Attorneys' Fees.} The prevailing party in any action to enforce this Agreement shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.
