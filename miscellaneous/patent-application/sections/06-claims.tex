\section{Claims}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\setcounter{claimcnt}{0}
\begin{spacing}{1.2}

\claim{An adaptive thermal-management assembly for a solid-state battery module,
  comprising:\newline
  a plurality of microchannel plates interleaved between a corresponding plurality of
  solid-state battery cells, each microchannel plate comprising a network of microchannels
  having a hydraulic diameter in the range of 200\,\textmu m to 800\,\textmu m;\newline
  a phase-change material disposed within a sealed reservoir fluidly coupled to the
  microchannel network, the phase-change material having a melting onset temperature
  between 30\,\textdegree C and 45\,\textdegree C; and\newline
  an adaptive valve array comprising a plurality of microvalves, each microvalve being
  operable to selectively direct flow of the phase-change material within the microchannel
  network in response to a local temperature signal.}

\claim{The assembly of claim~1, wherein each microvalve of the adaptive valve array is
  actuated by a shape-memory alloy wire having a transformation temperature that
  corresponds to the melting onset temperature of the phase-change material.}

\claim{The assembly of claim~2, wherein the shape-memory alloy wire comprises
  Nitinol having a nickel content of 50.8 atomic percent and a free length of between
  10\,mm and 40\,mm.}

\claim{The assembly of claim~1, wherein the phase-change material comprises a binary
  paraffin eutectic of n-eicosane and n-octadecane in a weight ratio of between 55:45
  and 65:35.}

\claim{The assembly of claim~1, wherein each microchannel plate is fabricated from an
  aluminium alloy having a bulk thermal conductivity of at least 150\,W/m\,K, and wherein
  the microchannel walls are coated with a corrosion-resistant layer comprising titanium
  nitride having a thickness of between 20\,nm and 50\,nm.}

\claim{The assembly of claim~1, further comprising a battery management controller
  operatively connected to a plurality of temperature sensors embedded in respective
  microchannel plates, the controller being configured to execute a proportional-integral
  control law to command individual microvalves of the adaptive valve array.}

\claim{The assembly of claim~6, wherein the temperature sensors comprise
  negative-temperature-coefficient thermistors having a sensitivity of at least
  $-3\,\%\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}^{-1}$, and wherein the controller samples the temperature
  sensors at an interval of 100\,ms or less.}

\claim{The assembly of claim~1, wherein the sealed reservoir further comprises a
  flexible membrane configured to accommodate volumetric expansion of the phase-change
  material upon melting of at least 10\% without mechanical stress on seals of the
  reservoir.}

\claim{A method of thermally managing a solid-state battery module, comprising:\newline
  sensing, by a plurality of temperature sensors, a temperature differential between at
  least two solid-state battery cells exceeding a predetermined threshold;\newline
  actuating, in response to the sensed temperature differential, at least one microvalve of
  an adaptive valve array to redirect flow of a phase-change material within a microchannel
  network toward a battery cell exhibiting the higher temperature; and\newline
  passively re-solidifying the phase-change material within the microchannel network upon
  reduction of the thermal load below the predetermined threshold.}

\claim{The method of claim~9, wherein the predetermined threshold is 2\,\textdegree C, and
  wherein the passively re-solidifying step occurs without application of external pumping
  power or external heaters.}

\claim{The method of claim~9, further comprising embedding a flexible printed circuit
  within each microchannel plate, the flexible printed circuit electrically connecting the
  temperature sensors to a battery management controller that commands the microvalves
  via pulse-width-modulated drive signals.}

\end{spacing}
