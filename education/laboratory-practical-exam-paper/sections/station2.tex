\subsection{Task Description}
In this station, you must align the Michelson interferometer setup to observe concentric circular interference fringes. By moving the micrometer screw, you will count the number of fringe transits and calculate the wavelength of the Helium-Neon laser.

\subsection{Experimental Procedures \& Measurements}

\begin{itemize}[label=\checkbox, leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.8em]
    \item \textbf{Safety Warning:} Do not look directly into the laser beam or its specular reflections. Put on safety goggles before turning the laser on. \pointmarks{1}
    
    \item Align the laser beam so it strikes the center of the beam splitter. Verify that two separate spots of light are projected onto the viewing screen (corresponding to paths from Mirror 1 and Mirror 2). \pointmarks{2}
    
    \item Carefully adjust the vertical and horizontal alignment screws on the fixed mirror until the two light spots perfectly overlap on the screen. Adjust the micrometer drive slowly until circular fringe patterns appear. \pointmarks{4}
    
    \item Record the initial micrometer reading $d_0$. Turn the micrometer screw slowly and count the number of fringes passing a marked point on the screen. For every $N = 50$ transits, record the micrometer position $d_i$. Run 4 trials (up to $N = 200$). \pointmarks{5}
    
    \item Calculate the mirror displacement $\Delta d = d_i - d_0$ for each trial, and compute the experimental laser wavelength $\lambda$ using $2\Delta d = N \lambda$. Note down the micrometer scale calibration factor (if any). \pointmarks{3}
\end{itemize}
