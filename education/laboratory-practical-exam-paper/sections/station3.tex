\subsection{Task Description}
In this station, you are required to calibrate a Pt100 RTD sensor at the ice point ($\SI{0.0}{\celsius}$) and steam point ($\SI{100.0}{\celsius}$). You will determine the sensor's temperature coefficient of resistance ($\alpha$) and use it to measure the ambient room temperature.

\subsection{Experimental Procedures \& Measurements}

\begin{itemize}[label=\checkbox, leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.8em]
    \item Connect the four leads of the Pt100 sensor to the DMM using a 4-wire configuration (two force leads, two sense leads). Switch the DMM to the resistance measurement mode ($\Omega$) on the lowest manual range that covers $\SI{200}{\ohm}$. \pointmarks{2}
    
    \item Place the Pt100 probe alongside the reference glass thermometer in the ice-water bath. Wait at least 3 minutes for thermal equilibrium. Record the temperature from the glass thermometer $T_{\text{ice}}$ and the corresponding resistance $R_{\text{ice}}$. \pointmarks{2}
    
    \item Carefully transfer the Pt100 probe to the boiling water bath. Allow the temperature reading to stabilize. Record the reference thermometer temperature $T_{\text{steam}}$ and the corresponding resistance $R_{\text{steam}}$. \pointmarks{2}
    
    \item Use your recorded values to calculate the temperature coefficient of resistance $\alpha$ of the Pt100 sensor. Recall: $R(T) = R_0(1 + \alpha T)$, where $R_0 = \SI{100.0}{\ohm}$ at $T = \SI{0.0}{\celsius}$. \pointmarks{2}
    
    \item Remove the probe from the boiling bath, dry it, and let it cool. Record the resistance of the sensor at room temperature $R_{\text{room}}$. Use your calculated $\alpha$ to estimate the room temperature $T_{\text{room}}$. \pointmarks{2}
\end{itemize}
