\subsection{Task Description}
In this station, you are required to assemble a \textbf{non-inverting amplifier} using the LM741 operational amplifier. You will measure its low-frequency gain and determine the closed-loop $-3\text{ dB}$ bandwidth. Make sure to ground all instrument shields to the power supply common terminal.

\subsection{Experimental Procedures \& Measurements}

\begin{itemize}[label=\checkbox, leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.8em]
    \item Ensure the main power supply and the signal generator are switched OFF. Identify the pins of the LM741 operational amplifier (Pin 2: Inverting Input, Pin 3: Non-Inverting Input, Pin 6: Output, Pin 7: $+V_{cc}$, Pin 4: $-V_{ee}$). \pointmarks{1}
    
    \item Connect the dual power supply rails to the op-amp: Pin 7 to $+\SI{12}{\volt}$ and Pin 4 to $-\SI{12}{\volt}$. Connect the common ground of the supply to the breadboard ground rail. \pointmarks{2}
    
    \item Wire a non-inverting amplifier configuration. Place $R_1 = \SI{1}{\kilo\ohm}$ between Pin 2 and ground, and $R_f = \SI{10}{\kilo\ohm}$ between Pin 2 and Pin 6 (feedback path). Connect the input signal to Pin 3. \pointmarks{3}
    
    \item Turn on the function generator. Configure it to output a sine wave of $V_{\text{in}} = \SI{100}{\milli\volt}$ peak-to-peak ($V_{p-p}$) at a frequency of $\SI{1}{\kilo\hertz}$. Measure $V_{\text{in}}$ and $V_{\text{out}}$ on Channels 1 and 2 of the DSO respectively. Record the waveforms and their amplitude. \pointmarks{3}
    
    \item Sweep the frequency from $\SI{100}{\hertz}$ to $\SI{1}{\mega\hertz}$ as detailed in Table~\ref{tab:freq_response}. Record the output voltage amplitude $V_{\text{out}}$ at each step. Adjust the timebase on the DSO at high frequencies to resolve the output wave properly. \pointmarks{6}
\end{itemize}
