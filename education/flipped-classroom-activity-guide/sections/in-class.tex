\section{In-Class Session Plan}
\label{sec:inclass}

A single 55-minute period, structured around three rotating stations plus
a warm-up and a debrief. Groups of four, formed from the confidence
ratings collected in Section~\ref{sec:preclass}.

\begin{materialsbox}
\begin{itemize}[label=\textbullet]
  \item Station cards (Sections~\ref{sec:worksheet}, one set per group)
  \item Molar-mass reference sheet (periodic table with masses), 1 per student
  \item Whiteboards and dry-erase markers, 1 set per group
  \item Timer displayed at the front of the room
  \item Colour-coded exit tickets (Section~\ref{sec:assessment})
\end{itemize}
\end{materialsbox}

\subsection*{Agenda}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Time} & \textbf{Segment} & \textbf{What happens} \\
\midrule
0:00 -- 0:05 & Warm-up quiz & Three cold-call conversion problems on the board; self-scored, hands raised for 0--3 correct \\
0:05 -- 0:15 & Station A --- Mole Conversions & Grams $\leftrightarrow$ moles $\leftrightarrow$ particles drills on whiteboards \\
0:15 -- 0:25 & Station B --- Mole Ratios & Balance equations, extract ratios, answer ``how many moles of X per mole of Y'' \\
0:25 -- 0:35 & Station C --- Mass-to-Mass & Full stoichiometry problems using the ammonia synthesis reaction \\
0:35 -- 0:45 & Station D --- Limiting Reactant & Two-reactant problem set with a Nexa Labs sample scenario \\
0:45 -- 0:52 & Whole-class debrief & One volunteer per station walks the room through their group's hardest problem \\
0:52 -- 0:55 & Exit ticket & Individual, silent, submitted before leaving \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\begin{teachertip}
Stations run on a shared timer, not a per-group timer --- groups that
finish early get an ``extension card'' (a harder variant of the same
problem) rather than sitting idle. Keeps the fast-finishers thinking
instead of chatting through the remaining minutes.
\end{teachertip}
