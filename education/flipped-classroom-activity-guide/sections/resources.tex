\section{Resources \& Further Reading}

\subsection*{Adapting This Guide}

\begin{itemize}[label=\textbullet]
  \item \textbf{Shorter periods (40 min):} Drop Station D and assign the limiting-reactant problem as homework instead; keep the warm-up and debrief.
  \item \textbf{No LMS access:} Print the pre-class reading and guided notes; collect notes on paper at the classroom door instead of online.
  \item \textbf{Mixed-readiness classes:} Pre-sort using the confidence rating (Section~\ref{sec:preclass}, Q5) into two station tracks --- a supported track that starts with a worked example at Station A, and an independent track that starts directly at Station B.
  \item \textbf{Remote or hybrid students:} Replace whiteboard work with the shared Synapse Notes canvas; the teacher can view all group boards from one dashboard during circulation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Evidence Base}

This guide's structure --- transfer information before class, spend
class time on practice with feedback --- draws on the flipped-classroom
literature summarised in Table~\ref{tab:evidence}.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Source} & \textbf{Relevant finding} \\
\midrule
\citet{bergmann2012flip} & Introduces the flip model this guide follows: direct instruction moves out of class, class time moves to applied practice. \\
\citet{mazur1997peer} & Peer Instruction shows that structured group problem-solving surfaces misconceptions a lecture cannot. \\
\citet{bishop2013flipped} & Meta-review of flipped-classroom studies; found consistently positive but modest effects on exam performance. \\
\citet{freeman2014active} & Meta-analysis across STEM courses: active learning reduces failure rates by roughly a third relative to lecture. \\
\citet{talbert2017flipped} & Practical guide to the pre-class/in-class/post-class feedback loop used in Section~\ref{sec:assessment}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Evidence base for the flipped structure used in this unit.}
\label{tab:evidence}
\end{table}
