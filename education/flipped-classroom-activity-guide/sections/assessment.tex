\section{Post-Class Assessment}
\label{sec:assessment}

Two components: a two-minute exit ticket collected at the door, and a
short homework set graded for correctness and returned before the next
flipped unit. Both are designed to surface who needs reteaching before
the following class, following the formative-assessment loop described
by \citet{talbert2017flipped}.

\subsection*{Exit Ticket}

Printed on a half-sheet; students answer individually and silently in
the final three minutes.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item Solve: How many grams of $\mathrm{NH_3}$ form from 14.0 g of $\mathrm{N_2}$ with excess $\mathrm{H_2}$?
  \item Circle one: today's material felt \textbf{Easy} / \textbf{Manageable} / \textbf{Confusing}.
  \item Write one question you still have, or write ``none.''
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teachertip}
Sort exit tickets into three piles while students pack up: correct, close (arithmetic error but right method), and off-track. The ``close'' pile usually just needs a molar-mass double-check reminder --- don't reteach the whole method to that group.
\end{teachertip}

\subsection*{Homework Set}

Six problems assigned through the Nexa LMS, due before the next class.
Problems 1--4 mirror the station worksheets; problems 5--6 introduce a
new reaction (combustion of propane) so students demonstrate transfer,
not memorisation of the ammonia example.

\subsection*{Grading Rubric --- Station Participation}

Used by the teacher while circulating during stations; not shown to
students until after class.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X X X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Criterion} & \textbf{Proficient (3)} & \textbf{Developing (2)} & \textbf{Beginning (1)} \\
\midrule
Method shown & All four conversion steps written on the whiteboard, in order & Steps present but out of order or one step skipped & Final answer only, no steps \\
Units \& labels & Every intermediate value labelled with correct units & Units present but inconsistent & Units missing or wrong throughout \\
Group participation & All four members contribute to the whiteboard & Two or three members contribute & One member does all the work \\
Use of station time & Group completes the station within the allotted 10 minutes & Group needs an extra 2--3 minutes & Group does not finish the station \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\subsection*{Reteach Trigger}

If more than 25\% of exit tickets land in the ``off-track'' pile, open
the next class period with a 10-minute mini-lecture on mass-to-mass
conversion before returning to new content --- do not simply move on to
Unit 6.
