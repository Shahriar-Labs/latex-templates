\section{Course Schedule \& Milestones}

CS-350 is structured as a fast-paced journey through theory and implementation. The weekly topics, associated readings, and primary course milestones are detailed in Table~\ref{tab:schedule}.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Weekly Course Syllabus and Lab Milestones}
\label{tab:schedule}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{c l X p{4.2cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Weeks} & \textbf{Core Subject Area} & \textbf{Key Readings} & \textbf{Deliverable} \\
\midrule
Week 1--2 & Distributed Time \& Clocks & Lamport Clocks \citep{lamport1978time} & Lab 0 (Ungraded Go Lab) \\
Week 3--4 & RPC \& Socket Architectures & Tanenbaum Ch. 4 \citep{tanenbaum2017distributed} & Lab 1 (Multi-threaded RPC) \\
Week 5--7 & Log Replication \& Consensus & Raft Protocol \citep{ongaro2014search} & Lab 2 (Raft Leader Election) \\
Week 8 & Midpoint conceptual Sync & Practice exercises & In-class Midterm Exam \\
Week 9--11 & Replicated Key-Value Storage & Tanenbaum Ch. 7 & Lab 3 (Fault-tolerant DB) \\
Week 12--14 & Scaling \& Partitioning & CAP Theorem publications & Capstone Project Beta \\
Week 15 & System Showcases \& Demos & Peer team surveys & Capstone Final Presentation \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
