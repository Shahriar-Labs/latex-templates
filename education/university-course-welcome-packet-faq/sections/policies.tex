\subsection{Late Submission Framework}

Distributed systems development requires careful time management. To encourage progress, the late submission framework is defined as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Grace Days Pool:} Each student has three (3) grace days to use across the semester for individual programming labs. A grace day extends a deadline by 24 hours.
    \item \textbf{Standard Deductions:} Once your grace days pool is depleted, late submissions lose 15\% of the maximum points per 24-hour period. Submissions are capped at 72 hours late, after which a grade of 0 is recorded.
    \item \textbf{Exclusions:} Grace days cannot be applied to exams, quiz windows, or group project milestones.
\end{itemize}
