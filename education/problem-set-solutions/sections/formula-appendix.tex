\section*{Appendix: Formula Reference}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix: Formula Reference}

The identities below were treated as known background for this problem set
and did not need to be re-derived in any solution.

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{4.6cm}p{7.3cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Topic} & \textbf{Identity} \\
\midrule
Complex characteristic roots &
  $y = e^{\alpha t}(C_1\cos\beta t + C_2\sin\beta t)$ for $r = \alpha \pm i\beta$ \\
2$\times$2 determinant &
  $\det\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\{} c & d \end{pmatrix} = ad - bc$ \\
Bayes' theorem &
  $P(H\mid E) = \dfrac{P(E\mid H)\,P(H)}{P(E)}$ \\
Law of total probability &
  $P(E) = \sum_i P(E\mid H_i)\,P(H_i)$ \\
Second-derivative test &
  $f''(x_0) < 0 \Rightarrow x_0$ is a local maximum of $f$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
