\section{The Evaluation Rubric}
\label{sec:rubric}

The peer evaluation uses a 4-point Likert scale across five assessment criteria. Evaluators should read the descriptions carefully and award scores that accurately reflect the student's work throughout the project.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Peer Evaluation Rubric Standards}
\label{tab:rubric}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l *{4}{X}}
\toprule
\textbf{Criterion} & \textbf{Excellent (4)} & \textbf{Good (3)} & \textbf{Needs Imp. (2)} & \textbf{Unacceptable (1)} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Contribution} & Completed a large share of the work; went beyond requirements. & Completed their full share of the work; met all target goals. & Completed some work but fell short of their fair share. & Contributed little to no actual work on the project. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Quality} & Output was of exceptional quality; required no revisions. & Output was good; required only minor formatting or editing. & Output was mediocre; required substantial revision. & Output was unusable or had to be redone by others. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Communication} & Kept in touch daily; responded instantly; raised issues early. & Responded within 24 hours; participated in all team meetings. & Hard to reach; missed meetings; rarely updated the group. & Did not communicate at all or ignored team messages. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Collaboration} & Promoted harmony; helped others; resolved conflicts constructively. & Worked well with others; respected different ideas and views. & Occasionally argumentative or dismissive of other opinions. & Caused major friction; refused to compromise or cooperate. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Reliability} & Met all deadlines; arrived early to meetings; highly organized. & Met almost all deadlines; was punctual for team sessions. & Frequently late with tasks; missed several key deadlines. & Completely missed deadlines, forcing others to cover. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
