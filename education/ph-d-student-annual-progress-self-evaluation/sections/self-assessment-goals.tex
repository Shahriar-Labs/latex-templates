  \item Manuscript turnaround from ``results in hand'' to ``submitted'' has consistently run
    4--6 weeks longer than I budget for.

    job market is a gap.

\vspace{10pt}

Overall, I consider this a strong year: both lab-based research aims closed out on schedule and
produced publishable results, and the field-deployment pilot validated the Aim 3 protocol
before committing to the full 40-participant study. The primary risk to the overall timeline is
recruitment lead time at the partner facility, which the Year 5 plan below now accounts for
explicitly by starting recruitment earlier and in parallel with manuscript preparation.
