\section{General Grading Policies}
To maintain academic standards and ensure fair treatment for all students, you must adhere to the following course policies. Any exceptions to these policies must be approved directly by Dr. Vance~\cite{vertex2025pedagogy}.

\subsection{Late Submission Policy}
CS 201 has a strict late policy managed automatically by the Nexa LMS.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Submissions up to 24 hours late receive a flat \textbf{15\% deduction}.
    \item Submissions between 24 and 48 hours late receive a flat \textbf{30\% deduction}.
    \item Submissions exceeding 48 hours late receive a \textbf{score of 0}.
\end{itemize}
As a TA, you do not need to manually calculate these deductions. The Nexa LMS will apply the late penalty to the final grade. Your job is to grade the assignment as if it were submitted on time, and the system will handle the discount.

\subsection{Academic Integrity and Plagiarism}
Academic integrity is taken very seriously at Vertex. We use the Moss (Measure of Software Similarity) system to screen all programming assignments.

\begin{warningbox}{\faExclamationTriangle\ Handling Suspected Plagiarism}
    If you suspect plagiarism or notice code that is identical to another student's work or an online solution:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item \textbf{Do NOT grade} the submission or write feedback.
        \item \textbf{Do NOT contact} the student or accuse them of cheating.
        \item \textbf{Flag the submission} on the Synapse grading portal and email the student's name, ID, and matching evidence to Dr. Vance immediately.
    \end{enumerate}
    The instructional team will handle all academic integrity investigations.
\end{warningbox}

\subsection{Regrade Requests}
Students have exactly seven days from the release of grades to submit a formal regrade request via the Synapse portal.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Minor Errors:} If you made a simple clerical error (e.g., miscounting points), correct it immediately and notify the Head TA.
    \item \textbf{Conceptual Disagreements:} If a student disputes a rubric deduction, they must submit a written justification referencing the lecture slides or textbook. In such cases, the student's entire assignment will be subject to a complete re-evaluation, which may result in a higher, identical, or lower score.
\end{itemize}
Never argue with a student. If a discussion becomes tense, politely direct the student to the Head TA or Dr. Vance.
