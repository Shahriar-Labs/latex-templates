\section{Definitions and Interpretation}
\label{sec:definitions}

\subsection{Defined Terms}

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings
ascribed to them below unless the context otherwise requires.

\begin{tcolorbox}[notebox]
  \small
  \textbf{Note:} Where a term is defined in the Company's Articles of
  Incorporation or By-Laws, that definition applies unless expressly modified
  herein.
\end{tcolorbox}

\medskip

\begin{description}[
  labelwidth = 3.5cm,
  leftmargin = 3.8cm,
  labelsep   = 0.3cm,
  font       = \bfseries\color{NavyDeep},
  itemsep    = 6pt
]
  \item[Affiliate]
    With respect to any Person, any other Person that, directly or indirectly,
    Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with such Person.

  \item[Board]
    The board of directors of the Company as constituted from time to time in
    accordance with this Agreement and the Company's constating documents.

  \item[Business Day]
    Any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or a statutory holiday in the Province
    of Ontario.

  \item[Class A Shares]
    Common shares in the capital of the Company carrying full voting rights on the
    basis of one (1) vote per share, as more fully described in the Company's
    Articles of Incorporation.

  \item[Class B Shares]
    Non-voting common shares in the capital of the Company carrying economic rights
    only, convertible into Class~A Shares at the option of the holder at a one-to-one
    (1:1) ratio subject to \clause{5.3}.

  \item[Control]
    The direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50\%) of the
    outstanding voting securities of a Person, or the power to direct or cause the
    direction of the management and policies of such Person.

  \item[Drag-Along Right]
    The right described in \clause{7.2} pursuant to which the Majority Holders may
    compel Minority Shareholders to sell their Shares in a bona fide arms-length
    transaction.

  \item[Encumbrance]
    Any mortgage, charge, pledge, lien, option, restriction, right of first refusal,
    right of pre-emption, third-party right, or other encumbrance or condition
    of any kind.

  \item[Exit Event]
    A transaction or series of related transactions resulting in (i)~a sale or
    transfer of all or substantially all of the Company's assets, (ii)~a merger,
    amalgamation, or arrangement in which the Shareholders immediately prior do not
    retain a majority of the voting securities of the surviving entity, or
    (iii)~an initial public offering on a recognised stock exchange.

  \item[Fair Market Value]
    The price that a willing buyer would pay a willing seller for the applicable
    Shares, determined by an independent valuator appointed pursuant to
    \clause{9.4}, absent any discount for minority interest or premium for control.

  \item[Majority Holders]
    Shareholders holding, in aggregate, more than sixty percent (60\%) of the
    issued and outstanding Class~A Shares.

  \item[Permitted Transfer]
    A Transfer of Shares to an Affiliate of the transferring Shareholder or, in
    the case of an individual Shareholder, to a Holding Entity wholly owned by
    such Shareholder, subject to the conditions in \clause{6.4}.

  \item[Pro-Rata Share]
    With respect to any Shareholder and any matter requiring pro-rata
    allocation, the fraction obtained by dividing the number of Shares held by
    such Shareholder by the total number of issued Shares at the relevant date.

  \item[Tag-Along Right]
    The right described in \clause{7.1} pursuant to which Minority Shareholders
    may participate in a proposed sale of Shares by a Majority Holder on the
    same terms and conditions.

  \item[Transfer]
    Any sale, assignment, transfer, exchange, gift, bequest, pledge, hypothecation,
    or other disposition of Shares, whether voluntary or involuntary, by operation
    of law or otherwise.
\end{description}

\subsection{Interpretation}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{\arabic{section}.\arabic*.}]
  \item References to a \textbf{Clause}, \textbf{Section}, or \textbf{Schedule}
        are to a clause, section, or schedule of this Agreement.
  \item Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa; words denoting
        any gender include all genders.
  \item The term \textbf{``including''} is not limiting and means ``including,
        without limitation.''
  \item A reference to a statute includes any amendment thereto and any
        regulation made thereunder in force at the relevant time.
  \item Headings are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation
        of this Agreement.
  \item Where a period of time is expressed to run from a specified date, that
        date shall not be included in the reckoning of such period.
\end{enumerate}
