\subsection{Voting Rights}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{3.\arabic*.}, itemsep=4pt]
  \item Each Class~A Share entitles the registered holder to one (1) vote at any
        meeting of Shareholders or in any written resolution of Shareholders.

  \item Class~B Shares carry no voting rights whatsoever, whether in respect of
        ordinary resolutions, special resolutions, or matters requiring unanimous
        consent, except as expressly required by applicable law.

  \item The following matters shall require approval of Shareholders holding not
        less than \textbf{seventy-five percent (75\%)} of the issued Class~A Shares
        (a \defined{Super-Majority Approval}):
        \begin{enumerate}[label=\lbrack\alph*\rbrack, leftmargin=2em, itemsep=2pt]
          \item amendment of the Company's articles or by-laws;
          \item any merger, amalgamation, or plan of arrangement;
          \item a voluntary winding-up or dissolution of the Company;
          \item creation of any new class of shares ranking senior to Class~A Shares;
          \item any sale, lease, or other disposition of all or substantially all
                of the Company's assets outside the ordinary course of business;
          \item any amendment to or waiver of rights under this Agreement.
        \end{enumerate}

  \item Ordinary corporate business not listed in \clause{3.3} shall be decided by
        a simple majority (more than fifty percent (50\%)) of the Class~A votes
        cast at a duly convened meeting or by written resolution.

  \item The Company hereby reserves \textbf{300{,}000 Class~B Shares} exclusively for
        issuance pursuant to an employee stock option plan (\defined{ESOP}) to be
        adopted by the Board within sixty (60) Business Days of the Effective Date.
        Any modification of the ESOP pool size shall require Super-Majority Approval.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Anti-Dilution Protection}

Each of Apex and Synapse (each a \defined{Protected Investor}) shall be entitled
to maintain its Pro-Rata Share in respect of any future equity issuances by the
Company (other than ESOP issuances), exercisable within thirty (30) Business Days
following written notice from the Company of a proposed issuance.  Failure to
exercise within such period shall be deemed a waiver in respect of that issuance only.
