\subsection{Nomination and Appointment}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{4.\arabic*.}, itemsep=5pt]
  \item A nominating Shareholder or group shall deliver written notice of its
        nominee to the Company Secretary no later than twenty (20) Business Days
        prior to the relevant annual general meeting (or, in the case of a vacancy,
        within fifteen (15) Business Days of such vacancy arising).

  \item If a nominating party ceases to hold the requisite ownership threshold,
        its right to nominate shall lapse, and the incumbent Director nominated by
        that party shall resign within ten (10) Business Days of written notice from
        the Board.  The resulting vacancy shall be filled by a nominee approved by
        the Majority Holders.

  \item The independent Director (Seat~5) shall (i)~have no material relationship
        with any Shareholder or the Company, (ii)~meet the independence criteria set
        out in National Instrument 52-110, and (iii)~be approved by a simple majority
        of Directors.

  \item Each Shareholder covenants to vote its Shares in favour of the duly nominated
        candidates at every meeting of Shareholders at which Directors are to be
        elected, and to take all other steps within its power to give effect to the
        nomination rights set out herein.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Board Procedures}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{4.\arabic*.}, itemsep=5pt]
  \setcounter{enumi}{4}
  \item \textbf{Quorum.}  A quorum for the transaction of business at any Board
        meeting shall be three (3) Directors, provided that at least one (1) Director
        nominated by Apex is present.

  \item \textbf{Frequency.}  The Board shall meet no less than once per fiscal
        quarter, with at least one (1) in-person meeting per calendar year.

  \item \textbf{Reserved Matters.}  In addition to the Super-Majority Shareholder
        approvals required under \clause{3.3}, the following matters require
        unanimous Board approval:
        \begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
          \item Annual budget and any single expenditure exceeding
                \$250{,}000~CAD not within the approved budget;
          \item Entry into any Related-Party Transaction exceeding \$50{,}000~CAD;
          \item Incurrence of indebtedness exceeding \$500{,}000~CAD in aggregate;
          \item Material changes to the business plan or entry into a new line of
                business;
          \item Appointment or removal of the Chief Executive Officer, Chief
                Financial Officer, or Chief Technology Officer.
        \end{itemize}

  \item \textbf{Observer Rights.}  Synapse Capital shall be entitled to appoint
        one (1) non-voting Board observer who shall receive all materials
        distributed to Directors and may attend all Board meetings, but shall have
        no right to vote or be counted for quorum.
\end{enumerate}
