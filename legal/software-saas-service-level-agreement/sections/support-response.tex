\section{Support and Incident Response}
\label{sec:support}

\subsection{Severity Classification}
Provider classifies each support incident reported by Customer into one of four Severity Levels, with the corresponding Initial Response Time and Target Resolution Time set out in Table~\ref{tab:severity}. Provider assigns the initial Severity Level in good faith based on the information available at the time of the report and may revise the classification as additional information becomes available.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.1cm}p{5.0cm}p{2.6cm}p{2.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Severity} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Initial Response} & \textbf{Target Resolution} \\
\midrule
Severity 1 -- Critical & Production Service unavailable, or a core function (checkout, order sync) is unusable for all Customer users. & 30 minutes, 24/7 & 4 hours \\
\addlinespace
Severity 2 -- High & A major feature is degraded or unavailable for a significant subset of Customer users; no acceptable workaround exists. & 2 hours, Support Hours & 1 business day \\
\addlinespace
Severity 3 -- Medium & A non-critical feature is impaired but a reasonable workaround is available. & 8 business hours & 3 business days \\
\addlinespace
Severity 4 -- Low & Cosmetic issue, documentation question, or feature request with no operational impact. & 1 business day & Best efforts \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Support incident Severity Levels, Initial Response Time, and Target Resolution Time.}
\label{tab:severity}
\end{table}

\subsection{Escalation}
Severity 1 incidents may be escalated at any time to Provider's Director of Technical Support by telephone at the number designated in the Master Agreement order form. Provider will provide status updates to Customer at intervals not exceeding sixty (60) minutes for the duration of any open Severity 1 incident.

\subsection{Support Channels}
Customer may open support incidents through the in-application support console, by emailing \texttt{support@nexacloud.example}, or, for Severity 1 incidents only, by calling the 24/7 support line. \faPhone\ Provider will confirm receipt of every incident report within the Initial Response Time in Table~\ref{tab:severity}, regardless of the channel used.

\subsection{Target Resolution Time is Not a Guarantee}
The Target Resolution Times in Table~\ref{tab:severity} reflect Provider's operational goals and do not, by themselves, give rise to Service Credits. Only the Monthly Uptime Percentage calculated under Article~\ref{sec:availability} gives rise to Service Credits under Article~\ref{sec:credits}.
