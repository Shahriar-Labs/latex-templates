\section{General Provisions}
\label{sec:general}

\subsection{Governing Law}
\label{sec:governing-law}
This SLA is governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, and the parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Delaware for any dispute arising out of or relating to this SLA.

\subsection{Confidentiality}
\label{sec:confidentiality}
Each party's uptime figures, incident reports, and other information exchanged under this SLA are Confidential Information subject to the confidentiality provisions of the Master Agreement.

\subsection{Entire Agreement; Order of Precedence}
This SLA, together with the Master Agreement and any order forms, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the Service level commitments described herein and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements on that subject. In the event of a conflict between this SLA and the Master Agreement, this SLA controls solely with respect to availability commitments, Service Credits, and support response targets.

\subsection{Amendment}
Provider may update this SLA to reflect improvements in the Service, provided that no amendment may reduce the Monthly Uptime Target or the Service Credit remedies below the levels stated herein during Customer's then-current Term without Customer's prior written consent.

\subsection{Notices}
Notices under this SLA must be delivered in writing to the addresses set forth on the cover page, by email with confirmation of receipt or by nationally recognized overnight courier, and are deemed given upon confirmed receipt.

\subsection{Assignment}
Neither party may assign this SLA without the prior written consent of the other party, except that either party may assign this SLA without consent in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets.

\subsection{Severability and Waiver}
If any provision of this SLA is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect. No waiver of any provision of this SLA is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

\subsection{Counterparts}
This SLA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
