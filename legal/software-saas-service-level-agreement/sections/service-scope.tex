\section{Service Description and Scope}
\label{sec:service-scope}

\subsection{Overview}
Nexa Commerce Cloud is a multi-tenant SaaS platform providing order management, inventory synchronization, and storefront hosting for retail and e-commerce operations. This SLA applies to the Production environment identified in Customer's order form and does not apply to any Sandbox, staging, or development environment, which are provided without uptime commitment. The structure of this SLA, including its availability metric, service credit schedule, and severity-based support tiers, follows accepted commercial practice for cloud-hosted software agreements \citep{millard2021,lawrence2019,itstandards2026}.

\subsection{Included Components}
The Service, for purposes of the availability commitment in Article~\ref{sec:availability}, comprises the following components:

\begin{slalist}
\item The customer-facing storefront rendering layer and checkout flow;
\item The REST and webhook Application Programming Interfaces documented at \texttt{docs.nexacloud.example/api};
\item The administrative dashboard used to manage catalog, inventory, and order data; and
\item Authentication and session services required to access the foregoing.
\end{slalist}

\subsection{Excluded Components}
The following are excluded from the Service for purposes of the Monthly Uptime Percentage and are addressed, where applicable, in Article~\ref{sec:exclusions}: Customer's own network or internet connectivity; third-party payment gateways, tax calculation services, and shipping carriers integrated via the API; Beta Features; and any Customer customization deployed through the Nexa Scripting Extension.

\subsection{Changes to the Service}
Provider may modify the Service provided that any such modification does not materially reduce the core functionality of the Service during the then-current subscription term without at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Customer.
