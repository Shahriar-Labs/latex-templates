\section{Term and Termination}
\label{sec:term}

\subsection{Term}
This SLA commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months (the ``\textbf{Initial Term}''), and thereafter automatically renews for successive twelve (12) month periods (each, a ``\textbf{Renewal Term}'' and, together with the Initial Term, the ``\textbf{Term}''), unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current Term.

\subsection{Termination for Cause}
Either party may terminate the Master Agreement and this SLA if the other party materially breaches this SLA and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail.

\subsection{Termination for Chronic Failure}
\label{sec:chronic-failure}
If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 99.0\% for three (3) consecutive calendar months, or below 95.0\% in any single calendar month, Customer may terminate the Master Agreement for cause, without a cure period, by written notice delivered within sixty (60) days after the end of the calendar month giving rise to the termination right, and Provider will refund any prepaid Monthly Fees for the terminated portion of the Term.

\subsection{Effect of Termination}
\label{sec:effect-termination}
Upon termination or expiration of the Master Agreement for any reason, (a) Customer's right to access the Service ends immediately, (b) Provider will make Customer's data available for export in a commonly used format for thirty (30) days following the effective date of termination, and (c) Provider will delete Customer's data from production systems within ninety (90) days following the effective date of termination, except as required to comply with applicable law or as retained in routine backups pursuant to Provider's standard backup retention schedule.

\subsection{Survival}
Sections~\ref{sec:credit-cap} (Credit Cap and Application, solely as to credits accrued before termination), \ref{sec:effect-termination}, \ref{sec:governing-law}, and \ref{sec:confidentiality} survive termination or expiration of this SLA.
