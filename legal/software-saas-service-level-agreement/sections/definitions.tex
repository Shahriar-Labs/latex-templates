\section{Definitions}
\label{sec:definitions}

For purposes of this SLA, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

\begin{clauselist}
\item \textbf{``Service''} means the Nexa Commerce Cloud platform, including the hosted application, application programming interfaces, and administrative dashboard made available to Customer under the Master Agreement, but excluding Beta Features.
\item \textbf{``Downtime''} means any period during which the Service returns a server-side error rate greater than 5\% on production API endpoints, or is wholly unreachable, as measured by Provider's monitoring systems described in Article~\ref{sec:monitoring}.
\item \textbf{``Scheduled Maintenance''} means planned Service unavailability for which Provider gives Customer at least seventy-two (72) hours' advance notice in accordance with Article~\ref{sec:exclusions}.
\item \textbf{``Monthly Uptime Percentage''} means the percentage of total minutes in a calendar month, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Excluded Events, during which the Service did not experience Downtime, calculated as set out in Article~\ref{sec:availability}.
\item \textbf{``Service Credit''} means the remedy described in Article~\ref{sec:credits}, expressed as a percentage of the Monthly Fees payable for the calendar month in which the relevant Downtime occurred.
\item \textbf{``Severity Level''} means the classification assigned to a support incident under Article~\ref{sec:support}, determining the applicable Initial Response Time and Target Resolution Time.
\item \textbf{``Support Hours''} means Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Central Time, excluding Provider-observed holidays, except with respect to Severity 1 incidents, for which Support Hours means twenty-four (24) hours per day, seven (7) days per week.
\item \textbf{``Beta Feature''} means any Service functionality identified by Provider as ``beta,'' ``preview,'' ``early access,'' or similar designation, which is excluded from the Monthly Uptime Percentage calculation and provided without any service level commitment.
\end{clauselist}
