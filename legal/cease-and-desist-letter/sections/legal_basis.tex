\section{Legal Basis for Demands}

The conduct described in Section~2(C) gives rise to multiple independent causes
of action under federal and state law.

\subsection*{A.\quad Copyright Infringement — 17\,U.S.C.\,\S\,501}

Nexa is the registered owner of the copyrights identified above. Vertex's
reproduction and distribution of the Synapse source code in DataPulse Pro
without authorisation constitutes direct copyright infringement under
17~U.S.C.~\S~501. Nexa is entitled to:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item Statutory damages of up to \textbf{\$150,000 per work} infringed in
        cases of wilful infringement (17~U.S.C.~\S~504(c)(2));
  \item A permanent injunction under 17~U.S.C.~\S~502;
  \item Impoundment and destruction of all infringing copies
        (17~U.S.C.~\S~503); and
  \item Recovery of Nexa's attorneys' fees and costs (17~U.S.C.~\S~505).
\end{itemize}

Given that Vertex had actual notice of Nexa's copyrights through the Agreement
and its embedded copyright notices, infringement is \textit{wilful} as a
matter of law. \textit{See Fitzgerald Publishing Co.\ v.\ Baylor Publishing
Co.}, 807~F.2d~1110 (2d Cir.\ 1986).

\subsection*{B.\quad Breach of Contract}

The Agreement is a valid, binding contract supported by adequate consideration.
Vertex's distribution of DataPulse Pro incorporating Nexa's proprietary code
constitutes a material breach of at minimum:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Section~4.2} — prohibition on sublicensing or transfer of
        licence rights;
  \item \textbf{Section~4.5} — prohibition on competitive deployment; and
  \item \textbf{Section~7.1} — obligation to preserve all proprietary markings.
\end{itemize}

Nexa reserves the right to pursue expectation damages, consequential damages,
and specific performance under California Commercial Code and applicable
common law.

\subsection*{C.\quad Trademark Infringement — 15\,U.S.C.\,\S\,1114}

Vertex's use of the designation \textsc{SynapseCore} in connection with
competing software services is likely to cause confusion, mistake, or deception
as to the source or affiliation of Vertex's product with Nexa's registered
\textsc{Synapse} mark. This constitutes trademark infringement under the
Lanham Act, 15~U.S.C.~\S~1114, and unfair competition under 15~U.S.C.~\S~1125(a).

\subsection*{D.\quad Removal of Copyright Management Information — 17\,U.S.C.\,\S\,1202}

By stripping or altering Nexa's copyright notices embedded in the Synapse
source code, Vertex has violated the Digital Millennium Copyright Act's
provisions against removal of copyright management information, exposing
Vertex to additional statutory damages of up to \textbf{\$25,000} per
violation under 17~U.S.C.~\S~1203(c)(3)(B).
