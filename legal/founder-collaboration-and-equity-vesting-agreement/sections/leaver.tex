\section{Termination and Leaver Provisions}

\subsection{Classification} Upon termination of a Founder's Service, such Founder shall be classified as either a ``Good Leaver'' or a ``Bad Leaver'' in accordance with Table 2, which classification shall determine the Company's treatment of such Founder's vested and unvested Founder Shares.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \caption{Leaver Classification and Share Treatment}
  \label{tab:leaver}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l X X}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Classification} & \textbf{Triggering Events} & \textbf{Treatment of Shares} \\
    \midrule
    Good Leaver & Death; permanent disability; termination without Cause; resignation for Good Reason & Vested Shares retained in full. Unvested Shares repurchased by the Company at par value (\$0.0001/share). \\
    \addlinespace
    Bad Leaver & Termination for Cause; voluntary resignation without Good Reason within the first 24 months & Vested and Unvested Shares both subject to repurchase by the Company at the lower of fair market value or original issue price. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\subsection{Repurchase Mechanics} The Company may exercise its repurchase right under this Article IV by delivering written notice to the departing Founder within ninety (90) days following the termination of Service, specifying the number of shares to be repurchased and the aggregate repurchase price. Payment shall be made by check or wire transfer, and by delivery of a promissory note bearing interest at the applicable federal rate for any portion the Company's board reasonably determines would render the Company insolvent if paid in cash. If the Company does not exercise its repurchase right within such ninety (90) day period, the right shall lapse with respect to the shares that would otherwise have been repurchased.

\subsection{Voluntary Resignation After Month 24} A Founder who voluntarily resigns without Good Reason after the twenty-fourth (24th) month of continuous Service shall be treated as a Good Leaver with respect to Unvested Shares, provided such Founder has complied with the notice and transition obligations of Section 4.4.

\subsection{Transition Obligations} A resigning Founder shall provide the other Founders with no less than sixty (60) days' written notice, shall reasonably cooperate in the transition of duties and access credentials, and shall promptly return all Company property, including devices, source code repositories access, and confidential materials.

\subsection{No Severance Implied} Nothing in this Article IV shall be construed to create any entitlement to cash severance, continued benefits, or accelerated vesting beyond what is expressly provided in Section 3.5.
