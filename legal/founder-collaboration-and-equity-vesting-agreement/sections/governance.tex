\section{Roles, Responsibilities \& Decision-Making}

\subsection{Initial Roles} Until the Company's board of directors determines otherwise, the Founders shall serve in the following initial roles: Chen as Chief Executive Officer, with primary responsibility for fundraising, sales, and overall strategy; Osei as Chief Technology Officer, with primary responsibility for product architecture and engineering; and Ramanathan as Chief Product Officer, with primary responsibility for product design, user research, and roadmap prioritization.

\subsection{Decision-Making Authority} Corporate decisions shall require the approval thresholds set forth in Table 3. Where a threshold requires ``Majority,'' the affirmative vote of at least two (2) of the three (3) Founders is required. Where a threshold requires ``Unanimous,'' the affirmative vote of all Founders then providing Service is required.

\subsection{Board of Directors} Upon the Company's incorporation, the Founders shall constitute the initial board of directors. The Founders agree to negotiate in good faith to add one or more independent directors upon the closing of the Company's first institutional financing round.

\subsection{Deadlock Resolution} If the Founders are unable to reach the approval required under Section 6.2 for any matter requiring Unanimous approval, and such deadlock persists for fifteen (15) business days after the matter is first raised in writing, the Founders shall submit the dispute to non-binding mediation administered by a mutually agreed mediator in Austin, Texas. If mediation does not resolve the deadlock within thirty (30) additional days, the dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in accordance with Section 7.4.

\subsection{Time Commitment \& Outside Activities} Each Founder shall devote substantially all of such Founder's business time and attention to the Business, except that each Founder may continue any pre-existing board service, advisory role, or teaching engagement disclosed in writing to the other Founders prior to the Effective Date, provided such activity does not materially interfere with such Founder's duties to the Company. No Founder shall, without the prior written consent of the other Founders, engage in any business activity that competes with the Business or that would reasonably be expected to divert a corporate opportunity away from the Company.
