\section{Intellectual Property Assignment}

\subsection{Assignment of Company IP} Each Founder hereby irrevocably assigns, and agrees to assign, to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to any invention, discovery, software, source code, design, trade secret, know-how, or other work of authorship (collectively, ``Work Product'') that such Founder conceives, develops, or reduces to practice, whether alone or jointly with others, during the term of such Founder's Service and that relates to the Business or results from work performed for the Company, whether or not created using Company resources.

\subsection{Proprietary Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement} Concurrently with the execution of this Agreement, each Founder shall execute the Company's standard Proprietary Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement, attached as Exhibit B (the ``PIIA''), which shall govern the confidentiality and assignment of inventions in greater detail. In the event of any conflict between this Article V and the PIIA, the terms more protective of the Company shall control.

\subsection{Prior Inventions} Each Founder has listed on Schedule A attached hereto all inventions, original works of authorship, and other intellectual property that (i) were conceived or developed by such Founder prior to the Effective Date, (ii) are owned solely by such Founder or jointly with a third party, and (iii) are not assigned to the Company hereunder (``Prior Inventions''). If a Founder incorporates any Prior Inventions into Work Product, such Founder hereby grants the Company a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable, sublicensable license to make, use, reproduce, modify, and distribute such Prior Inventions solely as incorporated into the Work Product. Where no Prior Inventions are listed on Schedule A, the Founder represents that none exist.

\subsection{Moral Rights \& Further Assurances} To the extent permitted by applicable law, each Founder waives any moral rights in the Work Product and agrees to execute such further documents and take such further actions as the Company may reasonably request to perfect, register, or enforce the Company's rights in the Work Product, including in connection with patent prosecution and copyright registration. No Founder shall incorporate any open-source or third-party software into Company Work Product under a license that would require the Company to disclose or distribute its proprietary source code, without the prior written approval of the other Founders in accordance with Section 6.2.

\subsection{Third-Party Obligations} Each Founder represents that such Founder's performance under this Agreement does not and will not breach any agreement with a prior employer or other third party, including any agreement to keep confidential proprietary information of such prior employer, and that such Founder has not brought, and will not bring, onto Company premises or use in Company Work Product any confidential or proprietary materials of any former employer or other third party, except as disclosed in writing to the Company prior to the Effective Date.
