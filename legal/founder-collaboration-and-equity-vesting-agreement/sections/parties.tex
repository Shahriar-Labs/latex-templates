This Founder Collaboration \& Equity Vesting Agreement (the ``Agreement'') is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the ``Effective Date''), by and among:

\textbf{Nexa Dynamics, Inc.}, a corporation to be organized under the laws of the State of Delaware, with an anticipated principal place of business at 480 Harborview Terrace, Suite 210, Austin, TX 78701 (the ``Company''); and

\textbf{Maya Chen}, an individual residing in Austin, Texas (``Chen''); \textbf{Daniel Osei}, an individual residing in Austin, Texas (``Osei''); and \textbf{Priya Ramanathan}, an individual residing in Seattle, Washington (``Ramanathan''), each a ``Founder'' and collectively the ``Founders.''

The Company and the Founders may collectively be referred to as the ``Parties'' or individually as a ``Party.''

\centerline{\textbf{RECITALS}}
\vspace{0.5em}

\textbf{WHEREAS}, the Founders have jointly conceived of, and wish to develop, a business providing autonomous inventory-reconciliation software for mid-market logistics operators, to be conducted through the Company (the ``Business'');

\textbf{WHEREAS}, the Founders desire to memorialize their respective equity ownership, roles, and responsibilities, and to establish a vesting schedule under which their founder shares shall become non-forfeitable over time, so as to align long-term incentives and protect the Company against the departure of any Founder before their contribution to the Business is fully realized;

\textbf{WHEREAS}, each Founder has developed or will develop certain inventions, software, designs, and other intellectual property in connection with the Business, and the Parties intend that all such intellectual property be owned exclusively by the Company; and

\textbf{WHEREAS}, the Parties wish to set forth the governance procedures by which decisions of the Company will be made among the Founders prior to the appointment of an independent board of directors;

\section{Definitions}

\subsection{``Cause''} means, with respect to a Founder, (i) conviction of, or a plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or moral turpitude; (ii) willful and continued failure to substantially perform reasonably assigned duties after written notice and a thirty (30) day cure period; (iii) material breach of this Agreement, the Proprietary Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement (as defined below), or any other agreement with the Company; or (iv) an act of fraud, embezzlement, or misappropriation of Company property or funds.

\subsection{``Good Reason''} means, with respect to a Founder, without such Founder's written consent: (i) a material diminution in such Founder's title, authority, or duties; (ii) a reduction in such Founder's cash compensation, other than a reduction applied proportionately across all Founders; or (iii) relocation of the Company's principal office by more than fifty (50) miles.

\subsection{``Vesting Commencement Date''} means January 15, 2026 for each Founder, unless otherwise indicated on Exhibit A.

\subsection{``Change of Control''} means (i) a merger or consolidation of the Company in which the Company's stockholders immediately prior to such transaction hold, immediately after, less than fifty percent (50\%) of the voting power of the surviving entity; or (ii) a sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets.

\subsection{``Shares''} means shares of the Company's Common Stock issued to a Founder pursuant to this Agreement, together with any securities issued in respect thereof by way of stock split, dividend, or recapitalization.
