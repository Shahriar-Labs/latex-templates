\subsection{Basis of Allocation} The allocation set forth in Table 1 reflects the Founders' good-faith assessment of relative contributions to date, including pre-Effective-Date development work, capital contributed, and anticipated ongoing time commitment, consistent with prevailing practice for allocating founder equity at formation \cite{wasserman2012equity}.

\subsection{Future Dilution} The Founders acknowledge that the percentages in Table 1 will be diluted by the Company's option pool and by future issuances of equity approved in accordance with Article VI (Governance). No Founder shall be entitled to anti-dilution protection with respect to Founder Shares.

\subsection{Reservation for Option Pool} Concurrently with the Company's first institutional financing, the Parties agree to reserve an option pool of up to fifteen percent (15\%) of the Company's fully diluted capitalization for issuance to future employees, consultants, and advisors, with the precise size to be determined by unanimous agreement of the Founders in accordance with Section 6.2.

\subsection{No Additional Founder Issuances} Except as approved unanimously under Section 6.2, the Company shall not issue additional Founder Shares to any Founder, and no Founder shall be admitted as an additional ``Founder'' under this Agreement without the unanimous written consent of the other Founders.
