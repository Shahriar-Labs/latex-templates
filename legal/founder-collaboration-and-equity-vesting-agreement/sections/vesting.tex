\section{Vesting Schedule}

\subsection{Vesting Term} The Founder Shares issued to each Founder shall vest over forty-eight (48) months of continuous Service (as defined below) measured from such Founder's Vesting Commencement Date, subject to a twelve (12) month cliff as described in Section 3.2. ``Service'' means continuous full-time engagement by a Founder as an employee, officer, or director of the Company.

\subsection{One-Year Cliff} No Founder Shares shall vest prior to the first anniversary of the applicable Vesting Commencement Date. On such first anniversary, twenty-five percent (25\%) of a Founder's Founder Shares shall vest immediately, provided such Founder remains in continuous Service through that date.

\subsection{Monthly Vesting Thereafter} Following the first anniversary of the Vesting Commencement Date, an additional one forty-eighth (1/48) of the total Founder Shares shall vest at the end of each successive calendar month of continuous Service, such that all Founder Shares shall be fully vested on the fourth anniversary of the Vesting Commencement Date, as illustrated in Figure 1.

\subsection{Repurchase Right} Any Founder Shares that have not vested as of the date a Founder's Service terminates for any reason (``Unvested Shares'') shall remain subject to the Company's right to repurchase such Unvested Shares at the lower of (i) their original issue price or (ii) their then-current fair market value, exercisable by the Company within ninety (90) days of the termination of Service, as further described in Article IV.

\subsection{Acceleration on Change of Control} Notwithstanding Section 3.3, if, within twelve (12) months following a Change of Control, a Founder's Service is terminated by the Company (or its successor) without Cause, or by the Founder for Good Reason, fifty percent (50\%) of such Founder's then-unvested Founder Shares shall immediately vest as of the date of such termination (``double-trigger acceleration''). Vesting shall not accelerate solely upon the occurrence of a Change of Control.

\subsection{Leave of Absence} Vesting shall continue to accrue during a Founder's approved parental, medical, or short-term leave of absence of up to twelve (12) weeks in any twelve-month period. Vesting shall be suspended, but not forfeited, for any approved leave exceeding this period, and shall resume upon the Founder's return to active Service.
