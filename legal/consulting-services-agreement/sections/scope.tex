\section{Engagement Overview \& Scope of Work}

This Statement of Work (\textbf{``SOW''}) sets forth the specific consulting services
(\textbf{``Services''}) that \textbf{Apex Consulting Group} (``Consultant'') agrees to
provide to \textbf{Meridian Technologies Inc.}\ (``Client'') in connection with a
strategic digital transformation initiative. The Services are governed by the Master
Consulting Services Agreement between the parties and the additional terms herein.

\subsection{Business Objective}

Meridian Technologies has engaged Apex Consulting Group to design and implement a
company-wide \textbf{Enterprise Data Strategy} encompassing data governance, cloud
migration readiness, and a real-time analytics platform for the Client's North
American operations. The objective is to reduce time-to-insight for executive decision
making by a minimum of \textbf{40\%} within six months of go-live.

\subsection{Scope of Services}

The Consultant shall perform the following categories of work:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Discovery \& Assessment} --- Conduct a comprehensive audit of existing
    data infrastructure, identify gaps in governance maturity, and benchmark against
    industry best practices.
  \item \textbf{Strategy Design} --- Develop a multi-year Data Governance Roadmap,
    including a data ownership model, metadata standards, and a tiered data classification
    framework aligned with the Client's regulatory obligations.
  \item \textbf{Technology Architecture} --- Produce detailed architecture blueprints for
    a cloud-native data lakehouse on the Client's preferred cloud provider, including
    ingestion pipelines, transformation layers, and semantic query interfaces.
  \item \textbf{Change Management \& Training} --- Deliver structured training workshops
    for the Client's data engineering team (up to 25 participants) and produce
    onboarding documentation for end-users of the analytics dashboard.
  \item \textbf{Project Management \& Reporting} --- Assign a dedicated engagement
    manager who will issue bi-weekly status reports, maintain a project register, and
    attend weekly steering committee calls.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Out of Scope}

The following items are expressly excluded from this SOW and will require a separate
Change Order if required:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Procurement of third-party software licences or cloud infrastructure credits.
  \item Integration with systems outside the Client's North American data estate.
  \item Legal or regulatory compliance advisory beyond data governance recommendations.
  \item Ongoing support, managed services, or post-go-live operations.
\end{itemize}
