\section{Acceptance Procedure}
\label{sec:acceptance}

\subsection{Review \& Acceptance Process}

Upon submission of a Deliverable, the Client shall have \textbf{ten (10) business days}
to review the Deliverable and either:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
  \item issue written acceptance, confirming the Deliverable meets the criteria in
    Table~\ref{tab:deliverables}; or
  \item issue a written rejection notice specifying, in reasonable detail, all
    deficiencies that must be remedied.
\end{enumerate}

If the Client issues a rejection notice, the Consultant shall have \textbf{ten (10)
business days} to correct and resubmit the Deliverable. The Client shall then have a
further \textbf{five (5) business day} review period. If the Client fails to provide
written acceptance or rejection within any applicable review period, the Deliverable
shall be \textbf{deemed accepted} upon expiry of that period.

\subsection{Change Order Process}
\label{sec:changeorder}

Any request that materially alters the scope, schedule, or pricing of this SOW must be
formalised through the following Change Order process:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Either party may initiate a change request in writing, identifying the proposed
    modification and its rationale.
  \item Within \textbf{five (5) business days}, the Consultant shall prepare a written
    Change Order Proposal quantifying the impact on scope, timeline, and fees.
  \item The Client shall accept or reject the Proposal within \textbf{five (5) business
    days} of receipt.
  \item Work on changed scope shall not commence until a Change Order is executed by
    both parties. Verbal authorisations are not binding.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Intellectual Property \& Ownership}
\label{sec:ip}

\subsection{Work Product Ownership}

Upon receipt of full payment for each Milestone, the Consultant hereby assigns to the
Client all right, title, and interest in the Deliverables produced under this SOW,
including all copyright, trade secret, and other intellectual property rights therein
(\textbf{``Work Product''}).

Assignment is contingent on full payment. Until a Milestone payment is received, the
Consultant retains all rights in the corresponding Deliverables and grants the Client
a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use those Deliverables solely for
internal review purposes.

\subsection{Pre-existing Materials \& Tools}

The Consultant retains ownership of all methodologies, frameworks, templates, software
tools, and know-how that pre-date this SOW or are developed independently of it
(\textbf{``Consultant Tools''}). Where Deliverables incorporate Consultant Tools, the
Consultant grants the Client a \textbf{perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free} licence to
use those embedded Consultant Tools solely as part of the relevant Deliverable and for
the Client's internal business purposes.

\subsection{Third-Party Components}

If any Deliverable incorporates open-source software or third-party licensed components,
the Consultant shall disclose this in writing and ensure that the applicable licences are
compatible with the Client's intended use. The Consultant warrants that no Deliverable
will incorporate materials that would require the Client to disclose or licence its own
proprietary source code as a condition of use.
