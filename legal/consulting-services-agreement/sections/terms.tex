\section{Confidentiality \& Data Protection}

Each party (a ``Receiving Party'') agrees to hold the other party's Confidential
Information in strict confidence using at least the same degree of care it uses to
protect its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, but in no event less
than \textbf{reasonable care}. The Receiving Party shall:

\begin{itemize}
  \item restrict disclosure to employees, subcontractors, or advisers who have a
    need to know and are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as
    protective as those herein;
  \item not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than performing or
    receiving the Services; and
  \item promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon becoming aware of any unauthorised
    access or disclosure.
\end{itemize}

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this SOW for a period of
\textbf{three (3) years}. The parties shall execute Apex Consulting Group's standard
Data Processing Addendum (DPA-2024-R3) if any personal data is processed in the
course of the engagement.

\section{Term \& Termination}

\subsection{Term}

This SOW commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Deliverables have been
accepted and all fees paid, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Section.

\subsection{Termination for Convenience}

Either party may terminate this SOW for convenience upon \textbf{thirty (30) calendar
days'} written notice to the other. In such event:

\begin{itemize}
  \item The Client shall pay all fees due for Milestones fully accepted as at the
    date of termination.
  \item The Client shall pay a \textbf{pro-rata fee} for work demonstrably completed
    toward the next in-progress Milestone, not to exceed the value of that Milestone.
  \item Reimbursable expenses already incurred and approved shall be reimbursed.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Termination for Cause}

Either party may terminate this SOW immediately upon written notice if the other party:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
  \item materially breaches this SOW and fails to cure such breach within \textbf{fifteen
    (15) business days} of receiving written notice identifying the breach; or
  \item becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a
    receiver, administrator, or liquidator appointed over a material part of its assets.
\end{enumerate}
