\section{Fees, Invoicing \& Payment Terms}
\label{sec:fees}

\subsection{Fee Basis}

All fees in this SOW are \textbf{fixed-price} for each Milestone as listed in
Table~\ref{tab:milestones}. Fixed-price fees are not subject to adjustment based on
actual hours worked, provided the scope of work remains unchanged. If the Client requests
additional services via a Change Order, such additional work shall be priced at the
blended daily rate of \textbf{USD 1{,}850 per consulting day} (minimum one day per
engagement) unless otherwise agreed.

\subsection{Invoicing \& Payment}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The Consultant shall submit an invoice to the Client within \textbf{five (5) business
    days} of written acceptance of each Milestone's Deliverables.
  \item Invoices shall be addressed to the Client's Accounts Payable department and
    reference this SOW document number (\texttt{ACG-SOW-2025-047}).
  \item The Client shall pay each invoice within \textbf{thirty (30) calendar days} of the
    invoice date (``Net 30'').
  \item Overdue amounts shall accrue interest at \textbf{1.5\% per month} (18\% per annum)
    or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower, calculated from
    the due date until the date of full payment.
  \item All fees are stated exclusive of applicable taxes. The Client is responsible for
    any sales, use, value-added, or withholding taxes imposed by any governmental authority.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Withholding \& Set-off}

The Client may not withhold or set off any amounts against fees or expenses claimed by
the Consultant unless the Client has notified the Consultant in writing of a bona fide
dispute regarding the relevant Deliverable within the applicable acceptance review period
(ten business days). A valid dispute notification suspends the payment obligation only
for the disputed amount; undisputed amounts remain due in full.
