\PartHeading{CORPORATE DISCLOSURE STATEMENT}

\noindent Pursuant to Fed.\ R.\ App.\ P.\ 26.1 and 29(a)(4)(A), amicus curiae Meridian Alliance for Responsible Automation states as follows:

\medskip
\noindent Meridian Alliance for Responsible Automation is a nonprofit trade association organized under the laws of the State of Delaware. It has no parent corporation, and no publicly held corporation owns 10\% or more of its stock, because it issues no stock. Amicus's membership includes manufacturers and integrators of autonomous industrial systems, including Nimbus Dynamics, Inc.\ and members of the Apex Manufacturing Alliance, but no member corporation has a direct financial stake in the outcome of this appeal sufficient to require separate disclosure.
