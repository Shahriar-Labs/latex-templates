\label{sec:medical-instructions}

My Agent and my health care providers must give these instructions full effect.

\subsection{Artificial Nutrition and Hydration -- Additional Clarification}
The instruction above governs medically administered nutrition and hydration delivered through a tube or intravenous line. It does not affect assistance with oral eating and drinking that I am able to accept by mouth, which my Agent must continue to offer for as long as I can safely swallow.

\subsection{Additional Instructions}
\begin{itemize}[label=\textbullet,leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
\item I do not want to be transferred to a hospital from a hospice or home setting solely to prolong the dying process once the conditions in Section~\ref{sec:advance-directive} are met;
\item I want my Agent and providers to make reasonable efforts to allow me to be at home, or failing that, at Synapse Hospice \& Palliative Care, rather than in an acute care setting, during the final stage of a terminal condition;
\item I want my chosen clergy or spiritual advisor, if any, contacted and given reasonable access to me consistent with facility policy.
\end{itemize}
