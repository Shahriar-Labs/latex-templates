\section{HIPAA Authorization and Release of Medical Information}
\label{sec:hipaa}

\subsection{Authorization}
I authorize any physician, hospital, health care facility, or other covered entity that has provided or is providing care to me, including Nexa Regional Medical Center and Synapse Hospice \& Palliative Care, to disclose my complete medical records and any other protected health information, as defined under applicable health information privacy law, to my Agent and to each alternate named in Table~\ref{tab:agents}. This authorization is intended to satisfy the requirements of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act and its implementing regulations, so that my Agent may make informed decisions on my behalf without delay.

\subsection{Persons Authorized to Receive Information}
\begin{itemize}[label=\faLock,leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
\item My Health Care Agent and each named alternate;
\item My attending physicians and any consulting specialists;
\item Vertex Family Law Group, solely to the extent necessary to confirm the validity or terms of this Directive;
\item Meridian Trust \& Estate Partners, as custodian of the executed original of this Directive.
\end{itemize}

This authorization has no expiration date and remains effective until revoked by me in writing.
