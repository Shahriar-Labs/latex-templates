\subsection{Powers Granted to Agent}
Subject to any limitation stated in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}, my Agent is authorized to:
\begin{itemize}[label=\textbullet,leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=2pt]
\item Consent to, refuse, or withdraw any medical treatment, service, or diagnostic procedure, including surgery, medication, and life-sustaining treatment;
\item Select, retain, and discharge physicians, nurses, therapists, and other health care providers;
\item Admit me to, or discharge me from, any hospital, hospice, nursing facility, or other health care institution, including the providers referenced in Section~\ref{sec:providers};
\item Request, receive, and review any information regarding my physical or mental health, including medical and hospital records, and consent to disclosure of that information under Article~\ref{sec:hipaa};
\item Employ and discharge health care personnel, and pay reasonable fees and expenses for health care from my funds;
\item Take any other action necessary to carry out the instructions in this Directive or to advance my best interests as my Agent reasonably understands them.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations on Agent's Authority}
\label{sec:limitations}
My Agent has no authority to:
\begin{itemize}[label=\textbullet,leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=2pt]
\item Direct any action contrary to a specific instruction I have given in Article~\ref{sec:advance-directive} or Article~\ref{sec:medical-instructions};
\item Consent to voluntary admission to a psychiatric facility for longer than seventy-two (72) hours without a separate court order;
\item Consent to psychosurgery, sterilization, or participation in experimental research on my behalf, except research directed at a life-threatening condition from which I am suffering and approved by an institutional review board;
\item Revoke or amend this Directive; only I may do so, in writing, while I have capacity.
\end{itemize}
