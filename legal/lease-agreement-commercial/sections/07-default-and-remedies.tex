\section{DEFAULT AND REMEDIES}
\subsection{Events of Default}
The occurrence of any of the following shall constitute an event of default:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=2em]
    \item Failure to pay Base Rent or Additional Rent within five (5) days after the due date.
    \item Failure to perform any other covenant for more than fifteen (15) days after written notice from Landlord.
    \item Insolvency or bankruptcy of Tenant.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Remedies}
Upon default, Landlord may, at its option, terminate this Lease and re-enter the Premises, recover damages including all past due rent, accelerated future rent discounted to present value, and all reasonable attorney fees and costs incurred in enforcing this Lease.
