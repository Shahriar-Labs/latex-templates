\section{USE AND OCCUPANCY}
\subsection{Permitted Use}
Tenant shall use the Premises solely for general office purposes, software engineering, technology research and development, and administrative activities associated with Apex Systems Group Inc., and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent.

\subsection{Compliance with Laws}
Tenant shall, at its sole expense, promptly comply with all applicable local, state, and federal laws, regulations, and ordinances relating to its specific use of the Premises, including but not limited to zoning ordinances and occupational health and safety regulations.
