\section{FINANCIAL TERMS AND ESCROW HOLDINGS}

\subsection{Purchase Price Allocation}
The total purchase price to be paid by Buyer to Seller for the conveyance of the Property is \textbf{\$2,450,000.00} (Two Million Four Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars) (the ``Purchase Price''). The Purchase Price shall be paid in full on the Closing Date in lawful currency of the United States of America, subject to credit adjustments and prorations as detailed below.

\subsection{Earnest Money Deposit}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Initial Deposit:} Within three (3) business days following the Effective Date, Buyer shall deposit the sum of \textbf{\$50,000.00} (Fifty Thousand Dollars) (the ``Earnest Money Deposit'') via electronic wire transfer into the escrow account of Meridian Title \& Escrow Company (the ``Escrow Holder'').
    \item \textbf{Handling of Deposit:} The Deposit shall be held in a non-interest-bearing account. Upon closing, the Deposit shall be credited against the total Purchase Price. In the event of contract termination under the contingency clauses, the Deposit shall be returned to Buyer in full.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Prorations and Transaction Fees}
