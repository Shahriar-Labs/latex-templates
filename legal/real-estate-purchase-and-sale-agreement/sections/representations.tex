\section{REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES}

\subsection{Seller's Representations and Warranties}
Seller represents and warrants to Buyer, as of the Effective Date and the Closing Date, the following:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Authority:} Seller has corporate power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement. The execution of this Agreement has been authorized by all necessary company actions.
    \item \textbf{Litigation:} There are no pending, or to the best of Seller's knowledge, threatened legal actions, claims, or arbitrations affecting the Property or Seller's ownership thereof.
    \item \textbf{Hazardous Substances:} To the best of Seller's knowledge, the Property contains no hazardous materials, toxic waste, underground storage tanks, or other environmental liabilities in violation of applicable laws.
    \item \textbf{No Tenant Leases:} Seller warrants that no lease, rental agreement, or tenancy exists regarding any portion of the Property that will extend past the Closing Date.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Buyer's Representations and Warranties}
Buyer represents and warrants to Seller, as of the Effective Date and the Closing Date, the following:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Organization:} Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing under the laws of the State of California.
    \item \textbf{Financing Capability:} Buyer has, or will have at the time of Closing, sufficient cash resources and debt commitments to pay the full Purchase Price and all closing fees.
    \item \textbf{Independent Investigation:} Except as explicitly stated in this Agreement, Buyer is buying the Property based on its own independent due diligence reviews, physical inspections, and title examinations.
\end{enumerate}
