\section{Treatment Plan \& Recommendations}

To address Mr. Mercer's cognitive, occupational, and psychological challenges, the following multi-modal treatment plan is recommended:

\subsection{Pharmacotherapy \& Medical Follow-Up}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Psychiatric Consultation:} It is recommended that Mr. Mercer consult with a psychiatrist to evaluate the appropriateness of pharmacological interventions for ADHD (e.g., stimulant or non-stimulant medications) to improve working memory and sustained attention.
    \item \textbf{Cardiovascular Monitoring:} Since Mr. Mercer is currently taking lisinopril for hypertension, any initiation of stimulant medication should be closely coordinated with his primary care physician, Dr. Clara Synapse, to monitor blood pressure and heart rate.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Psychotherapy \& Executive Coaching}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) for Adult ADHD:} Engage in structured CBT specifically tailored for adult ADHD to develop organizational strategies, time-management protocols, and cognitive restructuring techniques to combat self-critical thought patterns related to performance.
    \item \textbf{Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT):} Utilize ACT to address dysthymia and work-related stress, focusing on values-clarification and psychological flexibility to reduce demoralization.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Occupational Accommodations}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Workspace Modification:} Minimize auditory and visual distractions in his workspace (e.g., using noise-canceling headphones, positioning his desk away from high-traffic office areas).
    \item \textbf{Task Chunking and Digital Organizers:} Break down large, multi-step system administration projects into smaller, discrete tasks with intermediate deadlines. Utilize digital scheduling tools with automated reminders to manage chronic lateness.
\end{enumerate}
