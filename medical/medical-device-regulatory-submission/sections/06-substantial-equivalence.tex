\subsection{Assessment of differences}
The subject device introduces electronic upload, a different adhesive perimeter, increased solid-state memory, and a redesigned event-detection pipeline. These changes do not alter the intended use or fundamental scientific technology. Upload security and integrity were evaluated through cybersecurity, transport, and end-to-end record-reconciliation testing. Adhesive changes were addressed through biocompatibility, peel, wear, and usability testing. Algorithm changes were evaluated against an independent, adjudicated clinical dataset.

\begin{noticebox}[title={\bfseries Substantial-equivalence determination}]
The similarities in intended use, operating principle, patient interface, monitoring duration, professional review, and output support equivalence. The identified differences are bounded by recognized methods and do not raise different questions of safety or effectiveness.
\end{noticebox}
