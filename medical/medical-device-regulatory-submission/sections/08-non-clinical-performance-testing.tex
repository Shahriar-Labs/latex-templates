\subsection{Essential performance}
Essential performance is accurate acquisition, preservation, and time alignment of ECG data such that no hazardous corruption is presented as valid data. Fault handling suppresses invalid segments, records diagnostic events, and prevents report release when integrity checks fail. EMC and single-fault testing demonstrated maintenance of essential performance or transition to a detectable safe state.
