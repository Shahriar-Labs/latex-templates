\subsection{Core warnings and precautions}
\begin{warningbox}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{No real-time alarm:} the system does not provide immediate notification of arrhythmia. Seek emergency assistance for symptoms of a medical emergency.
  \item \textbf{Skin safety:} do not place on broken, irritated, or infected skin. Remove the patch and contact a healthcare professional for severe discomfort or a spreading skin reaction.
  \item \textbf{Interference and procedures:} remove before MRI. Do not apply defibrillation pads over the patch. Follow clinical direction for other procedures.
  \item \textbf{Data completeness:} detachment, improper placement, electromagnetic disturbance, or depleted battery can reduce analyzable recording time.
\end{itemize}
\end{warningbox}

\subsection{Patient workflow}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Confirm the labeled patient and expiration date; prepare intact skin at the prescribed location.
  \item Apply the patch using the numbered liner tabs and press the perimeter firmly for 30 seconds.
  \item Press the center button once to begin recording; verify the green activation indication.
  \item During symptoms, press the button once and record the symptom and time in the diary or application.
  \item At the end of wear, peel the patch back slowly, place it in the return pouch, and follow the clinic's return instructions.
\end{enumerate}
