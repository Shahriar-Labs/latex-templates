\section{Biocompatibility}

The patient-contacting adhesive and electrode assembly is categorized as a surface device contacting intact skin for prolonged duration ($>$24 hours to 30 days). The biological evaluation follows ISO 10993-1:2018 and includes material characterization, supplier controls, toxicological assessment, and endpoint testing on the final finished device.

\rowcolor{Navy}\thead{Endpoint} & \thead{Method} & \thead{Result} & \thead{Outcome} \\
\midrule
Cytotoxicity & ISO 10993-5 & Grade 0 reactivity; cell viability met acceptance criterion & \statuspass \\
Sensitization & ISO 10993-10 / 10993-23 & No evidence of skin sensitization & \statuspass \\
Irritation & ISO 10993-23 & Negligible irritation response & \statuspass \\
Chemical characterization & ISO 10993-18 & Extractables characterized under exaggerated conditions & \statuspass \\
Toxicological risk & ISO 10993-17 & Margins of safety acceptable for identified constituents & \statuspass \\
\bottomrule

No new colorants, natural rubber latex, phthalates, or animal-derived materials are used in the patient-contacting assembly. Manufacturing residues are controlled through validated processes and change control. The biological evaluation report \docid{BIO-017} concludes that the device is biocompatible for its intended type and duration of contact.
