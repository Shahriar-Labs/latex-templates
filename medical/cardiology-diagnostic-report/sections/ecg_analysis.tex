\section{Electrocardiogram (ECG) Analysis}

Baseline 12-lead electrocardiogram was obtained prior to the stress test according to established standards~\cite{kligfield2007standardization}. The ECG demonstrates a normal sinus rhythm with a resting heart rate of 68 beats per minute. The electrical axis is normal, and there are no signs of acute ischemia or prior infarction.
