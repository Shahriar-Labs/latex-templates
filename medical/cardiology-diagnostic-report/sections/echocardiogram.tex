\subsection{Structural and Valvular Assessment}
\begin{description}
    \item[Left Ventricle:] The left ventricle is normal in size. Wall thickness is at the upper limit of normal, consistent with mild concentric remodeling, likely secondary to patient's history of systemic hypertension. There are no regional wall motion abnormalities at rest. Global systolic function is preserved with an estimated ejection fraction (LVEF) of 62\% by biplane method of disks. Diastolic parameters suggest mild impairment of relaxation (Grade I diastolic dysfunction) with an E/A ratio of 0.82 and deceleration time of 220 ms.
    \item[Right Ventricle:] The right ventricle is normal in size and demonstrates normal systolic function. Tricuspid annular plane systolic excursion (TAPSE) is 2.2 cm. No evidence of right ventricular hypertrophy or pressure overload is observed.
    \item[Atria:] Left and right atria are normal in volume. The interatrial septum appears intact by color Doppler.
    \item[Valvular Function:] 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Aortic Valve:} Trileaflet, thin, and pliable. There is no stenosis; peak velocity is 1.2 m/s. Trace aortic regurgitation is noted.
        \item \textbf{Mitral Valve:} Structurally normal leaflets. Normal inflow velocity. Trace mitral regurgitation is observed. No mitral stenosis.
        \item \textbf{Tricuspid Valve:} Normal leaflets. Trace tricuspid regurgitation is present, allowing estimation of right ventricular systolic pressure (RVSP) of approximately 26 mmHg (assuming right atrial pressure of 5 mmHg), indicating no pulmonary hypertension.
        \item \textbf{Pulmonic Valve:} Normal leaflets. Trace pulmonic insufficiency.
    \end{itemize}
    \item[Pericardium:] No pericardial effusion or thickening is identified.
\end{description}
