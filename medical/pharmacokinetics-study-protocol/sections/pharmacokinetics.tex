\section{Pharmacokinetics \& Statistical Modeling}
\label{sec:pharmacokinetics}

\subsection{Pharmacokinetic Parameter Estimation}

Pharmacokinetic analysis of plasma and urine concentrations of SYN-409 will be performed using non-compartmental analysis (NCA) methods \citep{gibaldi2007pk}. Calculations will be executed using Phoenix WinNonlin\textsuperscript{\textregistered} version 8.3 or validated PK packages in R (version 4.3.2). 

The following PK parameters will be estimated from individual concentration-time data:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{$C_{\max}$:} Maximum observed plasma concentration, obtained directly from the raw data.
  \item \textbf{$t_{\max}$:} Time to reach maximum plasma concentration, obtained directly from the raw data.
  \item \textbf{$\lambda_{\text{z}}$:} Terminal elimination rate constant, estimated by linear regression of the terminal log-linear phase of the concentration-time curve.
  \item \textbf{$t_{1/2}$:} Terminal elimination half-life, calculated as:
    \begin{equation}
      t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln(2)}{\lambda_{\text{z}}}
    \end{equation}
  \item \textbf{$\text{AUC}_{0-t}$:} Area under the plasma concentration-time curve from time zero to the last quantifiable concentration, calculated using the linear-up log-down trapezoidal rule.
  \item \textbf{$\text{AUC}_{0-\infty}$:} Area under the plasma concentration-time curve extrapolated to infinity, calculated as:
    \begin{equation}
      \text{AUC}_{0-\infty} = \text{AUC}_{0-t} + \frac{C_t}{\lambda_{\text{z}}}
    \end{equation}
    where $C_t$ is the last quantifiable concentration.
  \item \textbf{$\text{CL}/F$:} Apparent total clearance after oral administration, calculated as Dose / $\text{AUC}_{0-\infty}$.
  \item \textbf{$V_{\text{z}}/F$:} Apparent volume of distribution during the terminal elimination phase, calculated as $\text{CL}/F / \lambda_{\text{z}}$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Dose Proportionality Analysis}

To assess the dose proportionality of SYN-409 over the 10~mg to 600~mg dose range, a power model will be fitted to the primary parameters ($C_{\max}$ and $\text{AUC}_{0-\infty}$):
\begin{equation}
  \ln(\text{Parameter}) = \beta_0 + \beta_1 \cdot \ln(\text{Dose}) + \varepsilon
\end{equation}
where $\beta_1$ represents the dose-proportionality coefficient. True dose proportionality is concluded if the $90\%$ confidence interval for $\beta_1$ falls entirely within the interval $[0.90, 1.10]$.
