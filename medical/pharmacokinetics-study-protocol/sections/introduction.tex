  This Phase I study is designed to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetic (PK) profile of single ascending oral doses of SYN-409 in healthy adult subjects. SYN-409 is a novel, highly selective, small-molecule inhibitor of the intracellular kinase pathway designed for the treatment of inflammatory diseases. Preclinical models in rodents and non-human primates have demonstrated dose-dependent efficacy with no significant target-related toxicities. This trial represents the first-in-human (FIH) clinical evaluation of the compound.

\subsection{Background and Scientific Rationale}

Intracellular kinase dysregulation plays a pivotal role in the pathogenesis of chronic autoimmune and inflammatory diseases. Preclinical characterization of SYN-409, developed by Synapse Therapeutics, indicates high potency (IC$_{50}$ = 4.2~nM) and excellent selectivity against a panel of 450 human kinases. In animal safety studies, SYN-409 demonstrated clean cardiovascular, respiratory, and central nervous system safety margins at exposures up to 50-fold higher than the projected therapeutic range.

The pharmacokinetic profile in animals was characterized by rapid absorption ($t_{\max}$ between 1.0 and 2.5 hours), moderate volume of distribution ($V_{\text{d}}/F \approx 1.5$~L/kg), and a terminal elimination half-life supporting once-daily dosing. The scaling predictions based on physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) models suggest that the target therapeutic exposure in humans will be achieved at doses between 100~mg and 300~mg \citep{sterling2025scaling}. This trial is required to characterize the human PK profile and confirm dose proportionality over a broad range of doses.

\subsection{Study Objectives}

The clinical and scientific objectives of this protocol are defined hierarchically as follows:

\subsubsection{Primary Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item To evaluate the safety and tolerability of single ascending oral doses of SYN-409 in healthy male and female adult subjects.
  \item To characterize the single-dose pharmacokinetics of SYN-409 in plasma and urine following oral administration.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Secondary Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item To assess the dose-proportionality of SYN-409 exposure (C$_{\max}$ and AUC) over the studied dose range (10~mg to 600~mg).
  \item To determine the excretion profile of parent compound and major metabolites in urine.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exploratory Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item To assess the relationship between plasma concentrations of SYN-409 and potential pharmacodynamic (PD) biomarkers (e.g., inhibition of target phosphorylation in peripheral blood mononuclear cells).
\end{itemize}
