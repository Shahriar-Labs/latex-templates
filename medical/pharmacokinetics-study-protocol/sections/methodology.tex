\section{Study Design \& Methodology}
\label{sec:methodology}

\subsection{Overall Study Design}

This is a Phase I, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, single ascending dose (SAD) study in healthy adult subjects. The study will be conducted in five sequential cohorts, evaluating doses of 10~mg, 30~mg, 100~mg, 300~mg, and 600~mg administered orally as a liquid suspension under fasting conditions. 

Each cohort will consist of 10 subjects, randomized in an 8:2 ratio to receive either active SYN-409 (n = 8) or matching placebo (n = 2). Dose escalation will proceed sequentially, pending safety and tolerability review of the cumulative data from the preceding cohort by the Safety Review Committee (SRC). \subsection{Study Cohorts and Dosing Schedule}

Dose levels have been selected based on preclinical toxicology and PK scaling studies \citep{sterling2025scaling}. The starting dose of 10~mg is estimated to be well below the anticipated pharmacologically active dose and provides a safety margin of at least 100-fold relative to the animal no-observed-adverse-effect-level (NOAEL). 

The dosing schedule is summarized below:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cohort 1:} Single dose of 10~mg active or placebo.
  \item \textbf{Cohort 2:} Single dose of 30~mg active or placebo.
  \item \textbf{Cohort 3:} Single dose of 100~mg active or placebo.
  \item \textbf{Cohort 4:} Single dose of 300~mg active or placebo.
  \item \textbf{Cohort 5:} Single dose of 600~mg active or placebo.
\end{itemize}

Dose escalation will be halted if two or more subjects in a cohort experience a drug-related severe adverse event (SAE) or if grade 3 laboratory abnormalities are reported.

\subsection{Subject Eligibility Criteria}

Subjects must meet all of the following criteria to be eligible for enrollment in this study:

\subsubsection{Inclusion Criteria}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\lbrack\arabic*\rbrack]
  \item Healthy male or female volunteers aged 18 to 55 years, inclusive.
  \item Body Mass Index (BMI) between 18.5 and 30.0~kg/m$^2$, inclusive, and a total body weight $\ge 50$~kg.
  \item Medically healthy as determined by pre-study physical examination, 12-lead ECG, vital signs, and clinical laboratory evaluations.
  \item Agreement to use highly effective contraception methods throughout the study and for 30 days post-dose.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Exclusion Criteria}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\lbrack\arabic*\rbrack]
  \item History of significant cardiovascular, renal, hepatic, or gastrointestinal disease that might interfere with absorption, distribution, metabolism, or excretion of the drug.
  \item Positive screen for hepatitis B, hepatitis C, or human immunodeficiency virus (HIV).
  \item Regular consumption of alcohol exceeding 14 units per week, or active drug abuse.
  \item Participation in another clinical study within 30 days prior to screening.
\end{enumerate}
