\subsection{Primary Efficacy Endpoint}

The primary efficacy endpoint was met (Table~\ref{tab:primary}). At Week 16, the proportion of patients achieving PASI 75 was significantly greater in the \studydrug\ group (72.3\%) compared to the placebo group (14.2\%), with an adjusted treatment difference of 58.1\% (95\% CI: 52.4\%, 63.8\%; $p < 0.0001$). The superiority of \studydrug\ over placebo was consistent across all pre-specified subgroups defined by baseline weight, prior biologic use, sex, age, race, and geographic region.

\subsection{Key Secondary Efficacy Endpoints}

All key secondary efficacy endpoints were met with statistical significance following the pre-specified hierarchical testing procedure (Table~\ref{tab:secondary}). The proportion of patients achieving sPGA 0/1 at Week 16 was 62.9\% in the \studydrug\ group compared to 9.0\% in the placebo group ($p < 0.0001$). PASI 90 was achieved by 48.8\% and 4.9\% of patients in the \studydrug\ and placebo groups, respectively ($p < 0.0001$). A clinically meaningful and statistically significant improvement in DLQI was observed in the \studydrug\ group (LS mean change: $-10.4$) compared to placebo ($-2.3$; $p < 0.0001$).
