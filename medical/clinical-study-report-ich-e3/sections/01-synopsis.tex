\toprule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{\large\color{accent}Study Identification}}\\
\midrule
Name of Sponsor          & Valkyra Bio Pharmaceuticals Corporation \\
Name of Finished Product & \studydrug\ (film-coated tablet) \\
Name of Active Ingredient & Brexiprant \\
Study Title              & A Phase III, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Multicenter Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of \studydrug\ in Patients with Moderate-to-Severe Plaque Psoriasis \\
Protocol Number          & \protnum \\
EudraCT Number           & 2024--001234--56 \\
NCT Number               & NCT06123456 \\
Phase of Development     & Phase III \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{\large\color{accent}Methodology}}\\
\midrule
Study Design             & Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group, multicenter study. Patients were randomized in a 2:1 ratio to receive either \studydrug\ \studydose\ or placebo once daily for 52 weeks. The study comprised a 4-week screening period, a 52-week treatment period, and a 4-week follow-up period. \\
Number of Patients       & \\
\quad  Planned            & A total of 660 patients (440 \studydrug, 220 placebo) \\
\quad  Enrolled           & 950 patients were screened; 675 were randomized (Intent-to-Treat population) \\
\quad  Completed          & 590 patients (87.4\%) completed the 52-week treatment period \\
Diagnosis and Main Criteria for Inclusion & Adult patients (18--75 years) with a diagnosis of moderate-to-severe chronic plaque psoriasis for $\ge 6$ months, with a PASI score $\ge 12$, a static Physician's Global Assessment (sPGA) score $\ge 3$, and body surface area (BSA) involvement $\ge 10\%$ at screening and baseline. \\
Test Product, Dose, and Mode of Administration & \studydrug\ \studydose\ film-coated tablet, administered orally once daily. \\
Reference Therapy, Dose, and Mode of Administration & Matching placebo film-coated tablet, administered orally once daily. \\
Duration of Treatment    & 52 weeks \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{\large\color{accent}Evaluation Criteria}}\\
\midrule
Primary Efficacy Endpoint & Proportion of patients achieving a $\ge 75\%$ reduction from baseline in Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI 75) at Week 16. \\
Key Secondary Endpoints  & \begin{enumerate}[nosep, leftmargin=*]
  \item Proportion of patients achieving sPGA score of 0 (clear) or 1 (almost clear) at Week 16.
  \item Proportion of patients achieving PASI 90 at Week 16.
  \item Change from baseline in Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI) at Week 16.
  \item Proportion of patients achieving PASI 75 at Week 52.
\end{enumerate} \\
Safety Endpoints         & Treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs), serious adverse events (SAEs), clinical laboratory parameters, vital signs, electrocardiograms, and physical examinations. \\
Pharmacokinetic Endpoints & Trough plasma concentrations of brexiprant at Weeks 4, 16, and 52 (subset of patients). \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{\large\color{accent}Statistical Methods}}\\
\midrule
Primary Analysis         & The primary efficacy analysis was performed on the Full Analysis Set (FAS) using a Cochran--Mantel--Haenszel (CMH) test stratified by baseline weight ($\le 90$ kg vs.\ $> 90$ kg) and prior biologic use (yes/no). \\
Sample Size Determination & A sample size of 660 patients (440 \studydrug, 220 placebo) provided $\ge 95\%$ power to detect a 25\% absolute difference in PASI 75 response rates at Week 16, assuming a placebo response rate of 15\% and a two-sided significance level of 0.05, accounting for a 15\% dropout rate. \\
Multiplicity Adjustment   & A hierarchical testing procedure was applied to control the family-wise Type I error rate across the primary and key secondary endpoints. \\
Interim Analyses          & One pre-planned interim analysis for futility was conducted by an independent Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) when 50\% of patients completed Week 16. \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{\large\color{accent}Summary of Results}}\\
\midrule
Efficacy                 & \textbf{Primary Endpoint Met:} The proportion of patients achieving PASI 75 at Week 16 was 72.3\% in the \studydrug\ group versus 14.2\% in the placebo group (adjusted difference: 58.1\%; 95\% CI: 52.4\%, 63.8\%; $p < 0.0001$). All key secondary endpoints were met with statistical significance ($p < 0.0001$). \\
Safety                   & \studydrug\ was generally well tolerated over 52 weeks. The most frequently reported TEAEs in the \studydrug\ group were nasopharyngitis (18.2\%), upper respiratory tract infection (12.4\%), and headache (8.9\%). SAEs were reported in 4.2\% of \studydrug-treated patients and 3.6\% of placebo-treated patients. No deaths occurred during the study. \\
Conclusion               & \studydrug\ \studydose\ administered orally once daily demonstrated superior efficacy compared to placebo for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis, with a favorable safety profile consistent with the known mechanism of action. \\
