\section{Study Objectives}

\subsection{Primary Objective}

The primary objective of this study was to evaluate the efficacy of \studydrug\ \studydose\ administered orally once daily compared to placebo in the treatment of adults with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis, as measured by the proportion of patients achieving a $\ge 75\%$ reduction from baseline in Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI 75) at Week 16.

\subsection{Secondary Objectives}

The secondary objectives were to evaluate the efficacy of \studydrug\ compared to placebo with respect to:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item Proportion of patients achieving a static Physician's Global Assessment (sPGA) score of 0 (clear) or 1 (almost clear) at Week 16.
  \item Proportion of patients achieving PASI 90 at Week 16.
  \item Change from baseline in Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI) at Week 16.
  \item Long-term maintenance of response (PASI 75 at Week 52).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Safety Objective}

To evaluate the safety and tolerability of \studydrug\ \studydose\ administered orally once daily for up to 52 weeks, including the incidence and severity of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs), serious adverse events (SAEs), changes in clinical laboratory parameters, vital signs, and electrocardiogram findings.

\subsection{Pharmacokinetic Objective}

To characterize the trough plasma concentration ($C_{\text{trough}}$) of brexiprant in a subset of patients at Weeks 4, 16, and 52.
