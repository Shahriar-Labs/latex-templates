\section{Implant Specification \& Visual Setup}

Based on the CBCT dimensions, a tapered implant system has been selected to optimize primary stability in the D3 cancellous bone while preserving the thin buccal plate.

\subsection{Implant Specifications}
The surgical plan specifies the following implant componentry:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Brand / Line:} Nexa Dental Systems -- ActiveTaper Grade 5 Titanium
    \item \textbf{Diameter:} $\varnothing$ 4.3 mm (platform size: 4.5 mm)
    \item \textbf{Length:} 11.5 mm
    \item \textbf{Connection Type:} Conical internal hex (11$^\circ$ taper, 2.5 mm depth)
    \item \textbf{Surface Treatment:} Sandblasted, large-grit, acid-etched (SLA)
\end{itemize}
