\section{Surgical Guide \& CAD/CAM Specifications}

To ensure precise transfer of the 3D virtual plan to the clinical environment, a CAD/CAM surgical template will be fabricated based on the patient's CBCT and intraoral optical scans.

\subsection{Guide Fabrication Parameters}
The surgical guide will be modeled using Synapse Dental CAD (v4.2) and fabricated via stereolithography (SLA) using biocompatible resin:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Guide Type:} Tooth-supported, fully guided. Anchored on teeth \#18, \#20, \#21, and \#22 to guarantee three-dimensional stability.
    \item \textbf{Sleeve System:} Meridian Guided System -- 4.3 mm diameter guide sleeve.
    \item \textbf{Planned Offset:} 9.0 mm (distance from the implant platform to the top of the guide sleeve).
    \item \textbf{Fabrication Material:} Clear surgical guide resin (Class I medical device, sterilized via autoclave at 121$^\circ$C for 15 minutes).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Pre-Surgical Inspection Checklist}
The clinician must verify the following parameters prior to the patient encounter:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Fit Verification:} Place the template intraorally and verify intimate adaptation. Inspect through the inspection windows to confirm full seating on the occlusal surfaces.
    \item \textbf{Stability Check:} Confirm there is no rocking or displacement under alternating digital pressure.
    \item \textbf{Sleeve Integrity:} Check that the metallic guidance sleeves are securely bonded to the resin body and show no signs of micro-fractures.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Prosthetic Plan}
Following a healing phase of 12--16 weeks, osseointegration will be assessed clinical-radiographically. The prosthetic phase will consist of:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Restoration Type:} Screw-retained monolithic zirconia crown.
    \item \textbf{Abutment:} Nexa Custom Ti-Base abutment (transgingival height: 2.0 mm).
    \item \textbf{Retention:} Occlusal screw access hole sealed with composite resin.
\end{itemize}
