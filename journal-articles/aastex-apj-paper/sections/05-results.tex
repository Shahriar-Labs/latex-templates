\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Our best-fit system parameters are summarized in Table~\ref{tab:params}.
The revised planetary radius, $R_p = 2.71 \pm 0.09\,R_\oplus$, is
4.2\% larger than the initial \textit{TESS} candidate estimate of
$2.60\,R_\oplus$, with the difference attributable to the dilution
correction from the nearby companion star (Section~\ref{sec:star}).
The impact parameter $b = a\cos(i)/R_\star = 0.220^{+0.031}_{-0.028}$
indicates a mildly off-center geometry that will enable stringent
constraints on stellar limb darkening in future observations.

Figure~\ref{fig:lc} shows the phase-folded, binned \textit{TESS}
light curve together with our best-fit model.  The root-mean-square
scatter of the residuals is $\sigma_\mathrm{rms} = 38\,\mathrm{ppm}$,
consistent with photon-noise expectations.
