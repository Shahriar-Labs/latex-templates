\section{Stellar Parameters}
\label{sec:star}

We derived stellar parameters for TOI-4521 by fitting the broadband
spectral energy distribution (SED) with the \texttt{ARIADNE} code
\citep{Vines2022}, which simultaneously fits \textit{Gaia} DR3, 2MASS,
and WISE photometry against a grid of Phoenix stellar atmosphere models.
The best-fit parameters are $T_\mathrm{eff} = 5840 \pm 70\,\mathrm{K}$,
$\log g = 4.42 \pm 0.05$, $[\mathrm{Fe/H}] = -0.06 \pm 0.08$, and
$R_\star = 1.03 \pm 0.02\,R_\odot$, consistent with a G1\,V spectral type.
The stellar mass, $M_\star = 1.01 \pm 0.04\,M_\odot$, was inferred from
the empirical relations of \citet{Torres2010}.
