\section{Methods}
% ----------------------------------------------------------------

This review was registered on PROSPERO (CRD42024382910) and adheres to the
Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Synapse-Analyses (PRISMA) 2020
guidelines~\cite{Page2021}. Quantitative synthesis employed a bivariate random-effects
model; heterogeneity was quantified with \(I^2\) and Cochran's \(Q\) test.

\subsection{Eligibility Criteria}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Population:} Adult patients (\(\geq 18\) yr) with diabetes mellitus (type 1 or 2).
  \item \textbf{Index test:} Deep learning algorithm (CNN, ViT, or hybrid) classifying colour
        fundus photographs or OCT images.
  \item \textbf{Reference standard:} Ophthalmologist grading or validated grading scale
        (ETDRS, UK NSC, or equivalent), accepted if performed by \(\geq 2\) graders.
  \item \textbf{Outcomes:} Sensitivity, specificity, and area under the ROC curve (AUC)
        for referable DR (moderate NPDR or worse) or any DR.
  \item \textbf{Exclusion:} Conference abstracts without full data, studies with
        \(<200\) images, non-fundus modalities other than OCT, and non-English reports.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Search Strategy}

Searches were executed on 1 November 2024 across MEDLINE (via PubMed),
Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, and IEEE Xplore using Boolean combinations of controlled
vocabulary (MeSH/Emtree) and free-text terms:
\texttt{("diabetic retinopathy" OR "DR") AND ("deep learning" OR "convolutional neural
network" OR "vision transformer" OR "artificial intelligence") AND
("sensitivity" OR "specificity" OR "diagnostic accuracy")}.
Reference lists of included studies and relevant reviews were hand-searched.
