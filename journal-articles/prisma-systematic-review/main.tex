\textbf{\small Abstract.}\;{\small
Diabetic retinopathy (DR) is a leading cause of preventable blindness worldwide.
Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and vision transformers (ViTs) have demonstrated
promising screening performance, yet heterogeneity in model architectures, reference
standards, and patient populations hampers clinical translation. We systematically
searched MEDLINE, Embase, and IEEE Xplore for studies evaluating deep learning–based
DR grading systems (2015–2024), screened 4\,372 records, and included 41 studies
(\(n = 1{,}247{,}309\) fundus images). Pooled sensitivity was 92.4\% (95\% CI: 89.7–94.6)
and specificity was 90.1\% (87.3–92.5). Subgroup analyses revealed superior performance
for referable DR (AUC 0.97 vs.\ 0.91 for any DR). High risk of bias was present in
31\% of studies, primarily owing to retrospective single-site designs.
}
