\section{Conclusions}
This reproducible synthetic example shows how mobile-sensor research can distinguish the amount of street-tree canopy from its spatial continuity. In the generated data, both measures were independently associated with lower route-average heat exposure, especially in the afternoon. The findings are a demonstration of study design and reporting, not evidence about a real city. Future field studies can test whether continuity adds predictive and planning value when derived from observed street-level shade and calibrated thermal measurements.
