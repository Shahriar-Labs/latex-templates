\section{Deviations from Original Study}
\label{sec:deviations}
Although we aimed for a strict replication, a few notable deviations were introduced, either due to missing information in the original publication or compatibility requirements for the Vertex-VQA dataset.

\begin{deviationbox}{Summary of Deviations}
We identified three main areas of deviation:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Visual Feature Resolution:} The original study processed images at $320 \times 320$ pixels. We increased this to $448 \times 448$ pixels to preserve fine-grained spatial relationships on the denser Vertex-VQA images.
    \item \textbf{Object Detection Backbone:} The original paper utilized a proprietary, fine-tuned Faster R-CNN model. We substituted this with an open-source ResNet-101 based DETR detector.
    \item \textbf{Learning Rate Decay:} A cosine annealing scheduler was used instead of the original step decay because we observed optimization instability during early epochs on Vertex-VQA.
\end{enumerate}
\end{deviationbox}

\subsection{Impact of Backbone Substitution}
To quantify the impact of substituting the proprietary backbone with DETR, we ran an ablation study comparing the visual parser outputs.
Table~\ref{tab:ablation} illustrates that while object localization was comparable, attribute classification (color, material) was slightly less accurate in our open backbone, contributing to the overall $-1.3\%$ drop in downstream reasoning.
