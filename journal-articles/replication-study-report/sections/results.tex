\section{Results}
We successfully trained the NSR model on Vertex-VQA and evaluated it across five standard VQA question categories: Attribute Identification, Counting, Spatial Comparison, Single-Hop Relational, and Multi-Hop Relational.

\subsection{Performance Comparison}
Table~\ref{tab:results_comparison} compares the accuracy achieved in our replication study against the original results reported by Chen et al.~\cite{chen2024neural}.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Overall Accuracy Comparison by Question Category}
\label{tab:results_comparison}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l X X r}
\toprule
\textbf{Question Category} & \textbf{Original (\%)} & \textbf{Replication (\%)} & \textbf{Difference (\%)} \\
\midrule
Attribute Identification & $98.5$ & $98.2$ & $-0.3$ \\
Counting                 & $94.2$ & $93.8$ & $-0.4$ \\
Spatial Comparison       & $91.8$ & $91.0$ & $-0.8$ \\
Single-Hop Relational    & $89.5$ & $88.1$ & $-1.4$ \\
Multi-Hop Relational     & $85.3$ & $82.1$ & $-3.2$ \\
\midrule
\textbf{Overall Average}  & \textbf{91.9} & \textbf{90.6} & \textbf{-1.3} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\subsection{Effect Size Analysis}
To visualize the magnitude of differences across different task types, we present an effect size plot (Figure~\ref{fig:effect_size}) showing Cohen's $d$ and its 95\% confidence interval. For counting and attribute tasks, the difference is negligible ($d < 0.1$), whereas for multi-hop relational tasks, we observe a moderate effect ($d = 0.42$).
