\section{Data Synthesis and Presentation}

The synthesis and presentation of results will follow the PRISMA-ScR guidelines to ensure systematic and transparent reporting. We will combine quantitative mapping with qualitative thematic analysis to provide a comprehensive landscape of AI-driven trial matching.

\subsection{Quantitative Mapping and Analysis}
Basic descriptive statistics will be compiled for all included studies. We will analyze and present:
\begin{itemize}
  \item The chronological distribution of publications from 2018 to 2026.
  \item The frequency of clinical specialties (e.g., oncology, cardiology, rare diseases) using AI matching.
  \item The proportion of studies utilizing open-source versus proprietary models (such as Meridian Clinical Matcher or Nexa Clinical Suite).
  \item Technical metrics (accuracy, F1-score, AUC-ROC) summarized by model architecture class.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Qualitative and Thematic Synthesis}
We will conduct a thematic analysis of the qualitative text extracted from the discussion sections of the papers, focusing on the following areas:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Implementation Barriers:} Issues regarding integration with legacy Electronic Health Record (EHR) databases, data cleansing requirements, and system latency.
  \item \textbf{Algorithmic Bias:} The extent to which systems are evaluated for bias against minority populations, and whether demographic features are handled ethically.
  \item \textbf{Explainability:} Methods utilized to explain matching decisions to clinicians (e.g., SHAP/LIME values, highlighted text segments, or LLM-generated rationales).
\end{itemize}
