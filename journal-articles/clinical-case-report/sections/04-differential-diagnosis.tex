\section{Differential Diagnosis}

Given the presenting symptoms, the following differentials were considered:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Acute aortic dissection} (type A) — confirmed on CTA; supported by
        inter-arm BP differential and mediastinal widening.
  \item \textbf{Acute coronary syndrome} — troponin normal on two serial measurements;
        ECG non-ischaemic.
  \item \textbf{Pulmonary embolism} — D-dimer elevated but Wells score low (2 points);
        CT pulmonary angiogram negative for filling defect.
  \item \textbf{Musculoskeletal pain} — excluded by imaging findings and haemodynamic
        profile.
  \item \textbf{Oesophageal rupture (Boerhaave syndrome)} — no history of vomiting;
        excluded on CTA.
\end{enumerate}
