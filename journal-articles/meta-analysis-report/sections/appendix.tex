\section{Funnel Plot for Publication Bias}
\label{app:funnel}

Figure~\ref{fig:funnel} plots each trial's effect size against its standard error, with the
pooled estimate and its pseudo 95\% confidence funnel overlaid. Studies with smaller
standard errors (larger samples) cluster near the top and close to the pooled estimate;
studies with larger standard errors scatter more widely toward the base of the funnel. The
broadly symmetric distribution around the vertical reference line, together with the
non-significant Egger's test reported in Section~\ref{sec:pubbias}, does not support the presence of
material publication or small-study bias in this set of trials.
