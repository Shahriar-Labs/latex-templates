We measured the throughput of our system across varying batch sizes and compared it with the standalone NexaCore design. As shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:throughput}, the tightly-coupled SynapseLink + NexaCore architecture achieves significantly higher throughput at small batch sizes because the neuromorphic scheduler bypasses the overhead of traditional DMA transfers.
