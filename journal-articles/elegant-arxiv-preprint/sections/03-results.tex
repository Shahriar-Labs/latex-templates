\section{Results}
We evaluate the performance of \texttt{DeepTransient} on a simulated LSST dataset containing 50,000 light curves across four main classes: Type Ia Supernovae (SN Ia), Core-Collapse Supernovae (SN CC), Tidal Disruption Events (TDE), and Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN).

\subsection{Performance Comparison}
Table~\ref{tab:metrics} shows the comparison of \texttt{DeepTransient} with baseline models. Our model achieves a micro-averaged F1-score of 0.950, outperforming the classical Random Forest baseline by more than 10\%.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Classification metrics (Precision, Recall, and F1-score) of different models evaluated on the simulated Rubin LSST transient dataset.}
\label{tab:metrics}
\vspace{0.3cm}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Classifier Model} & \textbf{Precision} & \textbf{Recall} & \textbf{F1-score} \\
\midrule
Random Forest (Baseline) & 0.852 & 0.841 & 0.846 \\
Gradient Boosted Trees   & 0.871 & 0.865 & 0.868 \\
LSTM (Photometry only)   & 0.893 & 0.887 & 0.890 \\
\textbf{DeepTransient}    & \textbf{0.954} & \textbf{0.946} & \textbf{0.950} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
