\section{Discussion and Future Work}
The performance gains of \texttt{DeepTransient} are primarily driven by the joint learning of temporal features and host galaxy contextual metadata. Incorporating spatial coordinates and host galaxy morphology is expected to further improve classifier confidence, particularly for rare events such as kilonovae \cite{mutschler2021, villar2020}. Future work will focus on deploying the pipeline within an active broker framework to process live alert streams in real time.
