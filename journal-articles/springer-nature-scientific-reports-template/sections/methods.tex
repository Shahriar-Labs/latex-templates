\section{Methods}

\subsection{Membrane synthesis}

Graphene oxide was prepared from graphite flakes (Nimbus Materials Institute, battery grade) via a modified Hummers method, oxidizing in a 9:1 H\textsubscript{2}SO\textsubscript{4}/H\textsubscript{3}PO\textsubscript{4} mixture with KMnO\textsubscript{4} at 50\textdegree C for 12~h, followed by peroxide quenching and repeated centrifugal washing to neutral pH. Vertex-type transition-metal oxide nanoparticles (nominal diameter 8--12~nm, synthesized separately by co-precipitation and characterized by transmission electron microscopy) were dispersed in the GO suspension at 5\% and 10\% w/w relative to GO mass by 30~min of bath sonication, yielding the Vertex-GO-5 and Vertex-GO-10 precursor dispersions. Membranes were formed by vacuum filtration of each dispersion (2.0~mg total solids) onto 0.22~$\mu$m mixed-cellulose-ester supports, air-dried at 40\textdegree C for 24~h, and peeled to yield free-standing laminates approximately 18~$\mu$m thick, measured by cross-sectional scanning electron microscopy at five points per membrane.

\subsection{Batch adsorption isotherms}

Synthetic Cd\textsuperscript{2+} and Pb\textsuperscript{2+} solutions were prepared from Cd(NO\textsubscript{3})\textsubscript{2} and Pb(NO\textsubscript{3})\textsubscript{2} at concentrations of 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, and 500~mg~L\textsuperscript{-1}, buffered to pH~5.5 with 0.01~M acetate buffer to avoid metal hydroxide precipitation. Membrane discs (10~mg) were equilibrated in 50~mL of each solution at 25\textdegree C with orbital shaking (150~rpm) for 24~h, a duration confirmed by preliminary kinetic trials to reach equilibrium for all three membranes. Residual metal concentration was measured by inductively coupled plasma optical emission spectroscopy, and adsorption capacity was calculated by mass balance. Langmuir and Freundlich isotherm parameters were obtained by nonlinear least-squares fitting in R (v4.3).

\subsection{Regeneration cycling}

For each cycle, membranes were equilibrated in 100~mg~L\textsuperscript{-1} Pb\textsuperscript{2+} solution for 6~h, rinsed with deionized water, and desorbed in 0.1~M EDTA for 2~h to strip bound Pb\textsuperscript{2+}, followed by a further deionized-water rinse before the next adsorption stage. Removal efficiency at each cycle was calculated as $(C_0 - C_e)/C_0 \times 100$, where $C_0$ and $C_e$ are the initial and post-adsorption Pb\textsuperscript{2+} concentrations. Five cycles were run in triplicate for each membrane composition.

\subsection{Structural characterization}

Interlayer $d$-spacing was determined from the (001) reflection of powder X-ray diffraction patterns (Cu K$\alpha$, $\lambda = 0.154$~nm, 5--30\textdegree\ 2$\theta$) using Bragg's law, on dry membrane samples equilibrated at 40\% relative humidity for 48~h prior to measurement. Specific surface area was measured by N\textsubscript{2} adsorption--desorption at 77~K using the Brunauer--Emmett--Teller method after degassing at 80\textdegree C for 12~h. All structural measurements were performed on three independently synthesized membrane batches per composition.

\subsection{Statistical analysis}

Isotherm fits, cycling trends, and BET surface-area comparisons are reported as mean $\pm$ standard deviation across three replicates. Differences in BET surface area across membrane compositions were assessed by one-way ANOVA with a significance threshold of $\alpha = 0.05$.
