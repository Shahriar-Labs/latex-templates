\section{Data and Sample}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{SAMI observations}

The SAMI Galaxy Survey \citep{Croom2012} used a multi-object IFU
spectrograph on the 3.9-m Anglo-Australian Telescope to observe
$\sim$3000 galaxies at $0.004 < z < 0.095$.
Each galaxy is observed with a hexabundle of 61 optical fibres,
providing a $15''$ diameter field of view with $\sim$$2''$ seeing.
The blue-arm spectra span $3700$--$5700\,\mathrm{\AA}$ at
$R \approx 1800$; the red arm covers $6300$--$7400\,\mathrm{\AA}$
at $R \approx 4300$.

From the full SAMI ETG subsample we select galaxies satisfying:
(1) effective radius $\re > 5''$ to ensure at least five independent
    radial bins within $2\,\re$;
(2) median signal-to-noise ratio $\mathrm{S/N} > 20$ per
    \AA\ at $1\,\re$;
(3) no evidence of AGN emission based on the \citet{Kewley2001}
    diagnostic; and
(4) stellar mass $\log(M_{\star}/\Msun) > 10.0$.
This yields 32 ETGs spanning $10.0 < \log(M_{\star}/\Msun) < 11.6$
and covering a range of environments from field to cluster outskirts
(Table~\ref{tab:sample}).

\subsection{Radial binning}

To maximise S/N while preserving spatial resolution, we collapse
each IFU datacube into elliptical annuli aligned with the galaxy
photometric position angle and ellipticity measured from
\textit{r}-band imaging.
Bin boundaries are placed at
$r/\re \in \{0.1, 0.3, 0.6, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0\}$,
giving five independent radial bins per galaxy.
